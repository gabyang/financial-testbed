Should I wear a mask? Readers share their lingering coronavirus questions

  • Updated: Apr. 03, 2020, 1:15 p.m.
  • |Published: Apr. 03, 2020, 1:09 p.m.
By
  • Maura Zurick, cleveland.com

CLEVELAND, Ohio – As Ohioans adjust to the “new normal” imposed upon them by the extended Stay-at-Home order, more coronavirus questions keep popping up, including whether people should be wearing masks when they leave home and when will we see toilet paper on shelves again.

As we have been doing for several days, Friday we asked readers on cleveland.com’s Facebook, Twitter and Instagram pages what coronavirus questions they have on their minds. Some have been edited for length and clarity.

  • Should I be wearing a mask?
  • Can grocery stores work together and rotate one day off a week for each so employees can get a break and stores can close for sanitization?
  • For businesses that deem themselves essential and stay open, are they offering hazard pay for employees who have to work?
  • Is it necessary for Gov. Mike DeWine to hold a press conference every day when it doesn’t seem like there is new information every day?
  • How long does the virus stay in your system? When is it safe for infected people to be around loved ones?
  • Has the virus mutated? How many strains are there?
  • Can people be immune? If so, for how long?
  • How should I be handling my mail?
  • What is being done to address hoarding? Why is there still no toilet paper on shelves?
  • Why can’t regular people buy gloves and masks? Places like Home Depot and Amazon are only selling to medical professionals. What about the public who have other health concerns other than coronavirus and need these items?
  • What is being done to address the Amish community? I live in an area with a lot and they are still having large gatherings and going to church.
