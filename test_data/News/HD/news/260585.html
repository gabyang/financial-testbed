Home Depot hiring 200 for McCalla Distribution Center

  • Published: Apr. 06, 2020, 12:17 p.m.
By
  • William Thornton | wthornton@al.com

The Home Depot is looking to fill 200 positions at its McCalla Distribution Center.

The home retailer says the positions are for both part-time and full-time seasonal positions. Distribution center employees help fulfill online orders and replenish store inventory.

The company has also introduced several temporary benefits, including bonuses and double overtime, as a result of the COVID-19 outbreak.

Through April 19, hourly employees in stores and distribution centers who work more than 35 hours per week will receive an additional $100 per week, with hourly associates working 16 to 35 hours per week receiving an additional $50 per week.

Home Depot is also offering double-time pay to hourly employees for overtime hours worked between now and April 30.
