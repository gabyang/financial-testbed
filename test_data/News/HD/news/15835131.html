Do you want ants? Because not having a robot vacuum cleaner is how you get ants. Set up the bObsweep Pro and clean up all those crumbs and little bits that get dropped when you're not looking. This vacuum cleaner has plenty of power and intelligence to clean your floors and do it autonomously. It can handle up to 1,000 square feet by itself.

The robot vacuum's battery lasts for up to 100 minutes on a single charge. Plus, it's smart enough to detect when its battery is running low. When that happens, the robot part of the vacuum will send it back to its charging dock to juice up. It'll then get back to work finishing whatever job it was on.

When it comes to work, the bObsweep Pro can handle quite a bit. It can clean multiple surface types, and it switches between carpet and hard floor easily enough. You can set it up to clean multiple rooms at once, too. The vacuum can be scheduled to clean when it's the most convenient for you. Use the programmable schedule and tell the bObsweep exactly when to get to work.

The 0.1-gallon dust cup collects a large amount of dirt, and it makes emptying a simple chore. Plus the bin is easily washable, which makes maintenance a breeze. It comes with several tools for different jobs, and it's covered by a one-year warranty.
