THE HOME DEPOT TO PRESENT AT THE MORGAN STANLEY GLOBAL CONSUMER & RETAIL VIRTUAL CONFERENCE

Published: 2021-11-29 08:26:11 ET HD

ATLANTA- The Home Depot, the world's largest home improvement retailer, announced that Richard McPhail, executive vice president and chief financial officer, will present at the Morgan Stanley Global Consumer & Retail Virtual Conference. The presentation will begin at 12:10 p.m. ET on Thursday, December 2, 2021.

The Home Depot logo. (PRNewsFoto/The Home Depot) (PRNewsFoto/)

The presentation will be webcast live at http://ir.homedepot.com/events-and-presentations. A link will be displayed under 'Events and Presentations.' The webcast will be archived and available at the same location approximately one hour after conclusion of the live event.

The Home Depot is the world's largest home improvement specialty retailer. At the end of the third quarter, the Company operated a total of 2,317 retail stores in all 50 states, the District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, Guam, 10 Canadian provinces and Mexico, including 14 stores in the U.S. from a small acquisition completed during the second quarter of fiscal 2021. The Company employs approximately 500,000 associates. The Home Depot's stock is traded on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: HD) and is included in the Dow Jones industrial average and Standard & Poor's 500 index.
