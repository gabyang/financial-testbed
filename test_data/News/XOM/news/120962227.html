CorpGovhosted a fireside chat from the floor of the New York Stock Exchange with Eamon Nolan, Partner at Vinson & Elkins. Mr. Nolan was joined by CorpGov Editor-in-Chief John Jannarone to discuss the rapid pace of investment in solar, wind, nuclear and other sources of energy, challenges around early-stage energy sources such as hydrogen, bringing green energy to emerging markets and more. About Eamon Nolan

Eamon Nolan is a project finance attorney who focuses on large infrastructure and energy projects. Eamon regularly represents private equity sponsors, banks, and developers in financing transactions involving the acquisition, development, and divestment of infrastructure and energy assets in the U.S. and Latin America. He has significant experience advising on financing project developments in the renewables, hydrogen, power generation, LNG, onshore and offshore oil and gas, telecommunications and transportation sectors.

About Vinson & Elkins

For more than a century, Vinson & Elkins has provided outstanding client service across important industries that drive the global economy. Built on a strong culture of collaboration across 11 offices worldwide, V&E lawyers are committed to excellence, offering clients decades of legal experience in handling transactions, investments, projects and disputes across the globe.
