Boris suffers first post-No 10 blow as JP Morgan rubbishes £2m speech report

Published: 2022-09-08 01:41:08 ET JPM

JP MORGAN has not offered Boris Johnson a lucrative postpremiership speaking deal, despite reports to the contrary.

It was reported this week that Johnson had been offered £2m to give six speeches at JP Morgan events.

However, sources at the investment bank have told City A.M. that Johnson has not been courted for any speaking engagements and that it is highly unlikely he will ever receive an offer.

It has been speculated that

Johnson will not receive a warm welcome from some City institutions post-premiership, after being forced out under a cloud of repeated scandals.

JP Morgan's chief executive Jamie Dimon is also a staunch critic of Brexit and said last year that leaving the EU "cannot possibly be a positive" for the UK.

It is understood that Johnson wants to make millions of pounds in his first year out of office through speaking and writing engagements.

Persistent rumours suggest the former Prime Minister has had financial issues over the past three years, which have been exacerbated by the arrival of two new children with his wife Carrie.

The international speaking circuit has often been lucrative for former world leaders, with Theresa May making more than £1m between 2019 and 2021 for her efforts.

A spokesperson for JP Morgan declined to comment.
