Headhunters miss mark as bank goes back around

Published: 2022-03-24 02:21:12 ET JPM

WHAT's taking so long? It's been over a year since JP Morgan started looking for a new chairman for its main investment banking and trading legal entity in the UK. Sir Win Bischoff, who took up the role at the helm of JP Morgan Securities plc, must have been hoping to have put his feet up by now. After one preferred candidate fell through, though, I'm told the Wall Street behemoth has instructed headhunters to start a fresh search. The result will be intriguing.
