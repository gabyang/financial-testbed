Chevron: Q4 Earnings Snapshot

Published: 2024-02-02 06:23:03 ET CVX

SAN RAMON, Calif. (AP) — SAN RAMON, Calif. (AP) — Chevron Corp. (CVX) on Friday reported fourth-quarter earnings of $2.26 billion.

On a per-share basis, the San Ramon, California-based company said it had profit of $1.22. Earnings, adjusted for non-recurring costs, were $3.45 per share.

The results topped Wall Street expectations, but Chevron does not adjust its reported results based on one-time events such as asset sales. The average estimate of eight analysts surveyed by Zacks Investment Research was for earnings of $3.29 per share.

The oil company posted revenue of $47.18 billion in the period, falling short of Street forecasts. Six analysts surveyed by Zacks expected $52.59 billion.
