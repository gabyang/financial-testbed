What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Tesla (TSLA) reported better-than-expected Q1 sales and earnings, with the company repeating its call to grow manufacturing capacity "as quickly as possible" [more]
  • Elon Musk confirmed he is exploring whether to commence a tender offer to acquire Twitter (TWTR) [more]
  • AT&T (T) reported better-than-expected Q1 results, with 691,000 postpaid phone net adds in the quarter [more]
  • Carvana (CVNA) reported downbeat Q1 losses and said it won't provide specific near-term guidance for the year [more]
  • Alcoa (AA) reported upbeat Q1 earnings but lower-than-expected revenue [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS: 

  • Exxon Mobil (XOM) upgraded to Outperform from Sector Perform at RBC Capital, which paired the change with a downgrade of Chevron (CVX) to Sector Perform [more]
  • Morgan Stanley double upgraded M&T Bank (MTB) to Overweight from Underweight as analyst Betsy Graseck argued that rate sensitivity "trumps" credit fears [more]
  • E2open (ETWO) upgraded to Buy at BofA on accelerating revenue growth [more]
  • Carvana (CVNA) downgraded to In Line from Outperform at Evercore ISI [more]
  • Truist cut Twitter (TWTR) to Hold, sees Musk's offer as "fair" [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB: 

  • Meta (FB) COO Sheryl Sandberg pressured a tabloid to shelve articles on Activision Blizzard (ATVI) CEO Bobby Kotick, WSJ reports [more]
  • A judge has dismissed a class-action lawsuit against Activision Blizzard, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Exxon Mobil (XOM) is weighing a complete withdrawal from Russia by June 24, Reuters reports [more]
  • Delta (DAL) and United (UAL) will end restrictions on flyers banned over mask violations, FT reports [more]
  • Kolon made an offer to MagnaChip (MX) but is no longer pursuing a deal, MBN reports [more]


﻿4. MOVERS: 

  • Nucor (NUE) gains after saying it expects Q2 to be the “most profitable quarter in Nucor’s history” [more]
  • Liberty Oilfield Services (LBRT) and Steel Dynamics (STLD) advance after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Rite Aid (RAD) declines after confirming the rejection of Spear Point Capital acquisition proposal [more]
  • Didi (DIDI) falls in New York after Nikkei reported the company is shuttering its Japanese food delivery business [more]
  • Freeport McMoRan (FCX) lower after reporting quarterly results [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE: 

  • American Airlines (AAL) reported upbeat Q1 results [more]
  • United Airlines (UAL) reported downbeat Q1 results [more]
  • Equifax (EFX) reported upbeat Q1 earnings and revenue but cut its FY22 guidance [more]
  • Xerox (XRX) reported downbeat Q1 earnings [more]
  • Tractor Supply (TSCO) reported upbeat Q1 results and reiterated its FY22 guidance [more]


INDEXES: 

Near midday, the Dow was up 32.12, or 0.09%, to 35,192.91, the Nasdaq was down 71.89, or 0.53%), to 13,381.18, and the S&P 500 was down 8.07, or 0.18%, to 4,451.38.
