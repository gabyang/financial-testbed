What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS: 

  • Conagra Brands (CAG) reported upbeat Q1 results and reiterated its FY23 earnings guidance [more]
  • Costco (COST) reported September sales grew 10.1% from last year [more]
  • Shell (SHEL) expects its Q3 integrated gas results to be significantly lower than Q2 [more]
  • Constellation Brands (STZ) reported upbeat quarterly results and agreed to divest a portion of its wine portfolio to The Wine Group [more]
  • McCormick (MKC) reported lower-than-expected Q3 sales and earnings [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS: 

  • Pinterest (PINS) upgraded to Buy at Goldman Sachs amid improved user growth trends [more]
  • Opco upgrades Verizon (VZ), says "price is right" after years of underperformance [more]
  • Splunk (SPLK) downgraded to Neutral from Buy at UBS [more]
  • Take-Two (TTWO) upgraded to Buy from Neutral at Goldman Sachs [more]
  • Credit Suisse (CS) upgraded to Neutral from Underweight at JPMorgan [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB: 

  • Compass (COMP) denied a report of takeover interest from Vista Equity, Bloomberg says [more]
  • The U.S is proposing to ease Venezuela sanctions and allow Chevron (CVX) to pump oil, WSJ reports [more]
  • Apple (AAPL) told Reuters it plans to appeal a French court ruling on a sharply lowered antitrust fine [more]
  • Peloton (PTON) will cut 500 more jobs in a turnaround effort, WSJ reports [more]
  • Honda (HMC) will reduce car output at two Japanese plants in October, Reuters says [more]


4. MOVERS: 

  • Provention Bio (PRVB) rises after announcing a co-promotion pact with Sanofi (SNY) [more]
  • Transocean (RIG) jumps after Barclays double upgraded the stock to Overweight from Underweight [more]
  • AMMO (POWW) higher after clarifying its spin-off metrics [more]
  • AngioDynamics (ANGO) lower after reporting downbeat quarterly results [more]
  • Semtech (SMTC) falls after offering to sell $250M in convertible senior notes due 2027 [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE: 

  • Landec (LNDC) reported upbeat Q1 results [more]
  • Titan International (TWI) provided conservative Q3 revenue guidance [more]
  • Perion (PERI) reported upbeat preliminary Q3 revenue [more]
  • Resources Connection (RGP) reported better-than-expected Q1 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Integer (ITGR) cut its FY22 earnings and revenue guidance [more]


INDEXES: 

Near midday, the Dow was down 227.14, or 0.75%, to 30,046.73, the Nasdaq was down 62.33, or 0.56%, to 11,086.30, and the S&P 500 was down 27.15, or 0.72%, to 3,756.13.
