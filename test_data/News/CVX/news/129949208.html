What You Missed On Wall Street On Wednesday
Mid/Close Wrap
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • The Federal Reserve cut the federal funds rate by 50 basis points, citing progress on inflation and the "balance of risks" for its decisive policy adjustment
  • BlackRock (BLK), GIP, Microsoft (MSFT), and MGX confirmed an AI pacton data center investment
  • Organon (OGN) will acquire Dermavant from Roivant Sciences (ROIV) for $1.2B
  • Rogers Communications (RCI) has acquired BCE's (BCE) 37.5% stake in Maple Leaf Sports and Entertainment for $3.5B
  • T-Mobile (TMUS) announced a collaboration with Nvidia (NVDA), Ericsson (ERIC), and Nokia (NOK), announced a multi-year agreement with OpenAI, and raised its quarterly dividend 35%

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Nvidia (NVDA) initiatedwith bullish view at William Blair
  • GE HealthCare (GEHC) upgradedto Buy at BTIG
  • ResMed (RMD) downgradedto Underperform at Wolfe Research
  • VF Corp. (VFC) upgradedat Barclays, here's why
  • Edwards Lifesciences (EW) downgradedat Jefferies, here's why

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Nippon Steel (NPSCY) is slated to get an extension in the security review over its proposed takeover of U.S. Steel (X), Bloomberg reports
  • Google (GOOGL) offered to divest its AdX business to allay EU antitrust concerns, Reuters reports
  • Boeing (BA) will furlough tens of thousands of employees, CNBC reports
  • Meta Platforms (META) is facing an EU fine over its alleged efforts to dominate the classified advertising market, FT says
  • Chevron (CVX) CEO Mike Wirth says the Biden administration's oil and gas policy pushes up prices and "undermines energy security" for U.S. allies, FT reports

4. MOVERS:

  • Applied Therapeutics (APLT) gains after providing an update on its ongoing NDA review of govorestat
  • Intuitive Machines (LUNR) higher after announcing it was awarded the maximum valueof $4.82B for a NSN contract by NASA
  • PowerFleet (AIOT) increases after announcing it will acquire Fleet Complete for $200M
  • Casella Waste (CWST) falls after announcing a 4.5M share offering priced at $100
  • Trump Media (DJT) lower a day after Bloomberg reported Delaware Chancery Court Judge Lori Will ruled Patrick Orlando is entitled to almost 8.2M Class A shares of the company, rather than the 7.4M he was allotted

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • General Mills (GIS) reported Q1 results and backed its guidance for FY25
  • Aspen Technology (AZPN) reaffirmed its guidance for FY25
  • Nano Labs (NA) reported 1H of 2024 results, with EPS higher year-over-year
  • At its investor day yesterday, Electronic Arts (EA) said the company is on track towards the high end of its net bookings guidance for the second fiscal quarter and the fiscal year
  • FedEx (FDX) will report Q1 resultson September 19

INDEXES:

The Dow fell 103.08, or 0.25%, to 41,503.10, the Nasdaq lost 54.76, or 0.31%, to 17,573.30, and the S&P 500 declined 16.32, or 0.29%, to 5,618.26.
