34 Fuel Exporting Countries in the World

Published on September 25, 2023 at 7:08 am by Zarah Hamid in Commodities, Industries, Lists, News
Share Tweet Email

In this article, we will be studying the global fuel industry. We will also be looking at the current market conditions and some of the big players in this industry while covering the major fuel exporting countries in the world. If you wish to skip our detailed analysis, you can move directly to the5 Fuel Exporting Countries in the World.

The Fuel Industry: An Overview

Fuel, as a source of energy, powers the world. The fuel industry is diverse, ranging from fossil fuels such as crude oil and natural gas to solid fuels namely wood and coal. While upstream companies in the fuel industry generate cash flows from extracting oil and gas from reserves, downstream beneficiaries of oil and gas production include refineries that remove impurities from crude oil to convert it into gasoline, jet fuel, heating oil, and others. Oil and gas reserves around the world are declared proven if there is a 90% or greater certainty of them being present and economically viable for extraction.

Current Market Conditions

According to an outlook of the oil and gas industry for 2023, Deloitte reportedthat issues such as supply chain disruptions and fluctuation in oil prices have been common. Oil and gas companies have also been resorting to cleaner energy sources. There has also been a focus on reducing greenhouse gas emissions associated with natural gas.

In September, the International Energy Agency reportedan analysis of the global oil market for different regions around the world. In August, the Russian oil exports declined but the revenues from these exports rose due to high prices. China and India accounted for the majority of Russian oil exports. The oil supplies from Saudi Arabia also reduced which is further expected to create a market deficit in the fourth quarter of 2023, according to the IEA.

On September 7, Reuters reportedthat Russia and Saudi Arabia have further announced to extend their supply cuts till the end of 2023. The higher oil prices resulting from this tight supply are interwoven with global politics as the US believes that Russia needs to be prevented from earning higher revenues from rising oil prices which it uses to fund the Ukrainian war. While the European Union has imposed caps on Russian exports of oil, oil continues to be traded at higher prices.

A Competitive Landscape

The oil and gas industry is saturated with different incumbents who continue to strive for their market positions. Some of these include Exxon Mobil Corporation (NYSE:XOM), Chevron Corporation (NYSE:CVX), and Shell plc (NYSE:SHEL).

Exxon Mobil Corporation (NYSE:XOM) is one of the largest oil and gas companies both in terms of market value and production. The company is vertically integrated across the oil and gas industry and also serves to be a significant global oil refiner. On August 28, Reuters reported that the company has extended its carbon capture joint development partnership with FuelCell Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ:FCEL) till March 2024. FuelCell Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ:FCEL) is a leading manufacturer and distributor of fuel cell energy platforms. FuelCell Energy, Inc. (NASDAQ:FCEL) has been helping the firm through its carbonate fuel cells which reduce carbon dioxide emissions in the power generation process. Another recent development by the company is the startup of two new chemical production units at its Baytown facility. On September 19, the company reportedthat this $2 billion expansion was aimed to fulfill the rising demand for high-value products in the market. The company has also entered the linear alpha olefins market through its new unit which will produce 350,000 tons of branded products under the name Elevexx, the company’s new linear alpha olefins-producing subsidiary.

Another leader in the oil and gas industry is Chevron Corporation (NYSE:CVX). The company engages in the exploration, production, and refining of hydrocarbons. It is also one of the largest oil companies in the United States, by revenue. On September 8, the company’s Australian LNG plants at Gorgon and Wheatstone, which supply more than 5% of the global LNG, became subjected to labor strikes. On September 22, Reuters reportedthat full production at the company’s facilities had resumed as the company agreed to resolve all outstanding issues of the workers. The Offshore Alliance representing Chevron Corporation’s (NYSE:CVX) worker unions, called off strikes after accepting a deal from Australia’s Fair Work Commission. However, the details of the agreement have not yet been publicly disclosed.

Shell plc (NYSE:RDS) is a leading British multinational oil and gas company. On September 22, Reuters reportedthat the company entered into an agreement with Trinidad and Tobago’s National Gas Company to supply it with natural gas till 2036. The company will be supplying a minimum of 150 million cubic feet per day of gas to Trinidad and Tobago’s National Gas Company from the Manatee offshore field which is shared by Trinidad and Venezuela and is expected to supply gas from 2028. This agreement will also support Trinidad and Tobago with its petrochemical and power sectors which are subject to gas shortages and reduced refining capacities currently.

Now that we have taken a look at the global fuel industry and what the major players such as Exxon Mobil Corporation (NYSE:XOM), Chevron Corporation (NYSE:CVX), and Shell plc (NYSE:SHEL)are up to, we can examine the 34 fuel exporting countries in the world.

Methodology

In order to compile a list of the 34 fuel exporting economies in the world, we sourced data from the UN World Economic Situation and Prospectsreport from 2023. We referred to the fuel exporting countries in this report. These countries belong to developed, developing, and in-transit economies in the world.

We have used the percentage of fuel exports in total exports as our metric for identifying every fuel exporting country. These percentages have been extracted from the country profiles listed by the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD). According to UNCTAD, a country is considered a fuel exporter if its fuel exports account for greater than 20% of its total merchandise exports where the fuels include coal, oil, and natural gas. This criteria was the rationale behind our metric for this research.

Thus, we have ranked the 34 fuel exporting countries in the world in ascending order of their fuel exports as a percentage of the total exports.

34 Fuel Exporting Countries in the World:

34. Bolivia (Plurinational State of)

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 24%

Bolivia exports a major portion of its local oil and gas production. Proven natural gas reserves are significant in the country. In 2022, Bolivia exported fuel accounting for 24% of its total exports.

33. Indonesia

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 34%

Located in Asia, Indonesia is a major fuel exporter. In 2022, 34% of the country’s total exports were of fuel. Indonesia is a major coal producer and gas supplier in South East Asia.

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 36%

Bahrain is a major producer and exporter of oil, natural gas and petroleum products. The national economy heavily depends on oil and gas. The country exported fuel accounting for 36% of its total exports in 2022.

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 36%

Ghana exported fuel accounting for 36% of its total exports in 2022. This percentage is due to the several oil fields in the country which contribute to the overall oil production.

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 36%

Crude oil is one of the most important exports of Ecuador and contributes heavily to the country’s total exports. The United States, Chile and Panama are its main export destinations. In 2022, 36% of the country’s total exports were of fuel.

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 41%

Papua New Guinea is a leading global exporter of fuel. The country exported fuel accounting for 41% of its total exports in 2022.

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 47%

Another prominent country in the fuel export industry is Mozambique. The country is located in Africa and is known to hold one of the largest reserves of natural gas in the region, contributing to its fuel exports which were 47% of the total exports in 2022.

27. United Arab Emirates

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 48%

The United Arab Emirates holds abundant oil reserves. It is also a major oil supplier in the global market due to its strategic location in the Middle East. In 2022, the UAE exported fuel equivalent to 48% of its total exports, making it one of the biggest fuel exporters in the world.

26. Yemen

Percentage of Fuel Exports in Total Exports: 50%

Based in Western Asia, Yemen is known for its large fuel exports. The country is known to hold sufficient oil and natural gas reserves. Yemen exported fuel accounting for 50% of its total exports in 2022.

Cameroon also makes it to the list of the 34 fuel exporting nations in the world. The country exported fuel accounting for 55% of its total exports in 2022. Its high fuel exports are a result of the many proven crude oil reserves in the country.
