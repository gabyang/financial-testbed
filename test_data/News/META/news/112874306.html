What You Missed On Wall Street On Thursday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Walgreens Boots Alliance (WBA) reported upbeat Q3 results and reiterated its FY22 earnings outlook [more]
  • Pfizer (PFE) announced a new $3.2B deal with the U.S. government over COVID-19 vaccines and submitted an NDA to the FDA for PAXLOVID [more]
  • Constellation Brands (STZ) reported earnings and announced an agreement to eliminate its Class B common stock [more]
  • Universal Health (UHS) cut its FY22 earnings and revenue guidance [more]
  • RH (RH) cut its FY22 revenue growth guidance [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Analysts at Jefferies made a number of rating changes among their REIT coverage, including four upgrades and eight downgrades [more]
  • AMD (AMD) upgraded to Outperform at Northland [more]
  • Poshmark (POSH) downgraded to Market Perform from Outperform at Raymond James [more]
  • William Blair upgrades ShotSpotter (SSTI) to Outperform with 60% upside seen over two years [more]
  • Exane BNP Paribas downgraded SAP (SAP) to Neutral from Outperform [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Novartis (NVS) is leaning towards a Sandoz spinoff over a sale, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Dealreporter said the "odds seem good" that Icahn and Deason push for a Xerox (XRX) sale [more]
  • Kraft Heinz (KHC) has puled certain products from Tesco (TSCDY) amid a pricing dispute, Reuters says [more]
  • Coinbase (COIN) is seeking European licenses to boost international growth, CNBC reports [more]
  • Comcast's (CMCSA) NBCUniversal upfront meetings conclude with revenue commitments of over $7B, Adweek reports [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • FIGS (FIGS) up after Raymond James initiated coverage of the stock with a Strong Buy rating and $15 price target [more]
  • Spirit Airlines (SAVE) higher after announcing it intends to open and immediately adjourn its special meeting of stockholders relating to the proposed merger agreement with Frontier Group (ULCC) [more]
  • Duck Creek Technologies (DCT) falls after company's Q3 earnings report [more]
  • SGH (SGH) falls after reporting upbeat Q3 results and announced it will acquire Stratus Technologies [more]
  • Meta (META) lower after Chief Product Officer Chris Cox said in a memo seen by Reuters that the company must "execute flawlessly in an environment of slower growth" [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Acuity Brands (AYI) reported better-than-expected Q3 results [more]
  • AMMO (POWW) reported upbeat FY22 earnings and revenue [more]
  • MillerKnoll (MLKN) reported better-than-expected Q4 results [more]
  • Culp (CULP) reported a year-over-year decline in Q4 earnings [more]
  • Nemaura Medical (NMRD) reported downbeat 2022 earnings [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow fell 253.88, or 0.82%, to 30,775.43, the Nasdaq lost 149.16, or 1.33%, to 11,028.74, and the S&P 500 declined 33.45, or 0.88%, to 3,785.38.
