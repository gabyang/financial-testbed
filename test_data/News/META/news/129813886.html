What You Missed On Wall Street On Tuesday
Mid/Close Wrap
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Oracle (ORCL) reported upbeat Q1 results, with Cloud Revenue up 21% year-over-year
  • Analysts had somewhat lukewarm reactions to Apple's (AAPL) showcase on Monday, with Bernstein calling the AI demo "somewhat underwhelming" and JPMorgan saying the event offered limited surprises
  • The European Union's Court of Justice ruled against Apple in a EUR13B Ireland tax case, as the Court also upheld a EUR2.4B fine on Google (GOOGL)
  • Federal Reserve Vice Chair for Supervision Michael Barr said that"material changes" are warrantedto bank capital requirement proposals
  • New Starbucks (SBUX) CEO Brian Niccol has vowed to focus initially on the company's U.S. turnaround

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Costco (COST) downgradedto Neutral from Buy at Redburn Atlantic
  • Alphabet (GOOGL) initiatedwith neutral view at DA Davidson
  • Dell Technologies (DELL) initiatedwith a Neutral at Susquehanna on AI uncertainties
  • Meta Platforms (META) initiatedwith bullish view at DA Davidson
  • JMP Securities upgradedOracle (ORCL) to Outperform after the company reported better than expected fiscal Q1 results

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Two Delta (DAL) planes collided at an airport in Atlanta on Tuesday morning, NY Times reports
  • The FTC has informed Seven & I (SVNDF) of its intention to probe the Couche-Tard (ANCTF) deal,Reuters says
  • Norfolk Southern (NSC) CEO Alan Shaw is expected to leave the company as soon as this week, WSJ says
  • Honda (HMC) is cutting its workforce at its second joint venture in China, Nikkei Asia says
  • Amazon (AMZN) and Uber (UBER) are in the running for French start-up Livmed's, l'Informe reports

4. MOVERS:

  • Terns Pharmaceuticals (TERN) gains after announcing a$125M stock offering
  • Centessa (CNTA) higher in New York afterannouncing interim data from ongoing Phase 1 trial of ORX750
  • Viridian Therapeutics (VRDN) increases after announcing data from THRIVE trial of VRDN-001
  • Ionis Pharmaceuticals (IONS) lower after announcing a $500M stock offering
  • Relay Therapeutics (RLAY) falls after announcing a $200M stock offering

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Boot Barn (BOOT) reported August same-store sales rose 6%
  • Mission Produce (AVO) reported Q3 results, with CEO Steve Barnard commenting, "We are pleased to report another quarter of strong financial performance"
  • Rubrik (RBRK) reported Q2 resultsand raised its guidance for FY25
  • Cognyte (CGNT) reported Q2 results, which CEO Elad Sharon called "strong"
  • Academy Sports (ASO) reported Q2 resultsand provided guidance for FY24

INDEXES:

The Dow fell 92.63, or 0.23%, to 40,736.96, the Nasdaq gained 141.28, or 0.84%, to 17,025.88, and the S&P 500 advanced 24.47, or 0.45%, to 5,495.52.
