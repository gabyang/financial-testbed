Meta breaks up its Responsible AI team - The Information

Published: 2023-11-18 13:39:46 ET META

Nov 18 (Reuters) - Meta Platforms has split its Responsible AI team that worked on understanding and preventing harms associated with the artificial intelligence technology it is developing, as the company diverts more resources to its generative AI work, The Information reported on Saturday, citing an internal post.

(Reporting by Gursimran Kaur in Bengaluru)
