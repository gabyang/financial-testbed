Meta, challenging OpenAI, announces new AI model that can generate video with sound

Published: 2024-10-04 09:27:34 ET META

NEW YORK (Reuters) - Facebook owner Meta announced on Friday it had built a new AI model called Movie Gen that can create realistic-seeming video and audio clips in response to user prompts, claiming it can rival tools from leading media generation startups like OpenAI and ElevenLabs.

(Reporting by Katie Paul in New York; Additional reporting by Dawn Chmielewski in Los Angeles)
