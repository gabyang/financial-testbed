Dow Jones Futures Signal Coronavirus Market Rally Losses As Shanghai Win Streak Ends, Covid Cases Soar

Licensing
  • ED CARSON
  • 08:15 AM ET 07/10/2020

Dow Jones futures fell early Friday, along with S&P 500 futures and Nasdaq futures. The Shanghai composite retreated after running up sharply in recent days. Meanwhile, coronavirus cases continue to soar, with Covid-19 deaths in the U.S. trending higher.

The coronavirus stock market rally had a mixed, volatile Thursday, but quality names had a strong session. Costco Wholesale (COST), rival BJ's Wholesale (BJ), Inphi (IPHI), Dynatrace (DT), Lululemon Athletica (LULU) and Fortinet (FTNT) cleared buy points Thursday.

There is a concern that the coronavirus stock market rally is nearing some natural limits, at least for the Nasdaq. But so far the tech-heavy index has continued to advance, hitting a record high Thursday.

Costco stock, BJ's stock, Fortinet stock and Inphi stock broke out of conventional buy points. Dynatrace stock and Lululemon stock cleared short-term entries.

Fortinet is on IBD Leaderboard and IBD Long-Term Leaders. FTNT stock, LULU stock, DT stock and IPHI stock are on SwingTrader. Dynatrace stock and Fortinet stock are on the IBD 50.

Dow Jones Futures Today

Dow Jones futures fell 0.8% vs. fair value. S&P 500 futures sank 0.7%. Nasdaq 100 futures retreated 0.6%. Remember that overnight action in Dow futures and elsewhere doesn't necessarily translate into actual trading in the next regular stock market session.

The Shanghai composite retreated 1.95% as state media warned of investing risks, as well as selling by state pensions and other big institutions. The composite had soared 16.5% over the prior eight days, in part as state media touted stocks.

Join IBD experts as they analyze actionable stocks in the coronavirus stock market rally on IBD Live.

Coronavirus News

Coronavirus cases worldwide total 12.42 million. Covid-19 deaths have reached 558,000.

In the U.S., coronavirus cases have hit 3.22 million, with deaths above 135,000. New Covid-19 cases in the U.S. have already topped 61,000 today after Wednesday's record 61,848.

Coronavirus deaths are starting to trend higher after declining for many weeks. That partly reflects a lag between coronavirus infections and fatalities, as well as declining deaths in New York and the Northeast offsetting increases across the Sunbelt. Death rates also are likely to be lower now than in April given the younger age of current coronavirus patients and better standard of care.

Anthony Fauci, the government's top infectious disease expert, said that "any state that is having a serious problem, that state should seriously look at shutting down." But he added that "it's not for me to say."

Aside from official government restrictions and bans, businesses and consumers may rein in their own behavior and spending. That could slow the nascent economic recovery.

The coronavirus stock market rally had an up-and-down day, closing mixed. But it was generally a good day for leading stocks.

The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 1.4% in Thursday's stock market trading. The S&P 500 index retreated 0.6%. The Nasdaq composite rose 0.5%.

Meanwhile, the DJIA is stuck below its 200-day line, and no wonder. The Dow Jones today is filled with struggling companies such as Boeing (BA), JPMorgan Chase (JPM), Exxon Mobil (XOM) and Walgreens Boots Alliance (WBA). Walgreens plunged nearly 8% on an earnings miss.

The S&P 500 index has those laggards and many more, along with tech leaders and growth names.

The Nasdaq's "problem" is that's it's so fast and furious. It's 19% extended from its 200-day moving average. The tech-heavy index has been running at the top of a steep trading channel formed during the coronavirus stock market rally. Further, the Nasdaq is right at the top of a long-term channel line going back to 2009.

The Nasdaq could break above its channel lines, but would likely struggle to hold above them for long. That could mean the Nasdaq pulls back, moves sideways, or simply rises at a slower pace.

Among the best ETFs, the Innovator IBD 50 ETF (FFTY) rose 0.8%. The iShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF (IGV) rallied 0.9%. The VanEck Vectors Semiconductor ETF (SMH) popped 1.5%.

Costco Stock

Costco stock climbed 2.9% to 325.54, closing just above a 325.36 consolidation buy point, according to MarketSmith analysis. COST stock has now rallied for eight straight sessions.

Late Wednesday, the membership warehouse giant reported 11.5% same-store sales growth, crushing views. E-commerce sales, though still a relatively small share of total revenue, leapt 86%.

The relative strength line for COST stock has rebounded over the past month and especially in the last several days, but is still far off its April 1 peak. That reflects Costco's sideways action while the coronavirus stock market rally surged over the past three months. The RS line, the blue line in the charts provided, tracks a stock's performance vs. the S&P 500 index.

Costco Wholesale was the IBD Stock Of The Day.

BJ's Stock

BJ's stock rose 2.8% to 38.73, clearing a 38.45 buy point after attempting to do so earlier in the week. The RS line for BJ's stock is near record highs after jumping in May on a prior breakout.

Inphi Stock

Inphi stock rallied 3.6% to 130.04, clearing a 127.88 buy point. The consolidation was not quite long enough to be a proper base, but IPHI stock is still actionable. Last week, Inphi stock rebounded powerfully from its 10-week line.

Inphi stock was one of the first breakouts of the coronavirus stock market rally, and ran up until late May. It's been leading the data-center chip rally, along with Nvidia (NVDA). Advanced Micro Devices (AMD), which has lagged its peers, jumped 7.2% on Thursday, surging back above its 50-day line.

Fortinet Stock

Fortinet stock rose 2.2% to 149.60 after hitting a record 151 intraday. FTNT stock failed to close above a 149.79 conventional buy point but did move above a 146.73 entry.On Wednesday, FTNT stock popped 6.1%, clearing a pair of short entries.

The RS line for Fortinet stock is ramping up again, but is still not at record highs. But that follows a strong uptrend.

Fortinet was Thursday's IBD 50 Stock To Watch.

Lululemon Stock

Lululemon stock rose 1.7% to 315.20, breaking a short trend line from its June 10 peak of 324.76. LULU stock also briefly cleared a short-term peak, offering another early entry of 318.42. These entries are actionable for short-term trades or for existing Lululemon stock holders to add a few shares. Aggressive traders might nibble here and build a bigger LULU stock position after a more conventional breakout. Lululemon stock will have a proper flat base after Friday's close with a 324.86 buy point.

Dynatrace Stock

Dynatrace stock popped 4% to 44.06, breaking a mini-trend line and offering an aggressive entry. Intraday, DT stock rose to 44.37, a record high briefly clearing a short consolidation with a 44.26 entry. This is a short-term trade or a place to add a few shares to an existing Dynatrace stock position.

The Dynatrace IPO came public in August 2018 at 16 a share.
