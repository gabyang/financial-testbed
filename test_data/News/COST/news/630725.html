Coronavirus restrictions, cancellations and reopenings in Northeast Ohio for Friday, May 1, 2020

  • Published: May. 01, 2020, 5:00 a.m.
By
  • Cliff Pinckard, cleveland.com

CLEVELAND, Ohio — The most recent list of restrictions and delays because of the COVID-19 coronavirus is below. Wednesday’s list can be seen here.

Friday, May 1, 2020

Ohio Gov. Mike DeWine has extended the state’s stay-at-home order, but a new expiration date has not been set.

Cleveland Mayor Frank Jackson has extended his proclamation of civil emergency for the coronavirus crisis through the month of May.

Nonessential medical procedures at hospitals that do not require an overnight stay will be allowed to resume today, as well as dental and veterinary visits.

Customers are required to wear masks at Meijer, Costco and Menards stores.
