Costco to Pay Special Dividend of $15

Published: 2023-12-14 16:43:02 ET COST

By Sabela Ojea

Costco Wholesale declared a special dividend after posting its results for its fiscal first quarter.

The warehouse retailer on Thursday said its board of directors approved a special payout to shareholders of $15 per share.

The aggregate amount of the payment will be worth about $6.7 billion, Costco said.
