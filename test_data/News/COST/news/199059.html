Costco, Home Depot will limit number of people in stores for better social distancing in the coronavirus pandemic

  • Published: Apr. 01, 2020, 11:48 a.m.
By
  • Kaylee Remington, cleveland.com

CLEVELAND, Ohio -- Costco and Home Depot announced separately that they will limit the number of people in their stores amid the growing coronavirus pandemic.

Costo Wholesale announced Wednesday morning that stores will allow no more than two people to enter the warehouse per membership card. This change goes into effect Friday.

“This temporary change is for your safety and the safety of our employees and other members, and to further assist with our social distancing efforts,” the company said in a statement.

The wholesale store already took a number of steps to control the number of Costco members in its warehouses and asked that employees and members practice social distancing inside of the store.

“We’ve also reduced some services,” a statement from the company says. “We’ve increased our protocols in sanitizing surfaces, including shopping cart handles, merchandise shelves, front-end belts and registers.”

Costco on Monday implemented new weekday closing hours. All stores will close at 6:30 p.m., Monday through Friday. Gas stations will close at 7 p.m., according to a statement from the company.

Home Depot stores also will close at 6 p.m. seven days a week, according to a company statement.

The hardware store mega-chain also said it would limit the number of people in its stores, but did not give a specific number in its statement.
