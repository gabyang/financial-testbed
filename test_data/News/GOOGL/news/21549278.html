BPL Is Offering Virtual Classes In July To Help You Utilize Google Applications In Your Job Search

These sessions are funded by a grant from Libraries Lead with Digital Skills

Press Release Desk, News Partner
Posted Wed, Jul 8, 2020 at 8:04 pm CT

From the Birmingham Public Library:

July 8, 2020

By Jim Murray | Business, Science and Technology Department, Central Library

What: Two classes will be offered on Zoom: Find Your Next Job Using Google and Create Your Resume Using Google

Dates: Wednesday July 15, 2020 – Find Your Next Job Using Google

Wednesday July 29, 2020 – Create Your Resume Using Google

Time:11:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. CST


Details: Both classes will be taught on Zoom. The classes are free but registration is required. Attendance is limited to the first 20 registrants. To register for each class, please go to the Birmingham Public Library’s events calendar. Once registered, participants will receive a Zoom invitation included in the registration confirmation email with which they will log into the class on the scheduled date and time. For further information about the classes, please contact Maurice Harley at Maurice.Harley@cobpl.org.

The Birmingham Public Library is offering two virtual job search classes in July on Zoom. The first class, Find Your Next Job Using Google, is scheduled for Wednesday, July 15, at 11:00 a.m. CST. The one-hour long class will introduce participants to the Google job search engine and provide instruction on how to effectively navigate it to find the type of jobs that are of the most interest to you.

A follow-up class, Create Your Resume Using Google, will be offered on Wednesday, July 29, at 11:00 a.m. CST. The purpose of this class is to show participants how to use the variety of templates available on Google Docs to develop a job resume that will capture the attention of employers.

These classes are funded by a grant from Libraries Lead with Digital Skills, a project created by the American Library Association (ALA) and the Public Library Association (PLA), and sponsored by Grow with Google, the tech company’s economic opportunity initiative. The goal of the project is to provide support for public libraries in offering programming that helps everyone in the community—those who make up the workforce of today and those who will drive the workforce of tomorrow—have access to the best of Google’s training and tools to prepare for, and find employment.
