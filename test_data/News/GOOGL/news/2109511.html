Where the most expensive and cheapest homes were sold in Hertfordshire recently

You could find a flat in Hitchin for just over £50,000

hertfordshiremercury
  • Bookmark
  • Share
News
By
Matthew SmithLocal Democracy Reporter
  • 10:52, 8 APR 2020
  • Bookmark

    • The cheapest and most expensive homes sold in Hertfordshire over February have been revealed.

    Data from the Land Registry shows that the most expensive house in our county sold for just over £2million, with a number of properties all going for more than £1million.

    That price, for a five-bed house with its own cinema room, looks a bargain compared to the most expensive property in England and Wales, a property in Kensington and Chelsea which sold for an eye-watering £13million.

    There is still hope for most of us though, a lot of the properties in our list of cheapest properties are still in the most desirable parts of our county.

    Here are the top 10 cheapest and most expensive houses sold in the county in February:

    Most expensive

    Harpenden

    This detached house in Harpenden is by the far the highest price on this list.

    The five-bedroom property sold for a impressive £2,085,000, around £300,000 higher than any other property in Hertfordshire.

    Alongside the bedrooms are five bathrooms and four reception rooms.

    There is also a basement including an office, gym and games room, and home cinema.

    Whoever bought this house must be thinking they got value for money ahead of a long spring stuck inside.

    Berkhamsted - Detached house sold for £1.1million

    Least Expensive

    Hitchin

    The least expensive property sold in Hertfordshire recently was in Walsworth, near Hitchin.

    While the average house price in the town is around £450,000, one flat on Watersmeet was sold for £52,000 in Feburary.

    While this house isn't near the town centre, it's still less than a mile from the train station, so is well connected to the rest of Hertfordshire and London.

    In total, 78,243 sales were registered across the country, including land, residential and commercial properties.
