Devon churches asking God to make good things happen to the region

The Devon Awake event was held in Plymouth, Exeter and Barnstaple

    A church in Plymouth has joined with two others across Devon in hosting a historic prayer gathering so that good things happen for the region.

    Hope Baptist Church, based in Peverell Park Road, hosted the ‘Devon Awake’ event alongside Rediscover Church in Exeter and North Devon House of Pray in Barnstaple.

    In addition to those that attended in person, it was expected that thousands of Christians from across the county would tune in to the event via a livestream online.

    The belief is that many people of all ages will be changed and transformed by God in 2020 and as they partner with God breakthrough will come through in a fresh, new way within this region.

    Luke Mansfield, minister at Hope Baptist Church, said ahead of the event: “We are excited to be part of an incredible event, uniting with Christians from across Devon to pray for the county.

    “We believe that prayer is powerful and when we pray God moves mightily.

    “Last time we gathered in such a way it was a very special time and we would love to invite you to join us for what is going to be just as significant.

    “Our heart is to bring Christians together from all of the churches across our county to seek the Lord together for the people of Devon.”

    Those that don’t believe in God are likely to question this event but Mark Pugh, lead pastor of Rediscover Church, says he has seen first hand how prayers can be answered.

    He said: “Churches across Devon are involved in so many things from food banks, homeless projects and providing loving and supportive communities among many other things - but prayer can change lives and impact communities and is one of the most significant things Christians can do.
