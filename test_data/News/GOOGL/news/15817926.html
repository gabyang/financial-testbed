The Readout LOUD

Covid-19’s U.S. surge, GSK’s mystery whistleblower, and cancer care under coronavirus

By Damian Garde , Rebecca Robbins , and Adam Feuerstein June 25, 2020

Reprints

Is this a second wave or a rising tide? Who’s the real GlaxoSmithKline whistleblower? And what happens to cancer care in a pandemic?

We discuss all that and more this week on “The Readout LOUD,” STAT’s biotech podcast. First, we talk about the alarming spike in Covid-19 cases in states across the South and West. Then, we’re joined by David Barboza of The Wire to detail the latest revelations in GlaxoSmithKline’s salacious bribery scandal. Finally, STAT’s Elizabeth Cooney dials in to discuss how cancer doctors are adapting to the pandemic when it comes to patient care and clinical trials.
