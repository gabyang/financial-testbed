Breaking down the Browns 2020 schedule: Orange and Brown Talk Podcast

  • Published: May. 08, 2020, 5:00 a.m.
By
  • Dan Labbe, cleveland.com
  • Mary Kay Cabot, cleveland.com
  • Ellis L. Williams, cleveland.com

CLEVELAND, Ohio -- The Browns’ 2020 schedule was released on Thursday night. The NFL has managed to turn its schedule release into a holiday, so we’re celebrating by taking a deep dive into the schedule on our latest podcast.

Mary Kay Cabot, Dan Labbe and Ellis Williams took a look at the schedule and worked their way through it for Friday’s Orange and Brown Talk Podcast.
