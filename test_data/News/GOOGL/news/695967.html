Will Michigan coach Jim Harbaugh ever turn it around against Ohio State? Buckeye Talk Daily Pod

  • Updated: May. 08, 2020, 5:00 p.m.
  • |Published: May. 08, 2020, 1:47 p.m.
By
  • Doug Lesmerises, cleveland.com

COLUMBUS, Ohio -- This Buckeye Talk episode is all about the Ohio State-Michigan rivalry and the two guests joining me are writers who understand The Game from both sides. Kyle Rowland and Dave Briggs of The Toledo Blade hop on to talk about Jim Harbaugh’s approach to the rivalry, things he might ever adjust about that approach, what he’s like when he’s not standing at an interview podium, and if the current trajectory of the Ohio State-Michigan game is ever going to change.

Rowland is the Ohio State beat writer for The Blade but covered Michigan for three years, and Briggs writes about both teams as The Blade sports columnist, so they bring perspectives that understand both sides of this.

Has Harbaugh been a success or a failure at Michigan? At the end of the pod, we discuss whether Urban Meyer underachieved at Ohio State.

For fans of the Buckeyes, this is a look at Michigan within the context of Ohio State that you don't want to miss. Thanks for listening to Buckeye Talk.
