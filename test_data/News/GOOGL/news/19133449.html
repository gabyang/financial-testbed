Super Saturday 2020: Somerset pubs, hotels, restaurants and hairdressers which will open or stay closed - live updates

Pubs and restaurant have been hit hard by the coronavirus pandemic and many have been gearing up to reopen

    Huge changes will come into force when many hospitality businesses previously shut down by the coronavirus lockdown are free to open today (Saturday, July 4).

    These include pubs, hairdressers, hotels, campsites, restaurants, cinemas, museums and galleries with guidance on social distancing reducing from a minimum of two-metres to one-metre-plus.

    However, despite the reduction in distance, many businesses are expected to remain shut citing difficulties in reopening and maintaining a safe environment to visit and work in.

    There have also been concerns among some businesses over how cost-effective reopening will be with reduced customer numbers expected.

    It also comes as Prime Minister Boris Johnson has warned if virus infection rates surge again, the nation will be forced back into lockdown potentially on a regional basis, which would hamper business once more.

    Pictures show Taunton's Wetherspoons

    Two Wetherspoon pubs in Taunton have revealed the various changes they have made ahead of their reopenings on Saturday (July 4).

    JD Wetherspoon shut all of its pubs when the government introduced lockdown measures in the UK in March.

    Pubs and restaurants have been given the green light to reopen in accordance with government advice and Wetherspoon pubs across Somerset have been preparing to throw open their doors.

    Somerset Live has been inside Taunton's two Wetherspoon pubs, the Perkin Warbeck and Coal Orchard, for a sneak peek of the radically changed pubs.

    A full range of changes have been made to its pubs, including a controlled entry and exit system, new hand sanitiser stations and reduced seating.

    Its tables and chairs have been spread out to enforce social distancing while large perspex screens have been installed between some tables.

    Cheese and Grain won't reopen until its "financially viable" to do so

    The Cheese and Grain has confirmed it will not reopen its doors or host any more events until it is "financially viable" to do so.

    The venue confirmed the news in a monthly update and said all summer events that were intended for the Frome-based enterprise are being rescheduled.

    In a statement posted on its website and on social media, the venue confirmed all planned events in the summer months had been suspended and that the box office would be remaining closed "for a while yet".

    The chair of the board of trustees, Pepita Collins, said the venue is waiting until it was medically safe to put events on at the building again and where finances allow.

    She said: "The situation is under constant review, but it is likely that we will not be able to open until the live-music events are both allowed by law and are financially viable.

    Beefeater guidelines as restaurant chain set to reopen

    Beefeater has confirmed the opening dates for its Somerset branches while revealing the changes that will be made to ensure government guidance is followed.

    The chain has laid out its plans to re-open restaurants across the country alongside other non-essential businesses that are opening their from Saturday (July 4).

    Beefeater has three locations in Somerset - Yeovil, White Lodge in Taunton and the Frome Flyer.

    Beefeater has confirmed via its social media accounts that it will be re-opening locations "from July 4", although there will only be a certain number across the country re-opened on any one day.

    The first batch of outlets will re-open on July 4, with the last ones to accept customers not able to do so until August 5.

    Apex Hotels announces plans for phased reopening welcoming guests on July 20

    A major Bath hotel is to reopen this month for staycations.

    Apex Hotels has announced plans for its phased reopening – with four of its properties, including Apex City of Bath Hotel, set to welcome guests from Monday, July 20.

    The 177 bedroom hotel is now taking bookings for after this date.

    But under current Government guidelines, guests will be unable to use some of its facilities such as the swimming pool, sauna and gym.

    The hotel group has implemented an ‘Apex Assured’ plan – designed to give visitors complete peace of mind regarding cleanliness and safety, as well as ensuring flexibility on bookings.

    With family and interconnecting rooms, households can travel and be accommodated in their own ‘social bubbles’ for total peace of mind, the hotel says.

    Roman Baths due to open on July 6

    Residents of Bath and North East Somerset are being invited to visit the Roman Baths for free when it reopens next week.

    The iconic tourist attraction is opening its doors on July 6 and wants to welcome local residents free of charge, even if they are not Discovery Card-holders who usually get free entry.

    There is also a 25 per cent discount offered for guests of Discovery Card-holders and B&NES residents.

    But all visitors must have a pre-booked ticket to visit.

    An end date for the offer has not been announced, but currently, free slots are available to book until the end of July.

    There appears to be no restrictions on visiting at peak times and on the weekend.

    Music venue and pub in Bridgwater ready to open

    One of Somerset's most popular music venues and pubs is set to reopen its doors this weekend following a "stressful" period under lockdown.

    The Cobblestones, on Eastover, Bridgwater, has built up a great reputation in the Somerset music scene by regularly hosting local bands as well as the likes of Wheatus, Coolio, Anvil over the years.

    It was forced to shut its doors in March as the government closed down pubs and bars as part of lockdown measures.

    Landlord, Graham Hodgson, described lockdown as "stressful" as he was unable to open the pub he has put years of effort into rejuvenating into a popular music venue.
