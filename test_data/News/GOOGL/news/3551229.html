    UK Government plans to limit the number of English students going to university in Wales

    The UK government plans to control numbers of students from England coming to university in Wales in response to the coronavirus, it emerged tonight.

    Whitehall is expected to announce a cap on numbers next week in response to fears over competition for UK students in the face of falling recruits from overseas as a result of the coronavirus pandemic.

    Education Minister Kirsty Williams has tonight written to the UK’s Minister of State for Universities expressing “deep concern” over plans to announce the UK Government’s intention to introduce temporary student number controls in response to the pandemic.
