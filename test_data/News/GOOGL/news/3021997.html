While most of the information on Madden 21 had leaked prior to the official reveal, everything was confirmed on Tuesday. Let’s take a closer look at what we learned about Madden 21.


Release Date Info

  • Retail Release: August 28
  • Three-Day Early Access: August 25
  • Platforms: Xbox One, PlayStation 4, PC and Google Stadia


Trailer

Here is the trailer that leaked early on Tuesday morning, but was officially released at 10 am ET.


Cover Athlete

In another piece of information that got out far sooner than EA planned, the Baltimore Ravens’ superstar Lamar Jackson, and reigning league MVP is officially the cover athlete.

As you can see from the thumbnail above, Jackson is rendered with stunning detail. The various covers for the different versions of the game are examples of EA’s best work in this space.

Per a press release provided by EA, here’s what Jackson said about being named the cover athlete:

“I grew up playing Madden and owned every copy I could get my hands on, so to be on the cover of Madden NFL 21 is a dream come true, especially when the cover represents so much of my story,” said Lamar Jackson. “The welcome to the Madden family from around the league and from fans has been phenomenal and I think people will have a lot of fun with the new features coming in this year’s game.” 


Pre-Order Bonus

There will be a Standard, Deluxe and MVP Edition available.

Fans who pre-order the Madden NFL 21 MVP Edition for $99.99 ($89.99 for EA Access members)will get 3-Day Early Access, Lamar Jackson Elite Item with Power Up, 17 Madden Ultimate Team Gold Team Fantasy Packs, one Large Quicksell Training Pack, Madden Championship Series Ultimate Champion Pack, 1 of 32 Elite Players from Team Elite Pack and one “Your Choice” Uniform Pack. 

Here is the breakdown for the Deluxe Edition ($79.99 and $71.99 for EA Access members):

  • 3-Day Early Access to Madden NFL 21 (8/25/2020)
  • 12 Madden Ultimate Team Gold Team Fantasy Packs
  • 1 of 32 Players from NFL Team Elite Packs
  • 1 “Your Choice” Uniform Pack

And this is what customers who buy the Standard Edition receive for pre-ordering at $59.99 ($53.99 for EA Access members)

  • 1 of 32 Players from NFL Team Elite Packs
  • 5 Madden Ultimate Team Gold Team Fantasy Packs
  • 1 “Your Choice” Uniform Pack

EA is also offering “dual entitlement” which allows fans who buy an Xbox Series X or PlayStation 5 to upgrade their copy of Madden 21 to the next-gen console at no extra cost. This offer has been extended up to the release of Madden 22.


Features

If we’re being honest, the feature set doesn’t sound stacked for this year’s game. The press release speaks of an enhanced overall experience with an emphasis on improved ball-carrier mechanics. We can see a little of this in the trailer.

There also has been some work done to give more control to players on the defensive line, and to add some realism to the open-field tackling component. There are also new user-controlled celebrations. One interesting section of the initial spiel says, “some of the biggest changes will arrive in the overall authenticity and presentation with new on field camera shots and improved player awareness of their positioning and surroundings on the field.”

Madden 21 executive producer Sean Graddy said:

“Our players are some of the most passionate in the world. They love Madden and we want to make sure we’re fulfilling what they’d like to see most in the game and delivering new features to up level each iteration. In Madden NFL 21 there are a range of authentic new gameplay improvements that both our most engaged players and any fan of football will enjoy, plus unique experiences that will allow every player to live out their NFL fantasy with more still to come.”

The multiplayer franchise mode wasn’t mentioned, but the single-player version, which is called Face of the Franchise has a new layer. It’s called Rise to Fame.Here’s how it is described: “a playable documentary career campaign where players create themselves and play through their rise to fame from high school as a quarterback, to college where they can choose to pivot to running back or wide receiver, before experiencing the NFL Combine, NFL Draft, and cementing their legacy in the League.”

Lastly, there are 50 new Superstar X-Factor abilities, Live Playbooks 2.0, and of course, a new season of Ultimate Team content.

Hopefully, we’ll hear some additional information about the franchise mode, and it would be awesome if you could use broadcast mode during franchise mode in the event you wanted to play as a coach or general manager and simply call the plays and the shots for your organization.

It’s just a suggestion.
