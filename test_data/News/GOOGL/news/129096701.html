Google-parent Alphabet cuts stake in Crowdstrike

Published: 2024-08-02 06:11:34 ET GOOGL

Aug 2 (Reuters) - Google-parent Alphabet has cut its stake in cybersecurity company Crowdstrike, according to a Securities and Exchange Commission filing on Friday.

The company cut its stake to 427,895 class A shares from 855,789 shares. (Reporting by Zaheer Kachwala in Bengaluru; Editing by Arun Koyyur)
