Maharashtra partners with Google to provide remote learning tools to all students, teachers

Vinamrata Borwankar / TNN / Aug 6, 2020, 19:54 IST
Share

MUMBAI: As the Covid-19 pandemic continues to keep students out of the classroom, the Maharashtra school education department on Thursday announced its partnership with tech giant, Google to provide blended learning programmes to over 2.3 crore students from 1 lakh government and private schools in the state.
Under the partnerships, all teachers and students will be provided a G Suite ID to facilitate remote learning.
Under the ID, the teachers will have unlimited cloud storage enabling them to develop content and make it accessible to students even at a later date. Teachers can also use the Google Classrooms and Meet to conduct live classes and create, review, and organize assignments. The State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT) will also provide teacher training through the platform.
Education minister, Varsha Gaikwad said, âThe current crisis can be turned into an opportunity. We have always believed in the power of internet to take education to the masses, and reduce the learning gap. I foresee a blended learning environment in which the student mixes face to face learning with online that provides content in both synchronised and asynchronised formats and technology can play a vital role if all teachers and students can benefit from it. We havenât been able to reach each student in Maharashtra yet but we are committed to provide equitable access. The government will continue to work with community, NGOs and the schools to solve this problem.â
Last month, the government rolled out a survey asking teachers from the state to register for the first batch of training programmes and Google received 150,000 sign-ups in less than 48 hours. The programme will be accessible on three domains one each for government and local body schools, private aided schools and private unaided schools. Teach from Home, a Google information hub for information on technology tools, is also available in Marathi.
Speaking at the virtual launch, chief minister, Uddhav Thackeray said, âIn a crisis when the world is at a standstill, how to restart education is one of the primary concerns we face today. I feel this global crisis has led us from the present into the future. And so I am proud today to say that Maharashtra is the first state in the country to launch G Suite for Education and Google Classroom.â
