Live A120 Coggeshall and Colchester traffic updates as overturned lorry completely shuts road

Emergency services are at the scene

essexlive
  • Bookmark
  • Share
By
Brad GrayContent Lead
  • Updated12:02, 7 JUL 2020
  • Bookmark

    • The A120 has been shut whilst emergency services attend to an overturned lorry.

    Essex Police Roads Policing Unit revealed that the incident earlier this morning (Tuesday, July 7) caused minor injuries and will keep the road closed 'for some time'.

    The two-vehicle crash happened today on the A120 near Coggeshall, Essex.

    The North Essex Roads Policing Unit tweeted: "We are currently at the scene of a two vehicle RTC on the A120 Coggeshall.

    "There are minor injuries reported, however the recovery will take some time and the road will be closed whilst this takes place. Please avoid the area."

    Read More
    Related Articles

    Essex County Council Traffic Control confirmed earlier this morning that a lorry had overturned and caused long delays in the area.

    They tweeted: "A120 BLOCKED in both directions between Bradwell and Coggeshall because of an accident involving several vehicles and an overturned lorry.

    "Queues in both directions approaching the scene."

    Motorists are advised to find an alternate route whilst the incident is ongoing.

    Essex Police and the East of England Ambulance Service have been approached for further comment.

    We will be bringing you live updates on this situation in our blog below.

    Full Highways England statement

    Highways England have confirmed that the road is completely shut after some earlier confusion.

    Traffic is being diverted via local roads.

    Here is the full statement:

    “The A120 in Essex is closed in both directions between the A131(N) (near Braintree) and B1024 (Coggeshall), due to a collision involving a car and lorry. The lorry has overturned and come to rest on its side.


    “Essex Police are on scene and traffic is being diverted via local roads.


    “There is approx. 1.9 miles of congestion eastbound and 2.5 miles westbound on approach to the closure.


    “Road users are advised to allow extra time for their journeys today. “
