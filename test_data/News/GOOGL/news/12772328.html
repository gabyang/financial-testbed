Royals boast 3 league leaders on offense, pitching questions: AL Central Preview 2020

CLEVELAND, Ohio — Longtime Kansas City Royals beat writer Alan Eskew joined Monday’s Cleveland Baseball Talk Podcast to preview the 2020 American League Central Division and how KC should fare in a coronavirus-shortened season. Eskew shared his take on Royals’ hitters and the club’s search for viable starting pitching under new manager Mike Matheny.

Other topics covered on Monday include:

  • What Royals draft picks in recent years could have an impact on the AL Central in the near future?
  • What did Matheny do in the offseason to improve his knowledge of analytics?
  • When was the last time Alan Eskew heard a Hawk Call?
