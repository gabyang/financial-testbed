    Chester Zoo is live-streaming its fifth virtual tour after thousands of fans tuned in over the last few weeks to get up close to the animals in the comfort of their own homes.

    Virtual visitors will get another chance today to see their favourite creatures as the keepers give a tour from inside the zoo, which has been closed because of the coronavirus lockdown.

    After attracting thousands of viewers on Facebook and YouTube, the zoo is giving fans another chance to see their amazing animals once again.

    And this time, the tour is dedicated to the zoo’s endangered species.

    Sharing the news on Facebook, Chester Zoo wrote: “So, it looks like lockdown is going to go on a little longer for us and that can mean only one thing… we’re once again bringing the zoo to you!

    “This time we’re throwing open our ‘virtual gates’ for an Endangered Species Day SPECIAL!

    “Join us on our Facebook and YouTube pages THIS FRIDAY (15 May) from 10am for a WHOLE DAY of amazing animal antics, putting a spotlight on some of the most incredible, but sadly most endangered, species on the planet.”

    You don’t need a Facebook account to watch, just search ‘Chester Zoo Facebook’ on Google and you’ll be able to watch - or click here .

    If you’d prefer to watch in landscape on a TV or home computer, you can watch on the Chester Zoo TV YouTube page .

    Today, keepers will be visiting various animals across the site throughout the day, and while the running order may change here are the latest timings:

    10am - 10.20am - One Horned Rhinos

    11am - 11.20am - Mountain Chicken Frogs

    12pm - 12.20pm - Northern Cheetahs

    1pm - 1.20pm - Tropical Bird Feeding Time

    2pm - 2.20pm - Orangutan Hang

    3pm - 3.20pm - Visayan Warty Pigs
