Quiz: How much do you really know about Hull's pubs and clubs?

It's time to test your knowledge on the pubs and clubs in Hull

    Hull's pubs and clubs have always been popular.

    Over the centuries, the amount of pubs in the city has dwindled due to changing landscapes and the building of new areas, but Hull still has several strong social areas such as the Old Town, the Avenues and Hull Marina.

    Our number of nightclubs may have fallen but our memories of these establishments will never fade - whether it was Trog Bar or Tiffany's, Sugar Mill or Pozition, we have all had some incredible nights in nightclubs.

    It's time to test your knowledge on the pubs and clubs in Hull, with questions ranging from the mid-1900s to more recent events.
