How to choose

Whether you're tired of losing your keys, you're already in the process of automating your home, or you're setting up a bed-and-breakfast, a vital part of the process is choosing the best smart locks that fit your needs. There are plenty of different smart locks to choose from, each with its own unique abilities. Some have easy installation, some work with virtual assistants like Alexa, while others are loaded with tech like built-in cameras to give added flexibility.

Though there are some great picks out there for securing your home, the best smart lock is one of the highest-ranked August Wi-Fi Smart Locks (4th Gen). The built-in Wi-Fi means it can connect directly to your home Wi-Fi without the need of a bridge. Others like the ones from Schlage can be quite expensive, but there's always a great deal to find if you know where to look.

If you're looking for something more specific, our best smart locks list has something in it for everyone.

Unlike many of the other smart locks that you'll find on the market, it isn't immediately apparent when you have the August Wi-Fi Smart Lock installed on your front door. That's because the device is installed inside your home, so there's no indication of the smart lock from your front porch. The latest model is still a little bulky but is much smaller than previous generations.

In our review of this August Wi-Fi lock, we loved that it was so simple to install with existing hardware while including so many advanced features. One of the things that makes this particular lock so great is that buyers don't need any additional bridges when using this device since it has its own built-in Wi-Fi. That means that buyers can lock and unlock the door anywhere as long as they have access to the August app and a good internet connection.

You can also view the activity of any entry where this smart lock is installed by checking the app. If you want, the app allows you to change the settings to automatically lock when you leave home or unlock when you approach. Plus, it works beautifully with Google Assistant, Amazon Alexa, and other smart home assistants.

Getting the negative part of this lock out of the way — it's expensive with a full price of $500. But, if you consider this a smart lock and video doorbell in one, then it isn't too bad. The lock comes with a fingerprint reader that can store and remember up to 99 prints in addition to the digital keypad that scrambles the order of the numbers each time you press one. While it may take a few more seconds to enter your PIN, it means that it will be more difficult for potential evildoers to guess it.

The Lockly Vision Elite is packed with lots of features, like a door sensor that'll notify you when your door isn't closed, and a very long battery life that potentially could last forever thanks to the solar panel built into the keyhole cover. The vision part Lockly Vision Elite's name is thanks to the video doorbell functionality included in this device. This lock is an all-in-one solution for securing your door.

With all of these features, you can reassured your home stays secure. You'll even get a notification if the lock thinks someone is tampering with it.

If you're looking for something that securely bolts your door shut without costing too much, then this is the smart lock to get. It features a wireless keypad with an optional fingerprint model available, and because it uses your existing lock, you won't have to get new keys. Aside from the keypad and physical key, you can also unlock it with your phone, an NFC card, voice via your favorite smart assistant, and more. However, to unlock it from your phone when you aren't home or with voice assistants like Alexa, you'll need the SwitchBot Hub 2 or the Hub Mini. The Hub 2 is Matter-ready, so I recommend that.

It comes in two colors to match your door: black or silver. The installation process isn't necessarily difficult, but getting the parts to line up properly can be a bit cumbersome. You won't need to remove your old lock, as this model uses a motor to turn the knob of your existing lock. Keep in mind that if your lock is a bit tough to turn, the SwitchBot Wi-Fi Smart Lock motor may struggle to turn it. Oh, and that interior part you installed is a bit clunky-looking.

Battery life for this lock is pretty good, rated at six months via the two CR123A batteries. Being able to have multiple ways to control the lock with or without the hub is great. Adding in the fact that you won't need to remove your old lock to install this one is great, though.

Keys are a thing of the past. Instead, biometric options, like your fingerprint, make opening your door faster and more secure. The Flex Touch employs a 3D fingerprint sensor, ensuring the lock doesn't respond to lifted prints. During my review of this lock with my family, I found that the fingerprint scanner was fast and secure. But should you need it, you can always fall back on the included keys.

Owners can also add up to 99 fingerprints and control the lock remotely with their phones. However, to use this feature, you'll have to purchase the Lockly Secure Link Wi-Fi Smart Hub selling for about $80 on Amazon. With the hub, you can also monitor the unlock history to see if something strange has been going on with your door.

If you're the kind of person who prefers to keep your front door locked at all times, then you'll appreciate the auto-locking feature. Set it to lock anywhere between five seconds and five minutes after opening the door. Just be aware that the deadbolt will lock regardless of whether or not the door is open.

How to choose

Which smart lock is best?

Why you can trust Android Central Our expert reviewers spend hours testing and comparing products and services so you can choose the best for you. Find out more about how we test.

When looking for a smart lock, you want something that provides plenty of security features and added convenience for a reasonable price. Next, you'll have to determine if being able to lock via the app or voice assistant is enough or if you'd like the ability to key in codes, use a physical key slot, or even open the door via fingerprint scans.

There are a lot of great smart locks from August with a variety of features and formats to fit your needs. One of the best options out there is the August Smart Lock. It makes the front of your door look like any other lock, but you'll be able to monitor lock activity to know who is going or coming from the included August app. It's cheaper than several others because it uses Bluetooth rather than a Wi-Fi connection. Many of the smart locks on this list are also the best smart locks for Google Home, if you're already in Google's ecosystem.
