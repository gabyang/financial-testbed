Ofsted has visited 19 Hull and East Yorkshire schools this year - here's what they said

The education watchdog has uploaded reports from their latest inspections

    There are 19 schools in Hull and East Yorkshire that have been inspected by Ofsted so far in 2020.

    Before the coronavirus outbreak had put the school year on hold, Ofsted made the round across schools in Hull and East Yorkshire, colleges and nurseries.

    The reports published on their website includes six schools in Hull - three of which have been marked as ‘requires improvement’ and one that stood out as ‘outstanding’.

    Whether it was with full, two-day inspections, or short, one-day visits, areas the education watchdog inspectors check vary but their specifications generally include:

    • Effectiveness of leadership and management
    • Quality of teaching, learning and assessment
    • Personal development, behaviour and welfare
    • Outcomes for pupils
    • Early years provision

    Below are the latest Ofsted reports that were published in 2020:

    Buckingham Primary Academy - GOOD

    Buckingham Street, east Hull, HU8 8UG

    A short inspection was carried out at Buckingham Primary Academy on November 26 where the school jumped from ‘Requires Improvement’ to a ‘Good’ rating.

    Inspectors found that staff had “worked hard” since the last inspection in September 2017, which found four out of five specifications required improvement.

    Inspectors wrote: “It does not take long for a visitor to the school to experience the warm and caring relationships that exist between teachers and pupils.

    “Pupils are happy and smiling, and they enjoy coming to school because they enjoy their learning.”

    Kingswood Parks Primary School - GOOD

    School Lane, Kingswood, north Hull, HU7 3JQ

    Following an inspection in December Kingswood Parks Primary School improved their Ofsted rating to ‘Good’.

    Inspectors marked the North Hull school an outstanding for behaviour and attitudes and said “leaders have worked hard to make sure that learning is interesting and fun”.

    The report continued: “The quality of education is good. Leaders have planned the curriculum with lots of thought.

    “Pupils are very proud of the work they do in their ‘mastery of skills’ portfolios. They are right to be proud. Their work is of very high quality.”

    Newington Academy – OUTSTANDING

    Dairycoates Avenue, west Hull, HU3 5DD

    Newington Academy is “a great school” according to Ofsted.

    Inspectors marked the west Hull school as outstanding across all specifications when they visited in December.

    Education news

    The report reads: “Pupils love the school and enjoy their lessons. Staff truly believe that there is no limit to what pupils can achieve.

    “Leaders have thought carefully about what pupils need to learn and when and, as a result, pupils’ work is of high quality and they achieve high standards.”

    Sirius Academy West – REQUIRES IMPROVEMENT

    Anlaby Park Road South, west Hull, HU4 7JB

    Ofsted inspectors conducted a monitoring visit at Sirius Academy West after the school was rated as ‘Requires Improvement’ during an inspection in November 2018.

    Inspectors wrote: “Following the previous inspection in November 2018, leaders have been quick to take the effective action required to make the necessary improvements.

    “Leadership and management across the school have been strengthened. The curriculum policy of the school has been reviewed and evaluated.

    Since the previous inspection, leaders have identified that the curriculum offered to pupils was not meeting their needs.

    “Leaders have now embedded a curriculum which contains four pathways, all of which are designed to more closely meet the needs of the full range of pupils.”

    St Richard’s VC Academy – REQUIRES IMPROVEMENT

    Marfleet Lane, east Hull, HU9 5TE

    Inspectors visited the East Hull school in February where they rated the school as ‘Requires Improvement’.

    Despite being rated as ‘Good’ in three out of the five key areas Ofsted look into, the school requires improvement in the quality of education and early years provision.

    Inspectors wrote: “There have been many changes in staffing since the school was last inspected. The new leadership team has taken effective action to improve all aspects of school life.

    “There is now a stable staff team whose members work closely together to improve pupils’ education.

    “Leaders have undertaken a complete review and designed a well-sequenced curriculum. This has begun to be implemented but it is too soon for it to be effective in all subjects.

    “Leaders are aware that some pupils have gaps in their knowledge and skills. Teachers do not always consider this in their planning. Pupils do not always reach their full potential, particularly at the highest levels.”

    The Boulevard Academy – REQUIRES IMPROVEMENT

    Massey Close, west Hull, HU3 3QT

    Inspectors conducted a monitoring visit to The Boulevard Academy in February after the school was rated as ‘Requires Improvement’

    In their latest visit, inspectors wrote: “Following the previous inspection in November 2018, the new principal, with the support of trustees, embarked upon a programme of significant review and change.

    “The actions taken have been effective in making the improvements required. Several areas of leadership have been strengthened through a combination of appointments to key posts and a review of roles and responsibilities.

    “The school’s plans to tackle the areas requiring improvement identified at the recent inspection are effective. They outline clearly and accurately the priorities and actions needed to improve the school.”

    Driffield School and Sixth Form - GOOD

    Manorfield Road, Driffield, YO25 5HR

    Ofsted completed their first inspection of Driffield School and Sixth Form since it opened in February 2017.

    Inspectors visiting the school in January this year wrote that it is “a friendly and happy place” that provides a good quality of education.

    The report reads: “Leaders and staff have created a positive environment based on the school’s motto ‘Believe, Achieve’.

    “Staff ensure that all pupils feel welcome within the school. Pupils value each other’s differences. Staff and pupils work well together.

    Longcroft School and Sixth Form College – REQUIRES IMPROVEMENT

    Burton Road, Beverley, HU17 7EJ

    Ofsted inspectors say Longcroft School and Sixth Form College still ‘Requires Improvement’, after rating the school as such in November 2017.

    Marshlands Primary School – GOOD

    Hall Road, Old Goole, Goole, DN14 5UE

    Marshlands Primary School remains a ‘Good’ school but inspectors say they have “some concerns that standards may be declining”.

    Inspectors wrote: “Leaders give high priority to the safety and well-being of pupils. Positive relationships across the school foster a caring atmosphere for all.

    “The curriculum is not fully planned in all subjects. Although leaders have identified that curriculum plans need to be improved, they do not have a clear strategy for carrying this work out. Curriculum leaders and governors are unsure what is expected.

    “Teachers and leaders do not use assessment well enough to check what pupils have learned in subjects other than English and mathematics. Teachers do not consistently help pupils to build on what they already know in some subjects. Pupils do not achieve as well as they could in some subjects.”
