    Natede From Vitesy: More Than an Air Purifier

    By Nhx Tingson
    Apr 06 2020, 16:42 PM EDT
    Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on LinkedIn Share on Reddit Share on Flipboard Share on Pocket

    Air purifiers use technology to analyze air quality inside our homes and offices and remove impurities to guarantee our safety and health. But the Natede by Vitesy, is not just your regular air purifier. In essence, it's a natural and smart air purifier that uses a powerful combination of technology, nature, and design to remove air pollution in any indoor space.

    Natede by Vitesy incorporates real, live plants in their design to purify indoor air.
    Thanks to the process known as phytoremediation, these living plants can accumulate contaminants in soils, air, and water, rendering them harmless. With Natede, technology allows the product to boost the natural phytoremediation prowess of these plants to eliminate VOCs from indoor air, and therefore providing you with clean and refreshing air.

    Additionally, Natede also has a photocatalytic filter, which is coated in titanium dioxide.

    Thanks to a natural process known as photocatalysis, the filter captures and decomposes VOCs, bacteria, viruses, and even odors, which is incredibly crucial in today's world where even indoor air could be filled with impurities and microbes that can cause health issues.

    The filters used by Natede are reusable, so there's no need to buy any replacement, avoiding extra expenses, and eliminates waste. They are also easy to wash and require no special cleaning agent or equipment to clean. The interaction between the plant and Natede's technology and filter enables this product to eliminate 93% of VOCs and up to 99% of bacteria, fine-particulate, viruses, and odors.

    Besides being a natural air purifier, Natede also has other functions that every homeowner will deem as essential.

    Thanks to the most highly advanced sensors, the product can accurately measure temperature and humidity in your home or office, plus it can measure pollutants (VOCs), fine particulates (PM2.5), and carbon monoxide (CO).

    You can use the mobile app that goes along with Natede to check your home's air status, as the mobile app will show you real-time monitoring of the Internal Air Quality (IAQ) in your space.

    There are also four different fan speed modes that you can easily select and modify when you are working, sleeping, relaxing, or cooking.

    Natede can easily connect with other smart home devices, especially Amazon Alex and Google Assistant. It delivers reports of the IAQ with a simple voice command.

    Besides quality, Natede is all about sustainability and being eco-friendly.

    The product is made in Italy, with the company using recycled plastic and natural wood to help reduce waste that harms our surroundings. Plus, Natede has been designed, so it is easy to recycle, encouraging every Natede owner to repurpose the product eventually.

    All parts of the product are also interlocking and assembled without using any glue.

    Natede is committed to becoming a "green" company. True to that, Natede has pledged to plant ten trees for every Natede sold to rebuild our home planet--and you, as the consumer, could help with that project.

    Natede by Vitesy is not just a regular air purifier--it's a beautiful product that guarantees your home is always a safe place.
