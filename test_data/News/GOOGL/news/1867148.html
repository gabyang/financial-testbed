There have been seven films to date inside The Conjuring horror movie universe, and here's how and where they can all be watched online. A critical and commercial smash, The Conjuring gave birth to not only direct sequels but also a slew of spinoffs based on side villains introduced within the main films. One assumes that formula will eventually wear thin with audiences, but it hasn't yet, grossing nearly $2 billion worldwide on a combined production budget of less than $140 million.

✕ Remove Ads

With the release of the eighth film in The Conjuring universe, The Conjuring 3: The Devil Made Me Do It, it's a lock that the franchise crosses the $2 billion threshold within the next year. When exactly that happens remains to be seen though, since the Coronavirus pandemic is still dragging on, leading to more and more scheduled 2020 releases being delayed to 2021. With that in mind, The Conjuring 3's planned September release date may not stick, as Hollywood remains in turmoil.

Related: The Conjuring True Story: Bathsheba Sherman's History Explained

Whether or not The Conjuring 3 actually releases in 2020, fans still have seven prior films to get reacquainted with, thanks to the joys of instant streaming. Without any further ado, here's a rundown of where all prior Conjuring movies and spinoffs can be found online.

✕ Remove Ads

How to Watch The Conjuring Online

2013's The Conjuring, the James Wan film that started it all, is now streaming on Hulu, and can be rented for $1.99 (Microsoft Store), or $3.99 (Amazon Video, YouTube, Google Play, iTunes, FandangoNOW, VUDU), or bought for $7.99 (Microsoft Store), $9.99 (Amazon Video, iTunes, Vudu), or $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, FandangoNOW).

How to Watch Annabelle Online

2014's Annabelle, The Conjuring's first hit spinoff, can be rented for $3.99 (Amazon Video, YouTube, Google Play, iTunes, FandangoNOW, VUDU, Microsoft Store), or bought for $9.99 (Amazon Video, iTunes, VUDU), or $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, FandangoNOW, Microsoft Store).

✕ Remove Ads

How to Watch The Conjuring 2 Online

2016's The Conjuring 2, a direct sequel to the original, is now streaming on HBO, and can be rented for $3.99 (Amazon Video, YouTube, Google Play, iTunes, FandangoNOW, VUDU, Microsoft Store), or bought for $9.99 (Amazon Video, iTunes, VUDU), $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, Microsoft Store), or $19.99 (FandangoNOW).

How to Watch Annabelle: Creation Online

✕ Remove Ads

2017's Annabelle: Creation, a prequel to the first Annabelle, can be rented for $3.99 (Amazon Video, YouTube, Google Play, iTunes, FandangoNOW, VUDU, Microsoft Store), or bought for $9.99 (Amazon Video, iTunes, VUDU), or $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, FandangoNOW, Microsoft Store).

How to Watch The Nun Online

2018's The Nun, a spinoff of The Conjuring 2, is now streaming on HBO, and can be rented for $3.99 (Amazon Video, YouTube, Google Play, iTunes, VUDU, Microsoft Store), or bought for $9.99 (Amazon Video, iTunes, VUDU), or $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, FandangoNOW, Microsoft Store).

How to Watch The Curse of La Llorona Online

✕ Remove Ads

2019's The Curse of La Llorona, a stealth addition to the Conjuring universe, is now streaming on HBO, and can be bought for $9.99 (Amazon Video, VUDU, Microsoft Store), $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, iTunes), or $19.99 (FandangoNow). There are no rental options currently.

How to Watch Annabelle Comes Home Online

2019's Annabelle Comes Home, the first Annabelle movie to prominently feature the Warrens, is now streaming on HBO, and can be bought for $9.99 (Amazon Video, VUDU), or $14.99 (YouTube, Google Play, iTunes, FandangoNow, Microsoft Store). There are no rental options currently.
