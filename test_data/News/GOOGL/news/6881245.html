Comedian Milton Jones announces Belfast and Derry shows for 2020

Known for his flamboyant shirts and hairstyle he's his Milton Impossible show is coming to NI next year

    Comedian Milton Jones is bringing his new show to Belfast and Derry next year.

    Known for his flamboyant shirts and hairstyle on Mock the Week, Live at the Apollo and Radio 4, the hit comedian presents Milton Jones in Milton: Impossible.

    The English comedian will bring his show to Millennium Forum in Derry on April 24 and Ulster Hall , Belfast on April 26, 2020.

    Read More
    Related Articles
    • Love Island superfan Amy Schumer admits she feels 'very connected' to Irish model Maura Higgins

    In the new show, Milton reveals the truth about being an international spy, before being given a disappointing new identity which forced him to appear on Mock the Week, Live at the Apollo. But this is also a love story with the twist, or even a really bad sprain. Just gloriously daft nonsense or is there a deeper meaning?

    Tickets on sale this Thursday at 9am from:

    • The Millennum Forum: 028 71 264455 & online.
    • Ulster Hall Box-office: 028 90 334455 & Ulster Hall website.
    • Ticketmaster Outlets Nationwide & online.
