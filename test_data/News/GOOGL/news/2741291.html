Two people cut off by the tide near Plymouth - updates

HM Coastguard and Plymouth RNLI lifeboat are at the scene

    HM Coastguard is responding to reports that two people are "being cut off by the tide" near Plymouth this evening.

    The news comes as people on Plymouth Hoe reported seeing a lifeboat launched nearby.

    The coastguard has confirmed Looe and Tamar Coastguard Rescue Teams are attending the ongoing incident at Sharrow Point near Millbrook.

    Plymouth RNLI lifeboat is also at the scene.

    A spokesperson for HM Coastguard said the coastguard was called just before 6pm this evening (Wednesday).

    The search is ongoing.

    The search comes as police and the RNLI have advised people to "avoid the sea" on Friday afternoon.

    Surf magazine Carve says: "Please help save lives. A rapidly deepening low will produce a large swell combined with spring tides and good weather. There is no lifeguard cover.

    "A large swell arriving with a combination of spring tides and good weather is forecast to hit south facing beaches of England and Wales on Friday afternoon. With no lifeguard cover and a loosening of lockdown restrictions this could be recipe for disaster."

    Sharing Carve's warning on Twitter, RNLI lead for water safety in the South West, Steve Instance said: "This swell forecast is very worrying for north coast of Devon & Cornwall. A normal morning surf will build to over 15 feet in a couple of hours.

    "Combined with incoming spring tide & no lifeguards, this could be a recipe for disaster. Keep out of the water on Fri afternoon please."

    Plymouth RNLI shares guidance

    RNLI Plymouth has shared guidance for people visiting the coast.

    A spokesperson said: "The RNLI's lifeguards can't be everywhere this summer. If you're heading to the coast, #BeBeachSafe: check the weather and tides, keep an eye on your family and don't use inflatables. In an emergency call 999 for the Coastguard.

    "After the lockdown restrictions have been eased the RNLI urges all who are using the water to make sure they understand the risks and follow safety advice:

    * Take care near cliffs - know your route and your limitations
    * Have a plan - check the weather forecast and tide times
    * If you fall into the water unexpectedly, FLOAT TO LIVE. Fight your instinct to thrash around, lean back, extend your arms and legs, and Float
    * If individuals are choosing to go sailing or yachting it is important to ensure that equipment is properly checked and serviceable before going afloat."

    Update 21 May - SIX teenagers rescued

    An update from Plymouth RNLI today (Thursday, May 21) explains there were actually SIX people that required assistance from the RNLI and coastguard.

    A spokesperson for the RNLI said the charity located the six "trapped" teenagers near Sharrow Beach and took them onboard the lifeboat to safety.

    The people have been rescued

    A spokesperson for HM coastguard has confirmed the people have been "picked up," by Plymouth RNLI.

    HM Coastguard say they believe it was three people that required assistance, not just two people as initially thought.

    All three people are "safe and well".
