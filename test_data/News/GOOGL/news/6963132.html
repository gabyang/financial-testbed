    UFO sighting: Does Google Mars photo show crashed alien UFO on the Red Planet?

    AN UFO has been spotted on the surface of Mars, an alien conspiracy theorist has claimed.

    By Tom Fish
    14:04, Wed, May 22, 2019 | UPDATED: 15:15, Wed, May 22, 2019
    Link copied Bookmark

    Mars: Blue 'UFO' crash site found on planet's surface

    Scott Waring made the controversial claims about an oddly coloured anomaly discovered on Google Mars. In a video uploaded to YouTube he said: “This is a UFO that has crashed into a crater on Mars. “It really looks like a thick disc spacecraft and it is strange that nobody at Google edited it out.

    “This is one of the most detailed living ships I have ever seen.

    “I have seen a lot of blue structures on the planet Mercury, but never a blue UFO.”

    Waring proceeds to illustrate just how big this apparent anomaly is and the size of the so-called scorch marks.

    However Waring is happy to keep an open mind about the odd object’s origin.

    “From one angle it actually looks like a rock coloured blue, possibly by frozen gas.

    “Or could it be a frozen meteorite or asteroid that crashed.”

    The video, uploaded to YouTube last week, quickly garnered scores of comments with people offering their own interpretations.
