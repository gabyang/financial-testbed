Man arrested in Eaglesham in connection with attempted murder in Darnley

The 35-year-old man – who has not yet been charged – was collared by police after a substantial search including a helicopter

    A man arrested in Eaglesham on Sunday morning was traced by cops investigating an attempted murder.

    The 35-year-old man – who has not yet been charged – was collared by police after a substantial search of the Bonnyton area in the village.

    And the News can reveal the man is being questioned in relation to an attempted murder in Darnley on Saturday night.

    Emergency crews raced to Glen Esk Drive there at around 11.25pm after reports of a man found on the street with serious injuries to his arm and head.

    The 35-year-old is currently being treated for his injuries at Queen Elizabeth University Hospital where his condition has been described as ‘serious’.

    Detective Sergeant Nick Gribben of Helen Street Police Station said: “We are carrying out extensive enquiries to establish the circumstances surrounding this attempted murder, and trace whoever is responsible.

    “House-to-house enquires are ongoing and we are checking CCTV from in and around the local area to gather more information for our investigation.”

    We told you earlier how a police helicopter and a number of marked vehicles were seen in the conservation village of Eaglesham between 1am - 3am.

    Top news stories today

    Residents reported online of the helicopter search light in the Hill Drive, Polnoon Street and Bonnyton areas. A car there was taped off and a house searched.

    Detective Sergeant Gribben issued an appeal to the public to help police find out more about the incident.

    He added: “I would like to speak to anyone who witnessed this attack take place, or anyone who was driving in the area of Glen Esk Drive late on Saturday night who has dash-cam footage, as they may have information that will assist our inquiry.

    “Anyone with information on this attack should contact police on 101 quoting reference number 4699.”

    Information can be left anonymously by calling Crimestoppers on 0800 555 111.
