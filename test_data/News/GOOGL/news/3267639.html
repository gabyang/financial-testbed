    It can be a nightmare trying to get a doctor's appointment, which is why so many of us use Google as our GP.

    It's been revealed that the main health issue Cambridge residents can't stop searching is a migraine.

    The company Canabiotics looked through Google analytics to find the symptoms that Cambridge people searched and have discovered that 10 per cent of all health searches are from people concerned about their migraines.

    In second place for Cambridge health searches was hay fever, with 8.6 per cent of all searches from people curious about the symptoms. This was slightly more than other common issues such as flu (8.3 per cent), sore throat (7.9 per cent) and constipation (7.3 per cent).

    And the top 10 local searches weren't just physical symptoms, as mental health also contributed to Cambridge's biggest health queries. People looking for the signs of stress contributed to 5.4 per cent of the city's overall health searches.

    The top 10 was completed by chest pain, indigestion (both 4.8 per cent), nausea (4.4 per cent) and a headache (4.2 per cent).

    In the nearest cities analysed in the list, it was found that London is most concerned about hay fever, with 9.4 per cent of all Google searches relating to symptoms. A migraine was their third most Googled symptom with 8.8 per cent of all searches, narrowly beaten to second by sore throat (nine per cent).

    Up in Norwich, 9.6 per cent of all searches were related to migraines.

    If you do have a minor health concern that needs GP attention, it's best to book an appointment before April 1, when prescription prices are set to increase.

    Next month, all prescription charges in England will be £9.15. In Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, prescriptions will remain free.

    Additionally, charges for wigs and fabric supports, such as spinal aids, will also leap in line with inflation.

    Surgical bras will cost £30.05, abdominal or spinal supports will rise to £45.35, stock-size modacrylic (ie, synthetic) wigs will increase to £74.15, partial human hair wigs will increase to £196.40 and full bespoke human hair wigs will rise to £287.20.

    The cost of prescription pre-payment certificates (PPC) will also rise in April.

    The three-month PPC will increase by 55p to £29.65 while the 12-month PPC will increase by £1.90 to £105.90.

    Prescription charges

    • Single charge: £9.15
    • 3-month PPC: £29.65
    • 12-month PPC: £105.90

    Wigs and fabric supports

    • Surgical brassiere: £30.05
    • Abdominal or spinal support: £45.35
    • Stock modacrylic wig: £74.15
    • Partial human hair wig: £196.40
    • Full bespoke human hair wig: £287.20
