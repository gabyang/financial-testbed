    A top guide has named 27 restaurants in Kent it thinks you must try in 2020.

    Waitrose's 2020 Good Food Guide was released last September and pegged all of the foodie hot spots for the year ahead.

    And the good news is that no matter where you live in Kent there will be one near you.

    The long-list for potential entries is created from the huge volume of feedback that The Good Food Guide team receive from members of the public.

    ChomChom: The popular all you can eat Canterbury buffet which left me fat and desperately unhappy

    They collect their thoughts and recommendations throughout the year and then assess the recommendations, creating a long list of potential inclusions.

    The guide then sends a team of anonymous inspectors across the UK to sample the food just like any other customer.

    The mystery diners sit down to lunch or dinner and never announce who they are - and sometimes they are disguised by sharing the experience with a group.

    This can happen at any day of the week.

    Some restaurants, like The Sportsman in Seasalter, will appear in the guide regularly, but they must earn their place every year.

    There have been three new additions to the guide for 2020, alongside some returning favourites.

    Take a look at the full list below.

    Whits of Walmer

    Married couple Steve and Eva Whitney are at the helm of this restaurant, where its fish and shellfish that make up a large proportion of the French inspired menu.

    The couple say their mission is to offer a "reasonably priced, memorable dining experience which is packed with flavour."

    Steve's cooking has clearly made an impact with a mystery diner, and this he says comes from his extensive experience in classical French kitchens.

    Despite the heavily French influence - its the roast dinners that make it onto the Sunday lunch menu.

    Where : 61 The Strand, Walmer, Deal, CT14 7DP

    Contact : 01304 368881

    The Corner House, Minster

    The Corner House Restaurants (there is also one in Canterbury) are a collection of independent venues, situated in Kent, serving British produce, locally sourced.

    The Corner House Minster is critically acclaimed and has won the title of Kent Restaurant of the Year at the Taste of Kent Awards 2016 and 2018.

    Where: 41 Station Road, Minster, Ramsgate

    Contact: 01843 823000

    The Kentish Pantry, Margate

    The Kentish Pantry bistro serves and sells a range of Kent products including jams, chutneys, crackers and more

    Where : 1 Duke St, Margate CT9 1EP

    Contact : 01843 231150

    The Pig, Bridge

    When it comes to food, the boutique hotel's Coach House features a conservatory restaurant with open kitchen and views onto the kitchen gardens, and a charmingly scruffy barn has been converted into a very casual kitchen larder.

    Its derelict-chic style renovation sees walls lined with beautiful jars of preserved fruit and veg from the garden, and delicious wood-fired cooking.

    In a statement about where food is sourced from, bosses say: "Everything is driven by our gardener and forager – they grow and find the food – our chef then creates the menu.

    "Our commitment to a 25-mile menu not only supports our local suppliers, but means we can be 100% honest on the provenance of our ingredients."

    Where : Bourne Park Road, Bridge, Canterbury CT4 5LF

    Contact : 01227 830208

    Wyatt and Jones, Broadstairs

    Wyatt & Jones is a family run independent restaurant situated on Broadstairs seafront, serving seasonal dishes using local produce.

    The rustic cafe boasts stained-glass windows and sea views, and is known for its modern British menus.

    Where : 23-27 Harbour Street, Broadstairs CT10 1EU

    Contact : 01843 865126

    Albarino

    Instagram

    Albarino Tapas was inspired by a visit to a restaurant called Cal Pep, in Barcelona.

    From the outside it looks like a hole in the wall with a long queue of people waiting patiently (some not so patiently) outside.

    The locally sourced menu changes daily and is, of course, all about sharing.

    Where: 29 Albion St, Broadstairs

    Contact: 01843 600991

    Dory's of Margate

    What: Dory's is prided on its ethos - which it says is the consideration for people and the planet as a first priority.

    It even contains a shop where foodies can buy up any cheese, wine and beer that takes their fancy.

    The owners say they are working directly with growers, fishermen and suppliers who understand how to make the most of their produce and at the same time minimise the impact on our environment.

    Where : 24 High Street, Margate, CT9 1DS

    Contact : 01843 319978

    Frog and Scot

    Frog and Scot is a lively French bistro in the heart of the coastal town of Deal.

    The bistro is run by it’s owners Benoit and Sarah, the eponymous Frog and Scot. It has been included in both the 2018 Michelin Guide and the Good Food Guide 2018.

    Offering quality ingredients and superb cooking in a warm and friendly environment, Frog and Scot is bistronomy at it’s best.

    Where : 86 High Street, Deal CT14 6EG

    Contact : 01304 379444

    Angela's of Margate

    Angela’s philosophy is an uncomplicated one. They aim to serve delicious, ethically sourced, MSC certified seafood, alongside simple, seasonal vegetable-based dishes.

    The restaurant always endeavours to be sustainable, both in ingredients and in everyday practices, and work with the local community to specifically reduce the amount of waste they produce that might otherwise end up in landfill.

    Where : 21 The Parade, Margate CT9 1EX

    Contact : 01843 319978

    Chapter One

    Modern, stylish and comfortable, Chapter One is firmly established as one of the country’s top restaurants and is the proud holder of one Michelin star and four AA rosettes.

    At Chapter One, the restaurant’s ethos is to offer value for money for its award-winning food. Three courses at dinner is £38.50 and lunch is £27.00. There is also a less formal brasserie menu available Monday – Saturday lunchtimes.

    On a Sunday evening you can enjoy the a la carte menu for just £33.50 – the perfect way to round off your weekend.

    Where : Farnborough Common, Orpington BR6 8NF

    Contact : 01689 854848

    GB Pizza Co.

    GB Pizza Co started life in the back of a 1974 VW Campervan. Owners Rachel and Lisa travelled up and down the country with a "portable" wood-fired oven, serving their pizzas at farmers' markets, festivals and outdoor events.

    During the summer of 2012 they served pizza al fresco on Margate's Harbour Arm. This is when their love of Margate began. Very quickly afterwards, they found a site on the historic seafront and opened their first permanent restaurant.

    Where: 14 Marine Drive, Margate

    Contact: 01843 297700

    Hantverk & Found, Margate

    Instagram

    A seafood café and commissioning art gallery.

    The menu changes regularly, according to what is in season and what has been caught. As well as fish and seafood, they also have a small selection of vegetarian dishes and local cheese and charcuterie on offer

    Where: 18 King Street, Margate

    Contact: 01843 280454

    Harbour Street Tapas, Whitstable

    Harbour Street Tapas serve Spanish small plates inspired by the culinary traditions of Spain’s many regions.

    Showcasing the finest ingredients their menu features tapas classics and specials, with an emphasis on locally sourced produce, seasonality and quality.

    Where : 48 Harbour Street, Whitstable CT5 1AQ

    JoJo's, Whitstable

    Instagram

    Intimate, atmospheric, fun and honest, JoJo’s Meze, Meat & Fish Restaurant serves freshly cooked food with a Mediterranean influence.

    Sample dishes include gorgeous Greek salads, grilled sardines with lemon and black pepper, mouth watering mutton and feta koftas, marinated olives and authentic chorizo.

    Where : 2 Herne Bay Road, Whitstable CT5 2LQ

    Contact : 01227 274591

    Read's, Faversham

    Read's restaurant with rooms is set in an elegant Georgian manor house surrounded by tranquil grounds with lawns shaded by lofty cedar, weeping willow and horse chestnut trees.

    The restaurant's widespread reputation is founded on the distinctive cooking of Michelin-starred David Pitchford, whose seasonal dishes blend herbs and vegetables from the manor's own walled kitchen garden with local game and fish fresh from the quayside at nearby Whitstable and Hythe.

    Where : Macknade Manor, Canterbury Road, Faversham ME13 8XE

    Contact : 01795 535344

    Samphire, Whitstable

    Instagram

    Samphire is a Kentish Bistro firmly established within the Whitstable restaurant scene. Their style of food is simple with emphasis on great produce.

    Their food showcases the best seasonal ingredients sourced from local farms, allotments and producers in their regularly changing menus.

    Over the last ten years, they have built up close relationships with some incredible suppliers both locally, in the Garden of England, and the country as a whole.

    Where : 4 High Street, Whitstable CT5 1BQ

    Contact : 01227 770075

    Stark, Broadstairs

    Stark is a very intimate, relaxed restaurant, whose only demand of you is that you sit back, trust us with your dinner and enjoy the experience.

    The chef Ben has been lucky enough to work alongside Kent's finest chef Graham Garrett at The West House, helping to maintain Michelin star status for the past six years.

    Where: 1 Oscar Rd, Broadstairs

    Contact: 01843 579786

    Thackeray's, Tunbridge Wells

    The white clapboard building on the corner of London Road has stood there since 1660, but for the past 15 years it has become an icon for those who love fine dining, from Tunbridge Wells to further afield.

    Rated with one Michelin Star for over four years (until sadly losing it last year), Thackeray's has built up a reputation which reaches beyond Kent for the elegant service and artistic attitude to food which has earned it the culinary accolade.

    Where : 85 London Road, Tunbridge Wells TN1 1EA

    Contact : 01892 511921

    The Compasses Inn, Crundale

    A celebrity favourite is The Compasses Inn in Crundale, between Ashford and Canterbury.

    Paul Hollywood, who used to live nearby in Wingham, previously told his Twitter followers how much he loved their Sunday roast.

    Husband and wife team Rob and Donna Taylor took on The Compasses Inn near Canterbury in 2013. Donna looks after front-of-house while Rob works as head chef, and his food has already received a host of accolades.

    Where : Sole Street, Crundale, Canterbury, Kent CT4 7ES

    Contact : 01227 700300

    The Salutation, Sandwich

    Instagram

    The Salutation offers a wealth of dining options, from dawn ‘til dusk. With the North Wing opened up to accommodate the state of the art kitchen, and the dining rooms fully refurbished, there is now something for everyone at every time of day, all stemming from the same kitchen team and striving for the same sky-high standards.

    Whether it is residents’ breakfast, their laid-back lunch menu, afternoon tea on the terrace, a la carte dining or the tasting room menu, The Salutation is cult-like in their obsession with local sourcing and in-house production.

    Where : Knightrider St, Sandwich CT13 9EW

    Contact : 01304 619919

    The Small Holding, Kilndown

    Instagram

    The Small Holding excels at pairing classic techniques with a modern approach: focusing on innovating flavours, harnessing the fresh farm ingredients we grow on site here at Kilndown.

    Chefs at The Small Holding strive to gather seasonal produce for the restaurant's ever changing menus, ensuring that there’s constant evolution.

    Where : Ranters Lane, Kilndown, Cranbrook TN17 2SG

    Contact : 01892 890105

    The Sportsman, Whitstable

    The Sportsman, run by brothers Steve and Phil Harris, has held a Michelin star for several years, and serves high quality food in a low-key, cosy pub environment.

    It has gained acclaim on a national and international scale, including being named the UK's best restaurant in the National Restaurant Awards, and winning Outstanding Achievement of the Year in the Shepherd Neame Pub Awards 2016.

    The restaurant lets the area and surroundings dictate the menu, serving oysters and fish from the Thames Estuary and meat and vegetables from the land.

    Where : Faversham Road, Seasalter, Whitstable CT5 4BP

    Contact : 01227 273370

    The West House, Biddenden

    The West House was opened by Graham and Jackie Garrett in 2002 and is a small family run restaurant, with a reputation for fantastic food, earned through Graham's focus on excellent delivery of the finest ingredients.

    Housed in a sixteenth century weaver's cottage on Biddenden High Street, the small family run restaurant gained a Michelin star after its first year of opening and held it until last year.

    The restaurant has also acquired several other accolades in the 14 years since it opened, including appearing in Waitrose's Good Food Guide, featuring in Harden's top restaurants list in the UK, and holding 3 AA Rosettes.

    Where : 28 High Street, Biddenden, Ashford TN27 8AH

    Contact : 01580 291341

    Wheelers Oyster Bar, Whitstable

    Wheelers Oyster Bar the oldest restaurant in the town. You can come and dine in their famous but small Oyster Parlour, or at their Seafood Bar.

    Wheelers does not have a alcohol licence so you can bring your own wine to complement the food.

    Where: 8 High Street, Whitstable

    Contact : 01227 273311

    The Folkestone Wine Company

    Chef David Hart helped to set up the Frog And Scot in Deal and has also worked at the Michelin-starred Sportsman at Seasalter and Samphire in Whitstable.

    In 2017 he opened his own venture - The Folkestone Wine Company.

    It serves simple, seasonal food, mixing high quality British produce and cooking with French and Italian flourishes and much of the produce is sourced from Kent, while fish on the menu is caught from the nearby harbour.

    Diners can expect an array of exciting dishes on the menu at the eatery, including Whitstable native oysters, hams from the Basque country, fresh tagliatelle with rabbit, tarragon and wild mushroom, a braise of local Dexter beef and British cheeses.

    Where : Church Street, Folkestone CT20 1SE

    Contact : 01303 249952

    The Fordwich Arms

    This recently refurbished pub boasts a 1930s bar and an oak panelled dining room.

    Its riverside location promises a beautiful dining terrace during the summer, with lovely views of the river.

    Not only this, but Chef-patron Daniel Smith has been awarded the Observer's Young Chef of the Year award.

    Where : 1647 King St, Sturry, Canterbury CT2 0DB

    The Goods Shed, Canterbury

    Instagram

    Daily farmers' market and food hall in an early Victorian locomotive shed, selling an excellent variety of organic, free range and homemade produce.

    Hearty, rustic, daily changing dishes are served at scrubbed wooden tables.

    Where : Station Road West, Canterbury CT2 8AN

    Contact : 01227 459153
