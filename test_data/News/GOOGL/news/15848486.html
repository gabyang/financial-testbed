Google (Alphabet) Acquires North,formerly Thalmic Labs, which had raised $199M from investors like Amazon and Intel. The $180 million Google’s paying is, well, less. VC’s get their money back and then some, the founders get nothing except maybe a job at Google. The company’s tech, which includes IP from Intel’s failed acquisition, Vaunt, might find a role in a larger AR glasses play from the search giant. Google Glass 2.0 is a monocular microdisplay favored for certain enterprise applications. The consumer version of Google Glass soft launched in 2014 and died a quick death. North’s Focals use a micro-projection onto the lens of the glasses. Interestingly, this comes on the heels of rumors that Apple’s going to disrupt prescription optics.

Librestreamcloses $24 million series D funding round. Librestream, creators of an augmented worker platform Onsite, that combines remote experts and inspection with AI, has raised $55 million since 2003. The company reports an increase in use of more than 400% since the onset of the pandemic. The new cash will be used to expand to new verticals, telehealth and retail, and new markets outside of North America and Europe. The funding round was led by the Canadian Business Growth Fund, with participation from Export Development Canada, Emerald Technology Ventures, Pender Technology Inflection Fund, and BDC Capital.

Apple AirPods Go Spatial(Cnet)This is one of the few AR-ish announcements to come out of WWDC, which was remarkably mum about Apple Glasses. Spatial AirPods are still important however, despite Bose’s decision to kill its spatial audio glasses last week, because they enable all sorts of new applications, since the device would now know the precise orientation of the user.

Apple is reportedly unveiling an XR device next year (The Verge/Bloomberg)As we have been saying for months now, clearly they’re working on both a see-through AR device and a pass-through VR/AR device. The VR/AR device is said to be similar to an Oculus Quest but with higher fidelity visuals. A leaked internal announcement in late 2019 suggested a 2021 announcement for a 2022 launch. The AR centered device, Apple Glass, which may disrupt the prescription optics industry, will take longer to develop, so due a year later, 2023. Not surprisingly, Road to VR reports Apple is acquired spatial computing engineers laid off by Magic Leap in the spring.

Apple launches its first AR Cloud beginning in five cities. The AR Cloud provides highly accurate positional data using spatial imagery. The data is only available in publicly accessible areas but marks Apples official fore into imaging the world. So far developers only have access to the AR Cloud in the San Francisco Bay Area, Los Angeles, New York, Chicago, and Miami.

Teoohreceives more funding following spike in users during stay-at-home orders.Teooh is a virtual gathering platform that’s seen a 2,500% increase in users since the lockdowns began this Spring. Investors Spark Capital and General Catalyst have recently increased their investments in Teooh to a total seed funding of $4.5 million.

TheVive X Acceleratorhas nearly doubled its return on investments to date. In the past year, 17 Vive X companies have raised additional funding of over $60 million. Vive X has recently invested in 7 more companies, focusing mainly on enterprise use cases.

Omnidirectional treadmill KAT Walk C blows past Kickstarter goal. As of writing the KAT Walk C has raised over $1.2 million on Kickstarter with over 30 days remaining on an original goal of $100,000. Kat VR has been making VR treadmills since 2015 making this the third major version of their product. The KAT Walk C uses a circular bowl, a waist harness, and custom low-friction shoes to simulate treadmill style walking in all directions. Kat VR made sure a user’s walking movements create input just like a controller’s joystick making the omnidirectional treadmill compatible with most every PCVR and PSVR title.

Facebook has discontinued the Oculus Go.The 3DoF VR headset was made more or less obsolete by the release of the 6DoF standalone Oculus Quest. Though some are sad to see the Go go, as it was a cheaper option and central to some use cases such as providing tours for veterans or therapy. Facebook will continue to support the Go via security updates through 2022, but no new features are planned.

Oculus to provide developers with easier distribution of Quest applications. Currently, the Oculus Quest store is hard to get into, especially for small developers, as applications must pass a strict content guideline and approval process. Many smaller studios are releasing titles via direct sideloading and SideQuest instead. Starting in 2021 Oculus plans to open an additional path for developers to distribute applications, though the current guidelines for the official Quest app store will remain in effect. 

Facebook acquires Ready at Dawn, makers of Lone Echo.The acquisition comes at no surprise after years of the studio working closely with Oculus as a publishing partner and more. Echo Arena, their most popular title and one of the most successful multiplayer VR games to date, was made free for early Oculus owners helping solidify its popularity. Exact terms of the acquisition have yet to be announced, but the Ready at Dawn team will continue to operate independently like the other studio acquisitions Facebook has made. 

VRTO (VR Toronto) Virtual & Augmented Reality World Conference & Expo the academic edged group think in its fifth year, goes online, bringing us a thirty day summit called The Flotilla, which takes place from June 6 - July 6. The show features exclusive videos, VR worlds, & meetups to explore the future of remote work and play.

Fortnite livestreaming three Christopher Nolan movies for free.Players can join the Party Royale game mode and watch the movies at a theatre on the Party Royale island. This marks one of many events being held inside the game. US players can watch Inception on Friday, June 26 at 8 a.m., 8 p.m. and 11:55 p.m. ET.

Vive Sync updates VR conferencing to allow non-VR users to participate in meetings.Vive’s conferencing application has hosted over 1400 meetings since its beta launch two months ago. 

KitBash3Dhas given away $3 million worth of 3D assets for free during the pandemic thanks to the Epic Mega Grant.The free assets are a part of a #BrightArtDarkTimes initiative, a push to encourage artists during the pandemic. KitBash3D’s Utopia kit, which normally sells at $199, is now free. The kit includes loads of sci-fi city buildings and pieces perfect for a brighter looking future.

GIGXRlaunches free Hololens app for training the treatment of COVID-19 patients.The new app, HoloPatient: COVID-19, uses photorealistic volumetric video of patients exuding COVID-19 symptoms to help train caretakers in treating the disease.
