Smartech247 to use AI-powered Google technology in flagship platform

Published: 2024-03-26 08:02:40 ET GOOGL

(Alliance News) - Smarttech247 Group PLC announced on Tuesday a new partnership with a subsidiary of Alphabet Inc's Google LLC.

The Cork, Ireland-based artificial intelligence-enhanced cybersecurity services provider said the partnership will extend its existing suite of solutions by integrating advanced Google technology into its flagship VisionX platform.

Google Chronicle is a cybersecurity company that provides a cloud-native, AI-powered service that is designed to help businesses detect cyber threats.

By integrating with Google Chronicle's capabilities, organisations have access to threat detection and response leveraged by Google's technology.

Chief Executive Officer Raluca Saceanu said: "Smarttech247 remains committed to delivering innovative security solutions, and our collaboration with Google reinforces our commitment to staying at the forefront of cybersecurity excellence."

Shares in Smarttech247 were up 14% to 23.92 pence each at noon in London on Tuesday.
