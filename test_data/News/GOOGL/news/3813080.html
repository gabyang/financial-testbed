16 dog-friendly Cambridgeshire pubs and restaurants

There's a whole host of dog-friendly places to explore around the county - here are our picks

    Seeing as we're still deep within the throes of winter, it's always worth knowing where you can snuggle up for a pint in a good old British pub.

    But, while these establishments are accommodating for humans, it isn't always easy for those of us with friends of the furry four-legged variety.

    For some dog lovers, it can be hard to find a place that helps a hound feel at home, as many pubs decide against allowing dogs inside entirely.

    But that doesn't have to stop you from enjoying a sunny afternoon walk, followed by a bite to eat, with your canine companion.

    We've compiled a list of some top-notch dog-friendly places in Cambridgeshire where your best friend is allowed to join - just maybe keep them out of the kitchen.

    The Free Press

    The Free Press is a cosy backstreet pub within walking distance of Cambridge city centre.

    The century-old watering hole used to house a printing press which circulated a free local newspaper - hence the name.

    The pub, which offers large open fires during winter, is happy to accept dogs.

    The Free Press, 7 Prospect Row, Cambridge, CB1 1DU

    The Clarendon Arms

    The Clarendon Arms sits just a stone's throw away from Parker's Piece and Christ's Pieces, making it perfect for a pre-dinner stroll.

    The pub gets glowing reviews on TripAdvisor from the quality of food, to the atmosphere and the treatment of their doggy guests.

    Dogs are allowed throughout the pub, and if they're well behaved they may even be rewarded with a treat by staff.

    The Clarendon Arms, Clarendon Street, Cambridge, CB1 1JX

    The Champion of the Thames

    Dogs are allowed throughout the pub with water bowls and treats provided for their comfort.

    The Champion of the Thames is a cosy bar full of nooks and crannies with stencilled windows and a real coal fire burning away in winter.

    The pub is mostly known by many as a drinkers pub, but their menu has some pub classics in their repertoire alongside vegetarian and vegan options.

    Champion of the Thames, 68 King Street, Cambridge, CB1 1LN

    Cambridge Brew House

    Cambridge Brew House allows your furry friends anywhere except the restaurant, that includes their rooms upstairs, the terrace and bar area.

    The menu boasts a pretty good range of vegetarian and vegan options, alongside their burgers, pies and brew house classics.

    They brew their own beers on site and run beer tours every day between 12 noon and 6pm. They even suggest their own beer pairings for dishes on their menu, in case wine pairing isn't your thing.

    Cambridge Brew House, 1 King Street, Cambridge, CB1 1LH

    The White Hart

    A short journey out of Cambridge, the White Hart is located in Fulbourn.

    The cosy bar, perfect after a countryside walk, boasts an open fire and an impressive selection of beers, ales and pretty much anything else you could want.

    There's plenty of room in the pub for dogs, which are allowed in the bar area and on the terrace.

    The White Hart, 1 Balsham Road Fulbourn Cambridge CB21 5BZ

    The Fort St George

    The bar, snug and outside area of this pub overlooking the River Cam are all dog-friendly.

    As part of the Greene King chain they have a selection of food offers throughout the week - including Wing Wednesday, with 10 wings for £4.99 and 20 for £8.99.

    The location makes it a great end to a walk either in Jesus Green or Midsummer Common, or just on a warm day to sit outside and watch the rowers and punts go by.

    Fort St George, Midsummer Common, Cambridge, CB4 1HA

    The Snug

    This quirky cocktail bar and eatery has locations in Lensfield Road and East Road, and it's also dog-friendly.

    So whether you pop in for a bite to eat, a leisurely brunch or their happy hour - which runs 3pm until 9pm Mon - Sat and all day on Sunday, be sure to take your pooch along with you for wagging tails all round.

    The Snug Grafton, 170 East Road, Cambridge CB1 1DB

    The Snug Lensfield Road, 67 Lensfield Road, Cambridge, CB2 1EN

    The Corner House

    This pub has its own pub cat, Iris. The 9 year-old cat adopted The Corner House as her own local, so make sure your dogs are OK with cats, or keep them on a lead if you pop in.

    As a general rule The Corner House asks that dogs are kept on leads and away from diners who may not appreciate a wet nose snuffling around their dinner plate.

    The Corner House, 231 Newmarket Road, Cambridge, CB5 8JE

    The Black Horse

    This one may be a little out of the way for those in central Cambridge but it is worth the drive.

    Situated in the village of Elton there are plenty of places to let your four-legged-friend run off some steam.

    Dogs are welcome in the bar area, where you can order food from their À La Carte menu or one of their Sunday roasts, sourced from local butchers.

    There's also often a jar of dog biscuits at the end of the bar if you don't want your pooch to feel left out.

    The Black Horse, 1 Overend, Elton, PE8 6RU

    NOVI

    This dual identity venue says on their website that they "adore canine friends", so feel free to bring your doggy dinnerdate.

    A café and kitchen by day, and a cocktail bar with craft beers, wine and food by night, NOVI has something for everyone.

    The large windows overlooking Regent Street, it's the perfect place to people watch with your pal and a cup of coffee.

    NOVI, 12 Regent Street, Cambridge, CB2 1DB

    The Haymakers

    The Haymakers offers up traditional pub fare on their menu alongside Sicilian style thin and crispy pizzas for a hearty meal.

    If you're after something lighter then it's worth checking out their lighter dishes, it is worth noting that the kitchen closes between 3pm and 5pm.

    The Haymakers sells beers from Milton Brewery among other British micro-breweries and has a pretty impressive list of ciders to match.

    The perfect spot for some food after a walk by the river or the nearby recreation ground.

    The Haymakers, 54 High street, Chesterton, Cambridge, CB4 1NG

    Dog in a Doublet

    At one point the future of this building was uncertain, but landlord and lady John and Della revived this popular country pub with a welcoming atmosphere.

    If you're planning on bringing your furry friend for dinner they are happy to serve restaurant food in the bar area, where dogs are allowed.

    Dog in a Doublet, North Side, Thorney, PE 60RW

    The Earl of Beaconsfield

    Although Scampi Fries and Smoky Bacon have moved on (the spaniels, not the bar snacks) The Earl of Beaconsfield's new landlord, Robbie, has assured us that they are still very much a dog friendly pub.

    The pub is within an easy walk of Romsey Recreation Ground and Coleridge Recreation Ground for those wishing to stretch their canine friends legs before settling down to a well-deserved drink or bite to eat.

    Robbie took over the venue at the end of January, but don't worry, Scampi Fries and Smoky Bacon do still pop their heads in every now and then!

    The Earl of Beaconsfield, 133 Mill Road, Cambridge, CB1 3AA

    The Orchard

    The Orchard Tea Garden at Grantchester is a Cambridge tradition with a range of 1km, 2km and 3.5km walks with maps on their websites.

    Perfect in the summer for sitting outside and soaking up the country air with your dog while you enjoy a classic cream tea.

    The Tea Rooms, The Orchard, 47 Mill Way, Grantchester, CB3 9ND

    Cambridge Cookery School and Café

    Cambridge Cookery School and Café are so dog-friendly that they welcome your pets with a home-made doggy biscuit, made from beef stock, peanut butter and wheat germ.

    For the human companions they offer a selection of pastries, baked eggs, filled focaccia and a pretty scrumptious sounding french toast with roast plums, maple and ginger butter and lime gel.

    Cambridge Cookery School and Café, Purbeck Road, Cambridge, CB2 8EB

    The Blue Ball Inn

    The Blue Ball Inn dates back to 1767, with the cellars and foundations still surviving from the original building which was destroyed by a fire in the late 19th century and then rebuilt. It has also featured as part of the set on the ITV series Grantchester.

    Dogs are very welcome in the pub to settle down next to a log fire in the winter or to sit outside and enjoy its views overlooking Grantchester Meadows and across the river to the roof tops of Cambridge's colleges.

    The Blue Ball Inn, 57 Broadway, Grantchester, CB3 9NQ
