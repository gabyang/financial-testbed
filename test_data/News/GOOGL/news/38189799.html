            Most high-end phones cost more than a thousand dollars. Nonetheless, as flagship phones get pricier, cheaper phones are getting more desirable. This guide ranks all the best cheap phones, both landlines and mobile phones available at the moment. Many consumers are dropping expensive phones in favor of their cheaper and less complicated counterparts for good reasons.

            For instance, landline phones have vital features that smartphones can't match. According to testing reports, the best cheap phones have better voice quality than even the most expensive smartphones. Better coverage, dependable reception, and clear sound quality mean you will not experience dropped calls in the middle of a vital discussion or phone conference.

            So if the idea of buying a $1000 phone to get better sound quality makes you shrivel, don't worry, this guide has got you covered. The phones listed here are all less than $500. Whether it is a with house several rooms, a home office, or an office, these phones can act as an extra line to help you differentiate business calls from persona ones.

            Also, some landlines give you a lot of flexibility as they enable you to set up several phones and use them across your office or house. If you stay in an area that has bad cell reception, cheap blue tooth handsets will allow you to take calls via your phone to enhance sound quality. Bellow, you will find the best cheap phones based on your needs.

            The following tips will help you choose the right budget phone.

            Brand And Size

            Hundreds of manufactures are entering the US phone market, and it can be hard to determine which is the best. Of course, there are obvious giants like Samsung, Panasonic, and Motorola that have been in the game for a while, and you can't go wrong with them. For new entrants, there are a few that stand-out like Oppo and Xiaomi. But don't worry about that because all the products in this guide are a worthwhile purchase as they have been put through a full review process.

            ✕ Remove Ads

            Because of the recent annulment of Huawei's Google Play store license as a result of the US/China trade standoff, new Honor and Huawei phones will not have access to Google Playstore. This means important applications like Gmail and Youtube will not work. While Huawei is trying to address the issue by developing its own software and operating systems, this is likely to be an issue for you if you rely heavily on Google's applications.

            Mobile phones, particularly smartphones, are getting larger, with most modern models measuring up to six inches. While bigger handsets are ideal for browsing or watching films, they are not suitable for everybody. Some people find them too big or awkward, and would rather go for something they can easily use with one hand. One of the ways to determine which size is perfect for you is to check the inches on the phone's description. If you prefer a device that is not too big, go for a 4-5 inch handset.

            It is always safe to purchase from a certified retailer like Amazon if you are looking to purchase a phone SIM-free.

            Durability And Different Carriers

            It is best to avoid buying glass phones if the chances of dropping it are very high. However, if you must buy one, make sure you buy a protective case. Similarly, consider a water-resistant device. Most budget phones have an IP68 and IP67 water resistance nowadays, which means you can submerge them in water without any issues.

            You must go for a carrier that offers reliable coverage within your area. There are various platforms like Open Signal that can help you find detailed coverage maps for various carriers and areas. All you have to do is key in your location and then pick different carriers to see the network is like where you stay or work. If you will be doing data-intensive stuff, make sure that the coverage is strong enough in your area.
