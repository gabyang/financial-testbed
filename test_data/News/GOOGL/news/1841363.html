In this week’s update, Marvel Unlimited takes readers back to late October 2019 with a spooky selection of titles including Marvel Zombies: Resurrection and premiere issues from the hit series Excalibur #1 and Fantastic Four: Grand Design. And ghastly tales of magic and mayhem continue in the debut of Doctor Strange Annual and Red Goblin: Red Death. Every week, Screen Rant explores updates to the Marvel Unlimited archive — let’s take a look at the best of October 30, 2019, in our latest Pull List.

✕ Remove Ads

Two comic powerhouses, Fantastic Four and the X-Men comprise the standout debuts in the latest update to the subscription-based, Marvel Unlimited. Tini Howard’s mystical Excalibur #1 brings a newly-installed Captain Britain together with the X-Men’s Rogue, Gambit, and Jubilee, as well as their former nemesis, the sorcerous Apocalypse. Following an overwhelming response to Ed Piskor’s mutant chronicle X-Men: Grand Design, “the bestselling Grand Design franchise continues with Marvel’s First Family” in the premiere of Tom Scioli’s Fantastic Four: Grand Design. Including a fanciful overview of the Fantastic Four’s era-spanning career, Grand Design features the first appearances of centerpiece characters Doctor Doom, Black Panther, Namor, Galactus, Mole Man, and The Inhumans. Fantastic Four fans new and old alike will enjoy the 47-page introductory tale presented in a classic Silver Age-style with detailed annotations for additional reading by scholar Daron Jesen.

This week’s Marvel Unlimited update features comics new on stands back in October of last year and bewitching themes haunt a pair of new-to-subscribers #1 issues. The corpse of the planet-devourer Galactus washes up on Earth’s interstellar shores and Earth’s Mightiest Heroes, the Avengers, X-Men, and Fantastic Four set out to investigate, in Marvel Zombies: Resurrection #1. “Marvel Zombies - the horror smash-hit lives again,” with a subtle homage to the Alien franchise, a dead Galactus careening toward Earth with its deadly infestation, and even a portentous quote from Master of the Macabre, Edgar Allan Poe. And things get really creepy in Red Goblin: Red Death, with three nightmarish tales featuring the inhuman union of evil genius Norman Osborn, the Green Goblin, and the alien Carnage symbiote — “here, at last, are the untold stories of Red Goblin’s reign of madness and mayhem.”

✕ Remove Ads

Get spaced out while you’re stuck inside with Star Wars and the Silver Surfer. This week Marvel Unlimited brings subscribers a trio of titles ideal for intergalactic indulgence, Journey to Star Wars: Rise of Skywalker, Doctor Aphra Annual, and Silver Surfer: Black. The prelude-style Journey to Star Wars: Rise of Skywalker - Allegiance concludes in issue #4 with the Mon Calamari reuniting with Rebellion and the First Order gaining the higher ground in the galaxy. In Star Wars: Doctor Aphra Annual #3, series writer Simon Spurrier takes the anti-hero archeologist to a “wretched hive of scum and villainy” on Tatooine and unexpected encounters with the Wookiee bounty hunter Black Krrsantan and the notorious gangster, Jabba the Hutt. And cosmic castaway Norrin Radd’s acid trip through a black hole and into the primordial past concludes in Donny Cates and Tradd Moore's Silver Surfer: Black, as the sentinel of the spaceways, finally faces the Knull, the god of the symbiotes, before darkness and death consume the very light of the universe.
