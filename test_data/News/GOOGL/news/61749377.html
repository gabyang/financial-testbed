5G LLC Is Advancing Wireless Connectivity in Commercial Real Estate With Google Orion

December 15, 2020 03:04 PM Eastern Standard Time

BETHESDA, Md.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--5G LLC is pleased to announce its expanded solution offering through Google Orion. 5G LLC has established itself by connecting real estate portfolios to telecom carriers using outdoor 5G wireless solutions. Now with Google Orion, 5G LLC is bringing to market an exceptional platform of inbuilding solutions for Commercial Real Estate Clients.

One of the first tenant priorities when arriving at a prospective property is to monitor and review wireless signal strength. Employee and Guest connectivity has become increasingly meaningful for the decision-making of tenants, and the real estate market has taken notice. For many property owners, the goal is to be a technology enabled property that meets the tenant’s needs.

CRE owners, tenants and customers have come to expect connectivity within their buildings as a core amenity. 5G LLC is bridging the ecosystems of CRE and technology companies in a way that will benefit everyone. This begins with a portfolio solution that maximizes the CRE client’s ability to monetize rooftops at zero cost. Partnering with Google to provide a new and innovative solution brings the value indoors.

“I am very excited to see Google and 5G LLC working together to solve for the whole 5G and connectivity challenge outside and inside a property. It’s an innovative way for owners to differentiate their buildings, generate revenue, and provide better connectivity to their tenants.” -5G LLC CEO Matt Davis.

Orion Wifi is a new platform from Area 120, Google’s in-house incubator for experimental projects. It is an easy way for public venues—such as a grocery store, medical office, commercial building, MDU, or a mall—to sell Wi-Fi capacity to cellular carriers. With Orion Wifi, your carrier can automatically and securely connect you to the Wi-Fi as you enter the building.

Although it is effortless on the part of the guest or tenant, a lot is happening in the background. As you walk into a grocery store, Orion Wifi helps the local network tell the carrier about its price and quality. If the carrier decides the connection is sufficient and will provide an enhanced experience, you are auto connected. When you are connected, Internet traffic flows over the Wi-Fi network just as if you had connected directly to the Wi-Fi. Orion does not access users’ Internet traffic.

Technology and Real Estate professional Rick Varnell comments: “CRE wants to have good wireless connectivity that works in and around their buildings. Extending the 5G LLC offering to Google on the inside of a property supports that and solves for weak signals inside the building.”

“It is extremely exciting to be part of the Orion release with Google. CRE is always looking for the solution with connectivity that provides the least resistance. This is an amazing and extremely easy deployment for asset managers and property managers. It is a privilege to bring the Google Orion opportunity to my peers.” - Jason Lind, Sr. Real Estate Executive

The 5G LLC leadership, a unique combination of real estate and technology expertise, brings our solutions to new heights.
