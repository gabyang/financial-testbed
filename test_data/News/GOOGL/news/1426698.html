'No doubt' - The five Nottingham Forest players with the biggest points to prove when season restarts

Sabri Lamouchi's men will get their campaign back underway on Saturday against Sheffield Wednesday

    In less than a week’s time, Nottingham Forest will be back in action, ready to resume their promotion push.

    After being suspended for the past three months, the Championship season will restart on Saturday, with the Reds heading to Sheffield Wednesday.

    Earning a place in the Premier League will be the target for Sabri Lamouchi’s men. And with a packed fixture list, the manager will likely need to utilise his squad in his attempt to achieve that goal.

    So who will have a particular point to prove when the campaign is underway again? Here’s our verdict.

    Joao Carvalho

    The man who perhaps poses the biggest dilemma for Lamouchi - how, when and whether to use him in the final nine games.

    The head coach has consistently maintained he wants “more” from Carvalho, acknowledging his natural talent but hinting he hasn’t quite hit that top standard.

    There’s no doubting the club record signing’s ability. He’s just struggled to find the kind of form he’s capable of this season - and been in and out of the team as a result.

    Fitting him into his preferred No.10 role can be tricky, especially as Tiago Silva has enjoyed a fine first campaign at the club.

    Carvalho can still have a key role to play in the final nine games. But when he gets his chance, he needs to take it, if he wants to establish himself as a regular under Lamouchi - particularly as speculation about his future has never really gone away.

    John Bostock

    Plenty of fans have been calling for the midfielder to get a runout when matches resume, after he had impressed in the two fixtures before the shutdown.

    Bostock hasn’t been involved a great deal this term, making just five league appearances in total - and only one of them in the starting XI.

    The fact he’s had some tough competition for a spot hasn’t helped his cause, with Ben Watson and Samba Sow having been impressive when they’ve played.

    But the Toulouse loanee has done well whenever he has been on the pitch, and scored a cracking goal in the recent friendly victory over Wolves.

    He will surely hope he has done enough to earn another chance at some point.

    And with his loan running out, he’ll no doubt also want to impress with a view to what happens next - whether that be going back to his parent club or trying to secure a deal at Forest or elsewhere.

    Nuno da Costa

    The January signing has made just one appearance since his switch to the City Ground from Strasbourg, managing 20 minutes as a substitute in the 1-0 defeat to Charlton Athletic.

    Injury robbed him of the chance to add to that tally prior to games coming to a halt.

    Forest supporters still haven’t really been able to see what he has to offer. And likewise, the striker hasn’t yet been able to show Lamouchi what he can do in the Championship.

    He faces a fight to take Lewis Grabban’s place in the first-team, but the hectic schedule surely means others will get their opportunity at some point - be that from the start or off the bench.

    Da Costa’s time will come.

    Adama Diakhaby

    Another January addition who still has a lot to prove.

    The Huddersfield Town loanee hasn’t exactly pulled up many trees during his time so far at the City Ground.

    His pace is a great asset, and if used in the right way can offer the Reds a good attacking outlet. He’s just not been able to make the best of it in his nine appearances in the Garibaldi and had looked a little short of confidence.

    The break may well have done him good.

    And though he, too, faces a battle to get into the starting line-up, he could still make a difference from off the bench.

    Forest are understood to have an option to make his move a permanent one. These final nine games could help sway any decision one way or the other.

    Tendayi Darikwa

    The full-back perhaps doesn’t have much to prove to himself, or indeed the Reds faithful, but more so to the manager.

    Lamouchi hasn’t really had chance to take a proper look at him.

    Darikwa suffered a ruptured ACL on the eve of the campaign and has not yet featured for the Frenchman.

    The fact this season has been extended works in his favour, and he may well now get the chance to play his part.

    Unseating Matty Cash from the right-back position isn’t going to be easy by any means, and Carl Jenkinson is also waiting in the wings.

    Darikwa was in decent form last term, though, and he will no doubt relish the chance simply to get back out on the pitch.
