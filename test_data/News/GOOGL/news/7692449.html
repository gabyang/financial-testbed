SNORTY BOY

Richard Bacon forced to turn off his Amazon Alexa because it told kids about his Blue Peter drug shame

  • Joe Kasper
  • Published: 21:30, 19 Jun 2020
  • Updated: 12:55, 20 Jun 2020

    PRESENTER Richard Bacon has been forced to turn off his Alexa because it kept telling his kids about his past drug shame which resulted in his sacking from Blue Peter.

    His children asked the Amazon device who their father is before it exposed his BBC hosting background and why he was axed in 1998.

    Richard, 44, said: “I am sitting in a room in my house in Los Angeles and there is one of those Amazon Alexas next to me and my kids said, ‘Oh Alexa, who is Richard Bacon?’.

    “And it comes up straight away, ‘Richard Bacon is an English television presenter. He was fired from Blue Peter in 1998 for taking…’.

    “I jumped in and said, ‘Alexa stop. Stop’.

    “They said, ‘What are you saying stop for? Why did you get fired?’. And then they asked again.

    “And so it is right there at the surface even though it was 21 years ago.”

    Richard is dad to Arthur, 8, and Ivy, 6 who he shares with wife Rebecca McFarlane.

    He joined Blue Peter in 1997 but had his contract terminated after he was caught taking cocaine.

    The then head of BBC children’s programmes Lorraine Heggessey went on air to explain Bacon’s dismissal to viewers and Richard was forced to hand his badge in.

    In 2018 he revealed he had ADHD which he said helped him understand why he took the Class A drug.

    Richard told Andy Coulson's Crisis What Crisis? podcast: “So my answer is when you have something like that you are well known for and it is dominant then its important to own it.”

    The former The Big Breakfast host is currently working behind the scenes in TV in America.

    Last month he bagged a deal with broadcaster NBC to develop non-scripted shows.
