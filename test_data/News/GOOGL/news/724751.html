    iPhone 13, iPhone 13 Pro, and Galaxy Z Flip 3 • Latest Express.co.uk Reviews

    EXPRESS.CO.UK has been busy testing the all-new iPhone models, including the iPhone 13 mini, iPhone 13, iPhone 13 Pro and 13 Pro Max. Is it finally time to upgrade to the latest iPhone? We've got the verdict. Android fans need to check out our Galaxy Z Flip 3 review too.

    By Aaron Brown, Express Affiliate Development Editor with 10 years of experience writing about the latest developments in consumer technology, product reviews, and buying advice
    08:01, Wed, Sep 29, 2021 | UPDATED: 08:33, Wed, Sep 29, 2021
    Link copied Bookmark

    If you're looking for reviews, we've got 'em. Find an up-to-date list of all our latest reviews (Image: GETTY • EXPRESS NEWSPAPERS)

    Finding yourself umming and erring between two smartphones models, Bluetooth speakers, or headphones? You're in the right place!

    Scroll down to find a comprehensive list ofthelatest product reviews written by the Express.co.uk consumer tech team. We're always taking photos on phones, barking orders at smart speakers, replacing household goods with Wi-Fi enabled equivalents, re-wiring routers, and streaming boxsets from HDMI dongles– all so you don't have to!

    Here at Express.co.uk, we test everything from smartphones tosmart home gadgetry, TVs to fitness trackers, laptops to headphones, and much, much more.Here's a comprehensive list of some of the latest tech goodies that have passed through Express.co.uk HQ. We test everything we review thoroughly, usually living with the device in our own homes for a few weeks before putting pen to paper.

    Most recently... We've got our hands on all four new iPhone 13 models ahead of launch. The iPhone 13 and 13 mini supersede the iPhone 12 and 12 mini from last year, while the iPhone 12 Pro and 12 Pro Max are replaced by the 13 Pro and 13 Pro Max. All of these new handsets boast re-engineered cameras, a smaller Face ID notch, and new colours. There's plenty more to dig into – including the arrival of the first 120Hz ProMotion display in an Apple-branded handset – so make sure to read the full review below.

    It's also been an exciting new few weeks for Android fans too. Samsung has launched its third-generation of folding smartphones with the arrival of the Galaxy Z Flip 3. This clever design brings a 6.7-inch AMOLED display into a small form-factor that can easily fit into any pocket. The Flip 3 is the first folding handset from Samsung to arrive with a price tag under £1,000. So, is it finally time to buy a pliable phone? Read our Express.co.uk review to find out.
