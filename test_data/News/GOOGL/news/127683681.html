    Apple Inc.AAPL leverages advances in artificial intelligence (AI) to improve the user experience Gain experience and drive innovation.

    A recent demonstration by OpenAIwhich demonstrates the power of virtual assistants and multimodal AI on smartphones, has created a spark Excitement among investors.

    This demonstration not only highlights the potential for developers to create breakthrough apps, but also underscores Apple's position as a leader in AI integration. As Apple continues to seek new avenues for growth and differentiation, investors are watching closely to see how these developments will impact the company's future trajectory.

    The Apple analyst

    BofA Securities analyst Wamsi Mohan maintained its Buy rating on Apple stock with a price target of $230 per share. The price target implies an upside potential of 23.5% from the time of review.

    He recently shared important insights about this Apple Inc. AAPLThis sheds light on the implications of OpenAI's latest developments on iOS and the implications for Apple stock investors.

    Also read: Apple's largest iPhone assembler reports a 72% rise in profits, even as sales suffer from weaker demand for consumer electronics

    The Apple Thesis

    According to Mohan, the recent OpenAI demo showed the potential for developers to use virtual assistants and create a variety of innovative apps. While on-device functionality may be limited compared to cloud-based AI, there are various monetization options such as ads and subscription fees.

    Apple is expected to be “a net beneficiary of AI at the edge.”

    Disruptive nature of AI

    OpenAI's demo highlighted the possibilities of AI at the edge, particularly on Macs and iPhones, and demonstrated the seamless integration of voice, text and images. This demonstration highlighted the potential of AI to significantly increase productivity.

    Mohan believes Apple will benefit from GenAI on the sidelines, potentially boosting gross margins and driving momentum in the services sector.

    The demo also demonstrated real-time language translation, narration and tutoring capabilities, reflecting the work of professionals such as translators and educators. This opens up exciting opportunities for developers to create AI-driven apps.

    Mohan points out that OpenAI may not follow Apple's licensing model Alphabet Inc GOOG GOOGL dba Google, alternative monetization methods could be explored.

    On-device vs. on-cloud AI

    With OpenAI's GPT-4 model supporting up to 1 trillion parameters, compared to GPT-3's 175 billion, the richness of the AI ​​experience is best experienced in the cloud. However, on-device models can still handle a significant number of parameters.

    Apples Siri currently uses a mix of on-device processing and offloading queries to Apple servers.

    Investment justification forApple stock

    BofA Securities maintains a Buy rating on Apple based on several factors:

    • Expected Strong iPhone Upgrade Cycle in F25 Driven by Gen AI Features,
    • Higher growth in service revenue,
    • Increased margins through internally developed silicon,
    • Continuous returns on capital,
    • AI capabilities attract higher institutional participation and
    • Manageable legal risks.

    Mohan's analysis points to a positive outlook for Apple, driven by advances in AI and the potential for innovative app development. Investors are advised to consider these factors when evaluating their Apple stock investment decisions.

    AAPL Price Promotion: Apple stock was trading at $186.85 per share at the time of publication.
