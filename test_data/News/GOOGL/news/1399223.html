Man City's transfer stance on Leicester City star amid growing Chelsea interest

Leicester City transfer news: Pep Guardiola's side have been heavily linked with a move for King Power Stadium defender Ben Chilwell while the Blues are also keen

    Manchester City remain keen on strengthening their defensive options this summer but a move for Leicester City's Ben Chilwell is not a priority, according to reports.

    LeicestershireLive's sister title in the city, the Manchester Evening News, cites a report via media organisation ESPN that claims Man City do not want to sign a left-back.

    Instead, they would rather focus on upgrading their options at centre-back after a season littered by defensive errors.

    The MEN claim Citizens boss Pep Guardiola is keen on upgrading at the back, with injuries to Aymeric Laporte hampering the Etihad Stadium club's defence of their Premier League title with Liverpool set to clinch the trophy this season.

    ESPN say Man City are keeping track of Chilwell's current status with transfer interest growing in the 23-year-old.

    Reports coming out of London have claimed Frank Lampard will be backed in the transfer market this summer by Stamford Bridge owner Roman Abramovich, with the Chelsea boss keen to land Chilwell as he aims to upgrade his options at left-back.

    Chelsea are keen to improve their squad after two transfer windows without any new players having been banned from making signings.

    City, though, are relaxed about speculation surrounding Chilwell with the player contracted to 2024 and keen to play for City, who are in third place, in the Champions League.
