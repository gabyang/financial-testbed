Best smart plug for Google Home and Alexa 2024

Buying Guides
By Samuel Contreras
Contributions from
Patrick Farmer, Chris Wedel

Build a great smart home with these smart plugs.

When you purchase through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission. Here’s how it works.

Not every device in your home needs to be smart, but you can still stay in control with one of the best smart plugs for Google Home and Alexa. Whether you bought into either ecosystem or are balancing devices on both, having a smart plug that works with either is a huge relief. When it comes down to it, one of the main strengths of a smart plug is being able to control it from just bout anywhere, and getting one of the best smart plugs with good support for both AI assistants that don't need a hub makes controlling your devices much simpler.

Note about Works with Nest:If you're a Google Nest user, it's worth noting that on September 29, 2023, all Works with Nest connections will stop working. According to Google, "You can continue to automate your home by creating Routines in the Google Home app’s automation tab or the script editor", which will become available sometime in 2023. To learn more, head to Google's Nest Support page.

Upgrade your smart home with smart plugs for Google Home and Alexa

Amazon Echo and Google Home/Nest are some of the best smart speakers that crossed over from the early adopter phase and took their rightful place within mainstream culture. Luckily, there are thousands of smart devices that work with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. If you're just getting started building your smart home, Google offers a new smart home directory to help you pick the best devices.

But of all the ways to upgrade your smart home, the best smart plugs offer one of the most versatile. Being able to control your favorite reading lamp or other small appliance using just your voice and your preferred smart assistant legitimately feels like magic. The Kasa Smart Plug HS103P4 is one of the best picks to get you started as it's not only affordable but reliable, letting you operate your devices even when you're not at home. You can double your outlet capacity with the Govee Dual Smart Plug with independent plug controls if you're looking for more options.

For a more weatherproof solution to upgrading a bunch of your favorite tech all at once, you'll want to consider the Caséta outdoor smart plug from Lutron. It offers an IP65 rating so that you can set it up on the front porch or in your backyard. What's more, it's compatible with Alexa, Google Assistant, Apple HomeKit, and Ring if you own a smart bridge.
