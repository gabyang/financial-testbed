What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS: 

  • Target (TGT) reported lower-than-expected Q3 earnings and management guided Q4 comps and EBIT below the consensus view, sparking a selloff in the shares [more]
  • Lowe's (LOW) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q3 [more]
  • Micron (MU) said it will reduce DRAM and NAND wafer starts by roughly 20% and warned that the outlook for 2023 has weakened [more]
  • TJX (TJX) reported mixed Q3 results and lowered its FY23 earnings and revenue guidance [more]
  • Advance Auto Parts (AAP) reported downbeat Q3 results and cut its FY22 outlook [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS: 

  • Medtronic (MDT) downgraded to Hold from Buy at Deutsche Bank [more]
  • Texas Instruments (TXN) assumed with Neutral, down from Outperform, as new analyst takes over coverage at Credit Suisse [more]
  • Home Depot (HD) downgraded to Market Perform from Outperform at Raymond James [more]
  • Rocket Companies (RKT) cut to Sell at Argus on decline in mortgage originations [more]
  • Citi resumes Polestar (PSNY) with a Buy, encouraged by recent progress [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB: 

  • UnitedHealth's (UNH) pharmacy benefits manager pitting biosimilars on formulary against AbbVie's (ABBV) Humira, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Otonomo (OTMO) is in talks to merge with rival Wejo (WEJO), Globes reports [more]
  • Disney World (DIS) will raise park ticket prices, NY Post reports [more]
  • Apple (AAPL) is preparing to start sourcing chips from an Arizona facility, Bloomberg says [more]
  • FBI director tells U.S. lawmakers he is "extremely concerned" about TikTok, CNBC reports [more]


4. MOVERS: 

  • Natera (NTRA) higher after announcing a 11.43M share offering [more]
  • Alcon (ALC) gains in New York after reporting quarterly results and narrowing its FY23 guidance [more]
  • Restaurant Brands (QSR) rises after appointing Patrick Doyle as executive chairman [more]
  • Brookdale Senior Living (BKD) lower after commencing an offer of 2.5M tangible equity units [more]
  • Tattooed Chef (TTCF) declines after reporting quarterly results and cutting its FY22 revenue view [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE: 

  • Spire (SR) reported mixed Q4 results [more]
  • Similarweb (SMWB) reported better-than-expected Q3 results [more]
  • Sportradar (SRAD) raised its FY22 revenue guidance [more]
  • ZIM Integrated (ZIM) reported upbeat Q3 results and cut its FY22 EBITDA outlook [more]
  • Shoe Carnival (SCVL) reported upbeat Q3 earnings [more]


INDEXES: 

Near midday, the Dow was up 47.04, or 0.14%, to 33,639.96, the Nasdaq was down 140.60, or 1.24%, to 11,217.81, and the S&P 500 was down 20.21, or 0.51%, to 3,971.52.
