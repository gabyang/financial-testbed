UK watchdog objects to Google's ad tech practices

Published: 2024-09-06 05:17:48 ET GOOGL

(Reuters) - Britain's competition watchdog said on Friday it provisionally found Google had abused its dominant positions through the operation of both its publisher ad server and buying tools to restrict competition in the UK.
