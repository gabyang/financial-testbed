[The Bouqs in PR Newswire] The Bouqs Company Names Alejandro Bethlen as Chief Executive Officer

Jun 15, 2020 | News Center


Share:

The Bouqs Company (Bouqs.com) announced today the hire of its new Chief Executive Officer, Alejandro Bethlen. Bethlen joins the leading direct-to-consumer floral delivery company with over two decades of leadership and international experience in scaling global businesses for Fortune 100 companies including Amazon and Procter & Gamble. Bethlen will join Founder John Tabis and Co-Founder Juan Pablo Montufar to spearhead the company’s growth and catalyze existing momentum with expansion initiatives.
