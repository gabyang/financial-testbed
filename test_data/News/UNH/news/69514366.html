Wall Street in Fives - Must Read Lists at Midday
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these Top Fives, as compiled by the editors of The Fly.


1. STOCK NEWS:

  • General Motors (GM) trading lower despite "beat and raise" report for Q2 [more]
  • Ford (F) reported a 31.8% drop in July U.S. car sales [more]
  • The European Commission has approved a new Novavax (NVAX) COVID-19 vaccine contract for up to 200M doses [more]
  • Zymergen (ZY) plunges after business update, saying it no longer expects product revenue in 2021 and announcing that Josh Hoffman will step down as CEO [more]
  • VICI Properties (VICI) will acquire MGM Growth Properties (MGP) in a deal valuing MGP at $17.2B [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Under Armour (UAA) upgraded to Overweight with $30 target at JPMorgan [more]
  • Rhythm Pharmaceuticals (RYTM) downgraded to Neutral at Ladenburg and downgraded to Underperform at BofA [more] 
  • Zymergen (ZY) downgraded at no less than six firms, including BofA double downgrades to sell on "worrying" execution issues [more]
  • Capri Holdings (CPRI) upgraded to Outperform at Cowen on innovation and valuation [more]
  • Corsair Gaming (CRSR) downgraded to Neutral at Credit Suisse following Q2 results [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • The FDA aims to give Pfizer's (PFE) COVID-19 vaccine final approval by Labor Day or sooner, NY Times reports [more]
  • The DOJ is weighing blocking UnitedHealth's (UNH) planned acquisition of Change Healthcare (CHNG), Information says [more]
  • Boeing's (BA) 737 MAX heads to China for a key test, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) will face a widening campaign against its labor practices, NY Times says [more]
  • SoftBank (SFTBY) has quietly built a $5B stake in Roche (RHHBY), Bloomberg says [more]


4. ﻿MOVERS:

  • BeyondSpring (BYSI) surges after reporting “positive” topline data from its DUBLIN-3 registrational trial in plinabulin in combination with docetaxel [more]
  • eXp World Holdings (EXPI), Global Blood Therapeutics (GBT), and Cerus (CERS) higher after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Cardlytics (CDLX), W&T Offshore (WTI) and Alteryx (AYX) lower after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Tellurian (TELL) declines after announcing a 35M share offering priced at $3 [more]
  • Callon Petroleum (CPE) slides after entering agreement to acquire  leasehold interests and related oil, gas, and infrastructure assets of Primexx Energy Partners and affiliates [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Lyft (LYFT) reported company's first quarterly adjusted EBITDA profit [more]
  • Match Group (MTCH) provided upbeat revenue guidance for Q3, FY21 [more]
  • Akamai (AKAM) provided "beat and raise" Q2 report [more]
  • Activision Blizzard (ATVI) also provided a "beat and raise" report for Q2 [more]
  • CVS Health (CVS) raised its revenue guidance for FY21 [more]


INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was down 274.09, or 0.78%, to 34,842.31, the Nasdaq was up 1.91, or 0.01%%, to 14,763.21, and the S&P 500 was down 16.63, or 0.38%, to 4,406.52.
