Company Profile

Savannah Energy PLC is an independent energy company focused on the delivery of projects in Nigeria, Niger, and Cameroon. The Companyâs Nigeria assets include two oil and gas fields, Uquo and Stubb Creek, and the Accugas midstream business, all of which are located in Southeast Nigeria. It holds an 80% economic interest in the exploration, development, and production of gas within the Uquo field. The Companyâs Accugas midstream business focuses on the marketing, processing, distribution, and sale of gas to the Nigerian market. Its acreage in Niger covers approximately 50% of the Agadem Rift Basin (ARB), an area equivalent to the Central North Sea Basin. In Cameroon, the Cameroon Export Transportation System (ETS) comprises the Cameroon export pipeline, the Kome Kribi 1 floating storage and offloading unit (FSO) and related infrastructure. The Company is also focused on the development of the Bini a Warak Hydroelectric Project, located in the northern Adamawa Region of Cameroon.
