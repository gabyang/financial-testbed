Infoworks and Tech Mahindra Partner to Accelerate Enterprise Migration to the Cloud

Companies to host webinar w/ Databricks - connecting digital transformation and innovation

June 18, 2020 12:33 PM Eastern Daylight Time

PALO ALTO, Calif.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Infoworks.io, the leader in Enterprise Data Operations and Orchestration (EDO2) systems, today announced its expanded partnership with Tech Mahindra Ltd., a leading provider of digital transformation, consulting and business reengineering services and solutions, to deliver turnkey solutions that greatly accelerate migration of premises based enterprise data warehouse (EDW) and Hadoop environments to the cloud. Infoworks DataFoundry, the industry’s first end-to-end solution for EDO2, coupled with Tech Mahindra’s industry-leading services, provide customers fast and reliable Cloud migrations. The joint Infoworks-Tech Mahindra offering is a best-in-class solution for customers looking to improve analytics agility, increase operational efficiency and reduce dependence on costly and complex Teradata, IBM Netezza and Hadoop infrastructure.

“Digital transformation is now imperative for many organizations, yet the underlying complexity is delaying projects and preventing success,” said Russ Barck, Vice President, Strategic Alliances at Infoworks. “With this expanded partnership, we’re combining the power and automation of the Infoworks DataFoundry platform with the exceptional people and processes of Tech Mahindra to help customers – whether data engineers, scientists, analysts and even business users – accelerate their digital transformation projects and deliver business value.”

Rajesh Chandiramani, Senior Vice President and Global Business Head ESRM, AI & Data Analytics, Tech Mahindra, said, “This collaboration will accelerate our development in cloud-based applications and enable the analytics that empower enterprises with data-informed decision-making critical to their success in highly competitive environments. This partnership also lines up with our TechMNxt charter, Tech Mahindra is focused on leveraging next-generation technologies to enable its customers to drive growth and enhance customer experience.”

Legacy system integrators typically use a collection of in-house and/or external point tools to accomplish such migrations; this approach can be slow, error prone and fragile. The Infoworks-Tech Mahindra partnership delivers innovation to solve two major challenges enterprises face today – rapid migration to the Cloud and analytics agility.

Live Webinar
Next month, Infoworks, Databricks and Tech Mahindra will present a live webinar; please see registration page here: https://go.infoworks.io/webinar20200721 for additional information.
Title: “Migrate from Hadoop to Databricks: How and Why”
Date/Time: Tuesday, July 21 at 1 p.m. ET/10 a.m. PT
Presenters: Anand Venugopol, Global Director of Industry Solutions for Horizontals and Emerging Verticals, Databricks; Milind Maddi, Practice Head, Tech Mahindra; Sal Scalisi, Solution Architect, Infoworks

Topics:

  • The economics behind migration. From cost savings to accelerated time to value through rapid analytics use case development and application deployment.
  • The challenges and roadblocks common to Cloud migrations. Including migrating data from Hadoop to Delta Lake and migrating workloads from SQL or other languages to Spark.
  • Best practices to simplify the migration processes. From automated data ingestion, to data onboarding and operationalization, including a brief demo of Infoworks DataFoundry.
  • Case studies on data lake migration from Hadoop to Databricks.

Also, next week, Infoworks will staff a “virtual” booth at the Spark + AI Summit; event attendees are encouraged to learn more and may schedule 1-to-1 meetings here. Infoworks will also present“Make the Most of Your Talent and Time When Working on AI and ML Projects – Automate the Rest” on Wednesday, June 24 at 11 a.m. PDT.

DataFoundry for Databricks is supported on Microsoft Azure and Amazon Web Services (AWS) environments. Users are able to migrate data from any source system using highly automated, no-code tools. The EDO2 system eliminates the need for hand-coding data ingestion, data pipelines and workflows, while providing the built-in lineage, monitoring, auditability and governance required for enterprise data operations and orchestration.
