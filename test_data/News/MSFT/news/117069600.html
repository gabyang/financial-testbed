What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS: 

  • Rivian Automotive (RIVN) reported mixed Q3 results but maintained its production outlook [more]
  • Marqeta (MQ) reported upbeat Q3 revenue [more]
  • Six Flags (SIX) reported downbeat Q3 results but announced a pact to allow H Partners to boost its ownership [more]
  • RingCentral (RNG) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q3 [more]
  • Nio (NIO) reported better-than-expected Q3 revenue [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS: 

  • Activision Blizzard (ATVI) upgraded to Outperform from Market Perform at Raymond James [more]
  • JPMorgan downgrades Roblox (RBLX) on more modest sales growth outlook [more]
  • TJX (TJX) upgraded to Buy at Gordon Haskett ahead of expected off-price improvement [more]
  • Palo Alto Networks (PANW) upgraded to Buy at Loop Capital on accelerating enterprise momentum [more]
  • JPMorgan double downgraded CytomX Therapeutics (CTMX) to Underweight from Overweight [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB: 

  • Amazon (AMZN) has launched a cost cutting review, WSJ reports [more]
  • Amazon is the first public company to lose $1T in market value, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • AstraZeneca (AZN) dropped plans to submit its COVID-19 vaccine to the U.S. FDA, WSJ says [more]
  • Zoom (ZM) announces official integration into Tesla (TSLA) vehicles, Electrek reports [more]
  • Binance walked away from its deal to rescue FTX, WSJ reports [more]


﻿4. MOVERS: 

  • Repay Holdings (RPAY) rises after reporting quarterly results and backing its FY22 guidance [more]
  • Redfin (RDFN) higher after reporting quarterly results and providing Q4 guidance [more]
  • Magnite (MGNI), Digital Turbine (APPS) and TG Therapeutics (TGTX) gain after announcing quarterly results [more]
  • Egio (EGIO), Vacasa (VCSA), and Cano Health (CANO) fall after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Veru (VERU) declines after announcing an update on the FDA AdCom meeting on sabizabulin [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE: 

  • Yeti (YETI) reported upbeat Q3 results [more]
  • Beyond Meat (BYND) reported downbeat Q3 results and cut FY22 revenue guidance [more]
  • Viant (DSP) gave a Q4 outlook that missed expectations [more]
  • Ralph Lauren (RL) reported upbeat Q2 results and reiterated FY23 revenue guidance [more]
  • Digi International (DGII) reported better-than-expected Q4 results [more]


INDEXES: 

Near midday, the Dow was up 984.75, or 3.03%, to 33,498.69, the Nasdaq was up 643.67, or 6.22%, to 10,996.85, and the S&P 500 was up 176.12, or 4.70%, to 3,924.69.
