Capitolis Appoints Rahul Auradkar as Chief Product Officer

  • He will lead Capitolis’ product development efforts and is the latest addition to the firm's growing executive management team.

Gil Mandelzis-led technology firm Capitolis announced today it had appointed Rahul Auradkar as its chief product officer.

In his new role, Auradkar will lead Capitolis' product development efforts globally and is the latest addition to the company's growing executive management team.

Earlier in his career, Auradkar worked at Microsoft, where he supported the firm's virtualization efforts and led teams in product and engineering, driving transformative customer impact through the company's Azure, Office 365, and Windows Server businesses.

He also served as vice president of products at Apptio, an enterprise SaaS company pioneering technology business management, providing transparency and optimization to enterprises on their technology investments.

While working at Apptio, Auradkar and his team helped grow the business from a start-up to a publicly-traded company, with nearly 800 employees and more than $200 million in annual revenue. Most recently, Auradkar was chief product officer of AnsweriQ, a start-up driving customer service automation through machine learning and AI.
