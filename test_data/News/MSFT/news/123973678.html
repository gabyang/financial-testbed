OpenAI's $86 bln share sale in jeopardy following Altman firing- The Information

Published: 2023-11-18 14:04:40 ET MSFT

Nov 18 (Reuters) - A planned sale of OpenAI employee shares that would value the startup at about $86 billion on paper hangs in the balance after the sudden firing of CEO Sam Altman and a slew of top executive departures, the Information reported on Saturday.

The tender offer, which Thrive Capital is leading, has not yet closed but has been in its final stages and was expected to be completed as soon as next month, the report added, citing a person familiar with the matter. (Reporting by Gursimran Kaur in Bengaluru)
