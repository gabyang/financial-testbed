20 companies in Reading have been voted the best places to work by staff

Microsoft, Waitrose and Procter & Gamble are all in Glassdoor's annual Top 50

    Staff across Berkshire are working for some of the best companies in the country after 20 businesses were named in the top 50.

    Glassdoor has announced the winners of its 10th annual Employees Choice awards and nearly half of the companies included have staff in Reading.

    The list is based on feedback from anonymous employees who give information on their job, employer and work environment.

    The vast majority of the top 50 have offices in London, so it's highly likely they have employees living in Reading, Bracknelland Wokingham.

    Employers based in the town include Procter & Gamble, which runs Gillette in South Reading and Microsoft, which has its head office in Thames Valley Park.

    Waitrose, which has its head office in Bracknell and employs numerous staff in supermarkets across Berkshire, is also in the top 50.

    PricewaterhouseCoopers, which has offices in Forbury Square, also made the list for the fourth year in a row.

    Looking for a job in Reading? Search Fish4Jobs for the latest opportunities.

    Robert Hohman, Glassdoor CEO and co-founder, said: "We know today’s job seekers are more informed than ever about where they go to work, researching everything from company culture to career opportunities to pay philosophy and more.

    "To help people find companies that stand out from the pack, the Glassdoor Employees’ Choice Awards recognise employers that are truly Best Places to Work because they’re determined by those who really know best - the employees.

    "Employers where employees love to work continue to prove that they have a recruiting and business performance advantage.

    "We’re proud to celebrate the 2018 winners as we mark our 10th anniversary of the Glassdoor Employees’ Choice Awards."
