Sea of Thieves provides a myriad of pirating adventures across this open-world seafaring adventure. The most notable component with this game is the PVP factor and this guide will lay out some tips and tricks to assist players to remain triumphant in the open sea.

✕ Remove Ads

Developed by Rare, Sea of Thieveswas originally released on March 20, 2018, and there has been a healthy stream of updates since its launch. These updates have transformed this game into a more robust online experience. At the start of the game, there will be a selection of pre-made avatars that players can select and will be tasked into finding a galleon of their own and begin finding ways to build up their ship and status. Even though there are solo-type missions to play, the entire notion of Sea of Thieves centers around working as a crew and becoming the biggest and baddest band of merry pirates on this side of the Northern Hemisphere. Each crew will be constructed with individuals with their own designated positions such as; navigating with the compass, scouting on the crow's nest, and hoisting sails.

While crews are busy with their missions, they will occasionally encounter other ships that are in the process of plundering the same treasure that the player's galleon is. This situation can unfold in a number of ways and personal vendettas can easily be forged between alliances. Through classic swashbuckling feats, ships are damaged in real-time. Players will need to scatter about and patch up holes, repair sails, and even steal from each other's ships while defending their beloved vessel that they have grinded for days to build up.Plus, there are taverns to visit, shanties to sing, sea beasts to slay, and loot to find that are strewn all over the map. It's a race to the top for crews to achieve the 'Legendary Pirate' status that will enable players to customize their ship and crew with epic loot and even have access to a hidden pirate base located deep in the caverns.

PVP Tips & Tricks in Sea of Thieves

PVP can be a daunting situation for new players of Sea of Thieves.A ship is only a good as its crew. So, there are a few things to keep in mind before engaging other players and preparing the ship for the utmost strategic advantage. During skirmishes, things can be pretty nasty at a hasty pace. This guide will show the basics to help with new players on how to gain the advantage during standoffs.

Master Swordfighting

✕ Remove Ads
  • There is a slew of blades to find in Sea of Thieves. The most common (and versatile) is the cutlass. This sword can do rather hefty damage and it enables the player to be quite nimble during duels. It comes with a 3-hit combo, blocking, charge attack, and even a dodge. The 3-hit combo is especially deadly only if all three hits connect. If one swing misses, the player will become "tired" and vulnerable to attack. To avoid this, make sure all 3-hits connect with the; opponent or even surrounding objects. The situation is the same with the 'charge attack', as well. Be doubly sure that it'll connect with the opponent or the player will become 'tired' and wide open for damage. To dodge, just hold down the 'block button' and 'jump' to the side. This will activate a side-by-side hopping animation that'll help avoid swinging blades and projectiles. This also proves handy when covering small distances in a moment's notice.

Know The Firearms

✕ Remove Ads
  • Pirates are not exactly known to be connoisseurs with guns, however, one of the most devastating firearms is the blunderbuss. Any player that has handled a shotgun in any FPS will be instantly familiar with its handing and hitboxes. A nicely squared shot will down an opponent in close range, or at least shave off half their life. Another pistol to look out for is the flintlock. It has rather high damage stats and an impressive range. Combining these two firearms make quite the duo for blasting holes into other pirates. Usually, the best tactic is instead of waiting to reload after a shot, simply switch over to the other pistol and use it. Then, finish off the opponent with the cutlass. At the very least, discharge shots with each gun then retreat into safety to reload and ready for the next standoff. Try and carve a decent path that leads to ammo crates during battles. Don't stray too far away from them since these weapons only come with 5 shots and they can be used up pretty quick.

Be Familiar With Cannons and Gunpower Kegs

✕ Remove Ads
  • There are few other things that can wreak havoc on a ship than cannons. It can be tricky to line up a shot with them, especially when stuck in stormy waters and high-speed winds. A good tactic to use is positioning the Captain of the ship as the 'spotter'. This will help the crew manning the cannons on which angle is most effective and where they stand with distance. Watch how the cannonball drops after firing. This is a good visual clue on how they should be positioned on which angle. Be patient and try to use simple math to calculate how the cannons should be adjusted to reach their target. Thus, using gun powder kegs (that are collected at skeleton forts) to double the damage. Simply drop them into the water and have them float toward the hull of the enemy's ship. When close enough, blasting it with cannons will take a chunk off their ship. Thus, swimming with a gunpowder keg underwater, sneaking up underneath the opponent's ship, and just releasing it will cause it to explode kind of like a proximity mine.
✕ Remove Ads

Always Mind The Ship

  • The player's ship is the heart and soul with Sea of Thieves. It doesn't have a mind of its own and players will need to manually move it, even during combat. Having one sail up will slow it down, but it'll be more nimble on the waters. If maneuverability isn't needed but rather more propulsion, then hoist up both the sails. Also, doing a 180-turn is possible by having everyone on the crew do their part. If being pursued, drop the anchor down and have the steering wheel set in one position. Once the anchor contacts the ground and timed correctly, the ship will do a "sharp turn" and will throw the pursuers into a loop. When chasing a ship, try and steer the ship in a straight line and have all sails up to ensure maximum speed. Also, when it comes to the ship, always take the initiative and stock up on supplies. Stopping by an island and sorting out weapons and consumables will help properly prep the ship for combat.
