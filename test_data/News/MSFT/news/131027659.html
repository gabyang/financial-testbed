What You Missed On Wall Street This Past Friday

By Staff of The Fly
Sunday, November 3, 2024 6:00 AM EST

Image Source: The Fly


1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Apple (AAPL) reported upbeat Q4 results, with CEO Tim Cook saying Apple Intelligence "sets a new standard for privacy"
  • Amazon (AMZN) reported better-than-expected Q3 results and provided revenue guidance for Q4
  • Intel (INTC) reported mixed Q3 resultsand provided its outlook for Q4
  • Exxon Mobil (XOM) reported mixed Q3 results while Chevron (CVX) beat estimates on the top and bottom line for the quarter
  • Boeing's (BA) machinist union has endorsed the latest offer, with a vote coming Monday


2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • PayPal (PYPL) downgradedto Accumulate from Buy at Phillip Securities
  • Estee Lauder (EL) downgradedat JPMorgan, here's why
  • Atlassian (TEAM) upgradedto Overweight at KeyBanc
  • Scotiabank upgradedComcast (CMCSA) to Outperform
  • TD Cowen downgradedHII (HII) to Hold


3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Microsoft (MSFT) will spend $10B from 2023 to decade-end with CoreWeave, The Information reports
  • Disney (DIS) has created a new unit to coordinate its use of AI and mixed reality, Reuters says
  • Elevance Health (ELV) sued U.S. health officials, saying Medicare was "arbitrary and capricious" in how it calculated its star ratings, Bloomberg reports
  • HSBC (HBSC), Barclays (BCS), and Standard Chartered (SCBFF) are looking to tap into a boom in U.S. commercial banking,Reuters reports
  • A jury determined Abbott (ABT) and Reckitt Benckiser's (RBGLY) unit Mead Johnson are not responsible for a young boy's debilitating intestinal disease, Reuters reports


4. MOVERS:

  • Adma Biologics (ADMA) increases after reaching a deal with KPMG to serve as its auditor
  • ChromaDex (CDXC) and Vir Biotechnology (VIR) gains in New York afterreporting quarterly results
  • ASP Isotopes (ASPI) higher after announcing a 2.4M share offering
  • Lexicon Pharmaceuticals (LXRX) lower after announcing the U.S FDA Advisory Committee voted 11 to 3 that the benefits of Zynquista do not outweigh the risks in adults with type 1 diabetes and chronic kidney disease
  • FuboTV (FUBO) and DigitalBridge (DBRG) fall after reporting quarterly results


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Wayfair (W) reported Q3 results, with CEO Niraj Shah commenting, "Q3 marked another proofpoint of resilience for Wayfair with further market share capture in the face of sustained challenges in the category"
  • Dominion (D) reported Q3 results and narrowed its guidance for FY24
  • Charter Industries (GTLS) reported Q3 results, with EPS and revenue missing consensus
  • Alliant Energy (LNT) reported Q3 results, with CEO Lisa Barton commenting, "We continue to deliver solid financial and operational results while executing our customer-focused strategy"
  • Magna (MGA) reported Q3 results and lowered its guidance for FY24


INDEXES:

The Dow rose 288.73, or 0.69%, to 42,052.19, the Nasdaq gained 144.77, or 0.80%, to 18,239.92, and the S&P 500 advanced 23.35, or 0.41%, to 5,728.80.
