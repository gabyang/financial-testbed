What You Missed This Week in Video Games
Fly Intel
Jessica de Sa-Mota - email
Barclays sees attractive point to accumulate Take-Two shares, Respawn Entertainment and EA unveiled "Star Wars Jedi: Survivor"

"Game On" is The Fly's weekly recap of the stories powering up or beating down video game stocks. 

ATTRACTIVE ENTRY POINT:On Friday, Barclays analyst Mario Lu lowered the firm's price target on Take-Two Interactive Software (TTWO) to $171 from $185 but kept an Overweight rating on the shares. The analyst believes much of the weakness in the shares was attributed to the pending Zynga (ZNGA) merger and lower than expected projections. Now that is deal is closed, he sees an attractive entry point to accumulate Take-Two shares. Lu expects near-term upside via new content from GTA Online and Star Wars: Hunters from Zynga.


STAR WARS:At the Star Wars Celebration, Respawn Entertainment, Electronic Arts (EA), and Lucasfilm Games unveiled Star Wars Jedi: Survivor, the next chapter of the Star Wars action-adventure series chronicling the journey of Jedi Cal Kestis. Star WarsJedi: Survivor will expand upon the iconic Star Wars stories, worlds, characters, and combat first experienced in the series’ debut title, Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order. Star Wars Jedi: Survivor is being built for the current generation of gaming hardware to create a deeper and more expansive Star Wars experience for players worldwide when the game launches in 2023.


Star Wars Jedi: Survivor will be available in 2023 and is being developed for Microsoft’s (MSFT) Xbox Series X|S, Sony’s (SONY) PlayStation 5 and PC.
