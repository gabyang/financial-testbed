As we come into a new generation of gaming, Microsoft is celebrating its best console launch ever with its latest gaming platforms, the Xbox Series X/S. Microsoft pulled out all the stops on Tuesday, as it released its much-anticipated gaming consoles to the world. With Covid 19 forcing swathes of the population to stay at home much more in 2020, the popularity of videogames has gone through the roof. Games have offered people the chance to connect and engage with each other even when remaining physically apart. As such, Microsoft welcomed the new generation of gaming in with style and panache. From spectacular light shows that celebrated the Xbox’s gaming history to giveaways and art installations, the launch day was one of great anticipation for the gaming community.

The launch of the Xbox Series X/S was slightly marred by the fact that – with Halo Infinite being delayed until no one knows when – there were not many new launch titles to explore on the consoles’ release. This is an area that Sony has triumphed over Microsoft in. Its PlayStation 5 launches today, with Spider-Man: Miles Morales and Demon’s Souls both being released alongside it, and additional exclusives coming out further down the line. Regardless, sales for the Xbox Series X/S were still incredibly popular among gamers.

Xbox head Phil Spencer has taken to Twitter to express his gratitude to fans for what he has called the “largest launch in Xbox history,” announcing that Microsoft sold more consoles in 24 hours, and in more countries, than ever before. He does go on to address the big elephant in the room – when would more stock be available. Phil Spencer assured his followers that Microsoft is working alongside retail to “resupply as quickly as possible”. He then goes on to affirm that the power of the connectivity gamers experience through their play is “more important than ever”, no doubt alluding to the ongoing pandemic.

This is great, and encouraging for those still wishing to get their hands on the latest Xbox gaming platform, but that does not mean to say that the launch of the Xbox Series X/S was hitch-free. Xbox Live experienced substantial crashes yesterday as new owners were eager to try out their purchases quickly, putting a huge strain on the server. Then there were videos that began circulating the internet of Xbox Series X consoles billowing “Smoke” through the top as though they had caught alight. These videos have widely been condemned as fakes by many, with the cause of the smoking effect being linked to vapers hidden out of camera shot – something that Microsoft has addressed.

✕ Remove Ads

The launch of the Xbox Series X/S is at the start of a special time for the realm of video games, as the industry as a whole moves forward with a more real-world feeling to its games. As time goes on and more Xbox releases come to fruition the Xbox Series X/S will get its time to shine and show off what it is fully capable of.
