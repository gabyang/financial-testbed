Four Great Xbox Game Pass Games Are Leaving In January

Every month, Microsoft adds new games to its subscription service, Xbox Game Pass.

Often, older games are removed at the same time. Unlike Games With Gold, you don’t get to keep games once they’re removed from Xbox Game Pass. You have to purchase them if you want to keep them for good.

On January 15th, Microsoft is removing four games from Xbox Game Pass. One of them happens to be one of my all-time favorite indies: FTL: Faster Than Light.

FTLputs you in command of your very own spaceship and sends you out into a harsh galaxy filled with pirates, a pursuing fleet of enemy ships, exploding suns and more. It’s a “spaceship simulation rougelike” where you’ll have to manage your resources (such as fuel, power and so forth) and your crew and try to get to the end of the line without dying. Good luck with that.

FTLis included with Xbox Game Pass for PC, but if you do decide you’d like to buy it, it’s currently less than $3 during the Steam Winter Sale.

The other three games leaving Game Pass in January are:

  • My Friend Pedro (Console, PC)
  • Sword Art Online: Fatal Bullet (Console)
  • Tekken 7 (Console)

My Friend Pedrois stylish 2D shooter that combines a healthy dose of ultraviolence with seriously cool bullet-time combat and—just like FTL—a terrific soundtrack. You can do all sorts of cool tricks using “focus” which slows down time and allows you to dodge bullets, do flips in the air, and target multiple enemies at once. Like John Wick,it’s a gunfight ballet. Check it out before it’s gone from Game Pass.

I haven’t played Sword Art Onlinebut it gets pretty positive user ratings on Steam and seems to be a pretty fun JRPG looter-shooter. Tekken 7is, as you likely already know, a fighting game in the long-running Japanese series.

All four of these games leave Xbox Game Pass in less than two weeks. Microsoft should announce this month’s first wave of new Game Pass games shortly, so stay tuned.
