Well, the time is finally here. After a few cryptic, cinematic trailers, it seems as if we are finally going to get a look at the next generation of console games starting with the Xbox Series X. Microsoft is doing a new thing all summer longcalled “Xbox 20/20” where they have different shows focused on different aspects of their Xbox plans for release this fall. They have reiterated that both the Xbox Series X and Halo Infinite are slated to be released this holiday, despite the pandemic disruptions around the world.

With E3 cancelled, to kick off this process we have an Xbox 20/20 games reveal stream focused on “our global developer partners,” which to me, implies this will mostly be a third party games showcase, and that we might have to wait for a separate Halo Infinite deep dive at some other points.

When’s the show? Where can you watch it?

The Xbox Series X 20/20 games showcase is Thursday, May 7, 2020.

It will air bright and early at8 AM PT, 11 AM ET.

You can watch it at:

  • Mixer.com/Xbox
  • Twitch.tv/Xbox
  • Facebook.com/Xbox
  • Twitter.com/Xbox
  • YouTube.com/Xbox, and this one will have the 4K stream.

It is not exactly clear which “global partners” will debut footage at the show. It stands to reason that Ubisoft could choose to debut some Assassin’s Creed Valhalla footage, given that it’s just debuted and that it’s definitely confirmed as a next-gen game. Maybe Cyberpunk, given that we know CDPR is an MS partner.

We know that EA will no doubt have a variety of sports games out this fall, and a new Battlefield. Activision will have a new Call of Duty, reportedly another Black Ops. Bungie will have a Destiny 2 expansion, though given that’s being made in an old engine, may not be the best place to debut footage. And I suppose we could see more from Hellblade 2 or Halo Infinite if Microsoft does decide to debut some first party footage. I do not believe the dream fan wishes about a sneak preview of Grand Theft Auto 6 or Elder Scrolls 6, as I think it’s still way too early for either.

We’ll see what happens, and I would expect the show to run for at least an hour. There is not a dedicated 20/20 Xbox show in June, but there will be “news” of some sort, which may end up being the price of the unit, given that E3 is normally the time when that would have been revealed, and neither Microsoft nor Sony has shared that yet. And yes, this technically is a preview for a lot of upcoming PS4 games too, given that almost all third party titles will be released for both next-gen consoles.

See you at the stream.

Update:

Microsoft has just posted this tweet, saying that we will be seeing this “optimized for Series X” badge a lot during today’s stream.

The way I see it this could be mean two different things, or both of them. Given that this is mostly a third party show, I have a hunch that we are going to see a lot of existing games given this badge. As in, current titles like Doom Eternal or Borderlands 3 or Apex Legends might get this “optimized” tag, showing that they have been enhanced either graphics-wise or performance-wise in some way for the Series X.

I also think this could apply to new titles as well. As in, we could see Assassin’s Creed Valhalla or Cyberpunk 2077 featured, and those might have a special way they’re optimized for maximum performance in Series X that goes above and beyond what we might see from them on PS5, as all are cross-platform titles. Though without anything to contrast with via Sony, it’s hard to tell how meaningful the Series X-specific stuff might be.

My ultimate guess is a mix of both these options, and I would expect to see mostly existing or announced titles today, but perhaps with a couple new ones.
