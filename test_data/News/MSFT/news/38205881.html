The Xbox Series X now has an official launch window, fresh on the heels of Halo Infinite’srecent delay. The highly anticipated next-gen Xbox console was revealed last December, but until now players have only had a vague holiday 2020 release date to look forward to.

✕ Remove Ads

One of the many new games coming to the Series X is Halo Infinite, which was recently shown off during the Xbox Games Showcase a few weeks ago. Reception to the nearly 8-hour long Halo Infinitegameplay trailer was mixed, with fans decrying the game’s relatively lackluster graphics and lack of departure from previous entries in the franchise, although Xbox did gain a new mascot of sorts out of the social media response in the form of Infinite’s Craig the Brute. In light of this, Microsoft recently announced that the game will be delayed until next year to both address fan criticisms and counter production issues brought on by the recent COVID-19 outbreak that is still causing havoc in the entertainment industry. On the plus side, it has also given players a more specific indication of when the newest Xbox console is coming out.

Related: How Xbox One Launch Titles Compare To Xbox One X's Final Games

✕ Remove Ads

Earlier today, Xbox announced that in addition to Halo Infinite’s release date being moved back to some yet-unspecified time in 2021, the Xbox Series X will be arriving on store shelves sometime in November. Furthermore, up to 100 games will be optimized for the new system, including 50 new titles like Assassin’s Creed Valhalla, Yakuza: Like a Dragon, and Watch Dogs: Legion along with the shared library of games available on the Xbox Game Pass. As previously reported, all new Xbox games this year will feature Smart Delivery, which will allow players to access them on both Xbox One and Xbox Series X after only one purchase.

✕ Remove Ads

Although Microsoft initially had the upper hand on Sony by unveiling the Xbox Series X far ahead of the PlayStation 5 and putting a strong emphasis on the Series X's backward compatibility with games from virtually every Xbox console that came before it, they suffered a major setback after the PS5 was shown in full during a monstrously successful livestream event back in June. Since then, Xbox has been on the defensive, with the company recently deciding to abandon its previous monthly Xbox 20/20 streams in favor of more specific announcements going forward. With this new development, it seems like Microsoft is looking to get ahead of Sony once more.

✕ Remove Ads

While some fans might be disappointed in Halo Infinite’slengthy delay, a more definitive time window for the release of the Xbox Series X is certainly exciting news for those who are counting the days until they can jump into Microsoft’s newest console. There still isn’t a specific day for the Series X’s release, but the smart money would suggest that Microsoft will have the system out in time for Black Friday, which is a major shopping day in the US. In the meantime, fans should stay posted for further developments as the Xbox Series X nears its November launch window.
