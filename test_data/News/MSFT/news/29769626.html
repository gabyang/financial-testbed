LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Technavio has been monitoring the audio and video editing software market and it is poised to grow by USD 2.5 billion during 2020-2024, progressing at a CAGR of over 11% during the forecast period. The report offers an up-to-date analysis regarding the current market scenario, latest trends and drivers, and the overall market environment.

Technavio suggests three forecast scenarios (optimistic, probable, and pessimistic) considering the impact of COVID-19. Please request Latest Free Sample Report on Covid-19 Impact

The market is concentrated, and the degree of concentration will accelerate during the forecast period. Acon AS, Adobe Inc., Animoto Inc., Apple Inc., Autodesk Inc., Avid Technology Inc., CyberLink Corp., Microsoft Corp., Sony Corp., and Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH are some of the major market participants. Although the increase in the rate of content generation will offer immense growth opportunities, the rising use of open-source and free editing software will challenge the growth of the market participants. To make the most of the opportunities, market vendors should focus more on the growth prospects in the fast-growing segments, while maintaining their positions in the slow-growing segments.

The increase in the rate of content generation has been instrumental in driving the growth of the market. However, the rising use of open-source and free editing software might hamper market growth.
