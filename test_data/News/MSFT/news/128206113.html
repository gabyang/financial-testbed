Oracle To Provide Additional Capacity For OpenAI on Microsoft Azure AI Platform

Published: 2024-06-11 16:33:18 ET MSFT

By Ben Glickman

Oracle said its cloud services have been selected to extend the Microsoft Azure AI platform, providing additional capacity for OpenAI.

The company said in a statement that Oracle's cloud infrastructure would allow OpenAI to continue to scale.

Oracle also announced a deal Tuesday to provide cloud services in partnership with Google.
