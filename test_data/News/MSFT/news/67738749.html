Top-Line Growth Versus Margin Expansion. Is Salesforce Stock A Buy?

Licensing
  • REINHARDT KRAUSE
  • 08:20 AM ET 10/28/2024

The "new" Salesforce (CRM) has focused on improving profit margins for CRM stock. But to drive Salesforce stock higher, revenue growth from generative artificial intelligence products most likely needs to kick in.

In 2023, Salesforce refocused on improving operating margins amid cost-cutting spurred by activist investors. StarboardValue in October hosted an investor summit, noted TD Cowen analyst Derrick Wood in a report. "Starboard continued to make the case for CRM as an attractive investment and highlighted room for additional margin expansion," Wood said.

CRM Stock: Tuck-In Acquisitions Back

What's more, Salesforce is once again making acquisitions, albeit smaller ones. In its biggest deal since Slack, Salesforce in September acquired Own, a data protection and data management vendor, for $1.9 billion in cash. Further, Salesforce recently bought Zoomin, an unstructured data management company with existing ties with Salesforce.

Last year, a Salesforce reportedly was in talks with Informatica (INFA) but a deal did not materialize.

Meanwhile, Salesforce stock has gained about 10% in 2024. TheiShares Expanded Tech-Software Sector ETF (IGV), an industry index that includes many big-cap software companies, is up 13.6% in 2024. Also, Salesforce is one of many AI stocks to watch.

Third quarter earnings for Salesforce stock are due in late November. Salesforce reported second quarter earnings, revenue and operating margin thattopped consensus estimates. Revenue guidance came in below expectations.

At its DreamForce annual user conference, Salesforce touted AgentForce, its new autonomous AI agent for business customers. More software companies are pivoting to AI agents.

Meanwhile, Salesforce andWorkday (WDAY) recently  joined forces through the formation of a strategic partnership designed to "deliver a personalized, AI-powered assistant for employee service use cases such as onboarding, health benefits, and career development within Salesforce and Workday."

CRM Stock: AI Monetization Key

Further, Salesforce sells software under a subscription model. Its software helps businesses organize and handle sales operations and customer relationships. The company has expanded into marketing, e-commerce and data analytics. It's making a big push into the life sciences market.

Still,Microsoft's (MSFT) Dynamics software has gained traction as a lower-priced alternative to Salesforce tools, some analysts say

Artificial intelligence is a battleground. While Microsoft is aligned with startup OpenAI, Salesforce is working with several developers of large language models.

Meanwhile, here's a look at the enterprise AI market.

The customers of software-as-a-service, or SaaS, companies like Salesforce purchase renewable subscriptions, rather than on-premise software licenses. Also, customers receive automatic software updates via the web.

Salesforce Stock: Acquisition Spree

Further, a Salesforce acquisition spree that began in 2013 complicated its margin outlook for over a decade.

In 2018, Salesforce bought MuleSoft for $6.5 billion in cash and stock. MuleSoft's software automates the integration of new tools with legacy enterprise platforms and speeds application development.

Meanwhile, Salesforce in 2019 agreed to buy data analytics firm Tableau for $15.7 billion in an all-stock deal. Tableau provides data visualization software. In addition, it enables customers to build databases, graphs and maps using time series analytics, a technique that analyzes a series of data points ordered in time.

Then came the Slack Technologies deal. Amid growing competition with Microsoft, Salesforce agreed to pay $27.7 billion for the workplace collaboration software maker.

Is CRM Stock A Buy?

What's more, Salesforce stock hit an all-time high of 311.75 on Nov. 9, 2021. Then CRM stock retreated nearly 48% in 2022.

Amid buzz over artificial intelligence, CRM stock gained nearly 98% in 2023. That beat the Nasdaq index's 43% gain.

According to IBD Stock Checkup, CRM stock currently has a Relative Strength Rating of 75 out of a best-possible 99. The best stocks tend to have ratings of 80 and above.

Meanwhile, CRM stock holds an IBD Composite Rating of 95 out of a best possible 99.

IBD's Composite Rating combines five separate proprietary ratings into one easy-to-use rating. Also, the best growth stocks have a Composite Rating of 90 or better.

In addition, Salesforce stock has an Accumulation/Distribution Rating of B-plus. The rating analyzes price and volume changes in a stock over the past 13 weeks of trading. The rating, on an A+ to E scale, measures institutional buying and selling in a stock. A+ signifies heavy institutional buying; E means heavy selling. Think of the C grade as neutral.

As of Oct. 28, Salesforce stock needs to build a fresh base to be actionable.
