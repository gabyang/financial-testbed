AI summit secures safety commitments from 16 companies, statement says

Published: 2024-05-21 05:00:00 ET MSFT

SEOUL, May 21 (Reuters) - AI companies including Alphabet's Google, Meta, Microsoft and OpenAI as well as from China, South Korea and the United Arab Emirates committed to safe AI development on Tuesday, according to a statement from the UK government.

The announcement came as South Korea and Britain host a global AI summit in Seoul starting Tuesday.
