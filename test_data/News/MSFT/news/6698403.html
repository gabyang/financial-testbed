    When it comes to looking for a new job there are many things that need to be taken into consideration - the salary being among the most important

    Location and what the job role requires is obviously also very important - there is no point in applying for a job if it involves too much travelling or you do not have the right skill set.

    For those wanting a job which offers a salary of more than £25k, there are some cracking roles available in Burton.

    From helping people who have suffered abuse to offering support in sales, jobs are on offer for different skills and abilities.

    We have taken a look at the jobs currently on offer in Burton and beyond to help take some of the stress out of looking.

    All the jobs are available by visiting https://www.indeed.co.uk

    This is what we have found:

    FGM Project Worker for Barnardo's

    Location: Staffordshire County Council, in Grange Street, Burton

    Salary: £27,925 a year

    This is a new service which is to be delivered by Barnardo's on behalf of the police and crime commissioner for Staffordshire.

    The commissioner has received funding through the Home Office Violence against Women and Girls Fund to develop an effective, consistent, co-ordinated and culturally sensitive Female Genital Mutilation (FGM) service.

    Bosses are looking for someone who is dynamic, who wants to broaden their experience, someone interested in making a difference to the lives of children and families.

    They will be able to work independently, in a multi-agency environment, and be culturally competent and confident in their practice.

    Ideally the employer wants someone with experience of working with children and young people and knowledge of local authorities' responsibility to safeguard children.

    Sales support and project administrator for Harvey Beric Associates

    Salary: £25,000 a year

    Harvey Beric Associations is recruiting someone to join its client on the outskirts of Burton.

    This a new role within the company and bosses said it is an opportunity to join a "thriving and expanding business".

    The main responsibilities will include entering orders onto the system, project manage through the production process, chase artwork and confirm technical details, buying bespoke items, booking of travel, accommodation and services and supporting the field sales team.

    A spokesman added that is imperative the successful candidate has their own transport, excellent administration and organisation skills along with the ability to understand technical drawings.

    More information about what the client company does is available by contacting recruitment consultant Harvey Beric Associates.

    Head of department

    Location: The Linnet Independent Learning Centre, in Ashby

    Salary: £28,000 - £33,000 a year

    The Linnet Independent Learning Centre is looking to recruit a head of department for a soon-to-be-opened small, therapeutic, special school.

    The school will replicate the existing practices already set up at the Linnet Independent Learning Centre, based in Castle Gresley.

    However, the new school will be based in Ashby.

    Applicants will require management and leadership experience.

    Pupils at The Linnet have special educational needs associated with social, emotional and mental health difficulties.

    This does result in some challenging behaviours and all applicants must be willing and able to interact with pupils who can sometimes be verbally and physically aggressive.

    All teaching staff teach across the primary curriculum are supported by behaviour-support staff.

    The successful candidate will be responsible for overseeing the quality of the newly-set-up provision, managing the staff within the setting,chairing meetings and workingwith senior managers.

    Category buyer at Ceva Logistics

    Salary: £35,000 a year

    Also based in Ashby, bosses are looking for a highly-motivated and results-driven category buyer.

    Within this role new recruits will support category managers with "supplier and category strategies", ensuring that plans are delivered in line with the budget.

    To be successful in this role, you have "proven indirect category buyer experience" and enjoy working within a fast-paced and multi-tasking environment.

    You will need to have excellent time-management and organisational skills in order to manage a variety of tasks, as well as holding strong analytical skills and problem-solving methodologies.

    Planner for Moran Logistics

    Salary: £27,500 a year

    Based in Castle Donington, Moran Logistics is a growing business within the chilled distribution arena. The firm works with customers to provide tailor-made, cost-effective distribution solutions.

    The role will see the new recruit report directly to the transport planning manager to ensure all collections and deliveries are planned for the customer, ensuring customer requirements are met and all additional work is built into the required routes and plans.

    Those who apply must have previous transport and logistics experience and able to use Microsoft packages including Excel, Word and Outlook proficiently.
