Tech Advances on AI Optimism - Tech Roundup

Published: 2024-10-02 17:16:43 ET MSFT

Shares of technology companies rose amid renewed optimism about artificial intelligence.

Business software giant Oracle plans to invest more than $6.5 billion to establish a public cloud region in Malaysia, aiming to meet the country's growing demand for artificial intelligence and cloud services.

ChatGPT developer OpenAI has raised $6.6 billion in new funding, capping a complex fundraising process that involved negotiations with multiple tech giants and overseas investors as well as renewed scrutiny of the company's internal turmoil.
