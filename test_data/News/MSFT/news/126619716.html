Microsoft Agreed to Pay Inflection AI $650 Million While Hiring Startup's Staff, Reuters Reports

Published: 2024-03-21 14:32:41 ET MSFT

--Microsoft agreed to pay startup Inflection AI $650 million while hiring its staff, Reuters reports, citing a person familiar with the matter.

--The transaction between Microsoft and Inflection AI is mostly a licensing deal that would make the startup's models available for sale on the Azure cloud service, the person said, according to the report.

--Microsoft declined to comment and Inflection AI didn't immediately respond to a request for comment, Reuters reports.

--The news about the transaction was reported earlier Thursday by the Information, according to the report.
