Xbox Game Pass is one of the greatest video game subscription services ever devised by Microsoft. For a monthly fee of $16 or $1 for 14 days, if you’re a first-time subscriber, you can download and play hundreds of the best Xbox games on Xbox home consoles, Windows PC, and Xbox Cloud Gaming.

On top of that, subscribing to Xbox Game Pass will give you access to Xbox Perks. Xbox Perks can enhance your experience with Xbox systems with a huge variety of rewards such as in-game cosmetic items, free access to DLC, and even temporary access to the premium editions of third-party apps like Discord and Spotify.

With such a gigantic library of perks and games on offer, it can be quite daunting to browse through Xbox Game Pass to see which games are right for you. Well, no need to fret as we at Windows Central have organized and cataloged every Xbox title and Xbox Perk now available on Xbox Game Pass in one comprehensive, easy-to-use list.

With this list, you will have a much easier time picking out the best Xbox games and Xbox Perks and reap the benefits of your Xbox Game Pass subscription to its fullest. However, it would be best if you were made aware thatsome Xbox games and Xbox Perks are region-locked and may be unavailable to access in your home region.

Xbox games being added to Xbox Game Pass

These are the Xbox Game Pass games coming soon to consoles and PC over the coming days. Once their scheduled release dates have passed, new upcoming titles announced for Xbox Game Pass shall replace them.

Metal Slug Tactics (Console, PC and Cloud) – November 5

The iconic Metal Slug franchise is delving into world of turn-based strategy RPGs with a new explosive spin-off called Metal Slug Tactics. Arm up Peregrine Falcon Squad for combat and take on the Rebel Army in exciting tactical battles with a roguelike twist that will keep you coming back more.

Go Mecha Ball (Console) – November 6

Go Mecha Ball is a crazy twin-stick shooter with roguelike elements where the goal is to blow up wave after wave of killer robots. Master this game’s pinball-esque mechanics to traverse levels at the speed of sound, arm yourself to teeth with a metric ton of devastating weapons and upgrades, and take down gigantic bosses with your hulking-yet-agile warmech.

Harold Halibut (Xbox Series X|S) – November 6

Harold Halibut is a handcrafted narrative-adventure game where you play as Harold, a young lab assistant who lives aboard a spaceship that sank to the bottom of an alien world’s ocean. Join Harold on his adventures as he interacts with his crewmates and explores this strange alien world for a way to help the ship fly again so Harold’s crew can find a new place to call home.

The Rewinder (Console) – November 6

The Rewinder is a side-scrolling, puzzle-adventure game where you play as Yun the Rewinder. As a Rewinder, you are gifted with the supernatural ability to enter a person’s mind to obverse their memories, influence people’s actions, and even alter history to some degree.

With your Rewinder powers, you must travel to a remote village to help the Spirit Wardens of a cleanse their home of angry spirits and find out what caused this once-peaceful to be haunted with ghosts.

Turnip Boy Robs a Bank (Console) – November 6

Turnip Boy Robs a Bank is a cutesy action-shooter where you play as Turnip Boy, a troublemaking vegetable that has teamed up with the Pickled Gang to pull off one of the wackiest bank robberies in history.

Use your thievery tools to sneak past security, interrogate hostages for information, and battle cops as you make your way to Botanical Bank’s vault and escape with enough riches to set you up for life.

Goat Simulator Remastered (Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Cloud) – November 7

Goat Simulator Remastered is a remastered edition of one of the strangest sandbox games ever conceived – Goat Simulator. You play as a goat who exists for only one purpose: causing chaos. Accomplish your dreams of mindless mass mayhem by destroying public property, ram people so hard they go flying into space, and engage in all kinds of wacky and deliciously nonsensical quests that will turn the world upside down and inside out.

Spyro Reignited Trilogy (Console, PC, and Cloud) – November 12

The remastered remakes of the classic Spyro the Dragon trilogy has taken flight to Xbox Game Pass. Join the titular dragon and his best friend Sparx the Dragonfly as they charge, glide, and flame breath their way through magical worlds, and defend them from dastardly villains like Gnasty Gnorc, The Sorceress, and Ripton.

Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 (Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Cloud) – November 19

Xbox Game Studios’ Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 is coming to Game Pass on day one! Experience the challenges and immersive realism of piloting a large variety of aircraft with new dynamically generated missions, competitive multiplayer, an advanced physics system, and Microsoft Flight Simulator’s most meticulously detailed digital twin of planet Earth to date.

S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2: Heart of Chornobyl (Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Cloud) – November 20

Stalker 2: Heart of Chornobyl, the long-awaited sequel to the cult-classic FPS, Stalker: Shadow of Chernobyl, is coming to Xbox Game Pass, Day One. Take on the role of a lone Stalker and journey into the heart of a post-apocalyptic Chornobyl to loot ancient artifacts and uncover world-shattering secrets that could determine the fate of humanity.

Little Kitty, Big City (Console) – November 20

Little Kitty, Big City is a cutesy open-world sandbox game by Double Digger Studio. You play as an adorable, trouble-making kitty on a quest to find a way back home after getting separated from your human owners.

To reunite with your family, you must explore the city for clues, befriend the local stray animals for help, and cause a little mischief along the way.

PlateUp! (Console) – November 20

PlateUp! is an action-packed, rogue-like cooking management game developed by It's Happening and published by Yogscast Games. As the head chef of the local restaurant, you must command your fellow cooks to help you quickly prepare and serve a wide variety of dishes to a never-ending supply of customers while upgrade your abilities to keep up with the demand.

You can take this game’s culinary challenges solo or with your friends in 4-player co-op.

Nine Sols (Xbox Series X|S, PC, and Cloud) – November 26

Nine Sols is a side-scrolling, Souls-like, Metroidvania platformer developed and published by RedCandleGames. Set in a fantasy world inspired by Asian mythology, you play as Yi, an ancient hero on a quest to slay a race of alien gods known as the 9 Sols for bringing ruin to his realm.

To achieve your revenge, you must travel this decaying land for clues as to where the 9 Sols are hiding and master an intricate combat system inspired by FromSoftware’s critically-acclaimed title, Sekiro, to defeat your enemies.

Aliens: Dark Descent (Console, PC, and Cloud) – November 27

Aliens: Dark Descent is a real-time strategy game based on the iconic horror movie franchise, Alien. In this game, you oversee a squad Colonials Marines tasked with the dangerous missions of venturing to the moon of Lethe and cleanse it of a Xenomorph outbreak.

Your goal will not be easy as you will to need to plans in the heat of combat to overcome of Xenomorphs plaguing the moon while dealing with rogue operatives sent by the antagonistic Wey-land-Yutani Corporation and other terrifying monsters never before seen in the Alien franchise.

Xbox Game Pass (xCloud) list for iOS, Android, and PC

Xbox Game Pass cloud games (also known as xCloud) are available only with Xbox Game Pass Ultimate, which includes Xbox Game Pass Core, Console, and PC in one $15 per month package. This is the most cost-effective subscription for users who are very embedded in the Microsoft gaming ecosystem.

You can access Xbox Game Pass cloud games via any modern web browser via Xbox.com/play, or using the Xbox Game Pass app on Windows 10, Windows 11, Google Play or the Galaxy Store on Android. A lot of the games here also support touch controls, and the save files will sync to and from your Xbox console (and your PC as well if the game supports Xbox Play Anywhere, although you can just play the cloud version on your PC as well). All xCloud versions are in fact the Xbox console version, as the cloud servers are based on Xbox console architecture.

Discord Nitro (free three months of Nitro) — Claim by January 30, 2025, and redeem by January 31, 2025

If you're looking for a voice and text messaging app that synergizes with Xbox gaming, Discord is one of the best options out there. There is a free version of Discord and a premium version called Discord Nitro, which Xbox Game Pass is currently offering a three-month trial for with this Discord Nitro Xbox Perk.

Discord Nitro features an array of enhanced compared to the free version of Discord such as an upload capacity of 100MB, two server boosts per month, the ability to upload and stream HD videos, more emojis to use in chat, and more.

Bear in mind that this Xbox Perk is only valid for first-time users of Discord Nitro or people who haven't subscribed to Nitro within the past twelve months. Valid payment forms are required but you will not be charged until the free trial period is over. You can also cancel the subscription at any time before the trial ends without any cost (however Nitro and Xbox subscriptions must be canceled separately).

Once the free trial is over, your Discord Nitro account will automatically start charging you a monthly subscription fee of $9.99. The Xbox Perk for Discord Nitro is available for Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, and Windows PC and can only be claimed once per account.
