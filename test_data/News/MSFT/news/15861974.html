What employees say: Top Workplaces 2020

  • Published: Jun. 28, 2020, 6:00 a.m.
By
  • Yadi Rodriguez, cleveland.com

CLEVELAND, Ohio -- What do employees say about their bosses, and their workplaces?

For the2020 Top Workplaces list,cleveland.comand the Plain Dealer have compiled a record 175 Northeast Ohio employers, based on employee surveys. And we’ve focused on their responses and adaptations to the coronavirus pandemic.

We picked some of the best responses from employee surveys, to feature. Here’s what they said:

“I love my job because…”

LARGE Company

“I come into an environment every day where I laugh, have fun with some of my closest friends, but also work hard to help our clients. At Quicken we change lives and help Americans better their financial situation. There is nothing more gratifying than a client thanking you for saving their home or helping them get back on their feet.” – Quicken Loans employee

“I get to help students and community members understand their career passions and dreams and then help them to make that dream a reality.” – Quicken Loans employee

“I get to do new things every day. I am treated with respect from everyone I work with. I work with successful people and it motivates me to want to be the best I can be. I am given growth opportunities and management takes notice to my performance.” – Union Home Mortgage Corp. employee

“I feel a part of something that does good for our world.” – Union Home Mortgage Corp. employee

“The work/life balance is excellent. Family life is just as important as work life and recognizing and respecting that balance makes it easier to come in to work and give it my all.” – Charles Schwab & Co., Inc. employee

“It gives me the ability to tap my strengths but also work on improving areas of weakness.” – Charles Schwab & Co., Inc. employee

“I love that I can bring my full self to work. I am a very loving and talkative person and rather than toning myself down I am encouraged to do what I can to use those characteristics to be successful in my role.” – Progressive Insurance employee

“My concerns and ideas are always taken into consideration. I never feel like I am just a number to Famous.” – Famous Enterprise employee

“It uses every part of my brain. I feel we are genuinely trying our best to be great partners and help our customers with real world problems. We value hard work, creativity, alignment and have fun while solving challenging problems.” – National Interstate Insurance employee

“I am treated with respect, given daily encouragement, and feel part of the team.” – Fifth Third Bank employee

“I am able to do what I love and have the autonomy to make decisions, try new approaches, and learn from mistakes.” – Hyland employee

“It’s something different every day, it’s not stagnant. It’s a relaxed, fun atmosphere. The industry is absolutely fascinating.” – CrossCountry Mortgage, LLC employee

“I get the opportunity to make a difference in the lives of the Residents as well as the staff and families.” – Danbury Senior Living employee

MID-SIZE Company

“I have the autonomy to run my department and feel supported at the same time. I feel valued and appreciated.” – The Anderson DuBose employee

“I am paid well and get to be home every day. That's hard to come by as a driver.” – The Anderson DuBose employee

“I feel heard, respected, and appreciated—more so than I ever imagined feeling as a young woman in this industry. I get to be creative on a daily basis and work with great people at every level.” - Marcus Thomas employee

“I get to do what I'm best at, and my work is valued.” - Marcus Thomas employee

“I feel valued, I feel that I’m helping to make a difference, and I always feel inspired.” – The NRP Group employee

“It challenges me and we work as a team to accomplish a common goal.” – The NRP Group employee

“I work in a family Environment, and my hard work is Appreciated.” - Independence Excavating employee

“I work with great people who are also great lawyers and team members. I feel my contributions are recognized and I'm appreciated for what I do.” – Remington Co., LPA employee

“The work culture is so positive and supportive with everyone working together to maximize what we do.” – Fleet Response employee

“It gives me the flexibility to manage work and family. It's a job I love because I like the idea of helping someone find their happy home.” – Coldwell Banker Schmidt Family of Companies employee

“My colleagues are impressive, kind, collaborative people and my students are wonderfully curious young people who are going to change the world for the better.” – Bay Village City School District employee

“I feel at home while I'm here. I have friends here that are more like family here, and there is plenty of room to grow and new learning opportunities. You are not just a number here.” – Incept employee

“I have trust, autonomy and the backing of my leadership team. They trust my decisions and allow me to grow and flourish in my role.” – Nations Lending employee

SMALL Company

“I love my job because it is not a chore to go to work each morning. I am encouraged and valued.” – Valley Christian Academy employee

“I have the freedom to do what it takes to engage, educate and empower my students.” – Valley Christian Academy employee

“I feel like I have the freedom and support to orchestrate lessons and programs that make a difference in our students lives.” – Saint Albert the Great At Assumption Academy

“My principal is amazing and is always willing to offer helpful advice. The students at my school are extraordinary and have a positive attitude.” – Saint Albert the Great At Assumption Academy

“I'm allowed self-monitored work/life balance. A pervasive culture of "learn it all" vs. "know it all" backed up by extensive education resources. Great benefits.” – Microsoft employee

“My job is dynamic, evolving and very fun.” – Microsoft employee

“I get to work with great people, and great clients. I get to work on new challenges, and new technology.” – Vizion Solutions employee

“Every day I come into work and get to work with amazing co-workers who push you to "be the best"” – Vizion Solutions employee

“I get to positively change people's lives on a daily basis. I live to help people be successful.” – Ancora employee

“I love what I do and Ancora gives me the freedom to do it my way.” – Ancora employee
