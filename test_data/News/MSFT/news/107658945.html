Microsoft, Rogers Communications Enter Five-Year Alliance Focused on Digitization

Published: 2022-03-16 13:09:01 ET MSFT

By Denny Jacob

Microsoft Corp. and Rogers Communications Inc. said the two companies would enter a five-year alliance to help customers advance digitization efforts related to hybrid work and 5G solutions.

The two companies said they would work together to optimize networks for customers across cloud-computing-service Microsoft Azure and Rogers's network. Both technology companies would also explore collaboration on research-and-development opportunities to support the internet of things and mobile-edge computing to advance 5G and Canada's digital economy.

"Together, we'll provide the next-generation service innovations for business customers that will support Canada's growth and prosperity in the years ahead," Rogers Chief Technology & Information Officer Jorge Fernandes said.
