Bayonne man facing jail time over some loose change

  • Published: Jun. 22, 2020, 12:39 p.m.
By
  • Ron Zeitlinger | The Jersey Journal

A Bayonne man might agree that crime doesn’t pay after he was charged with stealing loose change from a car.

Police say Anthony Bermudez, 24, was arrested at 3:51 a.m. Sunday in the area of West 22nd Street after he broke into a victim’s vehicle and removed a small amount of money.

It was also found that Bermudez was wanted on three outstanding arrest warrants, two out of Bayonne and one from the Hudson County Sheriff’s Office, Bayonne Capt. Eric Amato said. Bermudez was charged with burglary and theft.

He was taken to Hudson County jail.

In another arrest Sunday, Ottanee D. Turner of Jersey City was charged with shoplifting and resisting arrest. Amato said the 36-year-old woman stole $573.58 from Walmart and during her arrest, Turner pulled away from the officers and refused to be handcuffed.
