Walmart Consumers Can Now Get Fast Delivery, Regardless Of Basket Size

BySharon EdelsonSenior Contributor
I write about retail and the forces that are redefining shopping.
Following
Mar 01, 2021, 01:04pm EST
This article is more than 3 years old.

Walmart Inc. WMT WMT WMT WMT said today that it’s removing a major pain point – the $35 minimum purchase requirement – from its Express delivery service, which allows consumers to get their orders delivered to their doors in less than two hours.

The service, which was being piloted in 100 stores in April, was rapidly accelerated as the Covid-19 pandemic gripped the country, rolling out to 1,000 units in May, and nearly 2,000 in the weeks that followed. It’s now offered in nearly 3,000 stores, reaching 70% of the U.S. population.

The service is Walmart’s answer to Amazon’s AMZN AMZN AMZN AMZN AMZN AMZN same-day delivery, which is free for Prime members with a $35 minimum basket of eligible items. To get free two-hour delivery, customers must meet the $35 threshold, however they can still utilize the service when ordering from Prime Now, Amazon Fresh and Whole Foods Market by paying a $4.99 fee for baskets under $35. Amazon also offers one-day delivery option, with products arriving the next day, which is free for Prime members with no minimum purchase requirement.

Walmart Inc. and Amazon AMZN have been engaged in a game of delivery chicken with each retailer upping the ante with faster speeds and lower costs to consumers. The two retailers exchanged salvos when Amazon in 2019 launched free one-day shipping for Prime members, and Walmart offered its own version of the service a month later.

Walmart’s food, consumables, produce, pantry staples, everyday essentials, toys, electronics and other general merchandise items are available for Express delivery, and picked and packed by the retailer’s team of 170,000 trained personal shoppers. Like Walmart’s pickup and delivery service, there is no markup on items, which carry the same prices as those on store shelves.

Express delivery costs $10 on top of the existing delivery charge. Walmart+ WMT WMT members simply pay the $10 Express fee.

There are several options that continue to carry a $35 minimum basket requirement. They include, free curbside pickup, however no other fees are added to a customer’s order. Delivery from a local store to a consumer’s door, carries a per delivery fee of $7.95 or $9.95, and requires an order of at least $35.

The Walmart+ free delivery option for members includes a $35 basket. No additional fees are tacked on to the membership fee of $98 per year, or $12.95 per month.

“Many customers use Express delivery when they’re in a pinch, whether they’re missing an ingredient for a weeknight dinner or they need a pack of diapers,” said Tom Ward, senior vice president of customer product at Walmart. “Customers told us sometimes the items they needed in a hurry didn’t meet the minimum, so we’re removing it, making it even easier for customers to get what they need when they need it.”
