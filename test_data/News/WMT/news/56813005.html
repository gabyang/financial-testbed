Walmart Reverses Course, Puts Guns Back On Shelves

Nicholas Reimann
Forbes Staff
News and explainers.
Following
Oct 30, 2020,06:44pm EDT
Updated Oct 30, 2020, 06:44pm EDT
This article is more than 4 years old.
  • Share to Facebook
  • Share to Twitter
  • Share to Linkedin

Topline

After Walmart pulled guns and ammunition off its shelves due to concerns about “civil unrest” ahead of Election Day, the nation’s largest retailer has reversed its decision, according to multiple reports Friday.

Key Facts

Employees were told in a letter Wednesday toremove guns and ammo from public displays out of “an abundance of caution.”

But Walmart said in a statement Friday that the incidents of unrest were “geographically isolated” and that firearms would return to sales floors.

Walmart sells guns in about half of its 4,700 stores, making it one of the largest firearms retailers in the country.

Walmart’s decision to pull guns in the first place seemed to be in response to looting at one of its Philadelphia stores, in which bullets were found on the floor during unrest after the deadly police shooting ofWalter Wallace Jr.

Walmart did not immediately respond to a request for comment from Forbes.

Crucial Quote

“As the current incidents have remained geographically isolated, we have made the decision to begin returning these products to the sales floor today,” a spokesperson toldNBC News.

Key Background

Concerns are growing that there could be unrest as election results come in, especially since it’s a process expected to take much longer than usual because of the record volume of mail-in ballots. President Donald Trump has continually claimed that will lead to widespread fraud, even thoughevidence overwhelmingly shows that an increase in mail-in voting does not lead to a significant fraud risk. Businesses in some cities across the country are also starting to board up windows ahead of Election Day, including someluxury stores near Trump Tower in Manhattan.
