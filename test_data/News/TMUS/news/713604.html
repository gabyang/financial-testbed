Health Officials Expand Free Coronavirus Testing

Health officials have announced a variety of new free testing locations, including the Tacoma Dome and T-Mobile park.

Charles Woodman, Patch Staff
Posted Fri, May 22, 2020 at 4:11 pm PT|Updated Fri, May 22, 2020 at 7:33 pm PT

WASHINGTON — Public health officials have announced a new wave of free coronavirus testing sites across western Washington.

Health officials say anyone with coronavirus symptoms, like coughing, difficulty breathing, fever, chills and muscle pain, should get tested as soon as possible to find out if they have the disease, so that they can avoid spreading it to others. That's why Public Health - Seattle & King County is providing this list of free testing sites:

Anyone still having a tough time accessing a coronavirus test can also call the King County COVID-19 call center, which operates from 8 a.m. to 7 p.m. seven days a week.
