T-Mobile to buy spectrum from Comcast for up to $3.3 bln

Published: 2023-09-12 16:12:55 ET TMUS

Sept 12 (Reuters) - T-Mobile US said on Tuesday that it has agreed to acquire spectrum in the 600 MHz band from Comcast for between $1.2 billion and $3.3 billion in cash. (Reporting by Akash Sriram in Bengaluru; Editing by Shailesh Kuber)
