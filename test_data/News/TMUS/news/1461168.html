Samsung Galaxy A Series Heads to T-Mobile, Sprint and Metro, Including the Un-carrier’s Most Affordable 5G Phone

T-Mobile customers can get the Galaxy A71 5G on the nation’s largest 5G network for just $300.

June 15, 2020 09:42 AM Eastern Daylight Time

BELLEVUE, Wash.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--The Samsung Galaxy A Series lands at the Un-carrier this summer. To kick things off, T-Mobile (NASDAQ: TMUS) and Sprint will be the FIRST in the U.S. to offer the new Samsung Galaxy A71 5G, the Un-carrier's most affordable 5G smartphone, beginning this Friday, June 19. And T-Mobile customers can get it for half off with bill credits when they add a new line of service — that’s a brand new 5G smartphone for just $300. It taps into T-Mobile’s 600 MHz 5G network and is ready to fly on 2.5 GHz spectrum as the Un-carrier lights it up. T-Mobile’s 5G network is the first and only nationwide network, and customers get 5G coverage in all 50 states. Following the Samsung Galaxy A71 5G launch, Metro will carry the Galaxy A01 on June 22 with T-Mobile, Sprint and Metro all carrying the Galaxy A51 5G, Galaxy A21 and Galaxy A11 this summer.

First up! The Samsung Galaxy A71 5G

The Galaxy A71 5G sports four rear cameras and a 6.7” edge-to-edge Super AMOLED Infinity-O screen for a smooth experience for your family to catch up on all the latest shows, thanks to Netflix on Us. Plus, the most affordable 5G phone just gets better at T-Mobile with an offer to get it for half off when customers add a qualifying line or switch to T-Mobile — that’s $300 after 24 monthly bill credits. Not looking to add a line or switch? Customers can get it for $25 per month ($0 down, FRP $600) for 24 months on T-Mobile’s no-interest Equipment Installment Plan with qualifying credit. And Sprint customers can get it for $0 down and $15 per month with 18-month Sprint Flex Lease — saving customers $10 a month off the regular price of $25 per month (FRP $600).

Four More A Series Smartphones Coming This Summer

The Galaxy A01, Galaxy A11 and Galaxy A21 are all affordable LTE smartphones while the Galaxy A51 5G brings the power by tapping into T-Mobile’s nationwide 5G network:

  • Galaxy A01: With a 5.7” HD+ Infinity-V display, multi-lens camera (5MP front-facing camera and dual 13MP and 2 MP rear cameras) plus a 3,000mAh battery, Galaxy A01 will arrive at Metro on June 22 at $159.99 FRP.
  • Galaxy A21: Kick it up a notch with Galaxy A21’s 6.55” HD+ Infinity-O screen, quad cameras with a 16MP main camera and 4,000 mAh battery with 15W Fast Charge support. Get it at T-Mobile and Sprint on June 26 and Metro on June 29. T-Mobile will have it for just $10 per month after $10 down for 24 months with the Equipment Installment Plan with qualifying credit. At Sprint, get it for $0 down and $10.42 per month with 18-month Sprint Flex Lease. ($250 FRP). Stay tuned for pricing at Metro.
  • Galaxy A11: Go for a larger screen and battery with Galaxy A11 and get a 6.4” HD+ Infinity-O screen with three camera lenses (wide lens, ultra-wide lens and depth lens), 4,000mAh battery and 32 GB expandable storage. Stay tuned for pricing when it becomes available later this summer.
  • Samsung Galaxy A51 5G:This 5G smartphone taps into T-Mobile’s 600 MHz 5G and 2.5 GHz 5G spectrum as it gets lit up and toutsa 6.5” FHD+ Infinity-O AMOLED display and a 48MP main lens camera. Designed for multi-taskers and gamers, it has a 4500mAh battery, 6GB of RAM and 128GB of on-board storage that is expandable up to 1TB. Stay tuned for pricing as it becomes available at T-Mobile, Sprint and Metro later this summer.

Even More Value

For a limited time, new customers who activate two voice lines on Magenta can get the third line for FREE! Magenta customers get taxes and fees included, Quibi on Us and Netflix on Us for families, a dedicated Team of Experts, free stuff every week from T-Mobile Tuesdays and free scam protection to fight robocalls. Plus at Sprint, Unlimited Plus customers get Hulu and Tidal included in their monthly plan.
