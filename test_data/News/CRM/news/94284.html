Bridge Connector Announces New Salesforce AppExchange Solution and Partnership With MuleSoft, Accelerating Interoperability in Health Care

Destinations, Bridge Connector’s no-code, no-maintenance integration solution for health care is now available on Salesforce AppExchange

March 09, 2020 08:00 AM Eastern Daylight Time

NASHVILLE, Tenn.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Bridge Connector, a technology company offering data-driven workflow automation to solve health IT interoperability challenges, today announced a new Salesforce AppExchange solution and its partnership with MuleSoft, provider of the leading platform for building application networks. Bridge Connector has been a Salesforce partner for its Healthcare & Life Sciences division since Bridge Connector’s inception in 2017, with Bridge Connector’s solutions made more readily available as a result of today’s announcements.

Bridge Connector’s no-code health care interoperability solution, Destinations, enables organizations to quickly and easily integrate disparate health care applications such as electronic health record (EHR) and customer relationship management (CRM) solutions, patient engagement platforms and more, utilizing its library of connectors. Today, Bridge Connector also announces Destinations’ availability on Salesforce AppExchange, the world’s leading enterprise cloud marketplace. This unique AppExchange iteration of the Destinations product will enable health care organizations to accelerate the interoperability of Salesforce Health Cloud by easily integrating EHR data.

MuleSoft and Bridge Connector are working together to synchronize patient data across Salesforce and third-party systems with API-led connectivity. MuleSoft’s Anypoint Platform™ unlocks the power of API-led connectivity, enabling organizations to connect and integrate apps, data and devices more rapidly and efficiently, both on-premises and in the cloud.

“Combining Bridge Connector’s experience in health care with MuleSoft’s Anypoint Platform will make EHR integrations easier and more readily available than ever before,” said Bridge Connector founder and CEO, David Wenger. “Bridge Connector has built an extensive library of connectors that automates bi-directional data-sharing between disparate health data systems. The ability to quickly and easily integrate mission-critical platforms such as enterprise resource planning (ERP) and health care CRM applications using both Anypoint Platform and the Destinations platform will be a tremendous resource for health care users looking to streamline their workflows and adopt more comprehensive approaches to interoperability.”

“This partnership will enable our mutual customers to gain competitive advantage by launching new applications faster, reducing deployment times, and eliminating time spent on complicated integration projects,” said Brian Miller, senior vice president of business development, MuleSoft. “Technology partner integrations with MuleSoft’s Anypoint Platform and the resulting application network simplify connectivity and eliminate silos of data within both small and large organizations.”

Destinations’ approach eliminates the need to write code and simplifies health care data integrations while making them affordable and easy to maintain for health care companies that do not have access to a dedicated IT team or integration consultants. Using Bridge Connector’s extensive library of connectors, Destinations app users can select from pre-built, workflow-based templates to connect disparate data systems — using clicks, not code — without having to leave the Salesforce platform.

“Destinations from Bridge Connector is a welcome addition to AppExchange, as it powers digital transformation for health care companies through streamlined data integrations," said Woodson Martin, GM of Salesforce AppExchange. "AppExchange is constantly evolving to enable our partners to build cutting-edge solutions to drive customer success."

“Integrating electronic health records (EHR) with Salesforce Health Cloud is critical to empower health care organizations to deliver connected, personalized patient care,” said Rob Seaman, SVP of Product Management, Industry Clouds at Salesforce. “With Bridge Connector making Destinations' capabilities available in AppExchange, together we are providing Salesforce admins with an easy way to synchronize EHR data with Salesforce Health cloud without writing a line of code."

Bridge Connector customers can learn more about simplifying connectivity between all of their software systems with MuleSoft’s Anypoint Platform at https://www.mulesoft.com/platform/enterprise-integration, and can learn more about Destinations on Salesforce AppExchange at https://appexchange.salesforce.com/appxListingDetail?listingId=a0N3A00000G4xuRUAR.

To learn more about Bridge Connector’s solutions for achieving health care interoperability, please visit https://bridgeconnector.co/.

About Bridge Connector
Bridge Connector delivers an end-to-end suite of solutions to transform the way health care communicates. Its core integration-platform-as-a-service (iPaaS) offering empowers health care provider organizations across the continuum of care with the ability to easily integrate traditionally disparate, mission-critical platforms such as electronic health records (EHRs) and customer relationship management systems (CRMs) without the use of code. Bridge Connector’s integration technologies help health care organizations unlock high-impact clinical and operational insights and automate workflows without the need for deep technical expertise. Bridge Connector is headquartered in Nashville, Tenn., and has offices in San Francisco and Palm Beach Gardens, Fla. To learn more about Bridge Connector, visit https://bridgeconnector.co and follow the company on Twitter and LinkedIn.

About Anypoint Platform
MuleSoft’s Anypoint Platform™ is a leading application network platform. It allows organizations to create composite applications that connect apps, data, and devices through API-led connectivity to form a flexible application network. Anypoint Platform is a unified, single solution for iPaaS and full lifecycle API management, both on-premises and in the cloud.
