Wall Street in Fives - Must Read Lists at Midday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these Top Fives, as compiled by the editors of The Fly.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Amazon (AMZN) reported record quarterly revenue and announced that it is increasing the price of its Prime membership in the U.S. [more]
  • Snap (SNAP) reported upbeat Q4 results, including a 20% year-over-year spike in daily active users [more]
  • Kohl's (KSS) has engaged financial advisors to work with interested parties after receiving takeover bids it considers to be too low [more]
  • Ford (F) reported downbeat Q4 earnings [more]
  • U.S. nonfarm payrolls rose 467,000 in January from the prior month, leaving the unemployment rate at 4% [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Penn National (PENN) upgraded to Hold at Loop Capital and upgraded to Buy at CBRE while being downgraded to Neutral at Roth Capital [more]
  • BuzzFeed (BZFD) initiated with a Neutral at BofA [more]
  • Biogen (BIIB) downgraded to Market Perform from Outperform at BMO Capital [more]
  • Estee Lauder (EL) upgraded to Buy from Neutral at Citi [more]
  • Nvidia (NVDA) shares could hit $150 on "crypto winter," says New Street [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • News Corp (NWS) was targeted by a cyberattack, WSJ reports [more]
  • General Motors (GM) workers in Mexico voted to adopt an independent union, NY Times reports [more]
  • A Senate panel has approved an app store bill that limits Apple (AAPL) and Google (GOOGL), WSJ says [more]
  • Meta (FB) CEO Mark Zuckerberg told employees to focus on Instagram Reels, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Nike (NKE) is suing StockX for selling unauthorized NFTs of its shoes, Reuters says [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • Sphere 3D (ANY) rises after acquiring 60,000 Numiner NM400 bitcoin miners [more]
  • Virtus Investment (VRTS) and iRadimed (IRMD) higher after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Vanda Pharmaceuticals (VNDA) drops after its Phase 3 tradipitant study did not meet its primary endpoint [more]
  • Carlisle (CSL) slips after announcing acquisition of MBTechnology [more]
  • Ubiquiti (UI) and Air Products (APD) lower after reporting quarterly results [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Clorox (CLX) reported downbeat Q2 earnings and cut its FY22 adjusted earnings guidance [more]
  • Royal Caribbean (RCL) reported downbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Bill.com (BILL) reported upbeat Q2 results [more]
  • Pinterest (PINS) also reported upbeat quarterly earnings and revenue [more]
  • Skyworks (SWKS) reported better-than-expected Q1 results [more]

INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was down 101.01, or 0.29%, to 35,010.15, the Nasdaq was up 107.26, or 0.77%, to 13,986.08, and the S&P 500 was up 3.86, or 0.09%, to 4,481.30.
