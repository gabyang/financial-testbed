What You Missed On Wall Street On Tuesday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Pfizer (PFE) reported better-than-expected Q1 earnings and revenue, but cut its FY22 adjusted EPS view [more]
  • Biogen (BIIB) reported lower-than-expected Q1 earnings that were negatively impacted by an inventory write-off for its Alzheimer's Disease drug Aduhelm [more]
  • Activist investor Elliott Investment Management is urging Western Digital (WDC) to conduct a strategic review of the value that could be created by separating its hard disk drives and NAND flash memory businesses [more]
  • Paramount (PARA) reported Q1 results and said its global streaming subscriber count grew 6.3M in the quarter [more]
  • Estee Lauder (EL) is lower after its Q3 report and FY22 guidance cut [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Piper Sandler downgraded Kellogg (K) and Tyson Foods (TSN) to Underweight from Neutral [more]
  • Oppenheimer upgraded JPMorgan (JPM) and Morgan Stanley (MS) to Outperform from Perform [more]
  • Carvana (CVNA) downgraded to Equal Weight from Overweight at Wells Fargo [more]
  • DocuSign (DOCU) downgraded to Underperform from Neutral at Wedbush [more]
  • Nvidia (NVDA) resumed with an Equal Weight at Morgan Stanley [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Welltower (WELL) remains interested in bid for Healthcare Realty Trust (HR), WSJ reports [more]
  • PayPal (PYPL) helped start EU antitrust complaint against Apple, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Roku (ROKU) and Apollo (APO) are teaming up to bid for a minority stake in Lionsgate's (LGF.A) Starz, WSJ reports [more]
  • Apple (AAPL) has hired a Ford (F) veteran to ramp up its car project efforts, Bloomberg says [more]
  • AT&T (T) is raising mobile plan prices amid higher costs, Bloomberg reports [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • Enovix (ENVX) higher after announcing it has received an initial order for its smartwatch battery from a consumer electronics company [more]
  • New Residential Investment (NRZ) and BP (BP) rise in New York after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Matterport (MTTR) declines after Wedbush downgraded the stock to Neutral from Outperform [more]
  • Hayward (HAYW) lower after pricing a 24M share secondary offering at $14.50 per share [more]
  • Rockwell Automation (ROK) and Sabre (SABR) fall after reporting quarterly results [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Chegg (CHGG) plunged and was downgraded by at least three Wall Street firms after management reduced guidance along with its Q1 report [more]
  • MGM Resorts (MGM) reported what CBRE analyst John DeCree said were "solid" results in Q1 [more]
  • William Blair and Raymond James downgraded EverQuote (EVER) following the company's Q1 report [more]
  • Logitech (LOGI) is sliding after the company's Q4 earnings miss and FY23 guidance cut [more]
  • Molson Coors (TAP) reported a better than expected start of the year and backed its targets for full year 2022 [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 67.29, or 0.2%, to 33,128.79, the Nasdaq gained 27.74, or 0.22%, to 12,563.76, and the S&P 500 advanced 20.10, or 0.48%, to 4,175.48.
