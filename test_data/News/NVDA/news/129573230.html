Nvidia sales seen doubling, but that may not impress investors

Published: 2024-08-27 03:51:40 ET NVDA

STORY: It's getting harder for Nvidia to impress investors.

The AI chip champion is forecast to say Wednesday that its second-quarter revenues more than doubled.

A forecast compiled by LSEG predicts they will hit almost $28.7 billion.

And yet even that might seem a comedown, when the previous three quarters saw growth exceed 200%.

Investors are also concerned that profit margins could suffer, hurt by the cost of ramping up production.

There's concern too over possible delays to the company's next-generation Blackwell chips, which some analysts have said face delays.

Customers are a worry as well, with some market-watchers questioning whether Big Tech can sustain its massive spending on AI.

Such doubts led to a 20% drop in Nvidia stock over July and August, though it later clawed back much of those losses.

Now any big beat or miss on Wall Street expectations could fuel, or shatter, an AI-driven stock rally.

Nvidia shares have gained over 150% this year, adding around $1.8 trillion to its market value, and driving the S&P index to fresh highs.

Also in focus Wednesday will be the company's prospects in China, where the U.S. government bars sales of its most advanced chips.

Investors hope new processors designed to comply with the regulations can drive sales growth - and fend off a growing challenge from Chinese rival Huawei.
