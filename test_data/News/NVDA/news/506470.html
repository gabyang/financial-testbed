    Minecraft RTX beta: Download Minecraft RTX beta today without Nether support

    MOJANG and Microsoft have released the new Minecraft RTX beta download today, but the new update will not be available across all parts of the game, including the Nether.

    By Gary Jones
    18:13, Thu, Apr 16, 2020 | UPDATED: 18:21, Thu, Apr 16, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    The Minecraft RTA beta is available to download on Windows 10 (Image: MOJANG)

    The Minecraft RTX beta has begun and Mojang has revealed how players can join, and what won’t be available. For those who might have missed the big news, Mojang is testing a new version of Minecraft on Windows 10 that uses RTX. RTX aims to provide real-time ray tracing into games and the new Minecraft RTX beta is just the latest. The good news is that this new version of Minecraft is now available to download but it should be noted that it comes with some serious limitations.

    First of all, not everyone will be able to join the new Windows 10 beta, which is not available on PS4 or Xbox One.

    You’ll need to have Minecraft for Windows 10 and you will need to join the beta. Your PC will also need a few things as well; minimum spec GPU of NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060, CPU of Intel Core i5 or equivalent, and 8GB+ RAM.

    The latest Game Ready Driver will be needed from Nvidia and after that, you will be ready to enjoy the new Minecraft experience. There will also be limitations to what content from Minecraft will be compatible with the Minecraft RTX beta.

    “Please be aware that this beta, like every beta, is a work in progress,” a message from Mojang explains.

    “Things may go wrong, be a bit unstable, not available in all parts of the game (such as the Nether or the End), or may not match gameplay rules – but that’s part of the process.

    “With that in mind, we’d love your help! Join the beta and let us know your suggestions on feedback.minecraft.net and of course please report all bugs on bugs.mojang.com.

    “Please remember that worlds opened on beta builds cannot be reopened on previous builds.

    “We recommend that you always make a copy of your worlds before opening them in the beta. Beta players can only connect to other players on the same beta build and do not have access to Realms or Partnered Servers while on the beta.”

    HOW DO I GET MINECRAFT RTA IN BETA?

    To access the Minecraft with RTX beta, Microsoft has provided these instructions if you are not currently on a Minecraft Win 10 Beta.

    • Install the Xbox Insider Hub app on your PC (it is available for free in the Windows Store)
    • Inside the app, click on the open box icon in the sidebar on the left
    • On the Insider content screen, you will see a beta available for Minecraft for Windows 10. Click on the Minecraft for Windows 10 beta
    • Click the “Join” button at the bottom of the screen
    • On the following pop-up you will see three radio buttons- one will be for Minecraft RTX Beta, one for Minecraft Beta, one for Unenroll. Choose Minecraft RTX Beta, then click Done.
    • If you have automatic updates on from the Windows Store, Minecraft will automatically update to the latest RTX beta build available.

      If you are already in the Minecraft for Win 10 beta, follow these instructions:

      • Open the Xbox Insider Hub app
      • Inside the app, click on the open box icon in the sidebar on the left
      • On the Insider content screen, you will just see one Minecraft for Win 10 Beta offering under Joined. Click on it.
      • In this screen, click on Manage.
      • In the Manage screen, you will have a radio button that will allow you to choose to enroll in the RTX Beta or to Unenroll from the beta program entirely. Select the RTX beta & click on the Done button to the left
      • You may need to uninstall Minecraft and reinstall it from the store in order to download the RTX beta. Please back up any worlds you wish you keep before uninstalling as otherwise the worlds will be deleted when you uninstall the game. Minecraft saved worlds can be found in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\Microsoft.MinecraftUWP_8wekyb3d8bbwe\LocalState\games\com.mojang\minecraftWorlds – each folder within the minecraftWorlds folder is a world. Please save it somewhere on your PC that is not within the Minecraft directory. You can also export worlds directly from Minecraft to your PC’s desktop.

      Microsoft has confirmed that Minecraft gamers can download a selection of free creator-built worlds that have been built to make use of ray tracing from the Minecraft Marketplace.

      These can be found by looking for content tagged with RTX and will only work with RTX compatible systems.
