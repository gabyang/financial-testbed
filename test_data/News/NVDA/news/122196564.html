UPDATE1 : Tokyo stocks extend rally to 4th straight day as chip shares gain

Published: 2023-08-24 03:38:06 ET NVDA

Tokyo stocks gained for the fourth consecutive day Thursday, led by heavyweight semiconductor-related shares following positive earnings from U.S. chip giant Nvidia Corp.

The 225-issue Nikkei Stock Average ended up 276.95 points, or 0.87 percent, from Wednesday at 32,287.21. The broader Topix index finished 9.54 points, or 0.42 percent, higher at 2,286.59.

Gainers were led by mining, construction, and oil and coal product shares.

Stocks rose from the outset and continued to climb in the afternoon, with chip-related issues tracking overnight gains among their U.S. counterparts on Nvidia's robust earnings report for the May-July quarter showing that revenue doubled from the previous year. The company also revealed a rosy revenue outlook for the August-October period.

But there was still some caution ahead of a speech on Friday by U.S. Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell at the Jackson Hole symposium in Wyoming, as investors looked for clues on the future direction of the country's monetary policy, analysts said.

Among heavyweight semiconductor-related stocks, Tokyo Electron, a chip-manufacturing equipment maker, was up 685 yen, or 3.3 percent, at 21,750 yen, while chip-testing equipment maker Advantest rose 310 yen, or 1.6 percent, to 19,660 yen.

Meanwhile, the yen's appreciation against the dollar weighed on exporter issues including automakers, as a stronger yen decreases their overseas earnings when repatriated.

Nissan Motor declined 6.6 yen, or 1.1 percent, to 598.4 yen, while Mitsubishi Motors fell 6.5 yen, or 1.2 percent, to 551.0 yen.
