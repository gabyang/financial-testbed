Nvidia receives DOJ subpoena, Dick's posts Q2 beat: Morning Buzz
Mid/Close Wrap
Brianna West - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Nvidia (NVDA) has received a DOJ subpoena in an antitrust probe, Bloomberg reports
  • The Nordstrom family has offered to acquire Nordstrom (JWN) for $23 per share in cash
  • Dick's Sporting (DKS) provided a"beat and raise"report for Q2
  • Dollar Tree (DLTR)reported downbeat Q2 earningsand revenue and cut its FY24 guidance
  • U.S. Steel (X) employees will host a rally in support of the Nippon Steel (NPSCY) transaction

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • BofA gets more bearish on Amphenol (APH), downgrades shares on possible impact of Nvidia (NVDA) design change
  • Airbnb (ABNB) cut to Hold at Argus on slowing room nights growth
  • Booking Holdings (BKNG) downgraded at Jefferies, here's why
  • Supermicro (SMCI) downgraded to Equal Weight at Barclays
  • Nasdaq (NDAQ) double upgradedto Buy at BofA with IPO flywheel returning

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • HP Enterprise (HPE) intends to pursue the $4B damages claim in London against the estate of the recently deceased Mike Lynch, Bloomberg reports
  • Snap (SNAP) CEO Evan Spiegel is working to rally the company's employees amid a 48% drop in stock price this year, Bloomberg says
  • Intel's (INTC) contract manufacturing business has suffered a setback after tests with Broadcom (AVGO) using Intel's advanced 18A chipmaking process failed, Reuters reports
  • Volkswagen (VWAGY) management is set to confront workers about possible cuts that could include domestic factory closures, CNBC reports
  • Targa Resources (TRGP) rebuffed informal takeover interest from Williams Cos. (WMB) in recent months, Bloomberg says

4. MOVERS:

  • Clover Health (CLOV) gains after announcing its subsidiary entered a multi-year agreementwith The Iowa Clinic
  • Sweetgreen (SG) increases after TD Cowen upgraded the stock to Buy
  • AST SpaceMobile (ASTS) higher after providing an interim business updateon its upcoming orbital launch
  • Core & Main (CNM) falls after reporting Q2 resultsand cutting its FY24 guidance
  • REV Group (REVG) lower after reporting Q3 resultsand lowering its guidance for FY24

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Zscaler (ZS) reported Q4 results, with EPS and revenue beating consensus
  • Asana (ASAN) reported Q2 results, provided guidance for Q3, and announced its outlook for FY25
  • PagerDuty (PD) reported Q2 results and raised its guidance for FY25
  • GitLab (GTLB) announced Q2 results and provided guidance for Q3 and FY25
  • Ciena (CIEN) reported Q3 results, with CEO Gary Smith commenting, "We delivered strong results for the fiscal third quarter that reflect growing momentum with cloud providers"

INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was up 54.22, or 0.13%, to 40,991.15, the Nasdaq was down 15.34, or 0.09%, to 17,120.96, and the S&P 500 was down 0.95, or 0.02%, to 5,527.98.
