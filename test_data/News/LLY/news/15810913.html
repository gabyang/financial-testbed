LONDON--(BUSINESS WIRE)--Technavio has been monitoring the radiopharmaceuticals market and it is poised to grow by USD 3.58 billion during 2020-2024, progressing at a CAGR of 10% during the forecast period. The report offers an up-to-date analysis regarding the current market scenario, latest trends and drivers, and the overall market environment.

Technavio suggests three forecast scenarios (optimistic, probable, and pessimistic) considering the impact of COVID-19. Please Request Free Sample Report on COVID-19 Impact

The market is concentrated, and the degree of concentration will accelerate during the forecast period. Bayer AG, BWX Technologies Inc., Cardinal Health Inc., Curium, Eckert & Ziegler AG, Eli Lilly and Co., General Electric Co., Lantheus Holdings Inc., Novartis AG, and Siemens Healthineers AG are some of the major market participants. To make the most of the opportunities, market vendors should focus more on the growth prospects in the fast-growing segments, while maintaining their positions in the slow-growing segments.

Rising burden of neurological disorders has been instrumental in driving the growth of the market.
