    Why Air Products and Chemicals Shares Are Trading Lower By Around 15%? Here Are Other Stocks Moving In Monday's Mid-Day Session

    Benzinga
    Feb. 5, 2024, 12:56 PM







    Shares of Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. (NYSE:APD) fell sharply during Monday’s session after the company reported worse-than-expected first-quarter financial results and lowered FY24 adjusted EPS guidance below estimates.

    Air Products & Chemicals posted adjusted earnings of $2.82 per share, missing market estimates of $3.00 per share. The company’s quarterly sales came in at $3.00 billion versus expectations of $3.19 billion.

    Air Products and Chemicals shares dipped 14.7% to $220.02 on Monday.

    Here are some other stocks moving in today's mid-day session.

    Gainers

    • Yield10 Bioscience, Inc. (NASDAQ:YTEN) shares climbed 116.4% to $0.4964.
    • 4D Molecular Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ:FDMT) gained 100.9% to $35.13 after the company announced interim data from the Phase 2 PRISM clinical trial evaluating intravitreal 4D-150 in wet AMD patients with severe disease activity and a high treatment burden.
    • Chenghe Acquisition Co. (NASDAQ:CHEA) shares jumped 76% to $9.66 after shareholders on Friday voted to approve the merger agreement with Taiwan Color Optics.
    • Wrap Technologies, Inc. (NASDAQ:WRAP) gained 55.6% to $6.35. Wrap Technologies has reduced its labor force by 30%, representing $2.4 million in annual savings.
    • Adverum Biotechnologies, Inc. (NASDAQ:ADVM) climbed 31.1% to $1.8620. Adverum Biotechnologies has agreed to sell approximately 106.25 million shares in a private placement at $1.20 per share with gross proceeds of approximately $127.5 million.
    • MIRA Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NASDAQ:MIRA) surged 31.1% to $1.22. MIRA Pharmaceuticals announced a research collaboration with Pharmaseed to conduct pre-clinical studies on use of Ketamir for patients with major depressive disorder and post-traumatic stress disorder.
    • Envoy Medical, Inc. (NASDAQ:COCH) rose 27% to $1.5261.
    • Griid Infrastructure Inc. (NASDAQ:GRDI) shares rose 26.4% to $2.63 after dipping more than 50% on Friday.
    • Rail Vision Ltd. (NASDAQ:RVSN) climbed 24.9% to $14.79. Rail Vision received patent approval from the Indian Patent Office titled "System And Method For Object And Obstacle Detection And Classification In Collision Avoidance Of Railway Applications."
    • CalAmp Corp. (NASDAQ:CAMP) shares gained 21.1% to $3.7413 after dipping around 14% on Friday. CalAmp recently announced a 1-for-23 reverse stock split.
    • SRM Entertainment, Inc. (NASDAQ:SRM) surged 18.4% to $1.73.
    • Solowin Holdings (NASDAQ:SWIN) rose 18.2% to $4.8327.
    • Everbridge, Inc. (NASDAQ:EVBG) gained 18.2% to $28.13 after the company announced it will be acquired by Thoma Bravo for $1.5 billion.

    • MorphoSys AG (NASDAQ:MOR) gained 15.7% to $12.81.
    • Jianpu Technology Inc. (NYSE:JT) gained 14.4% to $0.6202.
    • The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. (NYSE:EL) climbed 14% to $152.93 after the company reported better-than-expected second-quarter financial results and announced a restructuring program, which includes an estimated net reduction of roughly 3 to 5 percent of its positions as of June 30, 2023.
    • Core Scientific, Inc. (NASDAQ:CORZ) gained 10.3% to $3.29. Core Scientific produced 13,762 Bitcoin in 2023.
    • Catalent, Inc. (NYSE:CTLT) rose 10% to $59.97 after the company announced it will be acquired by Novo Holdings.
    • ON Semiconductor Corporation (NASDAQ:ON) rose 7.3% to $75.97 after the company reported better-than-expected fourth-quarter financial results.
    • Eli Lilly and Company (NYSE:LLY) gained 5.2% to $702.38. Eli Lilly and Company is expected to release earnings results for its fourth quarter, before the opening bell on Feb. 6, 2024.

    Losers

    • DMK Pharmaceuticals Corporation (NASDAQ:DMK) fell 53.2% to $0.2433. The company filed voluntary petitions under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware.

    • Bit Origin Ltd (NASDAQ:BTOG) fell 29.7% to $6.57.

    • Sunnova Energy International Inc. (NYSE:NOVA) fell 11.3% to $8.78.
