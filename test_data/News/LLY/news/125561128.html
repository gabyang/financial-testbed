Trending : Eli Lilly 4Q Revenue Gets Boost From Weight-Loss Drug Sales

Published: 2024-02-06 09:56:06 ET LLY

09:40 ET -- Eli Lilly & Co. is one of the most mentioned companies in the U.S. across all news items in the last 12 hours, according to Factiva data. The pharmaceutical giant's 4Q revenue surged versus the year-ago quarter to $9.35 billion, led by weight-loss drugs Mounjaro and Zepbound. Eli Lilly reported a profit of $2.19 billion, or $2.42 a share, up from a year earlier and beating analyst estimates. The company said higher prices for Mounjaro offset lower prices for Humalog insulin and the diabetes treatment Trulicity. The higher realized prices improved gross margin by 2.1 percentage points to 80.9% of revenue. Dow Jones & Co. owns Factiva. ([email protected])
