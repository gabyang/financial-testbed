Eli Lilly Launches Genetic-Medicines Push With $700 Million Boston Site

Published: 2022-02-22 07:30:02 ET LLY

By Colin Kellaher

Eli Lilly & Co. on Tuesday unveiled plans to invest about $700 million to build a new facility in Boston as part of the launch of the Lilly Institute for Genetic Medicine.

The Indianapolis drugmaker said it plans to build a state-of-the-art facility in the Boston Seaport as part of its strategy to advance RNA-based therapeutics.

Eli Lilly said the move builds on the acquisition of New York gene-therapy company Prevail Therapeutics, which it completed early last year.

Eli Lilly said researchers in Boston and New York would work together to leverage promising RNA- and DNA-based technologies to develop therapies with the potential to treat or prevent diseases in a manner that is challenging or not possible with traditional medicines.

Eli Lilly said it expects the work of the new institute to fuel the development of genetic medicines, which already account for more than 20% of its diabetes, immunology and central-nervous-system research portfolio. The company said it expects the Boston site would grow to more than 250 research biologists, chemists, data scientists and other experts in genetic medicine within five year, while the New York site would grow to include up to 200 scientists.
