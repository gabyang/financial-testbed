Eli Lilly to Acquire Maker of Obesity Treatment for Up to $1.925 Billion

Published: 2023-07-14 08:36:06 ET LLY

By Ben Glickman

Eli Lilly said Friday it would acquire Versanis Bio, a private company developing a treatment for obesity, for up to $1.925 billion.

The pharmaceutical company said Versanis shareholders could receive as much as $1.925 billion in cash, which will consist of an upfront payment and additional payments if certain milestones are reached.

Versanis develops treatments for cardiometabolic diseases. Its lead candidate, bimagreumab, is currently in a Phase 2b study to test its affects on reducing fat mass in overweight and obese adults. The company said the treatment may lead to better outcomes for people living with obesity and related complications.

Lilly has been pushing for approval of Mounjaro, a Type 2 diabetes treatment, as an anti-obesity drug. Meanwhile, Ozempic, another diabetes treatment made by Novo Nordisk A/S, has rapidly garnered attention for its own weight-loss effects.
