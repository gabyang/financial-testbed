What You Missed On Wall Street On Tuesday
Mid/Close Wrap
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Paramount (PARA) has ended its "Go-Shop" period with Edgar Bronfman's proposal withdrawn
  • Apple (AAPL) said that Kevan Parekh will replace Luca Maestri as CFO
  • Eli Lilly (LLY) has releasedZepbound single dose vials, which are available for self-pay
  • Two killed, one injured in an "incident" at a Delta Air Lines (DAL) facility, ABC News reports
  • Pfizer (PFE) has launched a new website to help patients get vaccines and medicines called "PfizerForAll"

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Hershey (HSY) downgradedat Citi to Sell
  • Seaport makes tactical steel call, upgradesCleveland-Cliffs (CLF) to Buy
  • Scotiabank upgradesThomson Reuters (TRI) with AI opportunity not priced in
  • Temu parent PDD Holdings (PDD) downgradedto Neutral from Buy at Citi
  • Truist upgradedEnergizer (ENR) to Buy on battery stability returning

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Nvidia's (NVDA) earnings report could spur a more than $300B move in the shares, Reuters says
  • Walmart (WMT) is extending its Amazon (AMZN) competition into fulfillment services, WSJ says
  • Amazon (AMZN) is planning to launch its delayed overhaul of Alexa in October as new competition rises with AI voice assistants, Washington Post reports
  • The Federal Trade Commission argued in court that Kroger's (KR) plans to takeover Albertsons (ACI) would eliminate competition at the expense of consumers and employees, NY Times reports
  • Sony (SONY) is hiking the price of its PlayStation 5 games console in Japan by about 20%, Reuters reports

4. MOVERS:

  • JD.com (JD) gains in New York after authorizing a new $5B share repurchase program
  • Vale (VALE) increases in New York after naming Gustavo Pimenta as its new CEO
  • XPeng (XPEV) higher in New York after Bocom upgradedthe stock to Buy
  • Cava Group (CAVA) lower after announcing a 6M share offering
  • Lumen (LUMN) falls after Kerrisdale Capital published a short reporton the stock

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Heico (HEI) reported Q3 results, with EPS beating consensus
  • Trip.com (TCOM) reported Q2 results, with EPS and revenue ahead of consensus
  • Scansource (SCSC) reported Q4 results, with CEO Mike Baur commenting, "Our results for the year reflect a soft demand environment"
  • Cognizant (CTSH) raised its guidance for Q3 and cut its outlook for FY24
  • Hain Celestial (HAIN) reported Q4 results and provided guidance for FY25

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 9.98, or 0.02%, to 41,250.50, the Nasdaq gained 29.05, or 0.16%, to 17,754.82, and the S&P 500 advanced 8.96, or 0.16%, to 5,625.80.
