Healthcare Sector Update: Life In The Slow Lane While We Wait For BIOTECH Bounce

By Rod Raynovitch of Raygent.com
Monday, October 14, 2024 1:40 PM EST

depositphotos
 

  • Technology stocks continue to outperform with fits of Momentum from generative AI theme.
  • Healthcare sector lags despite moderate growth. Note bad news from CVS and Humana.
  • Momentum plays exist with specific stocks where innovation becomes apparent. e.g.CorMedix.

The market hit new highs last week led by technology and communication stocks as bull market enters 3rd Year.  Defensive stocks such as Healthcare (XLV) up 12.84% YTD, lagged while the Financials (XLF) up over 2%, up 21.84% YTD beat the S&P over the past week with strong earnings from banks. This week we will get a good feel for the healthcare sector from companies reporting such as Johnson and Johnson (JNJ) and United Health (UNH). The XLV is weighted heavily with five stocks LLY, UNH, JNJ, ABBV and MRK. Over the past year we have focused on several large cap stocks with dividends: ABBV, LLY, REGN and VRTX. The top weighted MedTECH stocks in the XLV are: TMO, ABT, DHRand ISRG. You can get good diversification in the Tools and Device sector with 2 FUNDS the FSMEX up 8.88% YTD and PRHSX up 14.25 %YTD, as you can see performance of our top funds below.
