Global Medical Aesthetics Market (2019 to 2028) - Featuring Cutera, Zimmer Biomet & Cynosure Among Others - ResearchAndMarkets.com

June 25, 2020 11:11 AM Eastern Daylight Time

DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--The "Global Medical Aesthetics Market 2019-2028" report has been added to ResearchAndMarkets.com's offering.

The publisher, in its research report, estimates the global medical aesthetics market to proliferate with a CAGR of 5.53% in the forecast years 2019-2028.

The growth of this market is primarily driven by the growing adoption of home-use aesthetic products, along with the surging popularity of medical aesthetic treatments and the rise in popularity of non-invasive techniques. Moreover, the growing use of medical aesthetic measures and advancements in technology present enormous opportunities for market growth.

However, all medical procedures are associated with a certain level of risk. Therefore, the side-effects associated with medical aesthetic procedures are obstructing the growth of this market. The high cost of implantable medical devices and reimbursement issues are also creating hurdles in market growth. Furthermore, counterfeit products in the market are aplenty. These products are substandard and developed without adhering to the guidelines laid by regulatory bodies. Many a time, it is difficult to distinguish these products from the original; this is dangerous to customers, and also dents the market growth.

The global market spans across the regions of North America, Latin America, the Asia-Pacific, the Middle East and Africa, and Europe.

The market for medical aesthetics has a high potential for growth in the Asia-Pacific region, given its high population base, rising disposable incomes of people, and a rise in awareness among people about beauty & youth. The growing medical tourism industry and the advancements in technology have made the APAC an attractive market for medical aesthetics. India is one of the most preferred countries to undergo aesthetic surgical procedures, which is also aiding the market growth in this region.
