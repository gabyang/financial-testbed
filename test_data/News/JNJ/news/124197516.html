Johnson & Johnson Buys Laminar For $400 Million, Trims Profit Outlook

Published: 2023-11-30 09:22:04 ET JNJ

By Will Feuer

Johnson & Johnson said it has bought Laminary, a privately held medical device company, for an upfront payment of $400 million.

J&J also cut its full-year adjusted profit outlook to reflect charges associated with the deal. The company also said the deal will reduce earnings next year by about 15 cents a share.

J&J is now targeting 2023 adjusted earnings of $9.90 a share to $9.96 a share, down from $10.07 a share to $10.13 a share previously. The company expects operational adjusted earnings of $9.85 a share to $9.91 a share, down from $10.02 a share to $10.08 a share.

The adjustments are tied to an in-process research-and-development charge associated with the acquisition.

Laminar is focused on eliminating the left atrial appendage in patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation, or AFib. About 38 million patients around the world have AFib, which makes them more than five times as likely to have a stroke, J&J said.

Laminar will join J&J MedTech as part of the Biosense Webster business, which is focused on cardiac arrhythmia treatments. Along with the $400 million upfront payment, there could be additional clinical and regulatory milestone payments.
