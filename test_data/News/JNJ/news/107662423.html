What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS: 

  • Starbucks (SBUX) CEO Kevin Johnson will retire, founder Howard Schultz stepping in as interim CEO [more]
  • Pfizer (PFE) and BioNTech (BNTX) summited an FDA application for emergency use authorization of an additional COVID-19 booster dose for adults 65 and older [more]
  • Fidelity National (FNF) will partially spin off F&G Annuities & Life to shareholders [more]
  • Chinese stocks surged after China's Vice Premier Liu He said Beijing would add support for the economy and pledged to keep markets stable, according to Reuters [more]
  • SentinelOne (S) reported quarterly results and provided upbeat revenue guidance for Q1 and FY23 [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Micron (MU) upgraded to Outperform from Underperform at Bernstein [more]
  • SoFi Technologies (SOFI) downgraded to Equal Weight from Overweight at Morgan Stanley [more]
  • Bernstein downgrades J&J (JNJ) on "uphill battle" for Janssen unit [more]
  • JPMorgan upgrades Starbucks (SBUX), sees "very favorable risk/reward" [more]
  • Craig-Hallum starts 'lidar's new leader' Cepton (CPTN) at Buy [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB: 

  • Sycamore, Hudson's Bay plan bids for Kohl's (KSS) in high $60s per share, WSJ reports [more]
  • US Foods (USFD) is mulling changes to its board, Reuters reports [more]
  • Tesla (TSLA) has halted production at its Shanghai gigafactory for two days, Electrek reports [more]
  • Foxconn (HNHPF) has received approval to partially reopen two Shenzhen campuses, Bloomberg says [more]
  • Meta (FB) CEO Mark Zuckerberg said that NFTs are coming to Instagram, Engadget reports [more]


4. MOVERS:

  • Coinbase (COIN) rises after announcing launch of "Coinbase Pay" [more]
  • Butterfly Network (BFLY) up after a regulatory filing showed director Larry Robins bought 500,000 shares of common stock in a total transaction of $2.25M [more]
  • NortonLifeLock (NLOK) falls in New York after issuing a statement in response to the CMA referring its merger with Avast (AVASF) for further review and stating it believes the deal will now close in mid-to-late 2022 [more]
  • CarParts.com (PRTS) declines after announcing its CEO will step down to join CarLotz (LOTZ) [more]
  • Endeavour Silver (EXK) lower in New York after announcing $40M bought deal financing [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Smartsheet (SMAR) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Jabil (JBL) provided "beat and raise" report for Q2 [more]
  • RumbleOn (RMBL) reported much better-than-expected Q4 results [more]
  • Arcos Dorados (ARCO) also reported upbeat Q4 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Telos (TLS) reported lower-than-expected Q4 revenue [more]


INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was up 426.83, or 1.27%, to 33,971.17, the Nasdaq was up 365.95, or 2.83%, to 13,314.58, and the S&P 500 was up 74.82, or 1.76%, to 4,337.27.
