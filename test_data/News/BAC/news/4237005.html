Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Which Bank of America credit card is the best?

There’s no one “best” card suitable for everyone. The best card for you depends on a combination of your needs, credit profile and spending habits. Bank of America offers numerous credit cards, each designed with a different type of consumer in mind.

What is the highest credit limit for Bank of America credit cards?

The highest credit limit you can qualify for with Bank of America depends on your initial creditworthiness—including your credit history, income and other determining factors. The higher your credit score, the more likely you will be awarded a higher line of credit.

For secured cards, your limit will be determined by the amount of the initial deposit. Secured cards in general usually have minimum deposits in the low hundreds and maximum deposits not exceeding $5,000.

How do you set up Bank of America’s credit card autopay?

To set up automatic payments, log into the app and click on the Bill Pay tab to enroll. Once enrolled, click on Add Account and enter the necessary information. To set up payment, select the account, amount, the deliver-by date and then Pay. You can also set up recurring payments.

If you have already received a statement, you will need to make a payment for the current month. Your automatic payment will begin when you receive your first eBill.

How often does Bank of America increase credit limits?

Bank of America does not offer guaranteed increases in credit limits, and there are no specific eligibility requirements. However, the bank periodically reviews accounts demonstrating excellent credit habits and may proactively increase credit limits. You can also request a credit line increase but try to wait for 12 months after opening the card and six months between requests to maximize the likelihood of approval.

What credit bureau does Bank of America use?

Bank of America reports information to all three major credit bureaus: TransUnion, Equifax and Experian. For eligible customers using consumer credit cards, the company also offers a free monthly FICO® Score from TransUnion.

What credit score is needed for a Bank of America credit card?

For many Bank of America credit cards, you should have a credit score of at least 670 or higher (FICO Score range of good to exceptional) if you want to maximize the chances of your application being approved. Bank of America offers cards for those building credit—like the Bank of America® Customized Cash Rewards Secured Credit Card*. In these cases, the company will accept a lower credit score but will typically require a security deposit upfront. The deposit is refundable upon account closure but may also be refundable upon an upgrade to an unsecured card once better credit has been established.
