Financials Up on Signs of Equity Meltup -- Financials Roundup

Published: 2023-12-19 18:23:03 ET BAC

Shares of banks and other financial institutions rose amid signs of a meltup in global equity markets.

Equities saw their fourth largest weekly inflow since 2008 last week, according to a survey of Bank of America Securities clients, as the Federal Reserve's "pivot" was viewed as a green light to investors in both funds and single stocks.

Accounting firm Accenture FactSet Research Systems shares fell after the financial information application cut its fiscal-year outlook.

UBS shares rose by more than 5% after investment firm Cevian unveiled a $1.31 billion stake in the Swiss-banking giant, saying the recent salvage of Credit Suisse was a coup for UBS and could generate more dynamic results.

Shares of Affirm Holdings soared toward the highest prices seen in nearly two years after the provider of buy-now-pay-later payment options expanded its service contract with discount retail giant Walmart to include self-checkout purchases.
