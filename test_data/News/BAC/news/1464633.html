If you aren’t a current Bank of America, Member FDIC, customer or if you’re in the market for a new credit card, now is the time to snag some bonus cash by checking out the best Bank of America promotions and offers. Like many banks, Bank of America regularly pays bonuses to attract new customers. All you have to do is open a new Bank of America account and meet the requirements.

Bank of America Promotions for November 2024

Banks often offer promotions to attract new customers. Promotions can range from $100 bonuses to $300 bonuses or more. These offers made the list because they’re lucrative promotions with requirements that are easily fulfillable by most people. Note that in order to get these Bank of America new account bonuses, you can’t have an existing account of the same kind. For example, you can’t immediately open the same credit card twice to score a second bonus. Here’s a look at the Bank of America checking account offers currently available.

How To Qualify for Bank of America’s Promotions

To qualify for Bank of America’s promotions, start by choosing the account or credit card that fits your needs, as bonuses are often tied to specific products. For most checking and business accounts, you’ll need to make a minimum opening deposit and set up direct deposits that meet the bank’s requirements. For credit card offers, meet the minimum spending limit within the first 90 days of opening.

Note that bonuses are available for new bank accounts only, and existing customers may not qualify if they already hold the same type of account. Keep an eye on the fine print for each promotion to ensure you meet all conditions to receive your bonus.

Bank of America Account Promos: Pros and Cons

Final Take To GO

Bank of America offers a full lineup of banking products and services with branches across the country. It may be worth checking out if you like the convenience offered by a large, national bank. Make sure it meets your needs and explore the bank’s offerings, including savings accounts and CD accounts before opening yours.

Cash bonuses are great perks, but they shouldn’t be the only reason you choose a bank. Bank of America accounts can come with hefty maintenance fees. Over time, they can cost you more than you earn through a bonus, so you’ll want to be aware of how to avoid the bank’s monthly fees.

FAQ

Here's more about Bank of America promotions.
  • Does Bank of America have a bonus?
    • Yes, Bank of America currently has bonuses for signing up for some of the rewards credit cards it offers. Also, the bank is currently offering a $300 bonus for eligible customers who create a checking account and complete qualifying activities.
  • How do I get a $100 bonus from Bank of America?
    • The $100 bonus offer from Bank of America expired in August 2023. It was for opening a Bank of America Advantage Banking account. Bank of America is often running new account promotions throughout the year which you can get for signing up and maintaining a minimum daily balance so be sure to check the website for all updated promotions. It's possible the Bank of America $100 bonus will return in the future.
  • Is there a Bank of America $600 offer?
    • As of writing, Bank of America doesn't have a $600 bank bonus available. However, that could change in the future.
  • How to get $200 bonus from Bank of America?
    • Bank of America currently offers eligible new customers a $300 cash bonus when they open a checking account. To get the Bank of America checking bonus, you'll need to complete qualifying activities after opening your account.
