Street Wrap: Today's Top 15 Upgrades, Downgrades, Initiations
Fly Intel
Jessica de Sa-Mota - email
Bank of America upgrade, Beyond Meat downgrade and Unity Software initiation among today's top calls on Wall Street

Institutional investors and professional traders rely on The Fly to learn which companies the best analysts on Wall Street are saying to buy and sell.

Research analysts at Wall Street's largest banks issue recommendations on whether a stock should be bought, held, or sold. The Fly's team of financial market experts scours hundreds of research notes daily to uncover the best trading ideas. Check out today's top analyst calls from around Wall Street, compiled by The Fly.

Top 5 Upgrades:

  • Morgan Stanley analyst Betsy Graseck upgraded Bank of America (BAC) to Equal Weight from Underweight with a price target of $49, down from $51. The analyst cited its higher quality loan portfolio and above average sensitivity to higher rates.
  • JPMorgan analyst Tami Zakaria upgraded Deere(DE) to Neutral from Underweight with a price target of $440, up from $355. The analyst views the stock's risk/reward as evenly balanced at the current valuation.
  • Morgan Stanley analyst Betsy Graseck upgraded BNY Mellon (BK) to Equal Weight from Underweight with a price target of $58, down from $65. The analyst noted the bank is one of the biggest beneficiaries of a faster rate hike cycle as it will be able to more quickly recoup $1B-plus of annual money market fee waivers.
  • JPMorgan analyst Tami Zakaria upgraded Illinois Tool Works (ITW) to Overweight from Neutral with a price target of $255, down from $262. The analyst views stock's risk/reward as tilted more to the upside at the current valuation, and believes the company's incremental margins should outperform as volumes pick back up.
  • Benchmark analyst Bruce Jackson upgraded Staar Surgical (STAA) to Buy from Hold with a $97 price target after the company announced U.S. FDA approval of its EVO implantable lens for vision correction and said it plans to begin commercialization immediately.


Top 5 Downgrades:

  • Piper Sandler analyst Michael Lavery downgraded Beyond Meat (BYND) to Underweight from Neutral with a price target of $29, down from $50. The analyst remains bearish on the company's key fundamentals, saying competitive intensity is growing, including a launch by Motif Foodworks.
  • Cowen analyst John Kernan downgraded Foot Locker (FL) to Market Perform from Outperform with a price target of $34, down from $42. Despite what he calls a "cheap valuation," Kernan is moving to the sidelines as he is more cautious following inbound data into March regarding digital traffic and search trends.
  • Bank of America analyst Bryan Spillane downgraded Honest Company(HNST) to Underperform from Neutral with a price target of $5, down from $10. The analyst cited the company's weaker than expected fourth quarter revenue amid consumers' faster than expected shift out of e-commerce.
  • RBC Capital analyst Nik Modi downgraded Altria Group (MO) to Sector Perform from Outperform with an unchanged price target of $53. The stock's strong outperformance since December leaves limited room for further upside, Modi told investors in a research note. The analyst also downgraded Campbell Soup (CPB) to Sector Perform from Outperform with a price target of $46, down from $47.
  • Wolfe Research analyst Truman Patterson downgraded KB Home (KBH) to Peer Perform from Outperform and removed his prior $50 price target on the shares. The company missed the final six weeks of the quarter's expected closings by nearly 20%, despite guidance being given midway through the quarter, and closings have come in below or at the bottom of the guided range the past five quarters, Patterson noted.


Top 5 Coverage Initiations:

  • Daiwa analyst Jonathan Kees initiated coverage of Unity Software (U) with an Outperform rating and $110 price target. While Unity's competition "remains quite strong," the addressable market is large and growing with much room for existing names, Kees told investors in a research note.
  • Daiwa analyst Jonathan Kees initiated coverage of Roblox(RBLX) with an Outperform rating and $56 price target. The analyst sees the mobile gaming market growing early teens and the young metaverse market with a small base growing nearly triple digits, and notes the company "has a lock on hard-to-reach users" under age 13 and differentiators including 11M developers and speed and ease of use.
  • Wells Fargo analyst Vik Chopra initiated coverage of Globus Medical(GMED) with an Overweight rating and $80 price target. The analyst cited Globus Medical's consistent mid-30s EBITDA margin profile, consistent above-market spine growth and share gains, and its position as one of only two companies to offer a spine robot, and now a new navigation system in Spine, which he expects will drive 150 bps of growth in 2023.
  • Credit Suisse analyst Timothy Chiodo initiated coverage of Toast(TOST) with a Neutral rating and $22 price target. Toast has one of the best business models in in payments, processors and FinTech space, but its current valuation adequately reflects expectations for meaningful longer-term share gains within its current core total addressable market and a degree of success in market expansion, Chiodo told investors in a research note.
  • Wells Fargo analyst Vik Chopra initiated coverage of NuVasive(NUVA) with an Overweight rating and $58 price target. The analyst bases his rating on Simplify becoming the cervical total disc replacement market leader over time, new product launches contributing to half of the growth in 2022, and potential upside from previously deferred spine procedures.
