Bank of America Raises 3Q Dividend to 22 Cents per Share

Published: 2022-06-27 17:08:01 ET BAC

By Kathryn Hardison

Bank of America Corp. plans to increase its quarterly dividend to 22 cents per share starting in the third quarter.

The company said Monday that the 5% increase is subject to approval from the board of directors.

Shares were trading at $32.38 after market close on Monday.
