Money-saving promotions offered by Amex, Bank of America, Capital One, Chase, Citi and Discover.

In addition to special welcome bonuses, credit card issuers frequently run amplified rewards rates and new promotions that allow cardholders to maximize benefits — but only for a limited time. These offers can allow you to more rewards than you'd typically earn on a purchase or take advantage of new money-saving perks.

With thousands of credit cards available, it can be hard to keep track of all these promotions, especially if you have multiple credit cards.

CNBC Select tracked down the best limited-time promotions currently offered.

Best credit card bonus offers and limited-time promotions

  • American Express
  • Bank of America
  • Chase
  • Citi
  • Discover
  • Capital One

American Express offers and promotions

  • Exclusive limited-time discounts at dining, retail and travel merchants with Amex Offers. Current Amex Offers include discounts with JetBlue, Delta, Marriott Bonvoy, Best Buy and GoPuff. You have to opt-in to take advantage of the offers. (Enrollment required.)
  • Check out Amex refer-a-friend offers where you can earn bonus points, miles or cash back for referring a friend to an American Express card.
  • Get access to exclusive entertainment events, ticket presales and preferred seating for concerts, sporting events and more in select cities.(Enrollment required.)
  • The majority of Amex cards offer a complimentary ShopRunner membership for free two-day shipping and free returns at over 100 stores. (Enrollment required.)
