Mastercard Blocks Financial Institutions Amid Tighter Sanctions on Russia

Published: 2022-03-01 03:14:04 ET MA

By Anniek Bao

Mastercard Inc. said it has blocked multiple financial institutions from its payment network after the U.S., European Union and other Western allies brought on more financial sanctions against Russia.

"We will continue to work with regulators in the days ahead to abide fully by our compliance obligations as they evolve," the company said late Monday.

The U.S. and key allies said over the weekend that they would hinder Russia's central bank from using its foreign reserves and exclude a number of Russian banks from the international Swift payment network, among other measures.

Mastercard said its Cyber & Intelligence and Corporate Security teams were working with governments and partners to ensure the stability, integrity and resiliency of its systems, which were operating as normal.

Separately, Visa Inc. said in a statement Tuesday that it is taking action to ensure compliance with applicable sanctions and will comply with additional sanctions that may be implemented.
