NovoPayment partners with Mastercard to provide Mexican payments

Published: 2024-02-08 09:11:10 ET MA

NovoPayment, a payments infrastructure provider, received certification for the Mastercard Network Enablement Partner program to process Mastercard transactions in Mexico. With this partnership, NovoPayment can test its products and solutions and increase speed to market, according to a press release.

"Our team has a long, successful track record of supporting clients across Latin America. We've worked in Mexico since 2009 and have a deep understanding of the market," Angelique Strauss, chief growth officer of NovoPayment, said in the release. "With this certification to process Mastercard transactions in Mexico, NovoPayment continues to execute on our multi-rail payments strategy to support our clients' ongoing demands, in addition to offering greater choice and flexibility."

NovoPayment also has certifications in Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and Venezuela.
