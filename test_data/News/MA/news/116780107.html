Here's What You Missed in Crypto This Week
Fly Intel
Kelly Carroll - email
MasterCard introduces Crypto Source program as Marathon enters settlement deal with former CEO

As bitcoin, ethereum and other cryptocurrencies get increasing attention from investors, Wall Street and its traditional banks continue to adjust to the shift. Catch up on this week's top stories highlighting the intersection of these old guard and new school areas of finance with this recap compiled by The Fly.

MASTERCARD INTRODUCES CRYPTO SOURCE: MasterCard (MA) introduced Crypto Source on Monday, a new program to enable financial institutions to bring secure crypto trading capabilities and services to their customers. In partnership with regulated and licensed crypto custody providers, Mastercard's financial institution partners will gain access to a comprehensive suite of buy, hold and sell services for select crypto assets, augmented with identity, cyber, security and advisory services. The company said, “This Crypto Source offering is complemented by Mastercard Crypto Secure to bring additional security to the crypto ecosystem and support card issuers in their compliance with complex regulations.”

MARATHON ENTERS SETTLEMENT DEAL WITH FORMER CEO: In a Monday regulatory filing, Marathon Digital (MARA) stated, “In connection with a dispute concerning the settlement of certain restricted stock unit awards previously granted to Merrick Okamoto, former CEO and Chairman of Marathon Digital Holdings, on October 12, 2022, the company entered into a settlement agreement with Mr. Okamoto, pursuant to which the company agreed to pay Mr. Okamoto $24M. Mr. Okamoto agreed to a settlement and a broad release of known or unknown claims against the company, which relate to the company's Amended 2018 Equity Incentive Plan or related restricted stock unit award agreements. The company entered into related settlement agreements in respect to certain restricted stock unit awards previously granted to five other individuals, including a director and our current CEO and Chairman, which total approximately $1M in the aggregate."

WISDOMTREE ENTERS COLLABORATION WITH STRIDE, GALILEO:WisdomTree Investments (WETF) announced Wednesdayits collaboration with Stride Bank and Galileo Financial Technologies, a subsidiary of SoFi (SOFI), as key banking and payments partners for its blockchain-native digital wallet, WisdomTree Prime. WisdomTree Prime, as made available by WisdomTree Digital Movement, will provide users with access to retail payments capabilities including demand deposit accounts, money movement capabilities and Visa-branded debit cards. The partnership with Stride Bank will allow users to attach a traditional bank account with traditional payment services, including a Visa debit card, to digital activities supported by a separate, unaffiliated custodian bank. The WisdomTree Prime Visa debit card will initially be available as a virtual card that can be used with Apple Pay, Google Pay and Samsung Pay, with a physical debit card to be released shortly after. In addition to Stride Bank, WisdomTree Digital is also partnering with Galileo Financial Technologies, a payment processor that powers fintechs and financial services and investment firms. WisdomTree Digital has connected these providers with its blockchain infrastructure, which is powered by Securrency and leverages Fireblocks MPC wallet technology.

COINBASE, PRIMER ANNOUNCE STRATEGIC INTEGRATION:Primer and Coinbase (COIN) announced Tuesday a strategic integration that allows merchants in markets across the globe to add crypto as a standard method of payment at checkout. Coinbase Commerce, Coinbase's platform to help merchants accept cryptocurrency payments, can be added to Primer's Universal Checkout with clicks, not code. "The payments landscape is evolving at a rapid pace, and consumers are expressing a tremendous amount of excitement for cryptopayments," said Roy Zhang, Group Product Manager, Coinbase Commerce. "Merchants are faced with the challenge of building seamless checkout experiences that offer consumers the flexibility they want. With Coinbase Commerce and Primer working together, any merchant can make secure cryptocurrency payments as readily available to their customers as traditional payment methods like credit cards."

SILVERGATE REPORTS Q3 EARNINGS:On Tuesday, Silvergate Capital (SI) reported third quarter earnings per share of $1.28, which compared to analyst EPS estimates of $1.38. The company also reported Q3 net interest margin of 2.31%, which compares to net interest margin of 1.26% for the same period last year. CEO Alan Lane commented, "Silvergate delivered another quarter of strong performance, achieving record net income available to common shareholders of $40.6M. While volumes on the Silvergate Exchange Network decreased this quarter compared to the overall industry, we remain confident in the power of our platform and the opportunities for expansion within the network. We continued to see demand for our SEN Leverage product and growth in our new customer pipeline, a testament to the strength of our platform against a challenging backdrop in the broader digital asset industry.”

Following the report, Wells Fargo analyst Jared Shaw lowered the firm's price target on Silvergate Capital to $50 from $70 and kept an Underweight rating on the shares. The analyst continues to believe these are early days of broader digital asset adoption, but the growth outlook for Silvergate as a pure-play crypto banking solution is significantly limited in the current environment. Silvergate is seeing volume reductions in its Silvergate Exchange Network, which is its genesis for growth and profitability, Shaw contended. While Silvergate is among the most asset-sensitive banks, balance sheet uncertainty from deposit outflows due to depressed cryptocurrency values more than offset the benefits from higher rates, he stated. Meanwhile, Wedbush analyst David Chiaverini lowered the firm's price target on Silvergate Capital to $85 from $120 and kept an Outperform rating on the shares. The analyst noted Silvergate reported a net negative quarter with a trifecta of disappointing items including larger than expected crypto deposit outflows of $1.4B versus his forecast which called for outflows of $0.5B, the use of higher-cost brokered deposits to shore up funding, which raised funding costs and is a pivot away from the bank's prior no-cost deposit strategy, and the delay in launching its stablecoin pilot to an unknown future date due to regulatory hurdles, compared to the previously expected timeline of launching prior to year-end.

Additionally, Canaccord analystJoseph Vafi lowered the firm's price target on Silvergate Capital to $150 from $200 and kept a Buy rating on the shares. The analyst said he believes relative to a very tough macro backdrop, Silvergate's Q3 was solid with EPS coming in ahead of his estimate although below the Street while noting the company doesn't provide guidance. Craig-Hallum analystGeorge Sutton also lowered the firm's price target on Silvergate Capital to $100 from $160 and kept a Buy rating on the shares. The analyst noted that on Tuesday, the stock was impacted by a combination of an earnings miss, a pushout of its key stablecoin project, and the reality that volumes and volatility are currently muted, and there is no signpost to suggest when it changes. The pushout of the stablecoin project is a function of a lack of regulatory and policymaker clarity, which aligns with nearly every crypto-related project he tracks, Sutton added. He is moderating his estimates and price target to reflect the current air pocket of limited volume, volatility, and unknown timing on the stablecoin project, although there is a firm belief that it will come to fruition.

CRYPTO STOCK PLAYS: Cryptocurrency revenues have been pointed to as reasons to be bullish on Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and Nvidia (NVDA) in select research. Ideanomics (IDEX), Riot Blockchain (RIOT), Overstock (OSTK), Pareteum (TEUM) and SRAX (SRAX) are other stocks that have been touted, or promoted themselves, as a way to play the crypto theme.

PRICE ACTION: As of time of writing, bitcoin dropped roughly 2% this week to $19,053 in U.S. dollars, according to TradeBlock.
