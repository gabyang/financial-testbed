‘The Masked Dancer’; ‘Death to 2020’; ‘Vikings’; New Year’s Eve shows: TV This Week

  • Updated: Dec. 27, 2020, 11:12 a.m.
  • |Published: Dec. 27, 2020, 11:10 a.m.
By
  • Kristi Turnquist | The Oregonian/OregonLive

Television

“The Masked Dancer”: Craig Robinson hosts the spinoff of “The Masked Singer,” featuring costumed celebs showing off their moves. Panelists include Ken Jeong, Paula Abdul, Brian Austin Green and Ashley Tisdale. (8 p.m. Sunday, Fox; after the NFL game)

“Shameless Hall of Shame”: In honor of the final season, this six-episode series features new and retrospective scenes featuring the show’s characters. (9 p.m. Sunday, Showtime)

“Laura Ingalls Wilder: Prairie to Page”: A new “American Masters” special explores the life and career of the writer who created the “Little House on the Prairie” books. (8 p.m. Tuesday, PBS)

“Dick Clark’s Primetime New Year’s Rockin’ Eve With Ryan Seacrest 2021”: By the time you get through saying the name of this show, it’ll be 2021. Ryan Seacrest, Lucy Hale and Billy Porter Host, from New York’s Times Square. (8 p.m. Thursday, ABC)

“New Year’s Eve Toast & Roast 2021”: Ken Jeong and Joel McHale host a farewell to a year nobody will miss. (8 p.m. Thursday, Fox)

Streaming

“Death to 2020”: Laurence Fishburne narrates a mockumentary from “Black Mirror” creators Charlie Brooker and Annabel Jones, which looks back on this miserable year. The impressive cast includes Hugh Grant, Kumail Nanjiani, Tracey Ullman, Lisa Kudrow, Leslie Jones and Joe Keery. (Available to stream beginning Sunday, Netflix)

“Best Leftovers Ever!”: A new show in which cooks “transform tired leftovers into tantalizing new dishes.” (Available to stream beginning Wednesday, Netflix)

“Vikings”: The final episodes of the series get an early debut on Amazon Prime Video, ahead of their airing on the History channel. (Available to stream beginning Wednesday, Amazon Prime Video)

“Chilling Adventures of Sabrina”: The series streams its final episodes. (Available to stream beginning Thursday, Netflix)

Already streaming

“Wonder Woman 1984”:In news that shook up showbiz, the big-budget sequel to “Wonder Woman” is premiering on a streaming service on the same day it plays in theaters (whatever theaters are open, that is). (Available to stream beginning Friday, HBO Max)
