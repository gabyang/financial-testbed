You know Netflix NFLX – and even if you don’t use the service, you likely know half a dozen people who do. The media streaming giant has made its mark in over 190 countries delivering necessary entertainment throughout the pandemic.

It should come as no surprise, then, that Netflix capitalized hard on the pandemic. Whereas business-oriented companies such as Microsoft MSFT made their profit from companies struggling to keep their employees on payroll, Netflix was used by…well, just about everyone. Whether you were stuck in your home office all day, furloughed, or laid off entirely, Netflix was – and is – likely one of your comforting solaces in these unusual times.

It follows then that Netflix’s stock has benefitted from the outbreak far more than other industries. The company started the year worth little more than $325 per share – a paltry amount compared to Tuesday night’s close. With the pandemic still sweeping the globe and the only remaining certainty being continual uncertainty, the company’s gains seem likely to increase further.

Netflix, Inc (NFLX)

Netflix, Inc ticked up 0.36% on Tuesday on the back of 5.4 million shares, continuing a strong start to the week at a final price of $490.80. This stock has had a slightly bumpy month throughout August, as Tuesday’s numbers show barely a blip over the 10- and 22-day prices averages (486.78 and $489.80, respectively).

Netflix has posted strong revenue since its conception. Even when the company wasn’t worth nearly a billion dollars, it climbed the ladder with unprecedented quickness and efficiency. Few could have predicted, though, that the company would grow to a total revenue of $20.2 billion in less than 25 years. This is a 12.3% increase over the past year alone, and a whopping 94% increase over its $11.7 billion revenue three years ago.

Operating income, too, has seen a significant boost of 44% in the last year. Even that pales in comparison to the absolutely astronomical 348% increase over the last year, bringing the company’s operating income from $838.7 million to a mammoth $2.6 billion.

EPS has grown right alongside the other metrics, up 43.4% in the last year. Once more, however, this is almost nothing against Netflix’s three-year increase from $1.25 to $4.13 – a 374% increase! And let’s not mention (or shall we?) Netflix’s skyrocketing ROE, currently sitting pretty at 29.1%.

As a result of all of these positive indicators, Netflix, Inc is trading with a forward 12-month P/E of 64.3. Furthermore, the company’s revenue is expected to grow by 8.6% over the next 12 months.
