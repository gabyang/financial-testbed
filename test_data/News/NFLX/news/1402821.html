From the time we have seen Selling Sunset's star Christine Quinn making a grand entrance in season 2, we have been wondering what her husband, Christian Richard, does for a living, and what would be his net worth? Well, for all you curious minds, we've done some digging and come up with answers. 

In season one, we don't see Christine romantically involved with anyone (at least on-camera). However, in the second season, viewers are surprised with a big announcement that she has not only found a special person, but she's engaged to him. The 31-year old says that she met her husband through a mutual friend and then quickly started dating him. Within a year, the two exchanged rings and bought a house together. In an on-camera confession, Christine says that her husband went to MIT and is now a retired software engineer. He's a big deal in the tech industry and has worked on "multiple development projects."

Looking at Christine's massive diamond engagement ring, we get an idea that her husband is definitely one of the uber-rich people in LA. No doubt! Good Housekeeping reports that the 41-year-old techy has a net worth of $20 million that is much more than any of the Selling Sunset realtors, (well except Jason Oppenheim who owns the firm and makes a million-dollar commission on every listing). Even Chrishell's ex-husband and the famous "This Is Us" star Justin Hartley has an alleged net worth of around $7 million. Mary's 26-year-old husband, who works as a model and project manager, has a net worth between $1 million to $5 million. Well, if we only consider the income, the blonde bombshell made the smartest decision because Christian is a real good catch. (dollar eyes)

✕ Remove Ads

But, keeping financials aside, he seems head over heels in love with his super gorgeous wife. The two often travel the world together and compliment each other's lives. Although, that's another thing that we don't see Christine's husband on her IG grid. During their engagement party in the latest season, Christian gave a lovely speech and told viewers how he met his (then) fiancee and fell for her. He said, "We had an instant banter, which is really special. You don't get that too often. And I pursued her; she's a tough catch. But here we are now. She's amazing, she compliments me perfectly, we're building an amazing life together, and I love you so much." Even though Christian looked a little uncomfortable on the camera, he made our hearts melt with his words.

Well, we're eager to watch their one-of-a-kind wedding in season 3. According to the reports, the couple got married last December in an ultra-goth wedding that was completed with black swans. The fashionable diva ditched the usual white gown and made a statement by wearing a black lace gown. Ooh la la!
