Everyone Makes It in ‘Hollywood’

Although a little far-fetched, it’s refreshingly encouraging for starving artists

Contains spoilers for Hollywood.

Many people are obsessed with Hollywood. They want to live in a place where even the most outrageous dreams can come true. But this city beats the hell out of you — up, down, and sideways. People come to L.A. with a suitcase full of high hopes and big dreams, but too many angels leave dragging their broken wings in the dust.

People love Hollywood because it has the power of turning a nobody into a star. With the right combination of talent, luck, and good timing, one can go from being on the verge of eviction to thriving in the Hills. We come to this town to make it, lured in by the lights and the magic. We stay for the possibilities and rarely leave, even when faced with complete failure; we continue to cling to the illusion of hope.

Based in post-World War II Hollywood, Netflix’s new dramais a TV show for people who have big dreams and are tired of loved ones coming down on them. Just like in real life, everyone in Hollywood is trying to make it in L.A. Given the setting, many of the characters are less than privileged, to say the least, but all have a common goal — to make a movie, at all costs.
