The start of each new month is an exciting time for streaming services: it’s when we get a brand new slate of films to watch from the comforts of our homes.

Unfortunately, the opposite also becomes true with each passing month: we lose a number of great movies we didn’t have time to watch.

To be exact, we’re losing 96 movies on May 1 from the three biggest streaming giants—Netflix, Hulu, and HBO. After this weekend, a multitude of films will be disappearing from our digital libraries. From heralded Hollywood classics to adored cult flicks to modern masterpieces, soon a plethora of films will only be available to rent or buy in the streaming realm.

So which movies will no longer be available this time next week? Here’s a list of 96 films that will soon no longer be available on Netflix, Hulu or HBO (with links included). That gives you a little time to find some new great films—or revisit some of your favorites—before it’s too late.
