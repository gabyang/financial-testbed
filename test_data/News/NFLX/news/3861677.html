Wedding Crashers 2 May Follow Vaughn & Wilson’s Characters In Their Late 40s

Wedding Crashers 2 may follow Vince Vaughn and Owen Wilson’s characters in their ‘40s. Released in 2005, the original Vaughn/Wilson vehicle concerned a pair of single friends who made a sport of crashing weddings and trying to hook up with women, until finally finding true love. Directed by David Dobkin, the film was a huge comedy hit with $285 million grossed at the worldwide box office.

✕ Remove Ads

Fifteen years after Vaughn and Wilson starred in that box office smash, both actors find themselves far from the top of the Hollywood heap (while their Wedding Crashers co-star Bradley Cooper has gone on to have a massive career). Dobkin meanwhile has also seen his career fall on hard times after his last feature film, 2014’s The Judge, bombed with critics and crashed at the box office. Dobkin however is plotting a comeback of a sort with this year’s Eurovision Song Contest: The Story of Fire Saga, a Netflix release starring Will Ferrell and Rachel McAdams as Icelandic singers.

Related: Chinese Zodiac: 5 Rom-Coms Horses Will Love (& 5 They Will Hate)

Naturally, Dobkin is frequently asked about the possibility of a Wedding Crashers sequel, and he again addressed the issue during an interview with Collider to promote Eurovision Song Contest. Unfortunately, the director had no solid updates to give about a possible Wedding Crashers 2, saying only that he’s turned down all prior offers to make a sequel. However, Dobkin did float an idea he’s toyed with that he thinks could work. He said:

✕ Remove Ads

“10 years later, when I was asked again and I hung up the phone after saying no, I thought about it and I’m like, ‘Well I’d be curious what it’d be like for guys in their late 40s who end up being single again and have to go back out in the world. What a weird, difficult, challenging story that is.’ And as long as there’s a real story in the middle of it, to me, it can be a movie.”

As fans remember, the original Wedding Crashers ended with both lead characters putting aside their womanizing ways for the sake of love after finding their perfect partners in Isla Fisher and McAdams. However, Dobkin’s idea would see both leads ending up single again and having to go back out on the market, but this time as much older men (Vaughn and Wilson are actually both in their fifties now).

✕ Remove Ads

In fact, such delayed sequels revisiting characters at later stages in their lives are becoming rather popular in Hollywood. Earlier this year, middle-aged Will Smith and Martin Lawrence had a surprise smash with Bad Boys for Life, the first Bad Boys movie since 2003. Later this year, middle-aged Alex Winter and Keanu Reeves will return as their iconic characters Bill and Ted for the first time since 1991 in Bill and Ted Face the Music. And in the biggest delayed-sequel of all, a near-elderly Tom Cruise returns as Maverick in the follow up to 1986’s Top Gun.

If those other delayed sequel ideas can work, perhaps Wedding Crashers 2 can rediscover the comedy magic with Vaughn and Wilson, in a story that makes a virtue of their being middle-aged and even explores the travails of finding romance past forty (or fifty). Now that Dobkin has established a relationship with Netflix via Eurovision Song Contest, perhaps his Wedding Crashers sequel idea should be his next pitch to the streamer (who indeed seem willing to go with almost any comedy movie idea, no matter how bad).
