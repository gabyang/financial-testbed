Netflix's The English Game's filming locations from across Yorkshire, how to watch and more

Downton Abbey creator Julian Fellowes has devised the new Netflix drama

    The English Game is the new Netflix drama series which charts football’s rise in the country. It comes from Julian Fellowes, the creator of Downton Abbey, and we’ve taken a look at the familiar Yorkshire locations featured in the programme.

    Starring Kevin Guthrie, Edward Holcroft and Charlotte Hope star in the series which has a particular focus on the class divide when football first came to prominence in England.

    Set in the 1880s, The English Game was added to Netflix on Friday, March 20 and you can stream it now.

    Holcroft is plays Arthur Kinnaird, an aristocrat footballer known as the First Lord of Football while Guthrie is Fergus Suter, considered the first professional footballer, who unlike the upper class players was paid to play the game.

    Other supporting roles are portrayed by Craig Parkinson, James Harkness and Gerald Kearns.

    The English Game filming locations

    The English Game filmed across Yorkshire with key locations being Broughton Hall, near Skipton, Little Germany, in Bradford, Saltaire, the Keighley and Worth Valley Railway and Sowerby Bridge, Calderdale.

    Screen Yorkshire spoke to Matt Bowden, The English Game’s location manager, about where the programme was filmed.

    He said: “Few regions demonstrate such a variety and wealth of manageable period locations as Yorkshire. Saltaire and Bradford provided the perfect backdrop for The English Game.

    “Filming such expansive period scenes can present many challenges to a location manager, yet the county’s amazing production support structure made the whole process not only possible, but hugely enjoyable. I am truly proud to see Yorkshire increasingly attract high quality period drama such as this, further cementing its reputation as one of the UK’s leading places to film.”

    The team at Broughton Hall Estate were delighted to feature in the drama.

    Roger Tempest, estate manager, said: “The Broughton Hall Estate has been host to so many films and TV series throughout the decades. Filming for The English Game took place in the main hall, the wider estate offers beautiful landscapes and many period properties which we have lovingly restored.

    “We are very proud to have been involved with the production, the crew were fun, considerate and professional and have produced a fantastic result.”
