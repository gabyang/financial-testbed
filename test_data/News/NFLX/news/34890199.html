What's New On Netflix: August 2020

Jamie Foxx & Joseph Gordon-Levitt star in the film "Project Power," while series like "Lucifer" and "Selling Sunset" return in new seasons.

By Beau Behan, Patch Staff
Jul 31, 2020 9:57 am PST

Summer is in full swing, which means temperatures are at an all-time high. Looking for an escape from the sizzling heat? Netflix has a plethora of new original shows, series and movies that are set to debut in the coming weeks — and the best part is you can enjoy them indoors.

Subscribe

This August, the streaming platform welcomes the premiere of the much-anticipated film, “Project Power,” an action-thriller about a mysterious, powerful pill that can unlock unpredictable superpowers with calamitous results. The film stars Jamie Foxx and Joseph Gordon-Levitt as a pair of unsung heroes.

When it comes to popular TV series, several are set to return with new seasons, including "Lucifer,” “Trinkets,” “The New Legends of Monkey,” “The Rain” and “Selling Sunset.”

Last but not least, a number of movie favorites will also be available for your viewing pleasure, including “Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind,” “Ocean’s Twelve,” “Ocean’s Thirteen,” “Les Misérables” and the entire collection of the “Jurassic Park” trilogy.
