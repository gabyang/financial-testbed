Keep the Faith: ‘The Believers’ Returns for Season 2 →

Published: 2024-05-09 06:20:26 ET NFLX

Entertainment

Responding to ardent viewer devotion, Netflix officially renewsThe Believers for a second season. Season 1 made a sensational debut, soaring straight to the top spot on the Netflix Thailand charts in its first week and sparking intense conversations across Thai society. An impactful campaign led by The Believers Ltd., the pioneering charitable company in the country, also helped to generate buzz for The Believersthroughout Thailand.

The series managed to spark a fervor for revered artifacts like the Pra Peung Luang amulet and turned the Phummaram Temple into a tourist attraction overnight. Alongside this phenomenon, the beloved character Monk Dol (Pup-Patchai Pakdeesusuk) has won over the hearts of audiences for months with his charisma and memorable quotes.

The Believerschallenged entrenched beliefs through a narrative that sheds light on Buddhist economics in Thailand. Led by talented young actors such as James-Teeradon Supapunpinyo, Peach-Pachara Chirathivat, Ally-Achiraya Nitibhon and more, the series boldly questioned the connection between faith and wealth with up-and-coming director Wattanapong Wongwan at the helm.

Get ready for the next chapter in The Believers Season 2, exclusively on Netflix!
