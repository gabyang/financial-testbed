Disney Is Getting Daredevil Back From Netflix Soon, But Will They Use Him?

One of the weirder sagas in the streaming wars was what happened with the mass execution of all Marvel’s Netflix shows over the past few years.

Before Disney Plus was a glimmer in Bob Iger’s eye, Netflix and Marvel joined forces to make a series of shows about The Defenders. That resulted in multiple seasons of Daredevil, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, The Punisherand a crossover Defendersmini-season. But eventually, one by one, regardless of quality or ratings, all the shows were killed off.

This was during a Netflix purge where they didn’t want to keep making expensive shows that they didn’t fully own. And Marvel didn’t really care to fight for these Netflix shows because they had always treated them like the black sheep of the family (they never once connected to the MCU in any meaningful way) and knew they were going to be building their own TV projects soon enough.

The catch with all this was that Netflix’s deal with Marvel reportedly contained a clause where Netflix would still retain the rights to these shows and charactersfor two years after the shows finished airing. Meaning Marvel couldn’t starting making their own Defenders character series in any capacity until then.

Well, now that stipulation is about to expire. Daredevilwas cancelled back in November of 2018 and we’ve arrived in 2020 with November just 6-7 months away. And here’s what Marvel had to say the day Daredevilwas cancelled:

“Marvel is extremely grateful to the huge audience that loved Marvel’s Daredevil. From the moment of young Matt’s first act of heroism to the birth of Page, Murdock, & Nelson, it has been a unbelievable journey. We are incredibly proud of the amazing showrunners and writers starting with Drew Goddard and Steven DeKnight, Marco Ramirez and Doug Petrie and Erik Oleson, Charlie Cox, Deborah Ann Woll, Elden Henson, Vincent D’Onofrio and our casts who brought our characters to life with such excellence, and every one of the fantastic crews in NYC. We look forward to more adventures with the Man without Fear in the future.”

High praise and a note about more Daredevil stories in the future.

But this Daredevil? That seems…very unlikely at this point.

Outside of the fact that the show was literally broken down and sold for parts (I remember them auctioning off the Daredevil suits after shutdown), it’s hard to see how Daredevil and the rest of the Defenders fit in with Marvel’s current plans.

Marvel already has an astonishing amount of TV planned for the MCU. It doesn’t start until this fall, but after that we have Falcon and the Winter Soldier, WandaVision, Loki, Hawkeye, She-Hulk, Ms. Marvel and Moon Knight. It’s hard to see where Daredevil and friends would fit into that.

Past that, if Daredevil did return, I cannot imagine we would see the kind of dark, brutal vision of the character we saw in the Netflix version on Disney Plus, which wants to be more family friendly. Daredevil, The Punisher, Jessica Jones all went to some dark places that it’s hard to imagine would work on Disney Plus, though Disney has indicated that perhaps harder edged stuff could move to Hulu.

But even if Marvel can technically start using Daredevil again in a few months once this Netflix deal expires, it’s just hard to see them actually doing it. The Defenders had a combined 13 seasons of content across six shows and it seems likely that Marvel will let them rest for a long while after that. And when they do return, it will not be with the same shows or actors, I would guess. But we’ll see.
