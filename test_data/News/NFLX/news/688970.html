                        Your Weekend Planner May 15: Paatal Lok se Aatmanirbhar Bharat tak

                        Great news! No, the lockdown isn't ending. But big, long-awaited movies are now releasing on OTT platforms. That, and more in this week's weekend planner.

                        Jaideep Ahlawat (L) in a still from Paatal Lok and a file photo of PM Modi (R).

                        New Delhi,UPDATED: May 16, 2020 07:26 IST

                        Dear Reader,

                        If you're also scratching your head over the heavy Hindi words that PM Narendra Modi's last address comprised, you have our empathy. But well, the catchphrase of the 33-minute speech was 'aatmanirbhar'. All of us have Googled, gone back to our grandmas and found out what the word means. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman also helped. So the point is, whatever you say, you will agree with us on one point: this virus has taught all of us to be aatmanirbhar. Some of us already were, but this is for the rest. Working from home while juggling cleaning, cooking and what not, we have all got our lessons in not depending on anyone.

                        advertisement

                        So keeping in tune with aatmanirbharta, we have several recipes from our Soul Kitchen series for you. If you're keen on shedding 'em calories you put on, be aatmanirbhar like Milind Soman and work out at home without depending on a gym. Your fashion is being taken care of with sweatpants and oversized T-shirts. There's nowhere to go anyway, but if you want to dress up at home to lift your mood a little, go back to Anushka Sharma's Bombay Velvet looks. We're reading Rumi this weekend. The Book Of Rumi is 105 stories in one place; quite the shot of positivity you need in these strange times. From Rumi's swarg, we will take you to Paatal Lok. Well, not us, Amazon Prime will.

                        Our review of the web series calls it a 'must-watch'. If you haven't already begun (or finished) Paatal Lok, the weekend is the time to binge. Amazon Prime has also opened up its own swarg of sorts with movie after movie skipping the theatre and releasing on this platform. We have Gulabo Sitabo coming up on June 12, and there's Shakuntala Devi too. But like every change is, this too is not without its hiccups. Theatre owners are up in arms against film producers and a full-blown fight seems just moments away. Well, what we cannot ignore is this change. This fact that this virus has changed the world as we knew it.

                        It will take a time to accept this new normal as the normal. But at least we will still have the weekends to ourselves. Hopefully!

                        Here's your Weekend Planner.

                        WHAT'S TRENDING

                        We know you are Aatmanirbhar enough to find out what's trending this week but still, here you go. Before Prime Minister Narendra Modi addressed the nation on Tuesday in what appeared to be a zabardast Hindi class of sorts, the internet speculated whether Lockdown 4.0 was the POA with memes and hilarious jokes. Well, their guessing game was unsurprisingly on point as the fourth phase of the nationwide coronavirus lockdown is indeed on the way. But "naye rang mein". Now whether you can go to office or visit your friend in whichever zone, is something that you will be able to figure out once the rules and regulations are announced before May 18.

                        #NarendraModi #Modi #PMModi
                        8 pm.. pic.twitter.com/Buw3V49TyS — Lokesh Ramekar (@LokeshRamekar) May 12, 2020

                        The highlight of PM Modi's speech though was the announcement of an Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan package of Rs 20 lakh crore. By the time people counted the zeroes in that figure, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman made an appearance in a press conference on Wednesday to elaborate on the details. By the way, if you are in the mood to shop, do invest in desi products as PM Modi wants you to be 'vocal for the local'. We couldn't agree more because small businesses are worst hit by the coronavirus crisis.

                        As far as our coronavirus gatha is concerned, it's the same old story. Just that the numbers are different. Higher, in fact. The number of cases in India crossed the 81,000-mark while the death toll surged past 2,600.

                        The Indian Railways did announce its decision of operating 15 special trains amid the lockdown but cancelled all regular train tickets booked for travel till June 30 on Thursday. Alas! What left us with a deep sense of regret were the disturbing visuals of migrants walking across states. Children sleeping on suitcases. Crowded trucks. It's tragic.

                        How much longer? How much more is the question but the fact of the matter is that coronavirus "may never go away" as per the World Health Organisation.

                        We won't really elaborate on this further here, so we'd rather leave you with a set of wildlife videos, especially of elephants, that netizens watched in an attempt to divert their mind slightly.

                        WHAT TO READ

                        The Book Of Rumi - 105 Stories and Fables that Illumine, Delight and Inform

                        If you are in the mood to introspect, pick up The Book Of Rumi - 105 Stories and Fables That Illumine, Delight and Inform. Translated by Maryam Mafi, this book provides vital life lessons with the most basic examples. The stories, based on the various mundane events of life, impart spiritual wisdom. It is a quick read and allows you to dig deep into your heart and soul.

                        KHAANE MEIN KYA HAI?

                        Try Prince Charles's recipe of Cheesy Baked Eggs.

                        Amid the nationwide lockdown, the process of preparing a dish has become one of the many things that keep us entertained. From our Soul Kitchen series, we bring to you the recipe of a sophisticated Middle Eastern salad - Tabbouleh, which is perfect for quarantine.

                        If salad is not your thing, try this mouthwatering recipe of Cheesy Baked Eggs shared by Prince Charles himself. This easy-to-make dish will be soul-satisfying.

                        While eating cheese and all that jazz is important, staying healthy is vital too. Celebrated dietitian Rujuta Diwekar, during an interview with India Today Television, explained why you should add ghee to your meals, eat homemade dishes and snacks, and consume medicinal drinks. Read her full interview here.

                        YOUR DOSE OF FASHION

                        Anushka Sharma in a still from Bombay Velvet.

                        Pyjamas, T-shirts and kaftans, especially for Kareena Kapoor Khan, are in at the moment because all you need to do is travel from the bedroom to the kitchen, or just around your house. So, that is the best deal for now. But if you intend to experiment with the retro style at the first party that you attend after all this is done with, you can take fashion inspo from Anushka Sharma's Bombay Velvet look. Frills and shrills, aur bohat zyaada bling. Red lipstick. Very old school but definitely impressive. Read our Fashion Friday here.

                        HUMARE PAAS CINEMA HAI

                        No matter the problem, cinema is the solution, perhaps the only solution at the moment.

                        Like when the novel coronavirus threatens to wipe off half of humanity, much like Thanos, you turn to good ol' Marvel Cinematic Universe to learn how the Avengers fought him. Start an Avengers marathon, we tell you what to watch where in this chronological guide.

                        If superheroes don't inspire you, how about stories of empowering women? Or perhaps some adrenaline-pumping sports dramas for regional cinema lovers? And if reality particularly sucks, escape into these 10 period-drama shows or rewatch old celebrity interviews.

                        Sometimes, real events are so bizarre that it will inspire cinema - like the assassination of Sri Lanka President inspired the climax of Shankar's Gentleman. In others, it will only hold a mirror to the society - like these Mrinal Sen movies, for example. But there's ample light content, too. Humpty Sharma Ki Dulhania, anyone?

                        FOR EVERYTHING ELSE, THERE'S OTT

                        WHAT'S ON AMAZON PRIME

                        What to watch: Paatal Lok

                        You will like it if you liked: Sacred Games

                        This gritty crime show brings forth the concept of heaven, hell and earth, and how they all co-exist in our world. The show stars Jaideep Ahlawat, Abhishek Banerjee, Gul Panag and Neeraj Kabi in lead roles. It is produced by Anushka Sharma's Clean Slate Films. It started streaming on Amazon Prime from May 15. Read our review here.

                        In exclusive conversations with IndiaToday.in, Jaideep Ahlawat, Abhishek Banerjee and Neeraj Kabi spoke in detail about their show.

                        What to watch: The Last Narc

                        Where to watch: Amazon Prime

                        You will like it if you liked: Narcos: Mexico

                        This four-part docu-series is about the 1985 murder and kidnapping of DEA (Drug Enforcement Administration) Agent Enrique Camarena, also known as Kiki. Actor Michael Pena played the role of Kiki in Netflix's series Narcos: Mexico. Now, Amazon Prime is bringing a whole new series, The Last Narc, dedicated to Kiki. It will start streaming from May 15.

                        WHAT'S ON NETFLIX

                        What to watch: Trial By Media

                        You will like it if you liked: Tiger King

                        If you are fond of crime docu-series, Netflix's new series, Trial By Media, is just for you. The show explores the most notorious criminal cases and how they were covered by media. The docu-series is about cases where news organisations declared culprits before any investigative authorities or courts could announce their judgment. The series helps understand how media forms perceptions. It started streaming on May 11.

                        What to watch: White Lines

                        You will like it if you liked: Money Heist

                        A new crime-drama series is available on Netflix from May 15. Laura Haddock plays the role of a librarian from Manchester, who flies to Ibiza when the body of her brother is discovered, 20 years after he disappeared mysteriously. The Netflix original is from Alex Pina, the creator of Money Heist.

                        What to watch: Schitt's Creek Season 6

                        You will like it if you liked: Modern Family

                        All the Schitt's Creek fans can rejoice as the final season of the popular sitcom is available on Netflix from May 13. Dive in to the world of Rose family and the new challenges they encounter in Season 6. The show stars Eugene Levy, Catherine O'Hara and Daniel Levy among others.

                        WHAT'S ON VOOT SELECT

                        What to watch: Illegal

                        You will like it if you liked: Suits

                        Starring Neha Sharma, Piyush Mishra, Akshay Oberoi and Kubbra Sait in pivotal roles, Illegal is a legal drama, full of twists and turns. The 10-episode series shows how truth is manipulated to suit the benefactors. The story revolves around passionate lawyer Niharika Singh (Neha Sharma), who has a past trauma and her set principles. If you are interested in watching something with several plots yet simple, Illegal should be on your watching list this weekend. The show released on May 12. Read our review here.

                        Check out what Piyush Mishra, Kubbra Sait and Akshay Oberoi had to say about their show.

                        WHAT'S ON DISNEY+HOTSTAR

                        What to watch: I Know This Much Is True

                        You will like it if you liked: Kapoor & Sons

                        This new series is based on the bestselling novel by Wally Lamb of the same name. It narrates the story of Dominick Birdsey, who discovers a hidden truth about his family while trying to take care of his twin brother, Thomas. The series stars Mark Ruffalo in the lead role.

                        WHAT'S ON ZEE5

                        What to watch: REJCTX 2

                        You will like it if you liked: Breathe

                        REJCTX is back with yet another season. The ZEE 5 original starts streaming from May 14 and stars Esha Gupta, Sumeet Vyas, Anisha Victor, Masi Wali and Ridhi Khakhar in pivotal roles. It is directed by Goldie Behl and takes us to the life of a group of youngsters, dealing with the challenges of life in their own way.

                        FILMS ON OTT

                        Seberg on May 15 and Doctor Sleep on May 17, both Amazon Prime. Malang on May 15 on Netflix.
