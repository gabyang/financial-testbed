EE launches impressive iPhone, Google and Samsung mobile deals alongside SIM-only savings

EE has dropped even bigger discounts on popular handsets and SIM-only deals with savings of up to £340

dailyrecord
  • Bookmark
  • Share
Lifestyle
By
dailyrecord.co.uk
  • 10:46, 22 JAN 2020
  • Bookmark

    • The biggest mobile phone providers across the UK are battling it out to win over bargain hunters in the January sales.

    EE has thrown the gauntlet down to Vodafone and is offering some fantastic savings on Apple, Samsung, Google and Sony devices to rival their 'Big Winter Sale', reports the Daily Express.

    The UK’s fastest 4G network is offering discounted contracts across much of its smartphone range, including a £120 saving on the Google Pixel 4.

    The tech giant’s latest handset is down to £44 per month with just a £10 upfront cost and a whopping 30GB of data, unlimited calls and texts.

    The offer includes EE’s standard benefits, including EU roaming, as well as £60 worth on in-game extras in Harry Potter: Wizards Unite ideal for those that are invested in Niantic’s app.

    The Samsung Galaxy S10+ comes complete with 128GB of internal storage for £49 per month with 4GB of data and includes a free Galaxy Watch Active worth £169 - making it an ideal choice for those upping their fitness game in 2020.

    Another great pay-monthly deal is the Sony Xperia 5, down to just £34 per month with 10GB of data - an incredible saving of £340.

    But it’s not just about pay monthly offers in the EE January sale, if you’re happy with your current phone, there are great SIM-only deals too.

    These offer the same benefits as pay monthly deals, with up to 60Mbps data speeds on 4G, unlimited calls and texts, plus the option of swappable benefits - including Amazon Prime Video, BT Sport, and data passes that ensure apps like Spotify or Netflix don’t eat into your data allowance.
