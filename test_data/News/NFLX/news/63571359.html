    Behind Her Eyes cast: Who is in the cast of Behind Her Eyes?

    BEHIND HER EYES is an upcoming psychological thriller expected to get hearts racing. So who is in the cast of the new Netflix series?

    By Hayley Anderson
    08:15, Tue, Feb 16, 2021 | UPDATED: 08:30, Tue, Feb 16, 2021
    Link copied Bookmark

    Behind Her Eyes: Netflix release psychological thriller trailer

    Behind Her Eyes has almost arrived with the season dropping all in one go on Wednesday, February 15. But who stars in the Netflix series and what have the actors been in previously? Here's everything you need to know about the cast of Behind Her Eyes.

    Who is in the cast of Behind Her Eyes?

    Louise - Simona Brown

    Actress Simona Brown plays Louise, a single mum who gets wrapped up in the dark relationship between husband and wife David (played by Tom Bateman) and Adele (Eve Hewson).

    Soon after starting up an affair with psychiatrist boss David, Louise becomes friends with his wife Adele but things get even more complicated as more secrets are revealed.

    Brown has been in numerous popular series such as BBC's Little Drummer Girl, ITV's HIM, The Night Manager and The Casual Vacancy.

    She is probably best recognised for starring in BBC drama Murdered By My Boyfriend.

    David - Tom Bateman

    Playing psychiatrist and Adele's unfaithful husband David is Tom Bateman.

    Bateman featured in an adaptation of Murder on the Orient Express starringSirKenneth Branagh.

    He will be returning to play Bouc in the sequel Death on the Nile.

    Bateman's other credits include Da Vinci's Demons, Cold Pursuit, Vanity Fair and Snatched.

    Behind Her Eyes: Adele is played by actress Eve Hewson. (Image: Netflix)

      Adele - Eve Hewson

      Adele is in a loveless marriage with David but to try and make him happy, she does what she is told.

      However, when she befriends Louise, Adele starts to feel like taking back control over her life.

      Adele is played by Irish actress Eve Hewson who was in BBC One's The Luminaries as Anna Wetherell.

      As well as the drama, she was in Taron Egerton and Jamie Foxx's 2018 Robin Hood, Bridge of Spies, and HBO's The Knick.

      Rob - Robert Aaramayo

      Rob is seen in flashback moments as he was a friend of Adele's when she spent time in a psychiatric hospital.

      He strikes up a friendship with Adele and is eager to maintain their bond in the outside world.

      Rob is portrayed by Robert Aaramayo who has been in Netflix's Mindhunter, Nocturnal Animals and appeared as a young Ned Stark in Game of Thrones.

      Aramayo is working on filming Amazon Prime's new Lord of the Rings TV series.

      Behind Her Eyes is based on the book of the same name written by author Sarah Pinborough.
      The novel came out in 2017 and Netflix picked it up for series one back in 2019.

      Behind Her Eyes will consist of six episodes, each one lasting around one hour long.

      Behind Her Eyes will be released on Wednesday, February 17, on Netflix.
