Unsolved Mysteries: What The FBI Could Learn From Alonzo Brooks's Exhumed Body

Netflix's Unsolved Mysteries has renewed public interest in the Alonzo Brooks case, which the FBI has since reopened; by exhuming Brooks's body, the new investigation may finally solve the mystery of the young man's death. The reboot series premiered on Netflix in July 2020, and since then, the long-running franchise has been the subject of much speculation online. Of the six mysteries featured in the first half of Unsolved Mysteriesseason 1, the Alonzo Brooks case is the most likely to be solved — particularly because it has the highest probability of witnesses coming forward. Given that the FBI has made the drastic move of exhuming Brooks's body, the investigation appears to be progressing — and may be that much closer to determining once and for all if Brooks was the victim of a hate crime.

Alonzo Brooks disappeared in 2004 after attending a house party in the rural area of La Cygne, Kansas. Brooks, who lived an hour away in Gardner, Kansas, was initially accompanied to the party by his friends; however, due to a communication breakdown, Brooks was left behind. The following morning, Brooks's mother discovered that her son never returned home and contacted his friends. They searched the property where the party had been held and discovered his shoes and hat by the side of the road. Fearing the worst, she contacted the police.

Despite extensive searches of the area by the police, Alonzo Brooks's body was not found for over a month, and was actually discovered by his family, who conducted their own search on May 1, 2004. Although the Medical Examiner ruled the cause of death as undetermined, the suspicious nature of his disappearance — not to mention the body's discovery — led the family to believe that Brooks was the victim of a hate crime. Now that the FBI has exhumed the body, its investigation may be able to shed some light on the inconsistencies revealed by the Unsolved Mysteriescoverage of the story.

There are numerous questions left unanswered about the night Alonzo Brooks died and why it took so long for his remains to be discovered. The coroner, Dr. Erik Mitchell, ruled the death as "undetermined," stating in the docuseries "I do not know his cause of death." Although Alonzo was found by a creek, there was no fluid in his lungs to indicate drowning; however, there were also no broken bones — which would be expected if Alonzo was jumped and beaten to death. Mitchell further indicated that it was impossible to determine strangulation due to the level of tissue decay. As shown in the Netflix docuseries, rumors have swirled for years in the community that Brooks was murdered and that his body was kept in a freezer, explaining why police were unable to locate his remains. Another theory is that Brooks drowned accidentally in another location, then his body floated down the creek to where it was discovered. Neither theory could be confirmed or ruled out by the autopsy.

✕ Remove Ads

Adding fuel to the fire of online speculation is the fact that the Medical Examiner involved in the case has previously been the subject of a misconduct investigation [via The New York Times], leading some reddit users to suspect incompetency — or worse, a coverup. Although 15 years have passed since Brooks's death, and much of the physical evidence is undoubtably gone, there is still potential for investigators to find new evidence for the case. A new investigation might uncover subtle signs of blunt force trauma in the skeletal remains overlooked by the previous examiner, confirming that Brooks was murdered — or evidence proving the death was accidental drowning.

✕ Remove Ads

The field of forensic science is always changing, and investigators today have access to tools and methods that didn't exist in 2004. Furthermore, the global attention this case has received, thanks to the Netflix series,means that current investigators likely have access to more resources than their counterparts did 15 years ago. That, coupled with the flood of tips Unsolved Mysteries has received about the case, means that the FBI could be close to a breakthrough.
