Selling Sunset 2 includes sparks still flying between Mary Fitzgerald and Jason Oppenheim. If there's one thing that's universally understood, it's that work and pleasure should never be mixed. But, by the looks of how things run at luxury real estate company The Oppenheim Group, exes can exist and even thrive in the same workplace. Setting the bar for former couples and co-workers all over, Jason and Mary look to have a fantastic friendship and work relationship. Anyone watching season 2 of the show can see that the chemistry between the two is truly undeniable.

Before delving into what exactly has been going on at The Oppenheim Group office, we'll advise a slight spoiler warning for anyone who's yet to watch the episodes. Jason and Mary's flirtations over season 2 are hard to miss, from dialing up the physical contact to almost getting giddy around one another like a pair of teenagers. Speaking to The Express, Jason acknowledged that he and Mary's relationship isn't the most conventional: "The truth is my three ex-girlfriends are three of the closest friends in my life right now, Mary being my best girl-friend."

The pair have a great connection, episode 3 of the new series even sees Jason exclaim: "I f**king love you, baby" after asking Mary what her favorite football team was (FYI it's the Washington Redskins - Jason's favorite team). Jason and Mary clearly have a long history, whereby she even can recall Jason's previous aspirations that he's now achieved including restoring a Ford Bronco. Mary said during season 2 episode 1: "Jason has wanted this Bronco for his whole life, basically. And he spent like $200,000 restoring it and getting it built."Taking BFF to the next level, Jason even offered his out his house to Mary as a wedding venue. Mary and Romain Bonnet were having trouble during season 2 when it came to wedding planning and who was there to lend a helping hand? Her ex, Jason, of course! Between the kind gestures, touchy-feely behavior and flirtatious comments, it's no wonder that Romain replied with a resounding"no" when asked if he'd like to marry at Jason's house.

✕ Remove Ads

While season 1 of Selling Sunset saw viewers introduced to Mary's now-husband Romain, there's a whole history prior to the show which involved Jason and Mary being in a relationship for four years and even living together. Mary explained during season 1 episode 1 how she met Romain when he arrived in California from France. While Romain is now signed to Wilhelmina Models, he previously worked as a pastry chef. The resident Oppenheim Group dogs, Zelda and Niko, are actually Mary and Jason's shared pets, adding another level of connection between the pair. However, to iron out any kind of uncomfortably around Mary and Jason's tight relationship, it seems that they didn't end on bad terms and because of this, they've remained close friends.

✕ Remove Ads

Many comments are made during season 2 in relation to both Oppenheim brother's relationship status' with many of the girls remarking that they don't like to commit. Jason's commitment issues have come up time and time again over both seasons 1 and 2 of Selling Sunset. Season 1 episode 8 saw Maya Vander say: "I don't think he can commit to a house long-term, I think he can only commit to one thing, that's the dogs." It's more than likely that these comments are all made in jest towards Jason and Brett. However, Mary was quick to ask if Jason had had many girls to stay in his new house in season 2. There's chemistry and then there's just plain nosiness - but that's none of our business...

The unusual dynamic between Mary and Jason also looks to pose a few issues for the rest of the real estate agents in the office with Christine Quinn piping up in season 2. While Davina Potratz negotiated with Jason over a $75,000,000 house in episode 3, Christine remarked: "If this was Mary's listing, he would be like 'yeah babe', 'get it babe', but that's just Mary, so..." Heather Rae Young also called out Jason on the office layout in Selling Sunset season 2 episode 2, stating that she's been at the company for five years but her desk is situated at the back. Jason replied, "It goes by how long I've known people, so my womb-mate, my ex, then my 20-year friend." Mary and Jason have known each other for 20 years, so at this point, rather than flirtatious friends, the pair could almost be like family to one another. Their chemistry is undeniable, and for anyone watching the show for the first time, Mary and Jason's interactions could induce the odd head scratch. But hey, this is 2020 and relationship norms aren't whatever we want them to be, right?
