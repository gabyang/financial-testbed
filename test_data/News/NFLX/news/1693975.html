Bad Boys for Life returns to the top of this week's film chart

The Top 40 also contains plenty of other new picks worthy of note

men
  • Bookmark
  • Share
What's On
By
Neil Bennett
  • 15:31, 3 JUN 2020
  • Bookmark

    • Bad Boys for Life and 1917 continue to trade blows in the battle to stay on top of the UK's film chart.

    The Number One spot has flip-flopped between the two now for the past month, with the 3rd installment of the Bad Boys series now selling 62,000 copies with 62% of those Blu-Ray and DVD.

    As well as taking back #1, a new box-set containing the previous two in the Will Smith-Martin Lawrence film franchise also enters into the Top 5.

    The other Top 10 newcomer is Tom Hanks is his 2019 role as Fred Rogers in A Beautiful Day in the Neighborhood.

    Elsewhere, the overall Top 40 has a handful of new and interesting picks including The Lighthouse, released late last year and produced and directed by Robert Eggers.

    It starred Willem Dafoe and Robert Pattinson, and chronicled two lighthouse keepers and their descent into madness when a storm leaves them stranded. The film falls just shy of the Top 10 in at #12.

    Snowpiercer, hot off director Bong Joon-ho's recent success with Parasite, comes in at #22. This is largely a result of the current Netflix TV series based off the 2013 Lionsgate film.

    Further down the list, Queen & Slim sneaks back into the Top 40 at #38 having previously peaked at the top of the charts upon it's earlier release.
