    Heather Rae net worth: How much is Selling Sunset star worth?

    HEATHER RAE YOUNG is one of the real estate agents on the hit Netflix series, Selling Sunset. What is Heather Rae's net worth?

    By Katie Palmer, Senior TV Reporter
    07:20, Mon, Jun 15, 2020 | UPDATED: 07:22, Mon, Jun 15, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    Selling Sunset: Christine Quinn on being the 'villain' of series

    Heather Rae Young is one of the main real estate agents on the reality TV seriesSelling Sunset on Netflix. She is one of the realtors at The Oppenheim Group, and she has plenty of experience selling luxury homes. Fans are keen to find out how much the Selling Sunset star is worth, and Express.co.uk has everything you need to know.

    Trending

      How much is Heather Rae worth?

      Heather Rae Young is from South California and she began her career as a professional model when she was 18.

      She is very business-focussed and quickly became an international star, later becoming a well-established realtor.

      Across her career as a real estate agent she has worked with celebrities, athletes and musicians who are all after fancy homes.

      The star is also passionate about fitness and health, as well as animal rights, and enjoys running with her three dogs.

      According to the website Screenrant, Heather Young has a net worth of $1million (£806,000).

      Some other news outlets have suggested the star is worth even more, with celebritynetworth.com quoting $3million (£2,395,000).

      Their website says her net worth was last updated in 2020, and there is plenty of information about her career.

      The website says: "She started modeling when she was 18 for Carrie Amber Lingerie, DOS eyewear, and others.

      "In February 2010 she was the Playboy Playmate of the Month. She's also worked as an actress."

      The star passed her real estate licensing exam aged 27 and within a few months she had sold a $1.5million (£1,197,000) condo and a $7.2 million house (£5,748,000).

      She joined the Oppenheim Group in 2014 and she has been in a relationship with Tarek El Moussa, star of HGTV's Flip or Flop and Flipping 101 with Tarek El Moussa since 2019.

      Celebrity Net Worth said: "The couple recently moved in together in Orange County. Heather maintains an apartment in Los Angeles as well to be closer to work.

      "For her 32nd birthday, el Moussa bought her a white hardtop Ferrari."

      The star opened up to People Magazine about being a Playboy model, and she said she does not regret posing nude.

      She said it was an amazing experience and she would never take it back, saying "everything happens for a reason".

      The real estate agent has been rubbing shoulders with some big names for a long time, and she has proved being successful in business is sometimes all about the people you know.

      She said her experience as a model taught her how to be confident in front of crowds, and she is not fazed by the spotlight.

      The star said real estate was a natural step for her due to the connections she made and her ability to build strong working relationships with others.

      Young said the Selling Sunset series was about "women's empowerment" and she hopes to see more women take on roles in the sector.
