New and existing Sky customers can now get Netflix for free

New and existing Sky customers can now get Netflix for free in their subscription - saving customers up to £468

birminghammail
  • Bookmark
  • Share
What's On
By
Neil Bennett
Katie Brooks
  • 18:39, 21 OCT 2019
  • Bookmark

    • Great news if you enjoy watching Netflix - Sky customers can now get the streaming service for free with their subscription.

    There are three new entertainment bundle deals available on the Sky website and they all include Netflix at no extra charge.

    The idea of the bundles is to join your separate streaming services into one single payment and with Netflix prices on the rise, the sum total of these bundle deals is said to be able to save customers a total of up to £468, reports Wales Online .

    The great news is that these bundles are available for new and existing Sky customers so no one has to miss out.

    Bundle 1 - Sky TV and Netflix

    Before these offers became available, a standard Sky Q subscription would cost £25 a month for 18 months.

    Now, the same  £25 a month  gets you everything you did before - including more than 300 Sky channels - plus Netflix entirely free of charge.

    For the duration of the 18 month contract, the current £8.99 Netflix cost removed from your monthly bank balance would save you £161.82.

    Bundle 2 - Sky TV, Sky Cinema and Netflix

    Along with all of the same Sky Entertainment Channels in Bundle 1, this Bundle Offer also gives you over 1,000 movies on demand - all in HD - with Sky Cinema.

    While Netflix remains a free added extra, the Sky Cinema channels cost an extra £10 a month taking the overall monthly bill total to  £35 a month  .

    The set-up cost for Bundles 1 & 2 is from £20.

    Bundle 3 - Fibre Broadband, Sky TV and Netflix

    If you're looking to change or upgrade your broadband, this offer includes unlimited fibre broadband that boasts speeds of up to 59 megabits per second.

    To put that number into context, 59Mbps is said to be good for streaming 4K TV, movies and music, gaming/hosting and running a small home office according to  this guide from cable.co.uk .

    This bundle costs £45 a month - that's a £25 discount off the usual monthly price of £71 and saves new customers a massive £468 over the course of their 18-month subscription.

    All three of the Sky Offers and Bundles are on the Sky website now for you to buy.
