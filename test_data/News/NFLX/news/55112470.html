This weekend, Netflix is bringing a psychological thriller, a new season of a crime series, a new documentary, and a psychological thriller drama series. While the coronavirus pandemic continues and theaters around the world are rethinking their plans as many studios keep delaying their releases, streaming services continue to be the go-to source of entertainment for many – and Netflix users can rest assured knowing that the streaming giant still has a lot of content to add every week.

✕ Remove Ads

Last weekend, Netflix added the sci-fi movie Midnight Special and the animated movie How To Train Your Dragon 2, along with some interesting original titles. The platform welcomed the coming-of-age drama Mignonnes/Cuties (which wasn’t safe from controversy), the documentary The Social Dilemma, the horror-comedy The Babysitter: Killer Queen, and the musical TV series Julie and the Phantoms. This weekend will see the addition of The Smurfs 2, Bastille Day/The Take, and all movies from the Japanese action franchise High & Low, including the spin-off High & Low The Worst.

Related: The Best Horror Movies On Netflix

As for original content, Netflix subscribers will get to enjoy a psychological thriller with a star-studded cast, the second season of a crime series, a new documentary series produced by J.J. Abrams, and Ryan Murphy’s latest project. Here are the best movies and TV shows coming to Netflix this weekend - September 18.

✕ Remove Ads

The Devil All The Time

Netflix's The Devil All The Time is a psychological thriller based on the novel of the same name by Donald Ray Pollock and directed by Antonio Campos. It follows the stories of the residents of Knockemstiff, Ohio, among those an unholy preacher (Robert Pattinson), a crooked sheriff (Sebastian Stan), and a twisted couple (Jason Clarke and Riley Keough). Their lives converge around Arvin Russel (Tom Holland), a young man fighting the evil forces that threaten him and his family. Also starring are Bill Skarsgård, Haley Bennett, Eliza Scanlen, and Mia Wasikowska.

✕ Remove Ads

Criminal: UK - season 2

Criminal: UK is back with a second season on Netflix this weekend, after the first episodes were released in September 2019. Season 2 follows the same premise as the first: set within the confines of a police interrogation room, interrogators engage in intense mind games with their suspects to find the answers they need to solve their cases (with a couple of twists here and there, of course). Criminal: UK season 2 arrives with a new cast of suspects: Sophie Okonedo, Kit Harington, Sharon Horgan, and Kunal Nayyar.

Challenger: The Final Flight

✕ Remove Ads

The newest addition to Netflix’s ever-expanding list of documentaries is Challenger: The Final Flight. Produced by J.J. Abrams, this four-part documentary takes a deep look at the 1986 Challenger space shuttle disaster with never-before-seen interviews and rare archival material, showing a different side of the diverse crew NASA assembled for that specific mission, which included a high school teacher that was selected to be the first private citizen in space.

Ratched

✕ Remove Ads

Netflix's Ratched is a psychological thriller drama series created by Ryan Murphy and starring Sarah Paulson. Based on the novel One Flew Over the Cuckoo’s Nest, the series tells the origin story of asylum nurse Mildred Ratched, who in 1947 arrives in Northern California seeking employment at a psychiatric hospital. What she wasn’t counting on was the hospital being the place where new and unsettling experiments on the human mind were taking place. Mildred presents herself as the perfect image of what a nurse should be, but as she starts to infiltrate the mental health care system, her inner, growing darkness begins to show.
