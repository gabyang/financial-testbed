What's streaming in August 2020: New films and series on Amazon Prime, Disney Plus, Netflix and Hayu this month

If boredom is starting to creep in during the school summer holidays, one solution is watch a good movie with the kids or unwind with a binge-worthy series after you've put them to bed.

And if you can't decide what streaming service is worth subscribing to, we've done the hard work for you by rounding up the best films and TV shows on Amazon Prime UK, Disney Plus UK, Netflix UK and Hayu this month.

Amazon Prime Video, from £5.99 per month or £79 per year

If you're not already an Amazon Prime member you can sign up for your free 30-day trial here. There are thousands of films and series to watch, plus new content is added every month. The last couple of months, Amazon Prime Video released some real gems, including Daniel Craig in Knives Out, the Michael B. Jordan-led drama Just Mercy, the James Baldwin documentary I am Not your Negro, and the debut biopic, Radioactive. 

Here are some of the top films and shows to look out for this month on Amazon Prime Video:

Scheduled for release on August 21, 2020, Chemical Hearts is an Amazon Original which sees high school senior and hopeless romantic Henry Page (Austin Abrams) fall in love with new transfer student Grace Town (Lili Reinhart), who hides a heartbreaking secret.

DC's Stargirl is an American superhero teen drama about high school student Courtney Whitmore, who inspires an unlikely group of young heroes to stop the villains of the past. DC's latest series is set to land on Prime Video UK on August 21, 2020.

Disney Plus, from £5.99 per month or £59.99 per year 

Disney+ features more than 500 films and more than 350 series (7,500 TV show episodes), plus 26 exclusive original shows and movies. These include films and TV shows from Disney, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, and National Geographic, as well as over 600 episodes of The Simpsons. New content, including Disney Original exclusives, is added monthly.

It might be hard to top July's lineup, which saw Frozen 2, Lin-Manuel Miranda's Hamilton, Beyonce's Black is King and Muppets Now being added to the streaming service, but August does have some great films and documentaries in store:

Howard is an original documentary film celebrating the life of academy award winning lyricist Howard Ashman, who worked on Disney classics such as Aladdin and Beauty and the Beast. The Disney Plus documentary premieres August 7, 2020.

Based on the award-winning book by Katherine Applegate, The One and Only Ivan tells the story of Ivan, a silverback gorilla who learns that life isn't defined by place and circumstance but by the power of friendship and the courage to make change happen. The One and Only Ivan is streaming only on Disney+ on August 14 and stars Angelina Jolie as Stella, Bryan Cranston as Mack, and Sam Rockwell as the voice of Ivan.

Phineas and Ferb The Movie: Candace Against the Universe premieres on August 28, 2020 on Disney Plus. It is an adventure story that tracks stepbrothers Phineas and Ferb as they set out across the galaxy to rescue their older sister Candace, who after being abducted by aliens, finds utopia in a far-off planet, free of pesky little brothers.

Netflix, from £5.99 per month

The widely popular streaming service is abundant with films and shows to keep families entertained during the summer holidays. New kid-friendly films being added this month include The Magic School Bus Rides Again Kids in Space and Super Monsters: The New Class. New releases on Netflix for the older audience include:

The Peanut Butter Falcon is a critically acclaimed 2019 film starring Shia Le Beoef, Dakota Johnson and Zachary Gottsagen. The heartwarming and feel-good comedy is about a man who has Down Syndrome and has run away to realise his wrestling dreams. On his adventure, he befriends a small time outlaw.

World's Most Wanted, the first series of the French true crime docu-series landed on Netflix yesterday (August 5, 2020). It investigates people suspected of heinous crimes who avoided capture.

Teenage Bounty Hunters is an all-new US teen drama set at an elite Southern high school which lands on Netflix on August 14, 2020. Twin sisters Sterling (Maddie Phillips) and Blair (Anjelica Bette Fellini) must balance their everyday life with their unlikely new career as bounty hunters.

John Was Trying to Contact Aliens premieres on August 20, 2020. The intimate, feel-good documentary is about a rural electronics whiz who broadcasts radio signals into space and monitors for extraterrestrial life. But rather than making contact with aliens, he makes a more important connection on earth.

Arriving on Netflix on August 26, 2020, Rising Phoenix is an inspiring sports documentary. Elite athletes and insiders reflect on the Paralympic Games and examine how they impact global awareness and understanding of disability, diversity and excellence.

Hayu, from £4.99 per month

The streaming service is home to some of the most popular reality on-demand shows, including Keeping Up With the Kardashians, The Real Housewives of Beverly Hills and much more. This month, Hayu is adding the following:

Season five of The Real Housewives of Potomac landed on Hayu on August 3, 2020. Episodes are shown weekly, every Monday. It sees new housewife Wendy Osefo join the returning wives Gizelle Bryant, Karen Huger, Ashley Darby, Robyn Dixon, Monique Samuels and Candiace Dillard Bassett.

Backyard Envy returned for a second season on August 5, 2020. Each episode continues to follow the team of Manscapers as they renovate their clients' outdoor spaces.

True Crime: License to Kill: Season 2 premieres on August 9, 2020 and is not an easy watch. The documentary seriestells the horrifyingstories of victims murdered by doctors and nurses.
