Sterling, De Bruyne, Gundogan - What Man City players have said during lockdown

A look at what Manchester City players have said about their routine while they have been away from the training ground

  • Share

    Manchester City players have, like other footballers in the UK, been adjusting to life training at home.

    It is not easy going from a routine built around training and playing matches as a squad before being allowed to do whatever you like in your free time to being confined in homes working individually to try to stay fit for the undefined date when the sport will return.

    The club have done what they can to help out with daily checks on players, meals sent to some and the right gym equipment delivered to whoever needs it to ensure that the players have as much as they could want while the lockdown continues.

    The players have also taken it upon themselves to do what feels right to them, though, depending on their individual circumstances.

    Here is what some of them have said about what they have been doing to help keep them healthy through the lockdown.

    Raheem Sterling, March 23

    "At the moment it's a bit rough but as long as you've got life you've got a blessing, you've got everything so life is good.

    "I'm keeping indoors at the moment which I strongly advise everyone to do. We're going through a difficult period at this moment in time so do your best to stay inside, make the most of being inside and try to do activities.

    "I try to stay fit by writing down stuff that I'm about to do tomorrow, don't wait for the day to come. I wake up in the morning and before I eat breakfast I go in the gym and do a workout, come back in ,eat breakfast then either me and Paige do some work with the kids, drawing or whatever and then hit the garden with the kids. I always try to plan things, especially while I've got no training, so plan my days ahead."

    Kevin De Bruyne, March 31

    “I told my wife I’m going to play a little longer.

    “After this lockdown, I cannot stay at home. I told her I’m going to take two years more.

    “It’s time to play football again. I miss it and it’s difficult. But we are not important, football is not important. People love football, but you need to stay safe.”

    Ilkay Gundogan, April 1

    "It's quite difficult staying all day at home, maybe just going out for a walk for half an hour. It makes everything a little bit tough especially when you don't have the choice to do something.

    "Obviously I try to keep myself fit which is the main thing I'm doing right now. We've got training plans from the club, individual plans which nearly every day has sessions whether its strength in the gym or cardio on the bike.

    "That's mainly what I'm doing and apart from that I try to watch some Netflix, I cook a little bit, recently I started to cook a bit healthier again - the first few days of quarantine were quite difficult I got a lot of sweets into myself it was frustrating but now I've started getting a rhythm again and I can't wait to get out of here and onto the pitch again."

    Kyle Walker, April 1

    “We have jumped on HouseParty a few times and kind of got the banter going. You have got the group chat that is always going off on some sort of madness. I think it is about mainly keeping the brain active.

    "I am kind of reliving my youth a little bit like when I was on the estate where you just had to fill your time. I have built little ramps for the kids in the drive. It is like a little assault course where they are in and out of the cones on their bikes, so we are trying to fill the time as much as we can.

    “[I listen to a lot of] music, I am always on iTunes or Spotify downloading music and seeing what is new and what is coming out. I am into Fortnite at the minute so that is taking up a lot of my time, probably more than it should be. I am reaping the benefits as I am actually getting half decent now, but I do need to come off it I can admit that! It is probably taking up a little bit too much time after I have put the kids down to bed.”
