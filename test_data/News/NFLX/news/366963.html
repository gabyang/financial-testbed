While things didn't work out for Carlton Morton on Love Is Blind, he's still looking for love in the time of coronavirus. Although he's best known for his blow-up fight with Diamond Jack soon after their engagement, Carlton is still hoping to find that special someone. Best of all, he's not letting a little lockdown get in the way of looking for love.

✕ Remove Ads

Carlton proposed to Diamond on the first season of Love Is Blind.Before asking Diamond to be his wife, Carlton didn't tell her that he is bisexual and had relationships with men in the past. When Carlton told Diamond the news, she was frustrated that he hadn't disclosed that information during their many pod dates. While discussing such a sensitive and personal topic, however, things quickly went downhill. Carlton accused Diamond of prejudice and their discussion devolved into a screaming match full of insults. Diamond threw her engagement ring into the pool and the couple went their separate ways.

Related: Netflix's Love Is Blind: 10 Changes We Want for Season 2

✕ Remove Ads

Although things were difficult for Carlton on the Netflix series, he hasn't given up on love. In a recent interview with Page Six, Carlton discussed the current status of his love life. First, he stated that he wasn't currently dating due to the global pandemic sweeping the nation. He assured fans that he's been social distancing, which has understandably taken on a toll on his search for love. He went on to say, "But, one thing about dating now, it's kind of like we're still in the pods because on Love Is Blind we had to date people without seeing them, so I guess it's no difference now. Hopefully, in coming weeks I can maybe virtually date some people."Carlton admitted that he's actively searching for someone and would love a buddy to cuddle up with during this year's holiday season.

✕ Remove Ads

Although Carlton is now looking for a brand new partner, he hadn't completely given up on Diamond by the time the Love Is Blindreunion rolled around. He apologized to his former fiancée for his treatment of her and even revealed that he dived into the pool to get her ring. Although he seemed opened to rekindling their relationship, Diamond didn't give him a clear answer. Since then, Carlton has revealed that Diamond blocked him after the reunion show. The pair appeared to patch things up, but Diamond clearly isn't over the explosive argument.

Coronavirus is altering all our lives and we don't have a clear idea of when these social distancing mandates will come to an end. With so much time on our hands and nowhere to go, virtual dating is becoming a more and more appealing possibility. If Carlton wants to find a cuddle buddy, it might be in his best interest to schedule a few Zoom calls with potential partners. Let's just hope this dating experience turns out a little better than the pods.
