RuPaul’s Drag Race season 12 is now behind us, which means that it’s time to move on to the highly-anticipated All Stars5. Ten fan-favorites have been cast on the fifth season of this successful spin-off, and they’re all ready to take their rightful place on the Drag RaceHall of Fame.

RELATED: RuPaul’s Drag Race: 10 Facts About The Show According To RuPaul

In preparation for RuPaul’s Drag Race All Stars5, let’s take a deep dive and learn more about these familiar faces. Read below to discover a few things you didn’t know about the All Stars5 cast!

Ongina

Diehard fans of RuPaul’s Drag Racehave been asking for season 1 contestant Ongina to compete on All Starssince the spin-off was first greenlit. It may have taken four seasons for this Drag RaceOG to get there, but she’s finally made it.

Ongina’s career started in New York City in 2003. Back in the day, the queen worked at Lucky Cheng’s – NYC’s longest-running drag cabaret venue – alongside a then-unknown Laverne Cox. According to Ongina, she was a server, and Laverne was a dancer. When it comes to being cast on season 1 of Drag Race, Ongina has revealed that she was very intimidated by those prospects because she didn’t consider herself “a drag queen.”

Shea Coulee

Many fans considered Shea Coulee the frontrunner of season 9 of RuPaul’s Drag Race. However, the crown changed heads when Sasha Velour revealed rose petals under her wig during the newly-introduced Lip Sync For The Crown format.

RELATED: RuPaul’s Drag Race: The Best Queens Who Never Won

Outside of drag, Shea Coulee identifies as non-binary and uses they/them pronouns. During an interview with The Advocate, Shea revealed that they plan on retiring by the time that they are 40 years old. However, before that, Shea intends on releasing an album and presenting a collection at Fashion Week, which means that fans are sure to see a lot more from this queen in the near future.

✕ Remove Ads

India Ferrah

Some viewers were surprised when season 3 contestant India Ferrah was cast on All Stars5. However, diehard Drag Racefans have seen this comeback being in the works for quite some time now.

In 2017, India Ferrah performed at the season 9 finale pre-show. Then, the queen was repeatedly featured on a variety of DragCon events. Finally, in 2020, India was cast in the RuPaul’s Drag Race Live residency in Las Vegas. What most fans do not know is that India Ferrah started doing drag as early as 12 years old. Back then, her also gay brother happened to date a drag queen, who put India in drag for the first time.

Derrick Barry

✕ Remove Ads

The world’s most famous Britney Spears impersonator wants to show the drag queen she has become since appearing on season 8 of RuPaul’s Drag Race.

Between season 8 and All Stars5, Derrick Barry has been hard at work on her two digital series on WOW Presents Plus: ASMR Queens, which she hosts with boyfriend Nebraska Thunderf**k, and Wait, What?, which she hosts with Kimora Blac. Given that Derrick is so connected to Britney Spears, it is certainly interesting to know that the queen’s favorite Britney song ever is “Toxic.”

Mariah Balenciaga

Mariah Balenciaga a.k.a. Mariah Paris a.k.a. Mariah Successful first competed on season 3 of RuPaul’s Drag Race, and she is now back for All Stars5. Many Drag Race fans may not realize, but Mariah Balenciaga is a ballroom queen through and through. During an interview with Werrrk in 2014, the queen mentioned her 14-year-old career in the ballroom scene, and how she was finally slowing down on competitions.

✕ Remove Ads

In 2020, the team behind the ballroom competition Legendaryon HBO Max acknowledged Mariah Balenciaga’s importance to the scene by sending her a kit full of gifts and merch.

Alexis Mateo

The Mateo family went through a renaissance when Alexis’ daughter, Vanessa Vanjie Mateo, was sent home first on season 10 of RuPaul’s Drag Race and became a worldwide viral sensation. Moreover, Alexis won the All American Goddess pageant in 2016, a title that had been previously won by India Ferrah (2008), Alyssa Edwards (2010), and Asia O’Hara (2012).

During an interview with Socialite Life, Alexis Mateo revealed that she was once an employee of the Walt Disney World Resort in Orlando, FL, where she worked for five years as a performer. The queen also referred to season 5 contestant Coco Montrese as a big source of inspiration, and stated that Beyoncé is one of her most inspirational idols.

✕ Remove Ads

Mayhem Miller

One of the most shocking things about Mayhem Miller is that the queen auditioned every year to be on RuPaul’s Drag Race starting all the way back on season 2. Mayhem finally got on the show on season 10, and has since developed a great relationship with World of Wonder, the production company behind this franchise.

Some fans may not realize, but Mayhem Miller considers Raven and Morgan McMichaels her drag sisters. Moreover, Mayhem is credited for helping Adore Delano when she was starting to do drag. In fact, Adore’s “Party!” catchphrase was actually created by Mayhem Miller.

Jujubee

✕ Remove Ads

Jujubee first competed on season 2 of RuPaul’s Drag Race, where she placed third. Then, Juju was cast on RuPaul’s Drag U and competed on the first-ever season of All Stars.

RELATED: RuPaul’s Drag Race: 15 Best Lip Sync Assassins, Ranked

There’s already been a lot of Jujubee on TV in 2020, and that’s all before All Stars5 even aired. For one, the queen was given a prominent role on Netflix’s AJ and the Queen. Moreover, Juju is one of the four queens featured on TLC’s drag makeover series Dragnificent. During an interview with The Standard, Jujubee revealed that she was 16 years old when she first did drag for Halloween. The queen also opened up about struggling with substance abuse, and how she’s been on a journey toward sobriety.

Blair St. Clair

✕ Remove Ads

Since Drag Raceseason 10, Blair St. Clair has been on an impressive “glow up” journey that has taken her drag from a young and romantic aesthetic to a more mature and empowered place. From the very first teaser of All Stars5, it became clear that fans would see a very different Blair this second time around.

Blair St. Clair revealed to Forbes in 2019 that she has been working on “a couple of movies,” as well as “some other TV shows, and working on a full-length album.” Fans may not realize it, but Blair started her drag career alongside Silky Nutmeg Ganache, who competed on season 11.

Miz Cracker

It was not entirely apparent on season 10, but Miz Cracker is actually Monet X Change’s drag niece. That is because Monet is the drag sister to Bob the Drag Queen who, in turn, is Cracker’s drag mother.

✕ Remove Ads

Even before her time on Drag Race, Miz Cracker was already a pretty established drag performer in New York City. Of note, season 9 winner Sasha Velour has stated that her only Manhattan gig in many years had been because Miz Cracker booked her. In 2020, Miz Cracker was set to headline her first-ever solo tour, American Woman, but the tour was unfortunately postponed due to the social distancing guidelines in response to COVID-19.
