Every Christmas & Holiday Movie On Netflix In November 2020

November has just begun, which means it's time to get excited about the holiday season! Although we're all in unusual circumstances at this time, there are plenty of pleasant offerings across the various streaming platforms to give you that Yuletide feeling. Look no further than Netflix to be merry.

RELATED: Christmas In July: 10 Great Holiday Movies To Stream Right Now On Netflix, Ranked According To IMDb

This month, the popular streaming service has tons of holiday-themed movies to explore. From Christmas favorites and sequels to romantic holiday flicks, there is no shortage of holiday movies that Netflix is offering to audiences of all ilks to make the season bright.

Christmas Break-In (2018) – November 1

For children who love Home Alone, Christmas Break-Inis a modern-day equivalent that has the same vibe. This movie centers on Izzy (Cameron Seely), a lively nine-year-old girl who gets trapped inside her school during a blizzard while waiting for her busy parents. Suddenly, two hoodlums infiltrate the school, prompting Izzy to save the day.

✕ Remove Ads

With Danny Glover and Denise Richards in the mix, this film will make for some Yuletide fun for the least demanding viewers.

Elliot The Littlest Reindeer (2018) – November 1

Animated movies are always a staple for the Christmas season. Here is one from Canada about a miniature horse named Elliot (Josh Hutcherson) who aspires to fill the spot left on Santa's team by Blitzen (John Cleese), who is on the verge of retirement.

RELATED: 10 Best Animated Christmas Movies According To IMDB

The eclectic voice cast includes Morena Baccarin, Samantha Bee, Jeff Dunham and Martin Short. And while not the best Christmas move out there, Elliot the Littlest Reindeer offers helpful lessons and colorful animation.

Midnight At The Magnolia (2020) – November 5

✕ Remove Ads

Netflix always offers plentiful doses of romance, and the holiday season is no different, including Midnight at the Magnolia. But this film is a fresh change of pace, as it centers around New Year’s Eve. The film is about two friends, Maggie (Natalie Hall) and Jack (Evan Williams), who run a local dating-themed radio show.

When they are booked for a New Year’s Eve countdown special, they decide to masquerade as having a romance for publicity. Hijinks and romance harmoniously ensue.

A New York Christmas Wedding (2020) – November 5

A refreshing twist for Hallmark, A New York Christmas Weddingrevolves around the life of Jennifer Ortiz (Nia Fairweather) as she prepares for her Christmas Eve wedding. But days before her dream wedding, she is visited by a guardian angel, who shows her an alternative life in which she expresses her feelings for a childhood best friend named Gabrielle.

✕ Remove Ads

RELATED: 10 Lifetime Movies That Were Based On A True Story, Ranked By How Accurate They Are

Filled with unexpected twists and sweet moments, including Chris Noth (who also produced the film), romance fans will surely love this one.

Jingle Jangle: A Christmas Journey (2020) – November 13

A musical Christmas adventure for the ages, Jingle Jangle: A Christmas Journey comes from producer John Legend and director David E. Talbert of Almost Christmas. This family film focuses on toymaker Jeronicus Jangle (Forest Whitaker) and his granddaughter, Journey (Madalen Mills), as they invent something that could make their dreams come true - if they can get it working on time.

RELATED: The 5 Best Christmas Movies On Netflix (& The 5 Worst)

✕ Remove Ads

Filled with songs penned by John Legend himself and with a cast that includes Keegan-Michael Key, Anika Noni Rose and Ricky Martin, this might be the perfect musical for the holidays.

Hometown Holiday (2018) – November 15

The holidays would not be complete without a cheesy romantic holiday movie. And one of Netflix’s offerings is Hometown Holiday. This film is about aspiring business owner Krista (Sarah Troyer), who is picky when it comes to her dates.

RELATED: 10 Things Fans Love About Hallmark Movies

After she moves back to her hometown, she falls in love with an entertainment lawyer named Ryan, who makes her face a career crossroads. Based on a Harlequin romance novel, the tender sweetness from the characters shines onscreen.

A Very Country Christmas (2017) – November 15

✕ Remove Ads

For those who loved Falling Inn Love, watch out for A Very Country Christmas. This romantic holiday feature is about a country music star named Zane (Greyston Holt), who decides to go back to his hometown and get away from his life of fame.

But rediscovering himself leads him to meet Jeanette (Bea Santos), who shows him what true love is all about. The title basically provides everything one needs to know: this is a family-friendly romance with a bit of country love.

Operation Christmas Drop (2020) – November 15

A Netflix Original romance, Operation Christmas Drop tells the tale of a striving Congressional aide (Kat Graham) who has to drop her travel plans with her family to assist her boss. During her work at an Air Force base, she butts heads with Captain Andrew Jants (Alexander Ludwig), but their constant clashing causes them to fall in love during the Christmas season.

✕ Remove Ads

This film will assure all hopeless romantics that miracles can happen in unexpected ways and sparks do fly during the holidays.

The Princess Switch: Switched Again (2020) – November 19th

Stacy and Duchess Margaret (Vanessa Hudgens) are back for more holiday hijinks. This time, Margaret finds herself at odds with Kevin, prompting newly crowned Stacy to help her save their relationship. But along the way, a new lookalike, a party girl named Fiona, comes to hinder their plans and cause another switch-up among the two.

The first Princess Switch won over fans who lauded Hudgens for making a convincing portrayal of two different ladies. Expect another family-friendly escapade this time.

Alien Xmas (2020) – November 20

✕ Remove Ads

Another animated film, but this one is a peculiar stop-motion feature. Alien Xmas is a Netflix Original about a horde of extraterrestrials who attempt to steal the Earth’s gravity in the middle of the Christmas season.

However, a small alien named X (Dee Bradley Baker) gets wind of the generosity that Christmas brings, making him a part of saving the world. Think of this movie as E.T. in a Christmas setting, but with a real otherworldly threat.

Dolly Parton’s Christmas On The Square (2020) – November 22

Country legend Dolly Parton never loses pace in producing tons of Christmas specials. For her next Netflix outing after Heartstrings, she features a story about a conceited woman, Regina Fuller (Christine Baranski), who goes back to her hometown to sell it, which has been suffering financially, to mall developers.

✕ Remove Ads

Parton finds herself in familiar territory, with a Scrooge-like character as a villain. However, with Christine Baranski in the film, it will certainly be a delight for viewers. Add to this Dolly’s music, and it is, undoubtedly, a treat.

The Christmas Chronicles: Part Two (2020) – November 25

Kurt Russell as Santa Claus is back in another Christmas Chronicles adventure. This time, a troublemaker named Belsnickel (Julian Dennison) intends to stop Christmas by stealing Santa’s source, the Christmas star. Thus, it is up to Santa, a pessimistic Kate Pierce (Darby Camp), a boy named Jack (Jahzir Bruno) and Mrs. Claus (Goldie Hawn) to stop him before Christmas.

The first film was a fun ride. But now with Chris Columbus as co-writer and director, The Christmas Chronicles: Part Two will be sure to be another spirited adventure.

✕ Remove Ads

Spookley And The Christmas Kittens (2019) – November 30

Spookley is a kind-hearted square-shaped pumpkin who sees the good in all the people he comes across. After starring in 2004's Spookley the Square Pumpkin, he now has his own Christmas-themed movie where he meets a green cat named Mistletoe, who got lost in the woods searching for his collar.

In response, the young pumpkin searches for Mistletoe and three other precocious kittens. Kids certainly already love Spookley for his compassion and willingness to always help his friends and putting him in a Christmas setting only adds to the fun.
