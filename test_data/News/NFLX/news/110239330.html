What You Missed On Wall Street On Friday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Apple (AAPL) reported upbeat Q2 results and raised its quarterly dividend, but said on its quarterly call that it is facing a $4B to $8B headwind from COVID shutdowns in China and silicon shortages [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) reported a quarterly loss, impacted by its investment in Rivian Automotive (RIVN), and provided worse-than-expected Q2 guidance [more]
  • Elon Musk sold over $8B of Tesla (TSLA) shares following his Twitter (TWTR) bid [more]
  • Intel (INTC) reported upbeat Q1 results and reaffirmed its FY22 revenue guidance [more]
  • Chevron (CVX) reported upbeat Q1 earnings and revenue while Exxon Mobil (XOM) reported downbeat quarterly results [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Accolade (ACCD) was downgraded by at least five Street firms after news that Accolade lost its "marquee customer," Comcast (CMCSA) [more]
  • MasterCard (MA) downgraded to Underweight from Neutral at Piper Sandler [more]
  • Comcast (CMCSA) downgraded to Neutral from Buy at BofA [more]
  • MoffettNathanson upgraded Roku (ROKU) to Neutral from Sell with a $100 price target [more]
  • BofA upgraded Fortune Brands (FBHS) to Neutral from Underperform after the company announced plans to spinoff its cabinets business, while KeyBanc downgraded the shares to Sector Weight from Overweight [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • China is planning to "pause" its campaign against domestic tech companies, WSJ reports [more]
  • A top HSBC (HSBC) shareholder supports a breakup of the bank, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Netflix (NFLX) has laid off a number of Tudum editorial staff, TechCrunch reports [more]
  • Tesla (TSLA) is recalling about 48,000 Model 3 Performance vehicles in the U.S., Reuters reports [more]
  • Authorities have searched Deutsche Bank's (DB) headquarters in Frankfurt as part of a money laundering probe, Reuters says [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • Zymeworks (ZYME) rises after receiving a $10.50 per share non-binding acquisition proposal from ABF [more]
  • TAL Education (TAL) jumps after reporting quarterly results and extending its share repurchase program [more]
  • Olin (OLN), Mohawk Industries (MHK), and Avantor (AVTR) higher after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • LianBio (LIAN) lower after partner Bristol Myers Squibb (BMY) announced the FDA approved Camzyos [more]
  • Accolade (ACCD), Western Union (WU), and Eldorado Gold (EGO) lower after reporting quarterly results [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Honeywell (HON) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q1 [more]
  • AbbVie (ABBV) reported upbeat Q1 results [more]
  • Wipro (WIT) reported lower-than-expected Q4 results [more]
  • Carlyle Group (CG) reported a year-over-year rise in Q1 earnings [more]
  • Clearwater Paper (CLW) reported upbeat Q1 earnings and revenue [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow fell 939.18, or 2.77%, to 32,977.21, the Nasdaq lost 536.89, or 4.17%, to 12,334.64, and the S&P 500 declined 155.57, or 3.63%, to 4,131.93.
