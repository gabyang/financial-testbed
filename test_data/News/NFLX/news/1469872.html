In this day and age, there are many ways that you could possibly meet your soulmate. You could bump into them at the coffee shop; perhaps you'll meet them through mutual friends on Facebook; or maybe you'll talk to each other through a wall for hours, not knowing what each other looks like, and then you'll get engaged! That's the premise of the Netflix show Love is Blind, and several couples did indeed meet this way.

With contestants finding romance and the series getting good ratings, the first season of the show has been deemed a success. Each episode has been ranked by fans on IMDb on a scale of one to ten, and some episodes did better than others.

Episode 1 Is Love Blind? - 6.0

It's the first day of the experiment! The girls are mingling, and the boys are getting to know each other before it's time to head into the pods. Over the first few days, Barnett seems to connect with multiple girls, including Jessica and Amber. Cameron and Lauren are already be falling in love, and they can't get enough of their pod dates together. They say those three magic words together, solidifying their feelings and getting engaged soon after.

Episode 2 Will You Marry Me? - 6.1

Lauren and Cameron become the first couple to meet face to face, but others are not far behind, with Diamond and Carlton getting engaged and meeting soon after.

Jessica wants to pick Barnett over Mark, but when Barnett doesn't want to be with Jessica, she awkwardly goes crawling back to Mark, trying to pretend like everything is fine. Kenny and Kelly get engaged this episode, and it ends with Damian proposing to Giannina.

Episode 3 First Night Together - 6.2

✕ Remove Ads

Giannina proposes to Damian after he proposes to her, and they both say yes to each other. Mark proposes to Jessica, who takes a while to say yes. Poor Mark! Barnett must narrow down the ladies he is interested in, and he gets engaged to Amber. Now it's off to Mexico for all of the couples so that they can get to know each other better and see if they have as much chemistry in person as they did in the pods.

Episode 5 Last Night In Paradise - 6.2

Jessica still doesn't want to sleep with Mark, and he claims he is fine with that. However, she later let's him know that she isn't completely feeling their engagement, and she takes off her ring. Things also aren't going very well between Giannina and Damian, as an argument breaks out when he reveals that he could get fired from taking off too much time to be on the show. Amber and Barnett are all about their love and chemistry together, even going a bit overboard with their PDA

Episode 9 Bachelor & Bachelorette Parties - 6.3

With only days left before tying the knot, the men and women split off to have their bachelor and bachelorette parties! The guys have a quiet evening at an art gallery playing casino games while the girls night is as wild as they are!

They ladies get a party bus and a stripper, and then they later attend a drag show. A drunk Jessica tells Amber that she wants her man before kissing her on the lips. The girls really are going wild!

Episode 11 The Reunion - 6.3

✕ Remove Ads

The original experiment was filmed over a year ago, and now everyone is back to catch up and see how things are going. The couples that got married in the end are still together, and two people who did not get married are taking a couple of steps back and dating. One thing viewers got to see on this reunion show is Carlton make amends with Diamond. He gives her the ring back but says that it's meant to be a symbol of the friendship he wants to build together.

Episode 8 Count Down To I Do's - 6.4

The weddings are getting closer, and it's time for the brides-to-be to try on wedding dresses! The ladies go shopping together and each get to pick out their special gown. Jessica gets upset because none of the dresses feel right, but the other girls seem happy with their choices. Later, Damian and Giannina get into a fight, which they have been doing a lot of. Amber and Barnett aren't exactly seeing eye to eye either, as she hasn't been working throughout the whole experiment while he works every day. She's over budget for her dress, and she revealed several days ago that she has massive amounts of debt.

Episode 4 Couples Retreat - 6.5

Everyone is in Mexico, and things are not going well between Carlton and Diamond. He decides to come out to her as bisexual, but she is hurt that he didn't tell her sooner. After an explosive fight, the two break up and leave both Mexico and the show.

Meanwhile, the girls and guys are gossiping in their respective groups, talking about who has gotten physically intimate with their partner. Some people even lie about how far they have gotten!

Episode 6 Moving In Together - 6.5

✕ Remove Ads

The next step after vacation ends is for the couples to move into a neutral apartment together! Well, everyone except for Kenny and Kelly because Kelly has to go away for work! As for the others, the couples go to their own homes to pack up their belongings, and in the process, some get to meet each other's roommates! Jessica puts her ring back on, but she's still not afraid to flirt with Barnett when she gets drunk at his birthday party. She also tells Mark just how attractive she thinks Barnett is, and Mark ends up sleeping on the couch.

Episode 7 Meet The Parents - 6.5

These couples are hitting big relationship milestones one right after another, and now it's time to meet the parents! Meeting their partners' moms goes well for Jessica and Cameron, but Giannina doesn't get to share the experience because Damian's parents cancel meeting her. This leaves her feeling hurt and upset. With Damian's parents not approving of the relationship, he wonders if they'll still be able to get married.

✕ Remove Ads

Episode 10 The Weddings - 7.3

The moment we've all been waiting for was ranked the highest out of all the episodes! It's finally time for the couples to head to the altar and either commit to a lifetime together or call it quits. Up first, Giannina says, "I do," but Damian says, "I do not." Bummer! Next, Amber and Barnett happily both say yes to marriage and seal it with a kiss! Kenny and Kelly both give emotional speeches about how much they appreciate each other, but in the end, Kelly says that she can't marry him. Then, Jessica and Mark are on separate pages as Mark wants the marriage, and Jessica does not. The episode ends on a happy note with Cameron and Lauren agreeing to marry each other with excited smiles.
