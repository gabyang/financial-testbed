    Money saving tips: How this one change in your life could see you save £100 or more

    SAVING money is something many people will no doubt want to do, however it can be difficult to achieve. However, there may be a way in which Britons are able to make their money go further.

    By Jess Sheldon
    07:01, Sat, Jun 13, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    Martin Lewis warns loyalty to a bank is a ‘money saving sin’

    From having a hectic lifestyle to not knowing where to start, there are countless reasons which may hold a person back from saving. However, some small changes in one's lifestyle may well mean big rewards when it comes to what's in the bank.

      It's something which TopCashback UK Director, Adam Bullock has addressed, while sharing some insight on ways in which people may be able to cut costs.

      Speaking exclusively to Express.co.uk, Mr Bullock highlighted a number of tips which savvy savers-to-be may want to think about.

      Among them was the practice of planning ahead when it comes to a trip around the supermarket.

      "An oldie but a goldie, meal planning may feel laborious at the start, but once you’re in the habit, you’ll reap the rewards," he said.

      Money savings tips: Reducing outgoings may be achieved by changing certain habits (Image: GETTY)

      "Planning ahead removes the need to browse and impulse buy which obviously results in less money in your pocket, or rather savings, each month.

      "Furthermore, whilst we may have felt the need to bulk buy in recent times, by ditching buying perishables in bulk, you’ll save on waste, space and ultimately cash.

      "Lastly, by making some switches to supermarket own-brand labels, you could be even more quids in.

      According to Mr Bullock, planningmeals and economising on food shops could lead to savings of around £100 or more.

      This is based on a family of four switching to supermarket own brand products, meal planning, and stopping unneccesary bulk buys.

      Another thrifty trick could be employed during the coronavirus UK lockdown.

      The ongoing pandemic has seen lifestyle habits and experiences alter dramatically - and Mr Bullock has suggested savvy savers consider ways in which they can make use of free things, vouchers and offers at the present time.

      "If like many of us right now, you’re seeking things to keep entertained, remember there are freebie alternatives (or at least cheaper alternatives), if you know where to look," he said.

      Money savings tips: Cutting costs can be achieved in a variety of ways (Image: GETTY)

      "For book worms, Project Gutenberg and Many Books are just a couple of sites that offer thousands of free books online.

      "Amazon also hold flash sales where you can pick up some real bargains (think best-sellers for a £1).

      "Many of us are loyal Netflix customers, but don’t forget about all the great TV available for ‘free’ with your licence fee on BBC iPlayer, 4OD, ITV Hub etc. Other providers such as NOW TV, Amazon Prime and Disney Plus all offer free trials too.

      "Now is a great time to absorb some free culture too. The National Theatre, Andrew Lloyd Webber and the Royal Opera House are just a few of the many companies offering their visual arts for free at the moment on YouTube.

      "When lockdown eases, get into the habit of checking sites like Vouchercloud, Wowcher, Groupon etc. for some great offers on going out (and staying in for that matter)."

      So, how much could a person hope to save by pursuing these different options?

      "The sky really is the limit and this does depend on personal preferences," Mr Bullock says.

      "A West End show usually costs in the region of £75 though, so this, plus a few free books and trials could mean you’re saving around £100 or more."
