The new Netflix reality series Too Hot To Handle features a group of overly attractive people having to fight the urge to not have sexual intercourse with one another. The show opens with a talking Alexa-like air freshener, which explains to the audience that contestants have 12 hours before the show rules set in. These rules are specified as "No kissing," "No heavy petting," "No sex of any kind" and "No self-gratification." In compensation for following these rules contestants are allocated a $100,000 prize, but if they break the rules, their prize is diminished depending on the infraction. The premise is that this show aims to steer the contestants to maintain healthier romantic interactions.

In the first episode, contestants make a steamy entrance and it becomes apparent that the aforementioned rules are unbeknownst to them. The audience is shown each contestant's introduction video while they become acquainted with one another. Initially, sexual tensions are high and just about every contestant looks forward to the prospect of having sexual intercourse with others. But what really steals the show initially is the outlandish quotes from some of the contestant's introduction videos. The following are some examples of what we learned from each contestant's introduction.

Chloe

Chloe is from the United Kingdom and is a self-proclaimed social media addict. Chloe also loves to use dating apps. She emphasizes this by claiming "dating apps are kind of like a part-time job for me."Unlike some of the other contestants Chloe seems to be critical of herself. She states, " I’m quite ditsy as well, you just got to be really patient with me because I’m not the brightest spark, um, in the book." While this quote is fairly humorous, it exemplifies that maybe Chloe lacks some self-confidence. It also seems forced and possibly Chloe is more intelligent than what she is letting on to the audience.

Sharron

Sharron is from New Jersey. He has a weightlifter's build and multiple body tattoos. Sharron, being an athlete, claims to treat his women like being on a sports team. This explains of his dating life, "there's always someone on the bench." He also claims to be a feminist and makes the statement, " I’m a feminist, yes, I study Women and Gender Studies at college. If you really pay attention, you get the blueprint to how to pick up women" Despite what he claims, from those statements the idea that Sharron espouses feminism is fairly doubtful. 

Haley

Haley is from Florida. She participates in a sorority at her university and shares some insight about the frat party scene:" I go to a lot of frat parties. Literally everyone’s hammered. Like a girl’s like broken her neck before like falling off the roof, but other than that it’s like fun." Haley is also bisexual and makes the statement that she is only into other girls that look exactly like her; blonde with big boobs. Based on that, it might be worth considering that Haley's soulmate might actually be herself.

Harry

Harry is a tall, baby-faced Australian man. He claims to love when women talk dirty to him and boasts about his tendency to hit future prospects up in their DMs. Although he claims to have success sliding into local girl's DMs, he states, " So I’ve hit everyone famous, I’ve DM'd Kourtney Kardashian,DM'd Kendall. I hit her with, ‘Hello, my little butter chicken.’ But my success rate with celebrities is absolutely zero." Surprisingly, it's hard to believe that Harry has had zero success with that pickup line.

David

David is one of two well-built British guys to appear on the show. One of his favorite things to do at parties is to immediately take his shirt off. He also emphasizes that he wants to give off the impression that he is a nice guy with how his mother perceives him being his primary motivation. Alternatively, David makes several fairly raunchy statements in his introduction such as:" Yeah, David: brains and brawn. Hashtag loves a sex party" and "I don’t really have lines, I don’t really have, like, a strategy. Basically, I just walk into a bar and take my shirt off. Sorted." David's mother must be beaming.

Francesca

Francesca is an Instagram model from British Columbia, Canada. She claims to have over 310,000 followers and in fairness will probably have a few more since appearing on the series. Francesca says that when she is interested in a guy she simply gives them a look to let them know and that tends to work. She also makes the statement," I think my personality is my best asset, but the eyes go to the boobs. But, like, my face isn’t too bad, I don’t think, so." Judging from Francesca's appearance it's hard to disagree with that sentiment.

Matthew

Matthew is a Jesus-like figure from Colorado in the United States. Matthew claims to be a deep thinker and he also questions the human construct of monogamy. He even goes as far as saying, " Monogamy is something that I sometimes question. I’ve often joked about, like, spreading my seed and just kind of rolling the dice of my genetic build with different women and races around the world." All things considered, Matthew probably wouldn't have much time for deep thought with all of the children he would be creating in such an endeavor.

Rhonda

Rhonda is from Georgia. She claims to have an affinity for men with tattoos and holds a confident head on her shoulders stating that she would rate herself "10 out of 10." Rhonda also makes a very important and powerful statement regarding that she feels as a female, she can do anything a man can do. From Rhonda's introduction, she effectively comes off to the audience as a level-headed, empowered feminine role for the show. None of Rhonda's introduction appeared particularly outlandish.

Nicole

Nicole is a blonde girl from Ireland. She claims to have gone to an all girls Catholic school and upon graduation she embraced more freedom and less judgement from her peers. She describes herself as a professional drinker who loves to party. In regards to the men of her life she states, " The lads that I’ve been dating with, in like three dates they tell me that they’re, like, falling for me. And then I’m like Eww no. Then I just give them the snip." Nicole seems to be exactly the type of person that this show aims to help.

Kelz

Kelz is the last contestant to enter the show in the premiere and is the second highly muscular British contestant. Kelz likes to view himself as the king of the jungle. He has a lion tattooed on his arm and his favorite film is the Lion King. He also is an avid video game player. In regards to this Kelz makes the statement, " Ballpark figure, I’ve played Fifa more times than I’ve slept with girls. Fact. It’s less than a thousand." Unsurprisingly, Kelz also doesn't believe in long term commitments.

In conclusion, the new Netflix reality show has reached far and wide to recruit polarizing, attractive and highly sexually active contestants. Whether it's self-proclaimed feminism, Jesus-like hairstyles, or talking air fresheners, there are plenty of oddities to draw the audience into this show. But perhaps what is most enthralling is the fact that each contestant displays addictive attributes towards sexual interaction. Yet, the question remains will these instinctual mechanisms be able to trump the allure of cold, hard cash?
