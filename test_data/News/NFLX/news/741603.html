    Still enjoying the virtual pub quiz? If the answer is yes, have we got a treat for you.

    It's been a very long time since any of us sat huddled around a table at a bar or a pub, frantically wracking our brains for those quiz question answers that, if correct, might win you a free round of drinks.

    We're in the tenth week of lockdown now and although some of our favourite places are open or opening soon - namely shops and restaurants (as takeaways), there are some places that will be closed for some time yet.

    As pubs are one of the last things that look set to reopen, if you're not well-versed in the virtual pub quiz yet, now is the time to get acquainted.

    So without further ado, here are 40 questions perfect for your virtual pub quiz. There are rounds on general knowledge, sport, film and TV, and a whole round on Lincolnshire - to test if you're a true yellowbelly!

    Round 1: General knowledge

    1. Prior to being Boris Johnson’s senior adviser, Dominic Cummings was the director of which political campaign?

    2. What is the name of the only London Tube Station that begins with the letter ‘I’?

    3. Which fashion designer reportedly had a relationship with Winston Churchill?

    4. What is the capital of Kenya?

    5. What is the largest big cat?

    6. How many valves does the heart have?

    7. What is the largest US state?

    8. Which two alcohols are used in a traditional margarita cocktail?

    9. The name of which popular Mexican dish translates as ‘little donkey’?

    10. Who was Henry VIII married to for the longest?

    Round 2: Sport

    1. Which footballer has the second-most Premier League goals ever, as well as the third-most assists?

    2. How many points is the brown ball worth in snooker?

    3. In which sport might you use a quiver?

    4. What colours are the five Olympic rings?

    5. Which athlete has the record for the most Olympic gold medals ever?

    6. Which sport do American teams Utah Jazz, San Antonio Spurs and Boston Celtics play?

    7. In which city was the final of Euro 2020 originally supposed to take place this summer?

    8. Who is the current number one tennis player in the female rankings?

    9. Who scored a crucial drop goal in the 2003 Rugby World Cup final to win England the tournament?

    10. How many Olympic gold medals did Chris Hoy win?

    Round 3: Film and TV

    1. Which TV journalist and documentary maker has made TV episodes where he met people including the Ku Klux Klan and Joe Exotic?

    2. Gillian Anderson plays Dr Jean Milburn in which Netflix comedy-drama series?

    3. How many episodes of Game of Thrones were there?

    4. Dean Norris played DEA agent Hank Schrader in which TV series?

    5. Joss Whedon directed which supernatural drama series starring Sarah Michelle Gellar?

    6. Which film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe was released most recently?

    7. Which is the shortest Harry Potter film, despite based on the longest book in the series?

    8. Which Academy Award-winning film, released in 2014, stars Miles Teller as a jazz drummer and J.K. Simmons as an abusive bandleader?

    9. Love Actually, About Time and The Boat That Rocked were all directed by which British filmmaker?

    10. The Damned United starred Michael Sheen as which legendary football manager?

    Round 4: Lincolnshire

    1. What is the second-most populous town or city in Lincolnshire, according to the 2011 census? Note: NOT including North or North-East Lincolnshire.

    2. Which famous mathematician and physicist was born in Woolsthorpe-by-Colsterworth in the mid-1600s?

    3. Which Lincolnshire-born actor played Horace Slughorn in the Harry Potter films, Colin Jones in Bridget Jones’s Diary and Frank Butterman in Hot Fuzz?

    4. What is the longest river in Lincolnshire with a length of around 80 miles?

    5. True or false: The Lincolnshire flag features red, blue, green and yellow?

    6. Is Lincoln Cathedral closer to Trafalgar Square or the Angel of the North?

    7. What is the largest county Lincolnshire shares a border with?

    8. Which Lincolnshire town has more than 600 listed buildings – more than half the total for the whole county?

    9. How old is Lincoln Cathedral? (Closest answer wins)

    10. Which Lincolnshire-born lyricist wrote the words for most of Elton John’s songs?

    Answers:

    General knowledge

    1. Vote Leave

    2. Ickenham

    3. Coco Chanel

    4. Nairobi

    5. The tiger

    6. Four - the mitral, tricuspid, aortic and pulmonic valves

    7. Alaska

    8. Tequila and orange liqueur/Cointreau

    9. Burrito

    10. Catherine of Aragon

    Sport

    1. Wayne Rooney

    2. Four

    3. Archery

    4. Blue, yellow, black, green and red

    5. Michael Phelps

    6. Basketball

    7. London

    8. Ashleigh Barty

    9. Jonny Wilkinson

    10. Six

    Film and TV

    1. Louis Theroux

    2. Sex Education

    3. 73

    4. Breaking Bad

    5. Buffy the Vampire Slayer

    6. Spiderman: Far From Home

    7. Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix

    8. Whiplash

    9. Richard Curtis

    10. Brian Clough

    Lincolnshire

    1. Grantham

    2. Sir Isaac Newton

    3. Jim Broadbent

    4. River Witham

    5. True

    6. Trafalgar Square - it is 144 miles away on the shortest route. The Angel of the North is 149 miles away on the shortest route.

    7. Norfolk

    8. Stamford

    9. 948 years old

    10. Bernie Taupin
