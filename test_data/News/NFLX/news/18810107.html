Thor 4's Story Is Insane & Chris Hemsworth Hopes It Doesn’t Change

According to Chris Hemsworth, Thor: Love and Thunder is going to be insane, and he doesn't want it to change at all. Following two solo films that were met with mixed responses, Hemsworth's Thor got the movie he deserved with 2017's Thor: Ragnarok, a colorful reinvention of the character directed by Taika Waititi. Ragnarokstripped much of Thor's well-known elements away, from his hair to his hammer, and rebuilt him from the ground up. Because of that, Ragnarokwas quickly considered the best Thormovie by many (including fellow MCU director James Gunn). Fans expected Hemsworth to exit the MCU following Avengers: Endgamelike his co-stars Robert Downey Jr. and Chris Evans, but were instead thrilled to learn he'd be returning for a fourth solo adventure.

Once again directed by Waititi, Love and Thunderwas originally scheduled for a November 2021 release before being pushed back to February 2022. Specific plot details still remain tightly under wraps, but some of the film's supporting players have already been revealed. In addition to seeing Tessa Thompson (Valkyrie) and Waititi (Korg) return, Love and Thunderwill bring back Natalie Portman's Jane Foster, this time as the Mighty Thor iteration of the character. A newcomer to the franchise will be Christian Bale, who will play Love and Thunder's still-unnamed villain. No word yet on if Loki (Tom Hiddleston) will once again cause mischief in his brother's life.

Love and Thunderis still a long ways away, but Hemsworth is already getting excited. While talking to The Philadelphia Inquirer about his new Netflix film Extraction, Hemsworth revealed he's already read the script for the film, which was co-written by Waititi and Jennifer Kaytin Robinson. Hemsworth had some high praise, calling it, "one of the best scripts I’ve read in years." He then went on to say, "It’s Taika at his most extreme, and at his best. If the version I read is the one we get running with, it’s going to be pretty insane."

✕ Remove Ads

Waititi recently said that Love and Thunderwill make Ragnaroklook "safe," which might sound as a ringing endorsement for fans who feel Marvel films have a tendency to follow the same format. Plus, fans already thought Ragnarokwas a wild, welcome departure from previous Thorfilms, and doubling down on that idea for Love and Thunderseems like an excellent plan. Considering how Waititi recently won an Oscar for Jojo Rabbit's screenplay, it's safe to say he's got a knack for crafting unique stories out of any given source material. Hopefully he can bring some of that luck into Love and Thunder.

Love and Thunderis arguably one of the most anticipated movies of Marvel's Phase 4, and Hemsworth's comments will likely only increase fans' excitement. Hemsworth has been open about how much he enjoyed making Ragnarokand switching up Thor's characterization, and Love and Thundercan provide the opportunity to continue pushing Thor to his limits. Since it might have some space sharks flying (swimming?) around, it really does sound like Love and Thunder could win the award for Craziest Marvel Movie Ever.
