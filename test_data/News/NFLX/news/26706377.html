    Sofia Hublitz net worth: How much is Ozark star Sofia Hublitz worth?

    SOFIA HUBLITZ is one of the stars of Netflix series Ozark, portraying teenager Charlotte Byrde. But how much is the actress worth?

    By Neela Debnath
    07:35, Thu, Jul 16, 2020 | UPDATED: 07:42, Thu, Jul 16, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    Ozark: Jonah shoots a gun at beer bottles

    Netflix series Ozark saw a huge spike in popularity this year after season three dropped. Sadly though, the drama will be coming to a close after the streaming platform confirmed Ozark would be ending with season four. The final outing is going to feature 14 episodes split in half, making the show last a little bit longer.

    Trending

      How much is Ozark star Sofia Hublitz worth?

      Ozark has made huge stars of its cast including Sofia Hublitz who plays teenager Charlotte Byrde.

      The actress has been part of the series from the start as the family has been forcibly relocated from Chicago to the Ozarks to launder money for the Navarro Cartel.

      Reflecting on her character in 2018, Hublitz told The Last Issue Magazine: “That’s one of the differences between Charlotte and myself, she’s so kind of ‘I don’t care’ even though death is knocking on their front door more often than not.

      “I would definitely be scared s***less, but she seems a bit more aloof.”

      The 21-year-old American actress from Richmond, Virginia featured in a couple of projects before landing the role in Ozark.

      She had small roles in TV shows Louie and Horace and Pete.

      In 2013, she was a contestant on MasterChef Junior in the States and appeared in three episodes of the American edition of the cookery programme.

      She entered the entertainment industry via her mother, who worked as an art director and godfather who was a key grip.

      According to IMDb, Hublitz will next be starring in a project called What Breaks the Ice.

      However, Ozark is the star’s biggest role to date and the character is likely to see more drama in the final season with the stakes raised even higher.

      Hublitz has appeared in all episodes of Ozark to date in a supporting role.

      Season three saw Hublitz and co-star Skylar Gaertner – who plays her onscreen brother Jonah Byrde – getting more screen time.

      The Byrde siblings also had a storyline running through season three, which gave them greater prominence in the show and potentially importance going forward.

      With the show’s growing popularity, the pay packets of the main cast members may have been increased including Hublitz’s salary.

      Season four of Ozark will again built on the up-and-coming actress’ income going forward.

      World Celebs suggests the star is worth an estimated £393,000 ($500,000).

      Additionally, any future projects will contribute to the actress’ fortune going forward.

      Speaking to Issue Magazine previously, Hublitz opened up about her career plans, telling the outlet: “I don’t know—when I was a kid and people would ask what I wanted to do when I grew up, I had no answer.

      “I still essentially have no answer but I like this for now.”
