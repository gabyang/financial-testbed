    Royal reveal: New book exposes Prince William and Prince Harry’s relationship

    PRINCE William and Prince Harry's relationship will be revealed in a new book which explores how their connections have changed over the years.

    By Manon Dark
    03:59, Sun, Jun 21, 2020 | UPDATED: 06:04, Sun, Jun 21, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    Prince William says the children cause 'explosion' in the kitchen

    The new book titled Battle of Brothers: William and Harry - the Friendship and the Feuds, is by the author Robert Lacey.Mr Lacey said he will “address the unique and complex relationship at the heart of the royal family’s recent woes”.

    Trending

      The book is set to be released in October and will reportedly put the two royal brother’s relationship into a historical context.

      It will allegedly examine how the “heir” and the “spare” dynamic has run throughout history.

      According to People magazine, the book’s synopsis reads: “Much reported on but little understood, Diana’s boys lived under constant scrutiny since birth.

      “Raised to be the closest of brothers, the last 18 months has seen a devastating breakdown of their once unbreakable bond.

      Prince William and Prince Harry’s relationship will be revealed in a new book (Image: getty)

      The new book is titled Battle of Brothers: William and Harry - the Friendship and the Feuds (Image: getty)

      “With an unrivalled knowledge of court life and access to impeccable sources, Robert Lacey investigates the untold reality of the brothers’ relationship, explaining what happened when two sons were raised for vastly different futures and showing how the seeds of damage were sown as their parents’ marriage unravelled.”

      The new book will also reportedly discuss how the siblings’ relationship has been affected by their wives Kate Middleton and Meghan Markle.

      It also promises to address why Prince Harry and Meghan Markle decided to step back as senior royals earlier this year and move to LA with their son Archie.

      Mr Lacey said he has been “astonished” by some of the revelations he has discovered while working on the project.

      “It has been both enthralling and painful to trace this drama through the memories of close witnesses and some of the people most intimately involved.”

      Mr Lacey is a historical consultant to Netflix series, The Crown.

      He is also the author of Majesty, a 1977 study of Queen Elizabeth.

      “We have seen conflicts between heir and spare in every recent generation of the royal family - but nothing so profound as this.”
