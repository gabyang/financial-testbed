Netflix : unveils the official trailer for Breathless

Published: 2024-07-31 09:18:14 ET NFLX

Netflix today unveiled the official trailer and new images of Respira, Netflix's first hospital drama in Spain created by Carlos Montero(Élite).

Respirastars Najwa Nimri(La casa de papel, Sagrada Familia), Aitana Sánchez-Gijón(Madres paralelas, La Regenta), Blanca Suárez(Las chicas del cable, Jaguar), Manu Ríos(Élite, El silencio), Borja Luna(Cristo y Rey, Sin huellas) and Alfonso Bassave(Estoy vivo, Antidisturbios) and will premiere onAugust 30 on Netflix.

This new 8-episode fiction series is produced by El Desorden Creaand counts with Carlos Montero, Diego Betancorand Carlos Ruanoas executive producers. It was directed by David Pinillosand Marta Font, and is written byCarlos Montero, Carlos Ruano, Guillermo Escribanoand Pablo Saiz. The cast is completed by Ana Rayo, Macarena de Rueda, Blanca Martínez, Abril Zamora, Xoán Fórneas, Marwa Bakhatand Víctor Sáinz, among others.

About Breathless

Joaquín Sorolla hospital is much more than a public hospital in Valencia where lives are saved on a daily basis. Doctors and residents work hard in the frenetic pace of the emergency room, where tensions, emotions and even desire accelerate the hearts of a staff who live increasingly on the edge. The arrival of a distinguished patient highlights the complicated situation of the public health system, lighting the fuse for what is to become an unprecedented and drastic strike.

About El Desorden Crea

El Desorden Crea is a production company founded by Carlos Montero who has been writing stories for more than twenty years. Carlos has created series such asPhysics or Chemistry, The Mess You Lived Behindand Élite. The production company was born from the desire to bring to fruition all the ideas that deserve to be told. In this new adventure he is accompanied by Diego Betancor, executive producer of Élite. Respira will be the second production of El Desorden Crea after In Love All Over Again.
