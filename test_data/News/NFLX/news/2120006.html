‘The Marshes’ | How to watch the Australian thriller on Shudder

  • Published: Jun. 16, 2020, 2:00 p.m.
By
  • Kathy Passero, eCommerce reporter

Terror stalks the Australian wilderness in the new movie The Marshes, streaming today, Tuesday, June 16, on Shudder.

The story centers on three idealistic young research biologists determined to save a marshland on the verge of extinction. But endangered wildlife and mosquitos are apparently not the only things lurking in the mud and tall grasses. One night around the campfire, the group hears the chilling legend of the Swagman, a killer whose ghost is rumored to haunt the area and is said to whistle while he hunts his prey. It’s not long before the trio begins to hear the ominous sound of whistling coming closer. The Marsheswhips up a chilling blend of Survivor meets slasher film. Stars Dafna Kronental, Sam Delich and Mathew Cooper.

How can I watch it?

Dubbed the Netflix of horror flicks, Shudder lets you stream your fears, with a vast collection of thrillers and chillers, from suspense to slasher films. Shudder is offering a 7-day free trial + plans starting at $4.75/month.
