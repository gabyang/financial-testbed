From Screen to Stage: Cho Jung seok's Electrifying Journey in 'A List to Playlist' Unveiled – Featuring Superstar Appearances by IU and Park Hyo shin!

Published: 2024-08-16 02:44:04 ET NFLX

Get ready to witness an extraordinary transformation as beloved actor Cho Jung-seok steps off the set and onto the stage inA-List to Playlist,premiering August 30. The newly released main trailer teases an exhilarating ride as Cho, known for his captivating performances, pursues his lifelong dream of becoming a singer-songwriter. This unscripted series promises to take audiences on an emotional rollercoaster, filled with raw moments of struggle, bursts of inspiration, and triumphant breakthroughs.

But the excitement doesn't stop there. A star-studded lineup of A-list "dream makers" - including icons like Gummy, Dynamic Duo, Kim Ea-na, and Kong Hyo-jin - rally around Cho, offering wisdom, support, and a little tough love to help him discover his musical voice. The trailer's surprise reveal of megastars IU and Park Hyo-shin joining the journey has sent anticipation through the roof, leaving fans eager for what's to come.

Adding a dash of humor and heart, Cho is flanked by his hilarious "right-hand and left-hand" men, Jung Sang-hoon and Moon Sang-hoon, whose antics provide a perfect counterbalance to the series' more intense moments. And for fans ofHospital Playlist, there's even more to celebrate: the beloved "99z" crew - Jung Kyung-ho, Yoo Yeon-seok, Jeon Mi-do, and Kim Dae-myeung - pop in for a spontaneous and unforgettable recording session that's sure to become a highlight of the series.

According to Producer Yang Jung Woo, A-List to Playlist is a "whirlwind of luck, surprises, and heartwarming moments," where unexpected guests play a crucial role in propelling Cho Jung-seok closer to his dream. This isn't just a story about an actor turning into a singer - it's a testament to the power of friendship, the magic of collaboration, and the unstoppable drive of someone daring to chase their passion.

In his own words, Cho shares, "This journey is my love letter to music, and finally debuting as a singer is a dream I've held close for so long. I'm overwhelmed with gratitude for everyone who jumped in to support me - it's been a deeply moving experience, filled with tears of joy and thankfulness." As the 100-day countdown to his debut intensifies, fans are on the edge of their seats, waiting to see how this rookie singer's story unfolds.

Mark your calendars: A-List to Playlist drops on Netflix on August 30. Don't miss the chance to be part of Cho Jung-seok's incredible musical journey!
