Martin Lewis warns every Netflix user in UK to check account

Netflix UK users are set to be hit by a price hike in the coming months, but MoneySavingExpert founder Martin has a key warning

    Martin Lewis has shared a key warning to every Netflix user - explaining they could be paying more for the service than they should be.

    Netflix UK users are set to be hit by a price hike in the coming months, but MoneySavingExpert founder Martin has a key warning.

    Netflix - which has shows like Bridgerton, The Queen's Gambit, The Crown, and RuPaul's Drag Race - has announced how each plan will change.

    On his ITV1 show The Martin Lewis Money Show, Mr Lewis shared that by heading to netflix.com/watch-free you can stream some Netflix Originals.

    Martin added: “What you may not know about Netflix is that you can go to netflix.com/watch-free and it has some of its shows available for free there and you don’t have to pay.

    “For example, Stranger Things and Our Planet. Not many of them, but it’s worth checking out.”

    The financial guru added that, if you have more than one screen on the account and others using it, it’s worth asking whether your relatives or housemates would consider splitting Netflix with you.

    By splitting the price between whoever uses it so they pay for their own screen, and sharing the account, you could save around £59 a year.

    “If there are lots of you with memberships in one household, cut that down and go for the premium and get four devices for £14 a month which is cheaper than multiple memberships,” he explained.

    Netflix customers will be informed of the changes, the streaming platform has confirmed, with 13 million said to be impacted.
