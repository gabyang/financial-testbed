The Mandalorian season 2 director Sam Hargrave admits he knows Baby Yoda's real name. The first season of Disney+'s The Mandalorianfeatured some notable names among its directors, including Taika Waititi and Bryce Dallas Howard. Season 2 of the Star Wars series looks to keep that trend going, with directors like Robert Rodriguez and Peyton Reed. Meanwhile, Hargrave was brought in to work on The Mandalorian's action, having spent a great deal of time working on stunts for various movies. Hargrave recently directed Extraction for Netflix, which is one of the biggest movies ever for the streamer

✕ Remove Ads

The Mandalorianwas already poised to be a success for Disney+ thanks to its Star Warsroots and Boba Fett-eque protagonist. However, The Mandalorianhad one more trick up its sleeve to help win fans over: Baby Yoda. The extremely powerful tyke was revealed at the end of the first episode and automatically became the fan favorite. There was some confusion over the character at first, as a few fans wondered if Baby Yoda was actually the Jedi Master Yoda in baby form. He is not, but he is a member of Baby Yoda's species, which remains relatively unexplored within Star Warscanon. Disney attempted to get fans to call him The Child, as that is his name within The Mandalorian.However, the nickname Baby Yoda stuck like glue.

Related: Mandalorian Baby Yoda Soup Drinking Scene Inspired By Director's Kids

✕ Remove Ads

Of course, Baby Yoda isn't his real name, and neither is The Child. There is another name for the mysterious baby, but it has yet to be revealed. However, it sounds like it could be in the future. Hargrave, while talking to Collider, confirmed he is one of the few people who knows Baby Yoda's true name. "Yes, I know the name of The Child," Hargrave said slyly. "I carry that secret. It’s a very heavy burden."

Like all things Star Wars,this is likely a major spoiler that Disney is unwilling to part with just yet. Could this perhaps be a plot point for The Mandalorianseason 2? There are still many questions surrounding Baby Yoda's existence, and the assumption (and hope) among audiences is that they will be answered within season 2. At the same time, season 3 of The Mandalorianis already confirmed to be in development, so the adventures of Baby Yoda could continue well past the next few episodes.

✕ Remove Ads

Luckily, The Mandalorianseason 2 is still on track for an October release. The coronavirus pandemic made some worry that it could be delayed, but post-production continues remotely. It will likely be a while before any major information - like, say, a premiere date - is revealed, but Disney+ recently released a making-of documentary to help ease the wait. Baby Yoda will be back on viewers' screens soon enough.
