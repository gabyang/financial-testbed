5 hidden gems on Disney Plus (for when you get tired of Netflix)

  • Updated: Apr. 11, 2020, 7:23 p.m.
  • |Published: Apr. 11, 2020, 5:00 p.m.
By
  • Joseph Rejent | NJ Advance Media for NJ.com

In the age of the coronavirus quarantine, the best way to flatten the curve is to stay home and remain socially distant. But while you’re doing that, why not sit down and binge-watch a few great movies?

Disney+, the official Disney streaming service, is currently available for $6.99 per month, with a $12.99 per month option if you bundle it together with Hulu and ESPN+.

Considering that a subscription to the service provides you with the entirety of Disney’s film and TV catalogue (barring a few problematic exceptions like “Song of the South”), there are few better deals on the streaming market.

So, if you’re looking for something to watch instead of binging “Stranger Things” again, here are a few of the best hidden gems you might not have known were streaming on Disney+:

1) 10 Things I Hate About You

“10 Things I Hate About You” is loosely based on “The Taming of the Shrew," but modernized enough in its 1999 high school setting that you would never know. Considered one of the quintessential romantic comedies of its time, the film served as a massive breakthrough for the careers of Julia Stiles, Joseph Gordon-Levitt, and Heath Ledger.

2) Holes

Perhaps the most ‘hidden’ of all the films on this list, “Holes” is a faithful adaptation of children’s author Louis Sachar’s acclaimed novel. The film is famous for making its complicated, multi-layer plot accessible to young audiences, and for helping to launch the career of Shia LeBeouf. It’s an imaginative look at serious, challenging subjects without ever losing its sense of humor.

3) Free Solo

“Free Solo” is a documentary about rock climber Alex Honnold and his mission to scale the Yosemite Park’s El Capitan rock formation without any protective equipment. But the film is so much more than that, taking viewers on a journey into the relentless ambition of Honnold as he seeks to undertake one of the greatest athletic achievements in the history of mankind. This is an unflinching look at passion in the face of incredible stakes.

4) Swiss Family Robinson

This 1940 movie, and its subsequent 1960 remake, is a foundational adventure story, following a family shipwrecked on a remote desert island as they attempt to leave London for Australia. Despite losses at the box office, “Swiss Family Robinson” has left an indelible mark on film history reaching far beyond many of the more recognizable selections in the Disney+ roster.

5) The Emperor’s New Groove

Overshadowed by behind-the-scenes turbulence and an underwhelming performance at the box office, the creation of “The Emperor’s New Groove” was so complicated that it became the subject of the 2002 documentary “The Sweatbox.” But for all of its trouble, this largely-forgotten film is a study in chemistry between its voice actors, pivoting between slapstick humor and sly wit as it tries to say something profound about class and friendship. It doesn’t always succeed, but it’s no less entertaining for the attempt.
