Hear From These Amazing Speakers at SaaStr Annual 2020

by Deborah Findling | Blog Posts, Featured Posts, SaaStr Events

We’re back in San Jose! Well, not QUITE yet but if you’re already planning your trip to SaaStr Annual 2020, here is our initial speaker lineup. Keep checking this space as we’re adding new speakers every week. After the jump, a little bit more insight on our speakers – what they’re all about and what kind of sessions they’re leading.

So what’s new this year? More braindates, more great content and of course more chances to connect with your SaaStr community. For now, check out a few of our speakers and a little bit on what you might see change coming up at this year’s Annual. If you’d like to check out our most up-to-date lineup, click here for more.

The Speaker Lineup

☁️ Bora Chung, Bill.com SVP of Product and Vinay Pai, Bill.com SVP of Engineering will be joining with a product and engineering focused tactical session. Bora was formerly the Chief Product Officer of eBay Korea, leading engineering, product and UX/UI design teams. Prior to this, she was a Director at Apple where she led Worldwide Payments and Financing Programs for Online Stores. Vinay was SVP of Engineering at First Data where he led the engineering for the Clover POS product line. Prior to that, he held an eight-year tenure at Intuit as a VP for the Intuit Developer Platform.

☁️ David Schneider, ServiceNow CRO, and Dennis Lyandres, Procore CRO will be leading a “CRO Confidential” on all the things that break, fail and explode during hypergrowth. The two will discuss different stages of growth from two different perspectives.

☁️Howie Lu, Airtable CEO will be leading a session on Wednesday, March 10th. Today, over 90,000 organizations, accounting for about half of the Fortune 1000, use Airtable. Netflix, Adobe, and Tesla count themselves among its customers. The company launched in 2015 and has raised $170M to date. Howie has hinted that an IPO could be a part of Airtable’s longterm plan so watch this session for the inside scoop on what’s coming down the line.

The CXO Summit
