    The Big Bang Theory cast: Who played Stuart Bloom? Meet the Ugly Betty star

    THE BIG BANG THEORY is streaming on Netflix now and fans are curious to know more about the cast who played their favourite characters for over a decade. Who played Comic book store owner Stuart Bloom?

    By Molli Mitchell
    20:00, Mon, Jun 22, 2020 | UPDATED: 20:03, Mon, Jun 22, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    The Big Bang Theory: Hilarious trailer for final series

    The Big Bang Theory ran for 12 seasons between 2007 and 2019. Fans have been watching the sitcom from the very beginning and are curious to know more about the actor who played Stuart Bloom.Express.co.ukhas everything you need to know.

    Trending

      Who played Stuart Bloom in The Big Bang Theory?

      Stuart Bloom is the owner of The Comic Center of Pasadena and close friend of Sheldon (played by Jim Parsons), Howard (Simon Helberg), Leonard (Johnny Galecki) and Raj (Kunal Nayyar).

      Stuart was played by American actor and comedian, Kevin Sussman.

      Ironically, Sussman worked in a comic book store before becoming an actor.

      Sussman had been part of The Big Bang Theory cast since season two of the show, which aired in 2008.

      In season six of the sitcom, he was promoted to series regular.

      Big Bang Theory fans may recognise Bloom from his role as Walter on the ABC comedy, Ugly Betty.

      In Ugly Betty, he starred alongside America Ferrera, Eric Mabius and Vanessa L. Williams.

      Sussman studied at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts, before studying with acting teacher Uta Hagen for four years.

      In the early years of his career, Sussman starred in adverts and in small guest roles.

      Sussman appeared in episodes of The Sopranos, ER, My Name is Earl, CSI: Crime Scene Investigation and Children's Hospital.

      In 2012, he starred in season eight of Weeds as Terry and in 2015 played the role of Steve in Wet Hot American Summer: First Day of Camp.

      He made his film debut in the 1999 film Liberty Heights as Alan Joseph Zuckerman.

      In Liberty Heights, Sussman starred alongside Adrien Brody, Bebe Neuwirth and Joe Mantegna.

      His other film credits include Hitch, Freeloaders and Burn After Reading.

      During his time on The Big Bang Theory, Sussman teamed up with Big Bang Theory co-star John Ross Bowie to create two television comedies: The Ever After Part on Fox and The Second Coming of Rob on CBS.

      Sussman is best known for playing Stuart Bloom on The Big Bang Theory.
      Originally, Sussman did not audition for the role of Stuart and instead tried out for the part of Sheldon Cooper.

      He later auditioned for the part of Barry Kripke, a role that went to John Ross Bowie.

      Speaking to Business Standard about his time on The Big Bang Theory, Sussman said: “It is by far my longest job of any kind -- be it acting or non-acting. It is amazing.

      “I had done enough TV stuff before 'The Big Bang Theory'.
