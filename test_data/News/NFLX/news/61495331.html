Global SVOD Platform Forecasts Report 2020-2025: Five Global Platforms to Have 678 Million Paying SVOD Subscribers - ResearchAndMarkets.com

November 25, 2020 10:52 AM Eastern Standard Time

DUBLIN--(BUSINESS WIRE)--The "Global SVOD Platform Forecasts 2020" report has been added to ResearchAndMarkets.com's offering.

Based on September 2020 results, the publisher expects five global platforms to have 678 million paying SVOD subscribers by 2025. Disney+ will add 112 million subscribers between 2020 and 2025 to take its total to 194 million. Netflix will increase by 73 million subscribers, revealing strong growth even for the longest established platform.

Simon Murray, Principal Analyst, said: "Much of Disney+' initial growth came from the US, mainly due to the attractive bundle of Disney+ with ESPN+ and Hulu. More recently, India's Disney+ Hotstar subs count has rocketed due to its coverage of IPL cricket. The US and India will account for nearly half of Disney+' subscriber base by 2025."

Netflix's revenues will reach $37 billion by 2025 - up by $17 billion on its 2019 total. Disney+ will generate $13 billion by 2025 - impressive since it only started in November 2019. However, this is a lot lower than Netflix due to lower ARPUs charged in developing markets.

Key Topics Covered:

Published in November 2020 and based on September results, this 38-page PDF and excel report contains extensive coverage of the main global SVOD platforms.

The report comprises two parts:

  • Profiles of the global SVOD platforms, including Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, HBO Max, Peacock and Paramount+/ViacomCBS.
  • Workbook for Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+ and HBO Max; providing subscriber and revenue forecasts for every year for each of 138 countries from 2010 to 2025
