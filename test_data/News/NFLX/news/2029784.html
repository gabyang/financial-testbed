A 55-inch 4K Smart TV for under $280: The best deal you will find for staying at home during coronavirus isolation

  • Published: Apr. 09, 2020, 2:54 p.m.
By
  • Tim Brown | The Oregonian/OregonLive

Are you looking to upgrade your living space with a new way to watch your favorite shows and movies during this pandemic? Now may be the best time to do that as one of the best-rated budget 4K high definition televisions on the market, the TCL 4-Series smart TV, is currently on sale for one of the lowest prices we’ve seen with the 55-inch model now going for $279.99 including free shipping from Amazon.

The 4-Series from TCL is equally rated by movie buffs, TV fans and gamers alike as one of the best value buys on the market in terms of consistently blending quality 4K ultra high definition picture at an affordable price. What’s even more attractive about this model is the built-in Roku Smart TV functionality which adds the ability to stream movies and television from the minute you plug it in at home with apps for popular services like Netflix, Amazon Prime, Hulu, HBO Go, Disney+ and much, much more. Having a built-in Roku means you won’t have to buy an extra streaming device like a Roku streaming stick, Apple TV or Amazon Fire Stick. That alone will save you around $50 or more since you won’t have to buy that additional device.

How to get Disney Plus: What does it cost, include?

The launch of Disney+ on Nov 12 brings subscribers new shows and movies, plus access to the DIsney library of animated classics, "Star Wars," Marvel, Pixar movies, National Geographic content, and more.

This model is rated a 4.5/5 stars by Amazon and Best Buy customers alike and review site Tom’s Guide has it listed as one of their best value models for video game players with this model’s quick response time.

If the $279.99 price tag is a little too steep for you, or the large size simply won’t work for your home or apartment space, there are other size models of the same television available at equally attractive price points.

• The 50-inch model goes for $259.99 and the 43-inch goes for just $229.

If for some reason this TV sells out at Amazon, you can also get the same model from Best Buy at the same $279.99 price tag.
