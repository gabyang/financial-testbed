    White Lines cancelled: Has White Lines on Netflix been cancelled? Star drops bombshell

    WHITE LINES dropped on Netflix in May and fans have been wondering when and if the show will return for a second season but has White Lines on Netflix been cancelled?

    By Molli Mitchell
    11:21, Tue, Aug 11, 2020 | UPDATED: 12:09, Tue, Aug 11, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    GMB: Angela Griffin teases new season of White Lines

    White Lines is the British-Spanish mystery thriller series on Netflix, created by Money Heists Álex Pina.Fans have been awaiting news about the show’s future but recently,one of the show’s stars expressed her fear the show had been cancelled after just one season. Express.co.uk has everything you need to know about White Lines season two. 

    Trending

      Has White Lines been cancelled?

      Season one of White Lines premeired on Friday, May 15 on Netflix worldwide.

      All 10 episodes were available to stream and download in one go and ever since the show's release, fans have been demanding more.

      Netflix renewals are usually announced between one and six months after the show's release, so fans could be waiting until November until they hear news of the show's future.

      However, White Lines star Angela Griffin has expressed her fears White Lines has been cancelled.

      The former Coronation Street Star who played Anna in the series was speaking during an online Q&A for the West End’s Theatre Royal Haymarket she believed the show would not be returning.

      She said: "Will there be a series two of White Lines? I don’t think so.

      "I wish there was but it’s very quiet on the White Lines hotline.

      "My gut feeling is that it was left in a place where it is nicely tied up.

      "I think that is where it is going to stay. Sorry to say.”

      Netflix has not yet officially confirmed the cancellation or renewal of White Lines.

      Express.co.uk has contacted Netflix for comment and this article will be updated when more infromation is available.

      WARNING: This article contains spoilers about White Lines season 1

      White Lines followed Mancurian librarian, Zoe (played by Laura Haddock) who travels to Ibiza to investigate the death of her brother Axel (Tom Rhys Harris), whose body was discovered 2020 years afeter his disaperance.

      However, when she arrives in Ibiza, Zoe uncoveres a world of sex, drugs and corruption, uncovering dark secrets about Axel and his friends.

      Although the ending of season one solved Axel's murder, there were many questions left unanswered, leaving the door open for a second series of the show.

      Speaking to Digital Spy earlier this ear, White Lines star Daniel Mays who plays Marcus had high hopes for a second season and spilled some potential plot detials.

      He said: "It's brilliantly left open, especially for Marcus, my character.

      "I think the last scene is him wanting to take over the drug operation from the Calafat family.

      "There's many, many loose ends that need to be tied up. I think there's definitely potential for the younger actors to still come back, because you could argue that there's, you know, the lost years.

      "There's lost flashbacks that we haven't addressed yet."

      The cancellation of White Lines would come as a bit of a suprising, considering the series made it to the top 10 on Netflix worldwide.

      Hopefully, Netflix decides to save the series and White Lines will be back with a second outing.

      If White Lines does return, fans can expect season two in May 2021 at the earliest.

      However, due to the current coronavirus outbreak, production on a second series could face delays.

      Fans could see Transformers star Laura Haddock reprise her role as Zoe Collins and Code 404's Daniel Mays as Marcus Ward.

      Victoria's Laurence Fox may be back as David alongside Coronation Street's Angela Griffin.

      Portugeuse actor and DJ Nuno Lopes may return as Duarte 'Boxer' Silva opposite Shazams! Marta Milans as Kika Calafat.

      Other cast members fans could see return are Juan Diego Botto as Oriol, Pedro Casablanc as Andreu, Belén Lópezas Conchita and Francis Mageeas Clint.
