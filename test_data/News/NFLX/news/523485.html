6 popular reality TV shows to binge-watch if you’re tired of Netflix dramas

  • Published: Apr. 18, 2020, 5:30 p.m.
By
  • Joseph Rejent | NJ Advance Media for NJ.com

“The Bachelor” franchise has a new reality TV spinoff called “Listen to Your Heart,” which airs on Mondays at 8 p.m. on ABC. Whether or not the show meets your quota for melodrama, that means there’s still six days a week where you may not be getting your fix of reality TV.

However, if you’re a reality TV fan or just tired of watching serious shows in the coronavirus quarantine era, there are plenty of streaming services with some of the best reality TV out there. Hulu, Amazon Prime Video, Fubo TV, and CBS All Access all feature tons of high-quality content that you’re never going to get to binge-watch with just a Netflix subscription.

Here are a few great reality TV shows you can watch if you’ve exhausted all your Netflix options and just want something different.

1) The Masked Singer

“The Masked Singer” is probably the herald of a new age of reality television, combining elements of elimination-style competition shows with its own version of a weekly mystery. The series features celebrities singing while hidden in elaborate costumes. Every week, one masked participant is eliminated, forcing them to reveal their identity. If you’re looking for a singing competition with an interesting angle, check this one out on Hulu.

2) America’s Next Top Model

The brainchild of supermodel Tyra Banks, “America’s Next Top Model” pits 14 contestants against each other as they seek the title, a $100,000 prize and a spread in Paper magazine. The show was absolutely massive when it launched, and since then has become one of the most iconic reality series in the genre. It combines all the lowbrow melodrama of “The Bachelor” with both genuine interest and insight into the fashion industry. “America’s Next Top Model” is available to stream on Amazon Prime Video.

3) The Real Housewives of Orange County

“The Real Housewives of Orange County” is the one that started it all, launching almost a dozen spinoffs across the country filled with glamor and havoc and ridiculous fights. The series is set in a community where the average home costs $1.6 million, as a handful of women who live there partake in shopping, drinking, working out and arguing with each other. Most of “The Real Housewives” franchise is available for streaming on Hulu.

4) Survivor

Twenty years and 40 seasons later, “Survivor” is still an icon. Perhaps the most original elimination-style reality TV show, it’s been a staple of the CBS schedule since it began. The series features a group of ‘castaways’ placed in an isolated location as they seek to provide food and shelter for themselves, compete in challenges and avoid elimination all in the name of $1 million. This is as high-brow and strategic as reality TV gets, and the entirety of the series is available to stream on CBS All Access.

5) My Strange Addiction

“My Strange Addiction” is a sobering look at how truly bizarre the human psychology can be. From more mundane cases, like a woman who cannot sleep without a blowdryer turned on beside her in bed, to the disturbing, like a man who eats glass, every episode of this series is an arresting emotional journey. Since its premiere in 2010, there’s never been anything quite like it on TV. The series is available to stream on Amazon Prime Video.

6) RuPaul’s Drag Race

Each episode focuses on a set of challenges in which the infamous RuPaul seeks to find “America’s next drag superstar.” Through its musical and dramatic weekly competitions, the series has received enormous critical acclaim, notching dozens of Emmy nominations and launching 10 spinoffs across the globe. Every part of “Drag Race” is an experience, and you can catch the show live as it airs on VH1 with Fubo TV. If you’re looking to binge-watch it as well, the first six seasons are currently streaming on Hulu.
