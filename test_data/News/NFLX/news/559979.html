Alison Brie’s 10 Best Movies, According to Rotten Tomatoes

During the 2010s, Alison Brie became one of the most prominent television actors in the entire world. She had a recurring role on the highly-touted Mad Men before landing lead roles in other lauded series like Community, GLOW, and Bojack Horseman. Almost nobody can match that resume.

With such a stellar TV career, Alison Brie's film work often gets overlooked. That's even with her scoring roles alongside stars like Kevin Hart (Get Hard) or in franchises like Scream. With her top shows named, it's time to look at her greatest movies.

Sleeping With Other People (64%)

Released in 2015, Sleeping With Other Peoplebrought together two of the top comedic actors around for a new age romantic story. Alison Brie played Lainey while Jason Sudeikis took the role of Jake. Both characters struggled with love due self-sabotage and focusing too much on sex.

The two pledge to keep anything physical out of their friendship but ultimately begin to fall for each other. A strong supporting cast featuring Natasha Lyonne, Adam Scott, and Amanda Peet was praised, as was the chemistry between the likable lead actors.

✕ Remove Ads

Horse Girl (72%)

The most recent entry on this list, arriving on Netflix in February 2020, holds a close place to Alison Brie's heart. That's because she not only starred in the film but she co-wrote it and was one of the producers. It was also a different avenue for Brie, being a psychological movie.

Horse Girl is an odd film and it doesn't shy away from that fact. It follows Sarah (Brie), a socially awkward and possibly mentally unstable woman who begins to have strange visions. Brie's committed performance received widespread acclaim.

The Kings Of Summer (75%)

✕ Remove Ads

It's the first film on the list to officially be certified as "fresh" on Rotten Tomatoes. In 2013, viewers were introduced to The Kings of Summer. This indie Sundance sensation centered around three teens who build a house in the woods so they can live independently.

The film takes place over the course of their time together during the summer. Brie played Heather, the sister of the protagonist Joe. The movie was considered charming and also helped launched the careers of Nick Robinson (Love, Simon) and Erin Moriarty (The Boys).

The Little Hours (78%)

2017's The Little Hours is one of the most unique comedies Alison Brie has ever starred in. It focuses on three nuns (Brie, Aubrey Plaza, and Kate Micucci) who live a sheltered life but see things change when a gardener (Dave Franco) posing as a deaf mute arrives.

This is an interesting movie in that it was well-received by critics but scores just a 48% from the audience on Rotten Tomatoes. The Little Hours is something of a slow burn but mostly got good reviews due to the work of its talented cast.

Joshy (81%)

Another Sundance Film Festival standout is 2016's Joshy.It stars Thomas Middleditch as the titular character and a majority of the dialogue was improvised by the cast. That gives it a different feel from most of the more traditionally scripted movies out there.

Joshy centered on a guy who has his engagement broken off, only for his friends to still take him on his bachelor party. It results in an unforgettable time. Brie has a small role in this but it's notable since she teamed with the real-life couple of director Jeff Baena and actress Aubrey Plaza for the first time before doing it again in The Little Hours.

The Lego Movie 2: The Second Part (85%)

It's kind of a shame that The Lego Movie 2: The Second Part didn't get nearly the amount of love as the original. This was a rare sequel that managed to continue to deliver something similar to the original but remain different enough to stand out. Most of the cast returned for this installment.

That meant Chris Pratt, Elizabeth Banks, Will Arnett, Charlie Day, and yes, Alison Brie as Unikitty, were all back. While it wasn't considered to be as fun as its predecessor, this sequel still delivered something enjoyable for everyone.

The Post (88%)

✕ Remove Ads

Sometimes, you see a movie and just know that it's going to do well come awards season. That was the case with 2018's The Post. It told the true story of journalists at The Washington Post, including the first female publisher, who looked to share sensitive documents depicting the US government's involvement in the Vietnam War.

RELATED: 10 Best Meryl Streep Movies, According to IMDB

A film led by legendary actors Meryl Streep and Tom Hanks was enough to make The Post a must-see picture for most. Alison Brie had a small but notable role as Lally Graham, daughter of Streep's lead character who went on to become senior associate editor for The Washington Post.

The Disaster Artist (91%)

✕ Remove Ads

Alison Brie is married to Dave Franco and the two have been together for nearly a decade. They've worked together a few times but the highlight was 2017's The Disaster Artist, where Brie delivered a supporting performance as Amber, the girlfriend of Franco's character.

The Disaster Artist, directed by Dave's brother James, chronicles the friendship between Tommy Wiseau (James) and Greg Sestero (Dave) as they worked together to film The Room, widely considered to be the "best bad movie" in history. This take was nominated for several awards at the Golden Globes, with James Franco winning for his acting.

Promising Young Woman (93%)

This film was technically released earlier than Horse Girl, but not many have seen it yet. Promising Young Woman premiered at the Sundance Film Festival in January 2020 but hasn't had its wide release. It looks like one of the most interesting movies of the year.

✕ Remove Ads

This stars Carey Mulligan as a woman who has her life derailed by a traumatic event. She then uses various skills to lead a vigilante lifestyle targeting predators. In the film, Alison Brie plays Madison, who is believed to be a friend of the protagonist. Bo Burnham, Laverne Cox, and Connie Britton help round out a stellar cast.

The Lego Movie (95%)

Alison Brie's first time taking on the role of Unikitty proved to be the one that was met with the best reviews. The Lego Movie came out in 2014 and was a tremendous animated film. It holds up today as one of the best of the entire decade.

The Lego Movie centers on Emmet Brickowski, a construction worker who is believed to be the "Special," a master builder who can help save Bricksburg. This film was praised for its gorgeous animation, heartwarming story, talented voice cast, and Academy Award-nominated music.
