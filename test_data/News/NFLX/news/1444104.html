After another ten episodes of tears, hugs, and makeovers, can fans look forward to seeing Queer Eye season 6? Unsurprisingly, Netflix's popular reality TV show has already been renewed for season 6, but there may be a longer wait for the Fab Five's return due to the coronavirus pandemic shutting down production.

Launching on Netflix in February 2018, Queer Eye is a reboot of the 2000s Bravo series Queer Eye For the Straight Guy, in which a group of gay men known as the "Fab Five" gave home, life, and personal appearance makeovers to struggling straight men. Queer Eye dropped the latter part of the title due to an expanded focus, with the new Fab Five helping out not only straight guys but also straight women, lesbians and gay men. Now the only uniting theme of the "heroes" is that they're people who need a little help in their lives.

Related: The Cast of Queer Eye Season 4: Where Are They Now?

The Fab Five of Netflix's Queer Eye are: Tan France, fashion consultant; Bobby Berk, interior designer; Jonathan Van Ness, who advises on hair, makeup, and personal care; Karamo Brown, a therapist and life coach; and Antoni Porowski, a chef and food guru. Each new season of the show has the group focus on people living in the different city - so where will Queer Eye season 6 take them?

Queer Eye Has Already Been Renewed For Season 6

Queer Eye continues to be one of Netflix's most popular shows, and was renewed for season 6 in March 2020. The next season of the show will feature Austin, Texas as the Fab Five's new base of operations, and will see them "scour the prairie in a search for a whole new roster of heroes in need of a little TLC." In contrast to the original series, which was based in New York, Queer Eye has been filmed around various places around the United States. Season 1 and 2 took place in Atlanta, Georgia, seasons 3 and were set in Kansas City, Missouri and Illinois, and season 5 was filmed in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.

Queer Eye Season 6 Release Date

Queer Eye season 6 was originally targeting a release later in 2020, but has since been delayed after the coronavirus pandemic led to a production shutdown. The series has so far kept up a brisk place, with two seasons releasing in 2018 and 2019, plus the specials "Yass, Australia!" (in which the Fab Five visit the town of Yass, Australia) and "We're In Japan!" (in which the Fab Five help out heroes in Japan). However, according to Berk, Queer Eye season 6 didn't get very far at all before it got put on pause. The star told Oprah Magazine:

"We were here [in Austin] filming season 6 and about five days into that, production got shut down because of COVID-19... We didn’t even get the first episode finished. The reveal had happened, our hero had seen the home. The only thing that hadn’t happened was our last scene where we get to watch what’s happened with them."

That means that whenever the restrictions on production do lift again, Queer Eye season 6 will still have almost the whole season still left to film. With that in mind, a 2021 release date is likely.
