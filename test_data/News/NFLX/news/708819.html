Netflix looked at bid to buy £2.4bn Regal Cinemas before it was snapped up by Cineworld

By JAMIE NIMMO FOR THE FINANCIAL MAIL ON SUNDAY

Updated: 14:42 GMT, 24 May 2020

  • e-mail

Netflix looked at buying the second largest cinema chain in the US before it was snapped up by British group Cineworld for £2.4billion, The Mail on Sunday can reveal.

Sources said the $200billion TV streaming giant was among the parties in the running to buy America’s Regal Cinemas before Cineworld struck its deal in November 2017.

The revelation underlines the interest from the streaming giants in major cinema chains, which have been shut during the coronavirus crisis. Cineworld sealed its takeover of Regal in February 2018.

Netflix was among the parties in the running to buy America’s Regal Cinemas

It follows our report earlier this month that Netflix’s streaming rival Amazon has shown interest in buying AMC Entertainment, the world’s largest cinema group which owns Odeon in the UK.

Owning cinema chains would give streaming giants greater control over Hollywood.

Netflix and Amazon are both reported to have looked at buying Landmark Theatres in 2018 before the US arthouse cinema chain was eventually sold to Cohen Media Group.
