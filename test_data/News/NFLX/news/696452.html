The times these Swansea locations became the backdrop for films and TV dramas

But do you recognised where they were shot?

    Wales has become a go-to for many big TV and film productions over the years and Swansea is no exception.

    With producers such as Netflix, BBC and Channel 4 pitching up around Swansea and its valleys, there are plenty of films and television shows with familiar landmarks and settings.

    Here's a line-up the locations around Swansea used in some of the most well-loved TV dramas and films in recent years.

    End of the F***ing World

    The  Netflix  and Channel 4 adventure drama The End of the F***ing World was filmed in Swansea in April 2019.

    Filming on the second series of the show centred on the Rowberry Fashion store in Port Tennant.

    Show star Jessica Barden, then 26, who takes on the lead character of Alyssa, happily greeted onlookers as shooting took place.

    In May 2019, filming crews for the show returned to Swansea, again for the second series.

    However, this time, crews took over the popular Kardomah Cafe in the city centre for two days of filming.

    Their Finest

    In 2016, parts of Swansea city centre were transformed into scenes from the 1940s.

    This was because Second World War drama,  Their Finest, was filmed at some of the city's most recognisable venues and landmarks including the Guildhall and The  Grand Theatre.

    Former Bond Girl Gemma Arterton plays Catrin Cole who lands herself a job writing copy for propaganda films. It also stars Jeremy Irons, Sam Clafin and Bill Nighy.

    In the film trailer, Gemma's Welsh accent can be plainly heard - but the English actress said she wanted to make it soft and worked hard to get it right.

    Keeping Faith

    In October 2018, Swansea Valley actress Eve Myles, who stars as Faith Howells, was seen walking down Wind Street in the city centre.

    She could be seen wearing a smart blue jacket with matching heels as she casually ate a sandwich and spoke to someone on the phone.

    The eight-part series broke a string of records when it was shown on BBC Wales in 2018.

    The first series was such a success the BBC had more than 9.5 million requests to view the series on BBC iPlayer - the highest ever number recorded for any non-network show on the BBC at that point.

    The Accident

    The Accident was completely filmed in Wales, mainly  Rhondda Valleys, Swansea and Caldicot before its release in October 2019.

    The Channel 4 drama stars Sarah Lancashire, Mark Lewis Jones, from Keeping Faith and Star Wars: The Last Jedi, and No Offence's Joanna Scanlan and explores a fictional Welsh community's fight for justice after an explosion on a construction site, which killed several local children..

    In later episodes of The Accident, the community of Glyngolau go to court. Those scenes were shot at Brangwyn Hall in Swansea.

    There are a few hospital scenes in the first episode when Polly (Sarah Lancashire) waits for news on daughter Leona (Jade Croot) and goes to see Martin, the mysterious man who pulled Leona out of the collapse.

    These were shot at Cefn Coed Hospital in Cockett, Swansea.

    Hunky Dory

    Musical drama Hunky Dory is set in Swansea during the hot summer of 1976.

    The crew set up to film at Bishop Gore school in Sketty and even involved some local teenage extras.

    In the story, a drama teacher, played by Minnie Driver, tries to pull off a rock 'n roll version of Shakespeare's the Tempest to try and motivate her students.

    Skins

    Channel 4's noughties teen drama, Skins, launched the careers of many well-known stars, such as Dev Patel, Nicholas Hoult and Kaya Scodelario.

    But did you know it was filmed in Gower in 2007? Scenes from the series two episode, Michelle, were filmed at Three Cliffs Bay.

    Britannia

    Dubbed as Britain's answer to Game of Thrones, the massive Sky production stars Mackenzie Crook, David Morrissey and Zoe Wanamaker.

    In Wales, it was Macross, Nash Point, Coelbren and Henrhyd that were used the most. However, it also featured Rhosilli Bay.

    Da Vinci's Demons

    The £20m TV series Da Vinci’s Demons was filmed in Swansea Bay first went world-wide on the Starz network in April 2013.

    It tells the story of Da Vinci’s young life in Florence was filmed using Port Talbot’s Margam Castle as a Medici Palace.

    The filming work which employed 2,500 actors, extras, set designers, costume designers, caterers and movie technicians, was carried out in the Bay Studios.

    Other sets included Neath Abbey, Margam Abbey and several Gower beaches.

    Submarine

    Submarine was filmed largely in Swansea in 2010 over a six-week period.

    Submarine is a coming-of-age comedy set in 1980s Swansea which follows the fortunes of 15-year-old Oliver Tate as he goes through the break-up of his parents’ marriage and his first relationship.
