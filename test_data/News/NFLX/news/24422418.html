With so many choices out there of TV shows to watch, it is hard for anyone to make the right decision for their personality type. The excess of shows available might seem great at first, as it delivers loads of choices but it also results in the mixing of genres, making it even more difficult for those with particular tastes to find the right TV show for them.

RELATED: MBTI: 5 Netflix Original Series That INFPs Will Love (& 5 They Will Hate)

However, fear not ENTJs! If you fall under this particular personality type within the MBTI® spectrum, we have a handful of Netflix series you will absolutely love - and others that you will want to avoid.

Love: Medici: The Magnificent

Medici: The Magnificent isn't a show for everyone. At times, it moves at a snail's pace, while at other times, feels disjointed from a story perspective. Not to mention that the main protagonist appears to never age throughout the series, making the whole thing more than a little bit confusing.

That said, Medici: The Magnificent has a strong main character who overcomes the odds not out of luck but out of skill. This will appeal to the business side of most ENTJs.

✕ Remove Ads

Hate: She Ra And The Princess Of Power

With a lack of originality plaguing most television series in almost every movie in every genre, it is no surprise that nostalgia has become the in-thing. Many fans are forced to resort to watching episodes of shows that ended years ago to get a taste of meaningful storylines or believable characters.

This, unfortunately, is the sad state of Hollywood and precisely why many ENTJs will hate She Ra and The Princess of Power because nostalgia is based on feelings, not fact.

Love: Peaky Blinders

✕ Remove Ads

It's just hard not to love this series as it just checks all the right boxes when it comes to gangsters in early 20th century England. None of the main protagonists or villains are cliche in any way and Peaky Blinders is one of the few shows not to use sex scenes as a crutch for the story.

RELATED: MBTI: 5 Netflix Original Movies That ESFPs Will Love (& 5 They Will Hate)

Plus, it doesn't waste time with filler episodes. The main protagonists are take-charge types of guys, who know what they want and will do anything they have to, to get it.

Hate: Stranger Things

Netflix's Stranger Things is gory, fun, and filled with unbelievably likable protagonists. It captivates the minds of fans with the unknown and strong character development that most TV series these days fail to deliver. Sadly, it also places the characters in unrealistic scenarios, scenarios that plague most series in the horror genre.

✕ Remove Ads

Another thing that Stranger Things does that will not sit right with ENTJs is it has a cast of characters that tends to be hesitant to take on the villains, making them reluctant heroes.

Love: Troy: Fall Of A City

For those who love Greek legends, Troy: Fall of A City is a must-watch television program delivering all the gory details that the legend of Troy brings to the table. In fact, Troy: Fall of A City might even push boundaries that the legend wouldn't cross. Overall, for most personality types this TV series would be a fun watch.

Fortunately, those with the ENTJ personality type fit into the droves of fans that enjoy the confidence and boldness of Achilles as he faces off against his rival Hector.

✕ Remove Ads

Hate: Dawn Of The Croods

Dawn of The Croods is a funny cartoon based off of an even funnier movie. So, it isn't surprising to know it has been a hit for Netflix, earning it multiple seasons on the air. Though, for realists, the idea of cavemen behaving in such a manner as shown on the cartoon is not only laughable but, at times, unbelievable.

RELATED: MBTI: 5 Genre Shows That ENFPs Will Love ( 5 They Will Hate)

Shows that are not based on factual evidence are shows that will ultimately be unappealing for those with the ENTJ personality type.

Love: American Vandal

✕ Remove Ads

Those who have the ENTJ personality type enjoy being in positions of power, which usually places them in careers where their sole job is to keep the peace. Many with this personality type end up as cops, teachers, and even principles of schools across the world over.

So, a show about high school students breaking the rules is not something that will ever be on an ENTJs must-watch list. Since, unfortunately, American Vandal gets its kicks from taking it to the system.

Hate: Hollywood

One of the best and worst things about having an ENTJ personality type is that it is so based in reality that those who have it sometimes dare not to dream. Making it big in Hollywood is never a rational desire but that doesn't mean it isn't worth pursuing.

For the lucky few who make it big, it pays big dividends. For the ones who fail, they live a life full of broken dreams. Netflix's Hollywood is full of big dreams with little payoffs.

✕ Remove Ads

Love: Money Heist

ENTJs are natural go-getters. They strive to make a difference and the only way to do such a thing is to make it happen themselves. This is primarily one of the biggest reasons ENTJs are groomed for leadership roles in the real world since they are one of the few personality types where such an important position isn't above their pay-grade.

RELATED: MBTI: 5 Period Dramas That ESTPs Will Love ( & 5 They Will Hate)

Wrong or right, Money Heist is about take-charge individuals, who come up with a plan and execute it to the best of their abilities.

Hate: The Last Kids On Earth

✕ Remove Ads

There are just some people who naturally enjoy spending their time being lost in their own little imagination land, full of all their dreams for the future. Sadly, this is a nightmare scenario for ENTJs, who prefer to spend out their days living in a reality-based world.

ENTJs have little patience for imagination which would make Netflix's The Last Kids on Earth a terrible fit for this personality type since it primarily deals with an irrational and over the top fantasy world that is in no way believable.
