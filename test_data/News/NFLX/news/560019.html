Here’s what’s new to watch on Netflix in May

Netflix and YouTube to reduce streaming quality in Europe to prevent the internet from collapsing due to the coronavirus outbreak.
By Kiersten Willis
April 23, 2020

Each month, Netflix announces a list of new movies and TV shows subscribers can watch on the platform. This month, new titles include some highly anticipated offerings in the form of original series and films.

Folks can tune into see Steve Carell return to the world of office life in his new new workplace comedy "Space Force." It comes to the service well before his former workplace sitcom, "The Office," exits the platform at the end of the year.

Additionally, the romantic comedy "Lovebirds" is heading to Netflix. The Issa Rae and Kumail Nanjiani-starring film was supposed to be released in theaters April 3, but concerns about the coronavirus pandemic pushed it to the streaming service.
