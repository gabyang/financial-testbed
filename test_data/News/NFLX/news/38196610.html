The TwilightSaga is one of the most popular franchises the entertainment industry has seen, and here’s where to stream each Twilight movie. While vampires had been portrayed as romantic figures prior to Stephanie Meyer's breakout series of teen fantasy books, going all the way back to Bram Stoker's Dracula, the author offered a very different spin on the creatures with her massive hit, Twilight. The Twilight books and movies follow Bella Swan, a teenage loner who falls in love with the mysterious Edward Cullen, who hides a shocking secret: he and his family are vampires, but unlike traditional vampires, they only drink animal blood and their skin sparkles under the sun.

✕ Remove Ads

The Twilight novels were a massive hit, so it was no surprise that they got film adaptations, with Kristen Stewart playing Bella Swan and Robert Pattinson bringing Edward Cullen to life. Although the Twilight movies weren’t a success with critics, they were a hit with fans of the novels and have now become part of pop culture. There’s an understandable sense of nostalgia in the Twilight Saga that makes the movies worth revisiting (along with the involuntary comedy in many scenes), and here’s where to stream each movie in the Twilight series, as well as where to buy and rent them online.

Where To Stream The Twilight Movies

Each book in Stephenie Meyer’s Twilight series got its movie adaptation, but the final novel, Breaking Dawn, was divided into two movies. That said, theTwilight Sagais formed by a total of five movies, chronicling Bella and Edward’s romance from the moment they met to their wedding and becoming parents. Thanks to the popularity of theTwilight Saga, all movies are available to stream, and here’s where to watch each Twilight movie:

Twilight

As Bella did her best to adapt to a new place, lifestyle, and school, she was instantly intrigued by the mysterious Edward Cullen, who seemed to not like her. Edward eventually confessed to Bella that he and his family are vampires, and though that put Bella in great danger, she chose to continue by his side.
