Best Netflix comedy movies, series and stand-up to watch on a Saturday night

From top rated movies like The Other Guy, must-watch sitcoms like Friends and The Office US to stand-up performances from comedy legends including Dave Chapelle and Chris Rock

    Today marks the third weekend of the latest national lockdown.

    And keeping us company as we stay in to save lives, are our TVs, smart devices and our Netflix subscriptions.

    Though it's hard to escape from the serious issues going on in the UK right now - there's one thing for certain.

    You can't go wrong with a comedy to bring some much-needed comic relief.

    From sitcoms and movies, to stand-up performances and wildly entertaining daredevil stunt series - Netflix has plenty of shows to give us a break from all the doom and gloom of the real world.

    There's never been a better time to catch up on must-watch box sets like Friends and The Office.

    Or if you prefer stand-up to sitcoms, settle in for a few performances from award-winning comedy legends including Chris Rock and Dave Chapelle.

    We've rounded up a series of must-see comedies that are ideal watches if you're in need of a good laugh.

    Comedians in Cars Getting Co ffee

    In Comedians in Cars Getting Coffee, we see comedy royalty Jerry Seinfeld joined by his A-list friends for a cup of coffee and a drive in a classic car.

    Sharing hilarious stories along the way, we hear tales from the a whole host of celebrities including Eddie, Murphy, Sarah Jessica-Parker, Barack Obama and Larry David.

    And there are plenty of the short episodes to work your way through.

    There are six seasons of the show to enjoy from the first series, First Cup, to the latest entitled, New 2019: Freshly Brewed.

    Dave Chapelle

    Dave Chapelle is an multiple award-winning American stand-up comedian, writer, actor and producer.

    He offers comedic observations in his distinguishable storytelling style touching on topics including politics, race, family and relationships, and world news.

    On Netflix, viewers have the choice to watch Dave Chapelle Sticks & Stones, two episodes of Chapelle and Dave Chapelle: Equanimity and The Bird Revelation.

    Derry Girls

    Amidst the political conflict in Norther Ireland, Derry Girls follows the lives of four high school students working their ways through the challenges of being teenagers.

    The series was created by Lisa McGee and stars Saoirse-Monica Jackson, Louisa Harland, Nicola Coughlan, Jamie-Lee O'Donnell.

    There is currently one series of Derry Girls - containing six episodes - available to stream on Netflix.

    Jackass

    Hilarious, exciting, and completely irresponsible, Jackass follows a group of friends and daredevils as they take on a series of adrenaline-pumping stunts that the show continues to advise viewers not to try at home.

    The cast includes the likes of Johnny Knoxville, Stephen 'Steve-O' Glover, Dave England, Bam Margera, Ehren McGhehey and Wee Man.

    With various stunts from super-gluing their bodies together and getting trapped inside a limo full of bees to manoeuvring across a tightrope over a pit full of alligators.

    On Netflix, viewers have the options to watch Jackass The Movie, Jackass Number 2, Jackass Unrated 2.5, Jackass 3, Jackass The Explicit Movie 3.5 and Jackass presents Bad Grandpa.

    Friends

    It may come as a surprise to many - but there are still people out there that are yet to watch hit 90s sitcom Friends.

    The hit show - which went on to receive global success - follows the stories of Monica, Rachel, Phoebe, Joey, Chandler and Ross.

    Across the world, die-hard Friends fans fell in love with the show's six lovable characters, hilarious lines and on-again-off-again love stories.

    As well as the main cast, throughout the seasons there are plenty of celebrity guest appearances from actors and actresses including Julia Roberts, Bruce Willis, Gabrielle Union, Brad Pitt, Susan Sarandon and many more.

    Tambourine: Chris Rock

    In Chris Rock's comedy special, Tambourine, we see the multi-award-winning comedian take the stage in Brooklyn to perform a series of searing observations on topics including American politics, infidelity and fatherhood.

    If you enjoy the show, you can also find Chris Rock in the feel-good comedy The Week Of which is also available to stream on Netflix.

    The Office (U.S.)

    The Office (U.S.) is the comedy 'mockumentary' TV series and adaptation of the BBC series of the same name.

    A documentary-making film crew follow the lives of a group of everyday co-workers and their tactless and childish yet ridiculously devoted boss Michael Scott, portrayed by Steve Carell.

    Throughout the nine series we see secret office romances and affairs, unlikely friendships, countless themed work parties - and Michael hit employee Meredith with his car.

    The Other Guys

    The Other Guys is an action adventure comedy with a star-studded cast list including Will Ferrell, Derek Jeter, Mark Wahlberg, Eva Mendes, Samuel L. Jackson and Dwayne Johnson.

    The plot follows two mismatched New York City detectives who seize an opportunity to step up like their idols - two their city's top cops.

    But things don't quite go to plan.

    The 2010 film - with a 1 hour 47 running time - went on to become a three-time award winner.
