How I Met Your Mother: 10 Ways The Show Got Worse & Worse

Over the last 20 years, the television industry has made some remarkable comedy sitcoms. The public continuously finds great pleasure in the likes of Friends, The Office, Veep,and Modern Family.There have also been some programs many wish they could erase from their minds ⁠— one of which is How I Met Your Mother.

RELATED: 12 Best Episodes of How I Met Your Mother

Back in 2005, fans tuned in to watch Ted Mosby tell his children the story of how he met their mother. Everyone was excited to see the woman who managed to steal the architect's heart. However, fans' opinions soon changed by the time the final season rolled around. But then, the show not only seemed dragged out, but fans also felt it got worse in several ways.

Barney's Playbook

First introduced in season 5, Barney's playbook was a book that contained strategies on how to pick up women. It caused much debate among the fans as the schemes became more sleazy and ridiculous.

✕ Remove Ads

For instance, in "The Playbook," Barney reveals that several women have fallen for the "My Penis Grants Wishes" play. Skip forward five years and Barney is still manipulating women with fanciful plays, such as 'The Mannequin.' or 'The Jim Nacho.' The more the book was included, the more creeped out by Barney the viewers became.

Marshall & Lily's Relationship

This may be a controversial entry, but Marshall and Lily's relationship was seen to grow worse as time went on. It has nothing to do with how perfect they fit together or their chemistry, but more with the fact that their relationship was built on lies and secrecy.

RELATED: How I Met Your Mother: 5 Times We Felt Bad For Marshall

In season 1, the cracks in Lily and Marshall's appeared once she hid her internship from him. In season 3, she hid a credit card debt. By season 9, history repeated itself when Marshall accepted a job without consulting her. It's surprising that they never divorced as their fights just became bigger and more monotonous.

✕ Remove Ads

Treatment Of Women

Many fans have also complained that the way the writers treated female characters got worse over the course of the series. When it came to the love interests of Ted, Barney, or Marshall, they were defined by three categories: unstable, ditzy, or "hot." Not once did the viewers ever hear the guys compliment a woman on her intelligence or her accomplishments.

It was also annoying that the writers minimized The Mother's role just as fans were learning what a funny and successful woman she was. Cutting off her development just emphasizes that several women of the show were used as simple plot devices.

Barney & Robin

✕ Remove Ads

Sadly, the relationship between Robin and Barney was also seen to get worse over time. From season 3 to 9, fans had the pleasure of watching the on-off relationship between these two fan-favorite characters. It was during these years the viewers saw Barney become considerate and Robin less guarded.

RELATED: 15 TV Shows For People Who Miss How I Met Your Mother

However, the writers decided to throw this development out the window. Despite season 9 being set at their wedding, the writers decided to split them up in the finale to have her reunite with Ted. What was even worse was that Barney regressed into a human man-child and Robin reverted back to her bitterness. It's no wonder the fans felt cheated...

Jokes & Gags

✕ Remove Ads

Although there were quite a few cute and memorable gags, there were also a few running jokes the fans wished the writers had left on the cutting room floor. For instance, some noticed how Robin's treatment of Patrice gets worse as the show goes on.

At first, fans found humor in their dynamic because Robin was reacting to the Barney and Nora situation. However, it started to irritate fans when Robin continued to mistreat Patrice for no reason. Even on her wedding day, Robin was seen to act completely rude towards her. It just got more cringey and uncomfortable.

Inconsistencies

Considering this is all from Ted's memory, the architect seems to be exaggerating his stories, as fans have found lots of inconsistencies within them.

✕ Remove Ads

For instance, in the "Rally" flash-forward, Marshall was shown to be bald. However, by "Last Forever," he had all of his hair. Similarly, the flashback in "Last Forever - Part 1" sees Robin say she had no friends yet the pilot shows her on a girls' night out. The little inconsistencies were confusing, to say the least.

Character Development

Most fans will agree that the gang's character development got a lot worse as the seasons went on. When the viewers first met the gang, they couldn't help but see what a good fit they were. They were loyal, caring, and extremely likable. However, this all changed once the writers began to develop their characters.

Lily became more manipulative and controlling; Robin became selfish and mean; and Barney became more sleazy and creepier. Even Marshall was a tad more selfish near the end.

✕ Remove Ads

Ted & Robin

Fans will also agree that Ted and Robin's relationship grew worse as the show progressed. While Robin had been Ted's love interest for the first two seasons, everyone believed the show was moving on once she got engaged to Barney. However, this was far from the case...

RELATED: How I Met Your Mother: 10 Ways Ted Got Worse & Worse

For years, Ted was seen pining for the news anchor even though she had chosen Barney over him. Robin even made it clear that she wasn't in love with Ted, which is why it came as a great surprise to all that her residual feelings came back in the finale. It was this constant yo-yo-ing that got annoying.

Ted's Lack Of Development

✕ Remove Ads

When the fans first got to know Ted, he was an optimistic and aspiring young architect. However, he was seen to regress once the writers confined him to his obsession with love.

Since Ted didn't meet Tracy until season 9, the viewers were forced to endure an endless cycle of whiny pessimism for years. It was quite ironic that Ted would moan about his failed relationships when he didn't take his own actions into account. He cheated on Victoria and ruined Zoey's marriage. Let's not forget his unwillingness to let Robin go. The fact that he fell into the same patterns shows he didn't grow.

The Lack Of The Mother

One of the ways the show became worse was through its concealment of The Mother. When fans first watched the show, they were excited to see who this mysterious woman was. However, the audience soon got bored with the storyline when the writers refused to reveal her identity seven years in.

✕ Remove Ads

When Tracy was first introduced, she was hardly featured. The writers rubbed salt in the wound further when it was revealed that the whole point of the story was to show Robin and Ted's love. Tracy deserved better than being used as a mere plot device.
