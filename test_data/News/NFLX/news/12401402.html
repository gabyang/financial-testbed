    Joel Schumacher dead: The Lost Boys and Batman director dies aged 80

    JOEL SCHUMACHER - who directed films including The Lost Boys and Batman - has died at the age of 80. The filmmaker died after battling cancer for a year.

    By Kathryn Ingate
    18:16, Mon, Jun 22, 2020 | UPDATED: 19:50, Mon, Jun 22, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    Joel Schumacher discusses the plot of Trespass in 2011

    Renowned film director Joel Schumacher has died at the age of 80. The filmmaker died on Monday morning in New York City after battling cancer for a year.



    Trending

      A spokesperson confirmed Joel’s death in a statement, which read: “Filmmaker Joel Schumacher, director of such films as 'St. Elmo's Fire', 'A Time to Kill', 'The Client', and 'Tigerland', passed away quietly from cancer this morning after a year-long battle.

      “He will be fondly remembered by his friends and collaborators.”

      He was best known for directing hit films including The Lost Boys and St. Elmo's Fire.

      Joel went on to take over from Tim Burton as director of the Batman franchise.

      He worked with Val Kilmer, Jim Carey and Nicole Kidman on Batman Forever, which was released in 1995 and grossed more than $300million worldwide.

      Joel then directed Batman & Robin in 1997 starring George Clooney in the lead role and Arnold Schwarzenegger starring as the film’s villain Mr Freeze.

      The star issued an apology in 2017 when his Batman sequel was not successful with critics and didn’t perform as well in the box office.

      He candidly said at the time: "Look I apologise.

      "I want to apologise to every fan that was disappointed because I think I owe them that."

      Joel’s directorial debut was in the sci-fi comedy The Incredible Shrinking Woman in 1981, which received positive reviews.

      However, he became well known in Hollywood with his work on hit movies St. Elmo's Fire, The Lost Boys, and Flatliners.

      He built a reputation of casting actors and giving them a big break into the film industry.

      The director worked with names including Colin Farrell, Matthew McConaughey and Keifer Sutherland early on in their careers.

      Joel also earned three Academy Award nominations with his adaptation of Andrew Lloyd Weber's musical, Phantom of the Opera.

      He most recently directed two episodes of Netflix’s series House of Cards in 2013.

      Schumacher started his career in the industry in the 1970s as a costume designer after graduating from the Fashion Institute of Technology in New York.

      The Lost Boys actor Alex Winter took to Twitter to pay tribute to the director following news of his death.

      “Joel was a creative genius; a master at clothing design, costuming, writing and of course directing," he wrote.

      "Joel saw something in me as an actor I didn’t see and gave me the confidence and space to pursue it.

      "Unfairly savaged by critics his entire career, his great work will live on.”

      Actor Ben Stiller also paid tribute on Twitter, saying: “He was kind, talented and made movies we went to the theatres for. A true professional, and a magnetic presence."
