HBO Max removes the available movie list after some aren’t actually available. Although the entertainment industry is struggling to deal with the long-reaching effects of the COVID-19 pandemic, streaming media has found considerable success as of late.

✕ Remove Ads

Perhaps one of the most successful businesses during this time has been Netflix, whose status as the top subscription-based streaming service has helped its profits to soar. And despite having their overall profits slashed by the pandemic, Disney has been faring very well with their own streaming service, Disney+. Having to stay home has made many people around the world reliant on these services and because of this, now seems like a great time for new streaming networks to launch. For months leading up to its release, HBO has been one of many networks working to get their own subscription streaming service off the ground. With its long history of considerable hits thanks to programming such as Sex and the City, Game of Thrones, and Westworld, HBO’s move into the streaming medium seemed like a natural one.

✕ Remove Ads

However, recent news courtesy of ComicBook.com has revealed that HBO’s new service, HBO Max, has removed their entire list of movie titles from the “Currently Available On HBO Max” section of their website. The move came after many of the films that HBO had promised subscribers did not actually turn up on the network. HBO Max officially kicked off on May 27 and although films such as Blade Runner, Annie Hall, Doctor Strangelove, Batman Begins, The Dark Knight Rises, The Matrix, Lethal Weapon 1-4 and The Texas Chainsaw Massacre were advertised as part of HBO Max’s extensive lineup, their absence is now quite clearly obvious.

✕ Remove Ads

A cynical perspective on this situation could determine that WarnerMedia took advantage of its customer base with the launch of HBO Max, drawing in subscribers with films that were never actually licensed to the network. At present, a specific reason for the lack of promised content hasn’t been given, but the fact that WarnerMedia was quick to pull the “Currently Available On HBO Max” page is evidence that the service has recognized its mistake and is currently taking steps to correct it. Of course, exactly when the previously promised titles will arrive on HBO Max – if ever, is unknown as of this writing. As things currently stand, HBO Max is one of the priciest subscription streaming networks available, making this newly revealed setback more than a little frustrating for its current subscribers.

✕ Remove Ads

In terms of value for money, this most recent debacle doesn’t bode well for HBO. It does seem that the often-celebrated network is continuing to deliver the bare minimum to its customers. This was also the case during the reign of physical media when HBO boxsets were notoriously some of the most expensive available, cheaply packaged, and suffered from a genuine lack of supplements. At the same time, HBO deserves the benefit of the doubt on their latest endeavor, but if this is a measure of the type of service they plan on providing, HBO Max won’t last long in such a highly competitive market place.
