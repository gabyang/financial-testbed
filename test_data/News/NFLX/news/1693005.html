Tiger King Joe Exotic's legal team to beg Trump for presidential pardon

Joe Exotic's legal team are currently travelling from Texas to Washington on the "Team Tiger" tour bus.

    Tiger King star Joe Exotic is set to beg Donald Trump to release him from his prison sentence with a presidential pardon.

    The 57-year-old’s legal team are travelling from Fort Worth, Texas, to The White House where they hope to catch the president’s attention.

    The latest move comes after it was revealed that Joe paid $1000 to meet Trump while he was on the campaign trail in Oklahoma City during the 2016 presidential campaign.

    The Netflix star is currently serving a 22 year stint behind bars after being found guilty of plotting to kill his nemesis rival zoo owner Carole Baskin.

    He was also convicted of animal abuse by killing tigers at his zoo to make room for more animals.

    Joe, whose real name is Joseph Allen Schreibvogel, made his own bid on the Oval Office by running as an independent with a libertarian manifesto four years ago.

    His campaign manager soon admitted that Joe didn't know what "libertarian" meant.

    Their push to free Joe isn't without precedent - Tiger King had everyone talking so much that even Trump weighed in, claiming he would "take a look" at Joe's conviction.

    His legal team are travelling to Washington on the “Team Tiger” tour bus which has a message to the president printed on its side.

    It reads: “President Trump Please Pardon Joe Exotic.”

    The team are travelling more than 1300 miles and have reportedly been getting lots of support from the TV star’s fans as they pass through Arkansas, Tennessee and Kentucky.

    It was recently revealed that the Tiger King has contracted coronavirus at Oklahoma’s Grady County Jail.

    He had been in self-isolation after the deadly virus was found in the prison but official reports later confirmed he had tested positive.
