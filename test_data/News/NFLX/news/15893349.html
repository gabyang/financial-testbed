Friends gave viewers a look at the personal lives of the main characters, but some of the supposedly important people in their lives ended up being forgotten after a while. Created by David Crane and Marta Kauffman, Friends made its debut on NBC in 1994 and came to an end in 2004 after 10 successful seasons. Friends continues to be quite popular, in big part thanks to its time at Netflix and now at HBO Max, which has introduced new generations to Phoebe, Monica, Chandler, Joey, Rachel, and Ross.

✕ Remove Ads

Friends followed the ups and downs of the aforementioned characters as they did their best to navigate through adulthood and juggle their personal, social, and professional lives. In 10 seasons, viewers got to meet their families and sentimental partners, of which some played big roles in their arcs at some point. Sadly, as the series progressed, many of these once important characters were left aside, to the point where they were completely forgotten in later seasons.

Related: Has Friends Really Aged Badly? We Don't Think So

Although the series gave closure to the main group, it failed to explain what happened to characters who either helped their arcs move forward or simply offered a deeper look at their family lives. Here’s every character Friends totally forgot about.

✕ Remove Ads

Ben Geller

Ben Geller was Ross and Carol’s son, and he appeared in the series many times, so viewers can say that they watched little Ben grow. However, he disappeared before his half-sister, Emma, was born, and was only mentioned a couple of times after that. It’s unknown what happened to him, but a popular fan theory suggests Ross lost custody of Ben, and Cole Sprouse has added to it saying that Ross wasn’t a present father.

Carol and Susan

✕ Remove Ads

On that note, Carol (Jane Sibbett) and her wife Susan (Jessica Hecht) also disappeared from the series. They were mentioned a couple of times after their final appearances, but Friends forgot about them after they were an important part in Ross’ arc in the first seasons. According to Jane Sibbett, Carol and Susan moved to London after sending Ben to a British boarding school, and they adopted more children.

Phoebe’s Parents

Phoebe had the most tragic and complicated backstory, as her mother killed herself, her stepfather went to jail, she ended up in the streets, and she never met her father. Phoebe later learned that her mother was actually her adoptive mother, and her biological one was the woman she thought was her parents’ best friend. Phoebe’s mother offered her guidance when she didn’t know if to be the surrogate for her half-brother’s baby or not, and that was the last time viewers saw Phoebe’s mother. As for her father, they met at the funeral of Phoebe’s grandmother, and though they tried to talk and connect, they were unable to. None of them were present at Phoebe’s wedding, so it’s unknown what happened to them.

✕ Remove Ads

Frank Jr, Alice, and the Triplets

Phoebe ended up agreeing to being the surrogate to Frank Jr. and Alice’s baby, and to the surprise of everyone, they had triplets. Alice was last seen in season 5’s episode “The One Hundredth”, where Phoebe gave birth to her babies, and Frank Jr’s final appearance was in season 10’s episode “The One Where Ross Is Fine”, where he and the triplets visited Phoebe. Frank confessed that he and Alice found it hard to raise three kids, and Phoebe told him she was available for babysitting whenever he needed. Frank Jr, Alice, and the triplets didn’t show up at Phoebe’s wedding either.

✕ Remove Ads

Joey’s Sisters

Joey mentioned a couple of times that he had seven sisters, and they all appeared together in one episode. His sister Dina appeared in season 8’s episode “The One With Monica’s Boots”, where she told Joey she was pregnant, but the series never mentioned her again. Gina Tribbiani showed up in the spinoff series Joey, as did Mary-Therese, who was renamed Mary-Theresa, but the rest of the Tribbiani sisters never appeared again.
