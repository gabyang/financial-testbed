Where is Josh Maja now? How Sunderland striker is faring at Bordeaux

We spoke to Goal's Bordeaux reporter Adrien Mathieu to get the lowdown on Maja's first year in the South of France

    Today saw the release of the latest series of Sunderland 'til I die on Netflix, and forward Josh Maja features quite heavily.

    After the indignity of back-to-back relegations, League One football offered Maja the opportunity to showcase his talents for the Black Cats first team on a regular basis.

    Maja seized that chance and scored 16 goals during the first half of the season as the Black Cats mounted a promotion charge.

    But with Maja's contract set to expire at the end of that season, teams from the Premier League, Championship, Scottish Premiership and La Liga circled amid the possibility of landing him on a free transfer.

    The documentary details the transfer saga in some detail. After his future was publicly played out, Sunderland offered Maja a new deal but, to everyone's surprise, it was French side Bordeaux who landed Maja for a nominal fee.

    The Black Cats decided to cash in and brought in Wigan Athletic's Will Grigg to replace him. Despite his League One pedigree, the Northern Ireland international struggled to emulate Maja's goal rate.

    Sunderland had to make do with the play-offs and they then missed out on automatic promotion by losing to Charlton in the play-off final.

    Many fans rued the decision to sell Maja and would have preferred Sunderland to lose Maja on a free transfer at the end of the season, so long as he continued to score the goals that would have sealed them promotion

    Instead, the youngster started his new life in South West France. It has been a mixed first year for him but when he has played, he has impressed and he has continued to score goals - including a hat-trick against Nimes last month.

    He could do with more regular game-time but his form has earned him a call-up to the Nigeria squad and reported interest from Chelsea.

    But how has Maja really done in his first year in Bordeaux? We spoke Bordeaux reporter Adrien Mathieu to find out the truth behind his first year abroad.

    One year on, how would you assess Maja's start to life at Bordeaux?

    "It wasn’t easy for him when he arrived, he had few times to play but he showed some good abilities after scoring his first goal against Nimes.

    "He started this season with a great goal against Montpellier but after that he didn’t play so much.

    "He had to wait Nimes again to score and he ended up getting a hat-trick.

    "He was amazing that night but it was against the worst defence of Ligue 1. It is expected he will play more now.

    Up until November, Maja largely struggled for game-time. Why is that?

    "Paulo Sousa preferred Jimmy Briand to play as centre-forward.

    "He counts on his experience but Briand isn’t a regular goalscorer and Maja can be a solution for that.

    "He can keep the ball and show his technique to help the team. He always knew how to seize opportunities."

    After four goals in five games, Maja is now Bordeaux's top scorer with seven goals. Do you expect him to end the season as Bordeaux's principal goal threat?

    "He can if he takes Briand's place, which is not at all obvious.

    "The latter wants to reach 100 goals in Ligue 1 and could even soon extend his contract (he is at 97).

    "On the other hand if Maja continues to be also invested, there is no doubt that he will end up becoming the main striker of Bordeaux."

    After one year, did you expect him to make more of an impact?

    "He has had little playing time. He may have to be a little more mobile in attack but what he has already done is not bad at all."

    How have the fans and media reacted to Maja's first year at Bordeaux?

    "The fans are happy about him and know that it will take some time to see him as a great forward.

    "He’s young and people will forgive him if he’s not good (against Marseille, for example.)"

    Describe Maja's relationship with Paulo Sousa. Does he suit his style of play?

    "It’s a strange relationship because Maja has the abilities to be his first forward but Sousa doesn’t give to him the keys.

    "He puts Briand on the field and some reports from French media said that Bordeaux is looking for a new forward (former Sunderland forward Connor Wickham from Crystal Palace was a target)."

    How has Maja adapted to life in France?

    "He arrived in France with a funny reputation, the 'Netflix player' with the documentary about his last club.

    "He was helped by some players to adapt well to life in Bordeaux.

    He does not speak perfect French yet, but the Bordeaux dressing room is quite international. He is rather at ease in the midst of his teammates."

    It's still relatively early but, in your opinion, has Maja's move been a success so far?

    "It’s difficult to say this but he was interesting when he played.

    "He always shows his determination and he scored some important goals. Nobody knew him when he arrived in France, so we can say that Bordeaux took a young player with real potential at least."

    What does the future hold for Maja at Bordeaux. How long do you feel he will remain at the club?

    "I think Paulo Sousa must trust him and give responsibilities for the second part of the season.

    "He can be important for the next months bu there i already some rumors he could go back to England.

    "I think it’s too early for him. He already has to prove it here in Bordeaux."
