11 top TV picks this week on RTE, BBC, Netflix, Virgin Media and more

Settle in for another week of great TV

    With another week of lockdown restrictions on the horizon, it's a good thing that there's plenty of TV to keep us entertained.

    On this note, here are some of the top TV picks for the upcoming week.

    Monday

    One Night With My Ex - Virgin Media Two at 9pm

    Brand new fly-on-the-wall documentary series in which three sets of former couples each spend one night together in a luxury flat to see if they should get back together or permanently go their separate ways.

    The Bay - Virgin Media One 9pm

    The Irish premiere of Season 2 sees DC Lisa Armstrong dealing with the repercussions of her actions from last year. DC Armstrong is given the opportunity to step up when asked to assist a murder investigation in Morecambe.

    Tensions remain high as Lisa struggles to do her job whilst following the orders of her previously lower-ranking colleague, Med. As the team tries to uncover why the victim was targeted,

    Lisa and her kids are unknowingly stalked by a mysterious figure, who has returned with an agenda. You can catch up on all of season one right now on the Virgin Media Player with all of Season Two landing on Monday.

    Euphoria: F*ck Anyone Who’s Not a Sea Blob - 9pm on Sky Atlantic

    Following December’s special focusing on Zendaya’s character Rue, the critically-acclaimed drama returns with another standalone episode.

    This special, set over the Christmas holidays, follows Hunter Schafer’s Jules as she reflects on her year.

    Tuesday

    Snowpiercer Season 2- Netflix

    This TV reboot of Parasite director Bong Joon-ho’s 2013 brilliant post-apocalyptic film about class warfare on a speeding train arrives for a second season.

    The arrival of Sean Bean’s Mr Wilford, the train’s creator and billionaire overlord, will certainly mix things up.

    Wednesday

    Finnè - 9.30 on TG4

    A deeply honest insight into the experiences of Brian Pennie, who grew up in Ladyswell, on Dublin's north side. As a teenager he witnessed alcohol and drug abuse, and he was a heroin addict for 15 years of his life.

    50m2 - Netflix

    After betraying his bosses, a hitman hides out in a vacant tailor shop, where he's mistaken for the late owner's son, an identity he decides to embrace.

    Netflix will be hoping for another foreign-language hit like Narcos and Money Heist with this one.

    Friday

    WandaVision on Disney +

    After the first two episodes really took the time to introduce the bizarre sitcom setup, the third episode really upped the ante and gave viewers a sense of the larger mystery and menace that's haunting Vision and Wanda.

    We have a feeling that the fourth episode will unravel some of the secrets that lie beyond Westville and how the show is setting up some of the events that will unfold in Phase 4 of the MCU.

    Hip Hop: The Songs That Shook America - 10pm on BBC4

    A celebration of the genre’s most formative tracks, produced by the Roots founders Questlove and Black Thought.

    Over the course of tHE series, the songs analysed by the experts include Kanye West’s Jesus Walks, Kendrick Lamar’s Alright, and Ladies First by Queen Latifah.

    The Late Late Show - 9.35pm on RTE1

    The national institution returns for another episode as Tubridy chats about the most important topics in Irish society.

    The Graham Norton Show - 23.15 on BBC1

    Hollywood star Sam Neill joins graham this week to chat about his new comedy-drama Rams and he'll be joined by Billie Piper, talking about her film Rare Beasts, which she wrote, directed and stars in.

    Elsewhere, Cold Feet star James Nesbitt will talk about his new Northern Ireland-set BBC series Bloodlands. Comedian Andi Osho will also be on the couch and music legend Tom Jones is also dropping in.

    Saturday

    The Tommy Tiernan Show - 9.30pm on RTE1

    After Zak Moradi's wonderful chat this week, we can't wait to see who the comedian will be talking to on Saturday.
