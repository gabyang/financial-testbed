    Kissing Booth 3 release date: When is Kissing Booth 3 out?

    KISSING BOOTH 3 may have seemed a distant dream to fans - but it turns out the third film has been in the works all along. When is Kissing Booth 3 out?

    By Jenny Desborough
    07:52, Tue, Jul 28, 2020 | UPDATED: 07:53, Tue, Jul 28, 2020
    Link copied Bookmark

    The Kissing Booth 3: Joey King stars in trailer for movie

    In the grand tradition of the movie business, if a film does well, there is often a reboot, sequel or spin-off waiting in the wings. Netflix recently released The Kissing Booth 2, the second instalment in their newly-created Kissing Booth franchise. Fans who were excited about this have now even more reason to tune into the platform, as a third movie has reportedly already been filmed.

    Trending

      When is The Kissing Booth 3 out?

      While fans were hoping and wishing there would be a third movie of The Kissing Booth, it turns out Netflix was way ahead.

      In fact, the third instalment of the movie franchise has already been announced, and most likely, already been filmed.

      The third film had its trailer released just as The Kissing Booth 2 hit the platform, with a release date of 2021 for the third instalment.

      While we have no idea when in 2021 the movie will be released, given The Kissing Booth 2 had a July release date, it makes sense the third movie will come out a year later.

      The Kissing Booth is based on a book from British authour Beth Reekles, who has, at present, only written two of the books.

      As a result, it is hard to tell what the third movie will be about, and who has been involved in writing it.

      However, Reekles announced on her Twitter that she had been keeping this a secret, implying she has been just as involved.

      "I’m totally over the moon that we’re doing a third one (and yes, with all the same cast!)

      "It’s been tough keeping this secret from you all but thank you for all the lovely messages about #TheKissingBooth3! Your support is everything to me"

      In the trailer, Elle (played by Joey King) is seen hanging out at the pool with her beau, Noah (Jacob Elordi) her best friend Lee (Joel Courtney) and Lee's girlfriend Rachel (Meganne Young.)

      Suddenly, her phone rings and she finds the team at Harvard University is calling her, but she quickly turns off the call.

      Fans of the franchise will know she and her bestie Lee have a pact to go to Berkeley together, a college in California.

      However her new boyfriend, Noah, is at Harvard, meaning she has a tough decision to make between her friendship or her relationship.

      When asked who the phonecall was from, she replies "nothing," instead tempting her boyfriend to the bedroom to avoid the discussion further.

      Evidently, The Kissing Booth 3 will see Elle trying to make this mammoth decision between Noah and Lee, though her relationship with Noah has already been tested.

      Of course, The Kissing Booth 2 has been a huge success for Netflix, with fans taking to social media to share their excitement at its arrival on the platform.

      There is no doubt the third film will create the same hysteria, and fans will just have to wait to find out what is in store for the characters.
