A new speedrunning world record has just been set for The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, and it's the fastest one ever by a large margin. Despite the postponement of the annual Summer Games Done Quick event, the speedrunning community hasn't lost any momentum and recently raised over $400,000 for coronavirus relief.

✕ Remove Ads

Ocarina of Time is one of the preeminent games in the speedrunning community. While the rest of the Legend of Zelda games have helped shape speedrunning, the groundbreaking N64 classic may still be the most popular. For years, speedrunners have been figuring out new and exciting ways to break Ocarina of Time in the endless quest to shave a few more seconds off their total run time. In the process, they've discovered many secrets hidden in the game's code, like how to spawn a Star Fox 64 Arwing without even using cheats.

Related: Doom Eternal Devs Have No Idea How Speedrunners Broke Their Game

Now there's a brand new world record speedrun for Ocarina of Time, according to Reddit. Speedrunner ZFG has shattered the previous world record for fastest 100% completion time by a whopping 13 seconds. In speedrunning time, that's a massive difference. ZFG's total time was 3:43:44, taking the record back from former champ Glitchymon.

✕ Remove Ads

The Reddit post states that this was ZFG's first completed run since his return to the game's 100% category, making it even more impressive. He was also blessed with great RNG at important junctures, like the fishing minigame and the underground race against Dampe the gravedigger's ghost (which only took him one attempt compared to Glitchymon's nine attempts). On the other hand, ZFG couldn't pull off the "collapse skip" exploit correctly at the very end of his run, which cost him dearly and added several minutes to his total. Even then, it ended up being a historic speedrun and ZFG will likely make up the difference in an even faster run soon.

Speedrunning continues to be one of the coolest elements of modern gaming. It's always fascinating to see speedrunners deconstruct all kinds of games in increasingly clever ways, even speedrunning demos of games. The community's work in game preservation and their major contributions to charitable causes have also had a positive impact in the industry and the world at large, raising general interest in video games even higher.
