Tesla China Sales Rise as Government Subsidies Lift Sentiment

Published: 2024-09-03 02:08:15 ET TSLA

By Jiahui Huang

Tesla's sales in China rose last month as more generous government subsidies for replacing old cars with electric or hybrid ones boosted demand in the world's largest electric-vehicle market.

The U.S. EV maker sold 86,697 China-made cars in August, preliminary data from the China Passenger Car Association showed late Monday, up 3.0% from a year earlier and 17% higher than the previous month.

Tesla's China sales strengthened despite still-subdued consumption in China, driven by recently doubled subsidies for purchases of EVs or hybrids to replace some old passenger cars, the association said.

Applications for government subsidies rose sharply from a month earlier, and sales of EVs and hybrids likely boosted China's overall auto sales for August, the CPCA said.

More than 800,000 applications have been submitted for the government's trade-in subsidies as of August, official data showed.

Tesla's China sales growth, however, still lagged behind its major Chinese competitors, which reported double-digit sales growth in the world's largest auto market.

Chinese EV giant BYD sold a record 370,854 units in August, 35% higher than a year earlier and the sixth straight month sales exceeded 300,000 vehicles. Hybrid specialist Li Auto delivered 48,122 units, up 38%.

Geely Automobile remained in third place, with sales of 75,484 units. Chongqing Changan Automobile took fourth place with 49,000 units.

The CPCA estimated that total sales of electric passenger cars in China last month rose 32% from a year earlier to about 1.05 million units, an 11% increase from July.

Sales of EVs and hybrids in China likely continued to surpass those of conventional cars in August, it said.
