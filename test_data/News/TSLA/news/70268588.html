Wall Street in Fives - Must Read Lists for Thursday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these Top Fives, as compiled by the editors of The Fly.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Baxter (BAX) agreed to acquire Hill-Rom (HRC) for $156 per share in cash [more]
  • Moderna (MRNA) has submitted initial data to the FDA for a booster shot for its COVID-19 vaccine [more]
  • Ford (F) reported a 33.1% year-over-year decline in U.S. vehicle sales in August [more]
  • Apple (AAPL) to allow "reader" apps to link to external website for payments in 2022 [more]
  • Virgin Galactic (SPCE) shares slid after the FAA banned the company from flying its SpaceShipTwo pending a probe into the company's July 11 space flight [more]

2. TOP CALLS:

  • AB InBev (BUD) downgraded to Underweight from Neutral at JPMorgan [more]
  • Unilever (UL) was also downgraded to Underweight from Neutral at JPMorgan [more]
  • Deutsche Bank initiated coverage of Starbucks (SBUX) with a Hold rating [more]
  • Okta (OKTA) upgraded to Buy from Hold at Needham [more]
  • Teradata (TDC) was initiated with a Buy rating at Craig-Hallum [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • The DOJ is preparing an antitrust suit against Google (GOOGL) over advertising technology, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Ford (F) is once again cutting F-150 production amid a chip shortage, CNBC reports [more]
  • General Motors (GM) plans to reduce North American production amid chip shortage, CNBC says [more]
  • Apple (AAPL) is in talks with Korean, Japanese suppliers over its Apple Car, DigiTimes reports [more]
  • Tesla (TSLA) has temporarily halted some operations at its Shanghai plant, Bloomberg says [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • Marathon Digital (MARA) higher after announcing an expanded collaboration with NYDIG [more]
  • Atossa Therapeutics (ATOS) increases after announcing it received approval from the Swedish Ethics Review Authority to begin a Phase 2 clinical study of oral Endoxifen [more]
  • Nutanix (NTNX) and ChargePoint Holdings (CHPT) gain after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Hall of Fame Resort & Entertainment (HOFV) lower after postponing the Highway 77 Music Festival due to an increase in COVID-19 cases in Ohio [more]
  • C3.ai (AI) and Smith & Wesson Brands (SWBI) decline after reporting quarterly results [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDACE:

  • American Eagle (AEO) reported upbeat Q2 earnings [more]
  • Chewy (CHWY) reported lower than expected Q2 revenue [more]
  • Ciena (CIEN) reported better than expected Q3 results [more]
  • Signet Jewelers (SIG) provided a "beat and raise" report [more]
  • Hormel Foods (HRL) lowered its FY21 earnings guidance [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 131.29, or 0.37%, to 35,443.82, the Nasdaq gained 21.80, or 0.14%, to 15,331.18, and the S&P 500 advanced 12.86, or 0.28%, to 4,536.95.
