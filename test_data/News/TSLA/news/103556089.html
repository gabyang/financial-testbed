What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Block (SQ) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Coinbase (COIN) reported upbeat Q4 revenue, though noted that Q1 trading volume is expected to be lower [more]
  • KAR Auction (KAR) agreed to sell its ADESA U.S. Physical auction business to Carvana (CVNA) for $2.2B [more]
  • Foot Locker (FL) reported better-than-expected Q4 results, but sunk after its worse-than-expected profit outlook for FY22 [more]
  • Zscaler (ZS) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q2 [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Tesla (TSLA) upgraded to Outperform from Neutral at Daiwa [more]
  • LivePerson (LPSN) downgraded by no less than nine firms after earnings [more]
  • Dish (DISH) upgraded to Overweight from Underweight at JPMorgan [more]
  • Benchmark initiated coverage of Curaleaf (CURLF) with a Buy rating and $12 price target [more]
  • Evercore ISI upgraded Carvana (CVNA) to Outperform following the acquisition of KAR Global's (KAR) ADESA [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Zendesk (ZEN) shareholders have voted to reject the company's proposed acquisition of Momentive (MNTV), CNBC reports [more]
  • An Activision Blizzard (ATVI) shareholder is suing the company over its proposed takeover by Microsoft (MSFT), Polygon reports [more]
  • Constellation (STZ) and Monster Beverage (MNST) deal talks are moving more slowly than previously implied, CNBC says [more]
  • Federal judge upholds Johnson & Johnson's (JNJ) talc-related bankruptcy strategy, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Electronic Arts (EA) CEO Andrew Wilson said the company has been "impeded" by the FIFA license, VGC reports [more]


4. MOVERS:

  • Aerie Pharmaceuticals (AERI) rises after reporting quarterly results and naming Gary Sternberg chief medical officer [more]
  • Playtika (PLTK) jumps after reporting upbeat Q4 results and announcing it is exploring strategic alternatives [more]
  • CarGurus (CARG), U.S. Silica (SLCA), and Eos Energy (EOSE) higher after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Latch (LTCH) falls after Cantor Fitzgerald downgraded the stock to Neutral from Overweight [more]
  • Everbridge (EVBG), Stem (STEM), and Opendoor Technologies (OPEN) lower after reporting quarterly results [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Etsy (ETSY) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Beyond Meat (BYND) reported downbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Ocugen (OCGN) reported downbeat Q4 earnings [more]
  • E.W. Scripps (SSP) reported lower-than-expected Q4 results [more]
  • VMware (VMW) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]


INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was up 703.07, or 2.12%, to 33,926.90, the Nasdaq was up 188.88, or 1.40%, to 13,662.47, and the S&P 500 was up 85.46, or 1.99%, to 4,374.16.
