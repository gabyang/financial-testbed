S.Korea's LG Energy Solution prices IPO at top of range to raise $10.8 bln -sources

Published: 2022-01-14 00:23:06 ET TSLA

Sydney, Jan 14 (Reuters) - South Korean battery maker LG Energy Solution (LGES) has priced its initial public offering at the top end of its indicative range, raising 12.8 trillion won , two people with direct knowledge of the matter told Reuters on Friday.

Shares of LGES - which supplies electric vehicle (EV) batteries to Tesla - were priced at 300,000 won, compared with a range of 257,000 - 300,000 won per share it announced in a regulatory filing last month. (Reporting by Scott Murdoch in Sydney and Heekyong Yang in Seoul; Editing by Jacqueline Wong)
