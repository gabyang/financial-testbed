War-induced surge in materials forces Tesla to increase prices

Published: 2022-03-16 02:21:08 ET TSLA

A SURGE in raw material costs induced by the Ukrainian war is continuing to hit the global automotive market, causing giants such as Tesla to increase prices.

The Austin-based car maker was yesterday forced to increase its Chinese and US car prices for the second time in a matter of days, following a surge in the price of key metals.

Tesla reported that all its US models will see prices increase between 5 and 10 per cent, while in China Model 3 and Model Y rose 5 per cent.

"Tesla and Space X are seeing a significant recent inflation pressure in raw material and logistics," the company's billionaire boss Elon Musk tweeted. "And we are not alone."

The prices of materials such as aluminium, palladium and nickel have increased dramatically following the Kremlin's invasion of Ukraine.
