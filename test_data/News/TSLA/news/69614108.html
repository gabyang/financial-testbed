Wall Street in Fives - Must Read Lists for Tuesday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these Top Fives, as compiled by the editors of The Fly.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Canadian Pacific (CP) submitted a revised, increased proposal to acquire Kansas City Southern (KSU) [more]
  • AMC Entertainment (AMC) reported better than expected Q2 results [more]
  • Robinhood (HOOD) will acquire Say Technologies for roughly $140M [more]
  • RealReal (REAL) reported downbeat Q2 results [more]
  • Pershing Square (PSHZF) has acquired an initial stake in Universal Music Group from Vivendi (VIVHY) [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Sohu.com (SOHU) upgraded to Buy from Hold at Jefferies [more]
  • BofA says buy Walmart (WMT) ahead of second-half catalysts [more]
  • Bluebird Bio (BLUE) downgraded to Neutral at Goldman Sachs, to Equal Weight at Wells Fargo and to Hold at Canaccord [more]
  • SmileDirectClub (SDC) downgraded at JPMorgan, William Blair, Stephens and Craig-Hallum after "obviously disappointing" Q2 report [more]
  • 3D Systems (DDD) downgraded to Hold at Craig-Hallum after "outsized" move seen driven by "Reddit traders" [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Apple (AAPL) could ship a refreshed MacBook Air in mid-2022, AppleInsider reports [more]
  • Marsh (MMC) collaborates with Amazon (AMZN) on Amazon Insurance Accelerator, IBA reports [more]
  • Tesla (TSLA) China shipments fell 69% in July month-over-month, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • NBC (CMCSA) drew the lowest TV audience ever for the Tokyo Olympics, WSJ reports [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) cloud executive Charlie Bell is leaving the company, CNBC says [more]

4. ﻿MOVERS:

  • Lightning eMotors (ZEV) gains after entering into a strategic partnership agreement with Forest River, a Berkshire Hathaway (BRK.A;BRK.B) company, to deploy up to 7,500 zero-emission shuttle buses [more]
  • Arcturus Therapeutics (ARCT) and Lufax (LU) higher in New York after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Yalla Group (YALA) falls in New York trading after Oppenheimer analyst Bo Pei lowered the firm’s price target on the stock following Q2 results [more]
  • Katapult (KPLT), Metromile (MILE), and Vuzix (VUZI) lower after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Fulcrum Therapeutics (FULC) share price doubles after company reports on trial of potential sickle cell disease treatment [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Jumia Technologies (JMIA) reported quarterly revenue that missed the consensus forecast [more]
  • Sysco (SYY) provided a "beat and raise" report [more]
  • Kornit Digital (KRNT) reported upbeat Q2 revenue [more]
  • Planet Fitness (PLNT) reported lower than expected quarterly earnings [more]
  • Coinbase (COIN) is set to report quarterly results after the market close [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 162.82, or 0.46%, to 35,264.67, the Nasdaq lost 72.09, or 0.49%, to 14,788.09, and the S&P 500 advanced 4.40, or 0.099%, to 4,436.75.
