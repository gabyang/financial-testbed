What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Costco (COST) reported upbeat Q2 results [more]
  • Gap (GPS) reported better than expected Q4 losses and gave upbeat fiscal year guidance [more]
  • Microsoft (MSFT) suspended new sales in Russia [more]
  • Disney+ (DIS) will introduce a new ad-supported subscription offering in 2022 [more]
  • Hellman & Friedman has taken a 7.5% activist stake in Splunk (SPLK) [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Deutsche downgrades C3.ai (AI) to Sell, says "all is not well" [more]
  • Six Flags (SIX) upgraded to Buy from Hold at Deutsche Bank [more]
  • Sea Limited (SE) downgraded to Neutral on near-term losses at JPMorgan [more]
  • Raymond James downgrades Best Buy (BBY) on "very challenging" setup [more]
  • Advantage Solutions (ADV) downgraded to Neutral from Buy at Goldman Sachs [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Tesla (TSLA) is set to receive approval for its Berlin gigafactory, Reuters reports [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) has set a deadline for the FTC to challenge its MGM acquisition, WSJ reports [more]
  • New York Times (NYT) tech workers voted in favor to certify a union, NY Times reports [more]
  • Google Cloud (GOOGL) is terminating "dozens" of support roles, Business Insider reports [more]
  • Shake Shack (SHAK) is offering bitcoin as a reward for Cash App (SQ) users, WSJ says [more]


4. MOVERS: 

  • Kroger (KR) continues higher after yesterday's earnings beat [more]
  • Broadcom (AVGO) rises after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Ocugen (OCGN) declines after announcing the FDA has declined to issue an Emergency Use Authorization for Covaxin to treat individuals 2 to 18 years of age [more]
  • ING Groep (ING) lower in New York after providing an update on Russia, Ukraine [more]
  • Enovix (ENVX) and Smith & Wesson Brands (SWBI) fall after reporting quarterly results [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Duolingo (DUOL) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Sweetgreen (SG) provided an upbeat FY22 revenue outlook [more]
  • Funko (FNKO) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]
  • Hibbett (HIBB) reported downbeat Q4 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Ooma (OOMA) reported upbeat Q4 results [more]


INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was down 455.77, or 1.35%, to 33,338.89, the Nasdaq was down 280.71, or 2.07%, to 13,257.23, and the S&P 500 was down 69.06, or 1.58%, to 4,294.43.
