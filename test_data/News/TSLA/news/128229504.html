Tesla May Raise Model 3 Price in Europe Due to EU Tariffs on EVs From China -- Update

Published: 2024-06-13 09:48:14 ET TSLA

By David Sachs

Tesla will likely raise the price of its Model 3 in Europe in response to planned EU tariffs on electric vehicles made in China.

Elon Musk's electric-car company posted the news on its European websites Thursday.

"We're anticipating a requirement for us to increase pricing for Model 3 vehicles as of 1 July 2024," a notification on the sites said. "This is due to additional import duties likely to be imposed on electric vehicles manufactured in China and sold in the EU."

The European Commission outlined the extra tariffs on Wednesday in response to what it said were excessive subsidies by the Chinese government that were undermining competition with European carmakers. The tariffs, which are scheduled to start in early July, currently range between 17% and 38%, with some companies receiving customized rates based on the outcome of the EU's antisubsidy probe.

Tesla, which imports cars to Europe from China, may receive a lower duty rate than other manufacturers, according the EU. The company claimed that it received lower subsidies from the Chinese government than other car makers, Bernstein analysts said in a note, adding that a lower duty--or even no new import fee--in the country is possible.

The Model 3 currently has a base price of 40,990 euros ($44,262). Tesla didn't specify the rate of a potential increase.

Tesla's Shanghai factory has the capacity to produce more than 950,000 Model 3s and Model Ys, according to a Tesla slide deck conveying its first-quarter results. The company doesn't produce the Model 3 in Europe, according to the presentation. Tesla delivered 386,810 of those two models in the first quarter, down 9% on year.
