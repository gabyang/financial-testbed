What You Missed On Wall Street On Friday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Wells Fargo (WFC) reported lower-than-expected Q2 revenue, though CEO Charlie Scharf said underlying results reflect "improving earnings capacity" [more]
  • Citi (C) reported better-than-expected Q2 earnings and revenue, with CFO Mark Mason saying the company will pause share repurchases on capital rules [more]
  • Activist investor Elliott has built a stake of over 9% in Pinterest (PINS), according to The Wall Street Journal [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) will begin drone delivery in Texas [more]
  • ISS formally recommended that Spirit (SAVE) shareholders vote against the Frontier (ULCC) deal, saying the JetBlue (JBLU) offer appears more attractive [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Qualcomm (QCOM) upgraded to Buy from Hold at Edward Jones [more]
  • Argus cuts Delta Air Lines (DAL) to Hold on weaker outlook [more]
  • Yelp (YELP) assumed with Neutral from Buy at Goldman Sachs [more]
  • Stifel upgraded "cheapest" rail stocks CSX (CSX) and Norfolk Southern (NSC) to Buy from Hold [more]
  • Rackspace Technology (RXT) downgraded to Underweight from Equal Weight at Barclays [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • A judge has set a July 19 hearing in Twitter's (TWTR) lawsuit against Elon Musk, Reuters reports [more]
  • Tesla (TSLA) has asked a U.S. court to dismiss a lawsuit over mass layoffs, Reuters says [more]
  • Volkswagen (VWAGY) maintained its 2022 sales target in China, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Taiwan is debating fining Foxconn (HNHPF) over its investment in Tsinghua, Reuters reports [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) reduces private label selection, considers exiting business, WSJ reports [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • Vertical Aerospace (EVTL) flies higher after announcing that that Flyinggroup has conditionally pre-ordered up to 50 VX4 aircraft and that American Airlines (AAL) has reserved delivery slots for the first 50 VX4 aircraft of its initial conditional pre-order [more]
  • Vonage (VG) rises after disclosing that it and Ericsson (ERIC) have now satisfied all requisite foreign and U.S. regulatory requirements for the closing of their merger [more]
  • Disney (DIS) increases after raising its ESPN+ subscription price to $9.99 per month from $6.99 [more]
  • Codexis (CDXS) falls after cutting its FY22 revenue guidance [more]
  • 10x Genomics (TXG) lower after providing downbeat Q2 revenue guidance [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • UnitedHealth (UNH) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q2 [more]
  • PNC Financial (PNC) reported a year-over-year increase in Q2 earnings and reiterated its FY22 revenue guidance [more]
  • BNY Mellon (BK) reported downbeat Q2 earnings [more]
  • BlackRock (BLK) reported lower-than-expected Q2 results [more]
  • State Street (STT) reported downbeat Q2 revenue [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 658.09, or 2.15%, to 31,288.26, the Nasdaq gained 201.24, or 1.79%, to 11,452.42, and the S&P 500 advanced 72.78, or 1.92%, to 3,863.16.
