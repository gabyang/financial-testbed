Wall Street in Fives - Must Read Lists at Midday
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these Top Fives, as compiled by the editors of The Fly.


1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Intel (INTC) reported better-than-expected Q2 results but analysts concerned by guidance [more]
  • Twitter (TWTR) reported upbeat Q2 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Snap (SNAP) reported a surprise profit for Q2 as revenue grew 116% year-over-year [more]
  • Magna (MGA) will acquire Veoneer (VNE) for $31.25 per share in a $3.8B deal [more]
  • General Motors (GM) announced a recall of 69,000 Chevy Bolt electric vehicles over a potential fire risk [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Baird upgrades Starbucks (SBUX) to Outperform with $142 price target [more]
  • Monster Beverage (MNST) upgraded to Buy from Neutral at Citi [more]
  • Domino's Pizza (DPZ) downgraded to Neutral from Overweight at JPMorgan [more]
  • Boston Beer (SAM) downgraded to Neutral from Buy at Goldman Sachs [more]
  • Alphabet (GOOGL) price target raised to $3,350, Facebook (FB) price target raised to $480 at Credit Suisse [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Tesla (TSLA) has put a hold on delivering a new Model S, Electrek reports [more]
  • Carlyle Group (CG) seeks to raise up to $27B for a new fund, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • P.F. Chang's (PFCB) is in early talks with advisors about an IPO, Bloomberg says [more]
  • BlackRock (BLK) and Canaccord (CCORF) are in advanced talks to buy Bridging, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Vodafone (VOD) CEO Nick Read teaased a spinoff of M-Pesa, according to Bloomberg [more]


4. MOVERS:

  • Alzamend Neuro (ALZN) rises after announcing it received ‘positive’ toxicology results for AL002 in a good laboratory practices study [more]
  • Trade Desk (TTD) higher after Stephens upgraded the stock to Overweight from Equal Weight with an $85 price target [more]
  • Moderna (MRNA) increases after the European Medicines Agency's Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use recommended market authorization for its COVID-19 vaccine to include adolescents 12 years and older [more]
  • Gaotu Techedu (GOTU), New Oriental Education & Technology (EDU), TAL Education Group (TAL), and 17 Education & Technology Group (YQ) drop in New York trading after Bloomberg reported China may require tutoring companies to go non-profit [more]
  • Adverum Biotechnologies (ADVM) falls after announcing it no longer has plans for the future development of ADVM-022 for diabetic macular edema [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Boston Beer (SAM) reported downbeat Q2 results and cut guidance [more]
  • Honeywell (HON) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q2 [more]
  • Kimberly-Clark (KMB) lowered its FY21 adjusted earnings guidance [more]
  • American Express (AXP) reported upbeat Q2 results [more]
  • NextEra Energy (NEE) provided earnings guidance for FY21 and FY22 [more]


INDEXES:

Near midday, the Dow was up 207.23, or 0.60%, to 35,030.58, the Nasdaq was up 113.36, or 0.77%, to 14,797.95, and the S&P 500 was up 35.83, or 0.82%, to 4,403.31.
