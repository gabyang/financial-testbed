Tesla reports sequentially lower quarterly gross margin

Published: 2023-07-19 16:05:27 ET TSLA

July 19 (Reuters) - Tesla said its gross margin fell in the second quarter from the previous three months, squeezing the electric-vehicle maker's efforts to boost sales through price cuts.

The company on Wednesday reported gross margin of 18.2% for the April-June period, compared with 19.3% for the first quarter. (Reporting by Akash Sriram in Bengaluru; Editing by Shounak Dasgupta)
