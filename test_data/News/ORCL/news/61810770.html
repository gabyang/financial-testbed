A Deep Dive Into nCino and the Silicon Valley Tech Exodus

December 21, 2020 — 03:12 pm EST

Written by Matthew Frankel for The Motley Fool->

In this episode of Industry Focus: Financials,host Jason Moser and Fool.com contributor Matt Frankel, CFP, take a deep dive into banking SaaS company nCino (NASDAQ: NCNO). Then, Frankel discusses why tech companies like Oracle (NYSE: ORCL) and Hewlett Packard (NYSE: HPE) are leaving Silicon Valley and how investors can play it. Finally, Frankel discusses why leading self-storage operator Public Storage (NYSE: PSA) is on his radar this week, while Moser has his eye on Korn Ferry (NYSE: KFY).
