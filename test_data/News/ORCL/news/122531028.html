Oracle reports quarterly revenue narrowly below estimates

Published: 2023-09-11 16:09:55 ET ORCL

Sept 11 (Reuters) - Oracle narrowly missed market expectations for first-quarter revenue on Monday, as companies pared back spending on cloud services due to an uncertain economic outlook.

Revenue for the quarter stood at $12.45 billion, slightly below analysts' estimates of $12.47 billion, according to LSEG data. (Reporting by Akash Sriram in Bengaluru; Editing by Devika Syamnath)
