Wix recently launched a suite of new AI-powered tools designed to help users produce more, higher quality blog content. These tools leverage AI, generating ideas, text, and images for use in blog posts.

Wix is one of the most comprehensive website builders we’ve encountered, and this is no different when it comes to small business websites. With a wide range of features, it’s no wonder Wix is the go-to platform for over 200 million registered users.

Tools and features

Wix’s website editor operates under the simple drag-and-drop principle, offering hundreds of different attractive design choices and customization tools. It also includes features such as an image editor, animations, video backgrounds, and proprietary and third-party apps.

Wix has also recently launched its full AI website builder, which helps small businesses create websites in record time. You start by answering a few simple questions about your business. The AI tool then uses the information you provide to create a full website, complete with images and text. Once you have reviewed your generated site, you can then use the Wix website editor to make all the necessary changes and tweaks.

Even when doing the required tweaks, we found that using the AI website builder drastically sped up the website building process, making it ideal for time strapped small businesses that want to build a great looking website with little time investment. Plus, as Wix's AI website builder creates a bespoke option, it helps overcome the issues of limited template customization on Wix. Wix Studio is another new addition to the Wix platform and allows users access to advanced tools for designing website and working with clients. It is totally free for all Wix users, making it one of the best website builders for agencies.

Finally, small businesses can take advantage of additional tools like Wix Turbo, Wix Bookings, Wix Restaurants, and Wix Hotels. This makes it ideal for businesses that want simple, yet powerful tools to support their business operations.

Alternatives

If you don't need all the advanced tools offered by Wix, you can save yourself a small amount of money by opting for Weebly instead. Paid plans start at $10/mo and although not as advanced as Wix, Weebly still provides you with a host of powerful website building tools and features.

You can read our comparison of Wix vs Weebly to learn more.

Alternatively, if you're in the market for an advances AI website builder, but aren't convinced by Wix's price tag you may want to check out Hostinger. We have covered a full comparison of the two in our Wix AI vs Hostinger AI guide.

Plans and pricing

With the free edition, you'll get a basic service with limited bandwidth and storage (500MB) and you’ll have to put up with ads on your website.

Opting for any of the paid plans - such as the Lite package at $17 per month (with a yearly subscription) - will get you a custom domain (free for the first year), 2GB storage, removal of Wix ads and much more. However, if you want to sell online and unlock features such as advanced analytics, you'll need to go for the Core plan or above, starting at $29/mo.

There’s also a 14-day money-back guarantee if things don’t work out.

Tools and features

Weebly scored high marks in our review for its easy-to-use drag and drop editor, good-looking website templates, and small business-friendly features such as its library of third-party apps and integrations (ecommerce, social media, marketing, customer support and more.)

It wasn't all good news - there are no blog templates, for instance, so you must design your own - but there were no critical issues, and overall Weebly is a strong all-round website builder for first-time users.

We found that although Weebly lacks some of the advanced functionality you find on other platform such as AI tools and a wide array of templates, unless you actually need those extras, its affordability and usability make it a great alternative to some others on this list.

Alternatives

Upgrading to the Professional plan ($12/mo on the annual plan) offers much more, getting you access to a free domain, SEO tools, advanced site stats, phone customer service, and support for a web store with unlimited items. Shopify is arguably one of the best ecommerce platforms on the market. Although it is widely used by business behemoths including Tesla, Gymshark, and PepsiCo, it also offers fantastic options for small businesses that want to set up, manage, and grow an online store.

Tools and features

The platform includes an intuitive website builder that makes building a site a breeze. Shopify’s website builder is the perfect choice for someone who is looking to start their online store, thanks to the huge range of ecommerce features that Shopify brings to the table.

These include advanced ecommerce tools that can be used to create a beautifully branded storefront and to manage products, inventory, payments, and shipping. Plus, you can integrate it with various marketplaces to further grow your business including Amazon, eBay, and even social media platforms, all making it a great option for omnichannel selling.

While doing a test run, we found Shopify’s dashboard quite neat and direct. It has a central hub for order tracking, customer management, analytics, and more. On top of that, the analytics suite gives detailed insights into customer behavior, sales trends, and the website’s performance.

It even has a wide variety of responsive themes that are customizable through a visual editor, where you can easily adjust layouts, fonts, colors, and other design elements without writing a single line of code. However, if you’re into coding and want to customize your theme further, Shopify gives access to the site’s JavaScript, CSS, and HTML, all giving you a free hand to make it your own.

You don’t get any call or email support with any of Shopify’s paid plans. However, there is 24/7 live chat customer support available on the platform. We tried reaching out to the support team on some queries we had, and they responded fairly quickly, which was impressive. So, if you want to build an online store and are looking for a one-stop solution, we are pretty confident that Shopify could be the right choice for you.

Alternatives

Although not a dedicated ecommerce platform like Shopify, Wix offers plenty of advanced ecommerce features and with plans starting at $12/mo, it is a lot more affordable.

However, if you really need all the extra tools that come with an ecommerce platform you can consider one of the leading ecommerce plaforms such as WooCommerce or BigCommerce. Our Magento vs WooCommerce vs BigCommerce vs Shopifyguide will help you decide which option is best for you.

Although not included in this guide, Square Online is a great alternative for ecommerce stores, offering plenty of specialist ecommerce tools, but with a lower price tag than other Shopify alternatives.

Plans and pricing

You can use Shopify’s website builder along with its other ecommerce features with a 3-day free trial, which is far from the most generous free trial. However, you can also opt to use the platform for an entire month for just $1, giving you ample time to build your site and test the platforms features.

After that, you’ll have to purchase a plan to continue using it. It has three different plans to choose from. The “Basic” plan is currently priced at $29/ month (yearly payment), the “Shopify” plan comes at $79/ month (yearly payment) where you get 5 additional staff accounts, and its “Advanced” plan costs $299/ month (yearly payment), offering 15 additional staff accounts.

How to choose the best small business website builder for you

The top website builder for small business will depend on your precise digital requirements, but there are some key site-building features which everyone needs to consider.

Ease of use

Ease of use is important for every level of user, but it's made up of many ingredients. The best services have quality templates to get you started right away, a simple drag-and-drop editor and in-depth knowledge base with simple explanations on how to create and run your site.

Design capabilities

Different website builders offer different approaches to designing websites. Which is right for you will depend on your confidence in design and how much time you have.

For example, some website builders offer a drag-and-drop approach to website building, allowing you to customize your site based on a number of predesigned sections and pages. Others offer full templates which can be edited to meet your needs. There are also several AI website builders that use information you provide to create a bespoke website in seconds.

Of course, many website builders offer a combination of all three of these approaches.

Marketing tools

Marketing tools help you engage your audience, bring more visitors to your website, and generate more sales. When looking for a website builder, look at which type of marketing tools they have on offer.

This may include email tools to help you design and send powerful marketing emails to SEO tools that help you rank your website higher on search engine result pages. Some of these may be built into the platform, or be included as an optional integration.

Integrations

Business integrations allow advanced users to link their websites to all kinds of essential business-oriented services. Common options include Google or Facebook analytics to track your visitors, Mailchimp for email marketing, or Social Stream to display social media streams on your site.

Check each website builder to see which integrations are on offer. Having these bundled into your website builder package can save you money (if you were going to pay for them anyway).

Ecommerce features

Ecommerce features are vital if you're hoping to sell online, but these vary hugely between website builders and plans. Make sure your preferred provider can handle the number of products you want to sell, and includes the payment, shipping and other tools you need.

Additional costs

It is also important to pay close attention to additional fees. For example, some website builders may offer ecommerce functionality on their cheaper plans, but charge a transaction fee on each sale. This can eat away at profits overtime, so is especially important for small businesses to consider.

Security

Website security is often overlooked, yet is an essential element to consider when picking a website builder. So, it is important to take some time to research what level of security is offered with your website builder.

Greater levels of security can help build authority and trust with your website visitors and protect your business from potential attacks.

Price

Pricing is important, but be careful when you're making comparisons. Some providers may offer good-looking deals, but only if you sign up for years, and sometimes costs soar on renewal. Read the small print carefully to be sure you understand what you're getting before signing up.

Best small business website builders FAQs

Does my small business really need a website?

All businesses can benefit from a website.

Why? Because a website can help potential new customers find your business online, act as an extension of your brand, and provide a central location where visitors can find all the information they may need such as where you are located, what you offer, and how they can buy your products and services.

Many small businesses can also use their website to sell directly to their customers, opening up an entirely new revenue stream.

Should I build my own website or hire a web designer?

Here are some key questions to consider when deciding whether you should build your own website or hire a web designer:

1. What is your budget?

Using a website builder will typically be a lot cheaper than working with a web designer or an agency. After all, you will be paying for the time, expertise, and experience that the designer brings to the table.

Ongoing costs will also need to be considered. Designers and agencies will typically charge an ongoing cost for maintenance along with a retainer or hourly rate for any updates you need to make. On the other hand, website builders offer a more predictable and usually more affordable monthly fee.

All things considered, if your budget is relatively low, a website builder is likely to be a better choice.

2. How much time do you have?

For busy small business owners, time can be a luxury. This is where working with a designer can be appealing. Although you will need to work with a designer to communicate your website needs and vision, they can work seamlessly behind the scenes whilst you get on with your day-to-day responsibilities.

Website builders, although easy to use, are going to take up more of your time as you work things out and get your website looking and working exactly as you want. However, with new AI tools that can automate everything from design to content writing, the time you have to spend on building your website is getting shorter.

3. What are your website goals?

What you want your website to achieve will help you decide whether a website builder or a designer is the best choice for you.

For example, those looking for a simple online store or a place to host information about their business would certainly only need the functionality offered by a website building platform. However, those who are looking for more advanced functionality, say an immersive shopping experience or bespoke design elements, may need to work with a designer to achieve this.

As website builders evolve, the gap between what they offer and what designers can achieve is getting smaller. So, even if you think you need something advanced for your website, it is worth checking whether it can be achieved via using a website builder before opting to hire a professional designer.

What's the best website builder for a creative business?

Although creative businesses could use any website builder we have listed on this page, they also typically have unique needs that can be better met by specific or dedicated website builders.

For example, Squarespace is widely considered as one of the best website builders for photographersdue to its beautiful templates and advanced image tools. Similarly, BandZoogle is a website builder dedicated to helping bands and musicians promote their talents and is viewed as one of the best website builders for musicians.

What is a website builder?

The answer to the question "What is a website builder?" is simple. A website builder is a tool that lets you build a website without any coding experience.

Typically you'll start by browsing a library of pre-built website designs, typically called 'templates.' A template might include common pages such as About, Services and Contact, for instance, with stylish stock images you could use right away.

Next, you'll use an editor to add new pages, and customize any content to suit your needs. Inserting your own text can be as easy as clicking in a box and starting to type, and usually you're able to add new types of content - forms, maps, videos, more - by dragging and dropping.

How long does it take to build a website for small business?

Estimating the time it might take to build your site is difficult, because it depends on so many factors: the website builder, its editor, the available templates, the features you're aiming to include, and more.

If your needs are simple and you have the main copy and images already, perhaps from an existing website, you could have something ready to go in two or three hours.

When you see what the best small business website builders have to offer, though, you might extend the site with more features: maps, product videos, image galleries, whatever works for you. Although these are easy to add, you might spend an hour or two learning how to produce the best results for a specific feature.

Web stores usually make for the most time-consuming of projects, extending into weeks or even months. Most of that is tied up in creating a product catalog, sorting out payment support, shipping and so on; the best small business website builders can still create a workable main store in as little as a day or two.

Can I create a small business website for free?

Yes, it is possible to create a website for free, but this won't typically be suitable for small business use.

Even the best free website builders typically have fewer features, for instance, more restrictions, as well as loads of ads, and no option to use your own domain. That could create such an unprofessional impression with potential customers that you'd be better off having no website at all.

Don't rule out free tiers completely, though. Wix has a free plan, and although it's not a patch on the paid offering, it's a great way to try before you buy and see if the service is right for you.

What is the easiest type of website to build?

Most small business website builders include templates for one-page websites, where users can find everything they need - product and service information, pictures, pricing, testimonials and contact details - simply by scrolling down a single page.

If your website needs are simple, or you can find templates covering your precise business type - wedding photography, say - then this as simple as website building gets. Just scroll down the template, replace the company name, product images, addresses, contact details and so on with your own, and you'll immediately have a polished and professional site.

If your company doesn't quite fit into a standard business niche, though, a one-page solution may not quite work for you. Fortunately, that's not a problem. Website templates are just a starting point, and if something's not right, you're free to rearrange sections, add pages, or do whatever else you like to build your ideal business home.
