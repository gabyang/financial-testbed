What You Missed On Wall Street On Wednesday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Netflix (NFLX) shares lost over a third of their value after the company unexpectedly lost 200,000 subscribers in the first quarter [more]
  • Procter & Gamble (PG) reported better-than-expected Q3 earnings and revenue [more]
  • IBM (IBM) also reported upbeat Q1 results [more]
  • Lululemon (LULU) announced a five year growth plan to double revenue to $12.5B by 2026 [more]
  • The Federal Reserve's latest Beige Book report said that economic activity expanded at a "moderate pace" during the period covered [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Pivotal Research and BofA double-downgraded Netflix (NFLX) to Sell-equivalent ratings after the company's "shocking" subscriber decline while a slew of others on Wall Street went to Hold ratings on the streaming giant after last night's earnings report [more]
  • Citi analyst Ronald Josey upgraded Alphabet (GOOGL) to Buy from Neutral after assuming coverage of the internet sector [more]
  • Halliburton (HAL) downgraded to Equal Weight at Stephens following outperformance [more]
  • Barclays upgrades Avis (CAR) to neutral on "constructive" rental stance [more]
  • Alkermes (ALKS) initiated with a Buy at Goldman Sachs [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • I-Mab (IMAB) is exploring a possible sale amid takeover interest, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Trump-linked Digital World Acquisition (DWAC) was named as a short idea by Kerrisdale Capital [more]
  • Shopify (SHOP) and Facebook (FB) are at adds over data, The Information reports [more]
  • Blackstone (BX) is targeting a raise of $6B-$7B for its newest green energy credit fund, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • Rite Aid (RAD) recently turned down a PE firm's $14.60 per share takeover bid, NY Post reports [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • AlloVir (ALVR) soars after announcing the FDA granted Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy designation for posoleucel [more]
  • Cano Health (CANO) rises after UBS analyst Andrew Monk initiated coverage of the stock with a Buy rating and $15 price target [more]
  • Healthcare Services (HCSG) advances after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Arcturus Therapeutics (ARCT) declines after reporting data from its ongoing Phase 1/2/3 trial evaluating its mRNA vaccine candidate against COVID-19 disease [more]
  • Shopify (SHOP) falls after Bloomberg reported that the company is in talks to buy Deliverr for over $2B [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Abbott (ABT) reported upbeat Q1 results and reiterated its FY22 earnings guidance [more]
  • Baker Hughes (BKR) reported downbeat Q1 results [more]
  • Anthem (ANTM) reported better-than-expected Q1 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Farmers & Merchants (FMAO) reported upbeat Q1 earnings [more]
  • Rogers Communications (RCI) raised its FY22 revenue guidance [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 249.59, or 0.71%, to 35,160.79, the Nasdaq lost 166.59, or 1.22%, to 13,453.07, and the S&P 500 declined 2.76, or 0.06%, to 4,459.45.
