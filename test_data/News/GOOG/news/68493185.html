Wall Street in Fives - Must Read Lists for Friday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these Top Fives, as compiled by the editors of The Fly.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Panasonic (PCRFY) has sold its Tesla (TSLA) stake for $3.6B [more]
  • Rockwell Automation (ROK) has agreed to acquire Plex Systems for $2.2B in cash [more]
  • Virgin Galactic (SPCE) rockets higher after FAA approval for a full commercial launch license that allows the company to fly commercial customers to space [more]
  • Amazon (AMZN) and Google (GOOGL) are facing a formal fake review inquiry in the U.K. [more]
  • IKONICS (IKNX) and bitcoin mining company TeraWulf (WULF) announced a merger agreement [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Netflix (NFLX) upgraded to Outperform from Neutral at Credit Suisse [more]
  • BlackBerry (BB) downgraded to Reduce from Hold at TD Securities, cut to Underperformer from Neutral at CIBC and lowered to Sell from Hold at Canaccord [more]
  • Redburn puts Sell ratings on AT&T (T) and Verizon (VZ), Buy on Comcast (CMCSA) [more]
  • NetApp (NTAP) upgraded to Outperform from Market Perform at Raymond James [more]
  • Baird upgrades Luminar (LAZR) to Outperform ahead of expected outlook hike [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Qualcomm (QCOM) sees high growth in the 5G basebands market in Q1, DigiTimes says [more]
  • Credit Suisse (CS) executives are seeking a new overhaul plan, which may include a merger with UBS (UBS), Reuters reports [more]
  • Robinhood's (HOOD) IPO plans are being slowed by the SEC's review of its prospectus, Bloomberg says [more]
  • Twilio (TWLO) and Asana (ASAN) are aiming to list their shares on the Long Term Stock Exchange, WSJ reports [more]
  • Southwest (LUV) plans to increase its minimum wage to $15 per hour, Bloomberg says [more]

4.﻿ MOVERS:

  • Tonix Pharmaceuticals (TNXP) rises after CEO Seth Lederman said that plans are advancing to construct a commercial-scale facility to develop and manufacture vaccines [more]
  • Big banks, such as Wells Fargo (WFC) and Bank of America (BAC), rally after the Fed said that it would end COVID event restrictions [more]
  • Nokia (NOK) higher in New York after Goldman Sachs analyst Alexander Duval upgraded the stock to Buy [more]
  • Oasis Midstream Partners (OMP) declines after announcing a 3.6M share Spot Secondary offering priced at $24.60 [more]
  • Rite Aid (RAD) falls after JPMorgan analyst Lisa Gill downgraded the stock to Underweight from Neutral with a price target of $12, down from $21 [more]

5. EARNINGS:

  • Nike (NKE) jumps after reporting Q4 earnings, giving long-term outlook [more]
  • FedEx (FDX) slips after reporting Q4 results [more]
  • CarMax (KMX) gains after reporting growth in units sold in Q1 [more]
  • JinkoSolar (JKS) rises after reporting surprise earnings for Q1 [more]
  • Paychex (PAYX) higher after providing FY22 guidance [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow rose 237.02, or 0.69%, to 34,433.84, the Nasdaq lost 9.32, or 0.065%, to 14,360.39, and the S&P 500 advanced 14.21, or 0.33%, to 4,280.70.
