The Good, The Flat, and the Volatile: Sorting Through the Sideways Market

Corey Rosenbloom0 Comments

August 1, 2023

Last week had the potential to be a major market mover with all the earnings and Fed and inflation data each day, but the week ended only slight higher. While we have fewer big earnings reporting this week, we do have two of the biggest and we also have the big Monthly Jobs Report on Friday. In tonight's video, we focus on trades and ideas in 'sideways stocks' that are flat with the market, rather than the more exciting trending popular names to try to find trades others may be missing - don't miss these opportunities!
