What You Missed On Wall Street On Tuesday
Fly Intel
Sam Martinelli - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.

1. STOCK NEWS:

  • Target (TGT) reported better than expected Q4 earnings and guided FY22 adjusted EPS growing by a high-single digits percentage [more]
  • Zoom Video (ZM) reported Q4 results Piper Sandler called "relatively mixed" and gave fiscal 2023 guidance that came in below expectations [more]
  • Workday (WDAY) reported better-than-expected Q4 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Chevron (CVX) raised its share buyback guidance to $5B-$10B per year [more]
  • Stellantis (STLA) announced its new strategic plan, known as "Dare Forward 2030" [more]

2. WALL STREET CALLS:

  • Kroger (KR) upgraded to Outperform at Telsey Advisory and added to "Tactical Outperform" list at Evercore ISI ahead of earnings [more]
  • Wedbush upgraded Logitech (LOGI) to Outperform [more]
  • GoodRx (GDRX) was downgraded to Market Perform at Cowen and to Neutral at Credit Suisse after its earnings report and guidance [more]
  • Truist downgraded Comcast (CMCSA) to Hold from Buy with a price target of $50, down from $70 [more]
  • Benchmark analyst Mark Zgutowicz initiated coverage of Meta Platforms (FB) and Twitter (TWTR) with Hold ratings and started coverage of Snap (SNAP) with a Buy [more]

3. AROUND THE WEB:

  • Uber (UBER) is accelerating the sale of its Yandex.Taxi joint venture, Bloomberg reports [more]
  • The FTC is preparing to challenge Amazon's (AMZN) $8.5B bid for MGM Studios, The Information reports [more]
  • WeWork (WE) CEO Sandeep Mathrani told Bloomberg it is not planning to retreat from Russia [more]
  • Alphabet's (GOOGL) Waymo will start charging money for autonomous tax rides in San Francisco, The Verge reports [more]
  • Graf Acquisition IV (GFOR) is in merger talks with Urgently, Bloomberg says [more]

4. MOVERS:

  • LivePerson (LPSN) higher after Starboard disclosed a 9.7% stake in the company and nominated several director candidates [more]
  • Peabody Energy (BTU) gains after launching R3 Renewables in a joint venture with Summit Partners and Riverstone Credit [more]
  • iQIYI (IQ) and Baidu (BIDU) rise in New York trading after reporting quarterly results [more]
  • Vroom (VRM) declines after Wells Fargo downgraded the stock on the heels of the company's earnings report [more]
  • Ambarella (AMBA) and Heron Therapeutics (HRTX) fall after reporting quarterly results [more]

5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE:

  • Domino's Pizza (DPZ) reported Q4 results that missed estimates and its CEO announced plans to retire [more]
  • J.M. Smucker (SJM) reported upbeat Q3 results [more]
  • Wendy's (WEN) reported upbeat Q4 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Sea Limited (SE) sank in New York trading after reporting Q4 results, issuing FY22 outlook [more]
  • Kohl's (KSS) reported better-than-expected Q4 earnings [more]

INDEXES:

The Dow fell 597.65, or 1.76%, to 33,294.95, the Nasdaq lost 218.94, or 1.59%, to 13,532.46, and the S&P 500 declined 67.68, or 1.55%, to 4,306.26.
