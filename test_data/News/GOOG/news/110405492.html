What You Missed On Wall Street This Morning
Fly Intel
Jason Keil - email
Five top five lists with the news and calls moving stocks, powered by The Fly.

Get caught up quickly on the top news and calls moving stocks with these five Top Five lists.


1. STOCK NEWS: 

  • Spirit Airlines (SAVE) said that JetBlue's (JBLU) enhanced proposal is not superior to that of Frontier Group (ULCC) [more]
  • Berkshire Hathaway's (BRK.A) Warren Buffett unveiled big investments in Chevron (CVX) and Activision Blizzard (ATVI) at Berkshire's annual meeting [more]
  • Electric car makers Nio (NIO) and Li Auto (LI) reported April deliveries [more]
  • Pfizer's (PFE) COVID pill failed to show a statistically significant benefit in preventive therapy trial [more]
  • G-III Apparel (GIII) will acquire the Karl Lagerfeld brand for $210M [more]


2. WALL STREET CALLS: 

  • Bath & Body Works (BBWI) upgraded to Outperform from Market Perform at Cowen [more]
  • Accolade (ACCD) upgraded to Equal Weight from Underweight at Wells Fargo [more]
  • Piper Sandler downgrades KeyCorp (KEY) and Community Bank System (CBU) to Underweight from Neutral [more]
  • TAL Education (TAL) and New Oriental Education (EDU) upgraded to Neutral from Underweight at JPMorgan [more]
  • Comerica (CMA) upgraded to Overweight from Neutral at Piper Sandler [more]


3. AROUND THE WEB: 

  • Google's (GOOGL) Pixel Watch will be powered by 300mAh, 9to5Google reports [more]
  • Twitter (TWTR) CEO Parag Agrawal admitted that things under Elon Musk will likely be "different," Business Insider says [more]
  • Starbucks (SBUX) has accused labor organizers of harassing workers and customers, WSJ reports [more]
  • Disney's (DIS) corporate affairs head Geoff Morrell has left the company, WSJ says [more]
  • Alimentation Couche-Tard (ANCTF) and EG Group in deal discussions, WSJ reports [more]


4. MOVERS: 

  • Hilltop Holdings (HTH) gains after commencing a modified “Dutch auction” tender offer to buy $400M in stock [more]
  • Innoviz (INVZ) higher in New York after being selected by a large vehicle manufacturer to become its direct LiDAR supplier [more]
  • Vaxxinity (VAXX) slips after receiving FDA Fast Track Designation for UB-311 [more]
  • Vertex Pharmaceuticals (VRTX) lower after providing an update on its Phase 1/2 clinical trial of VX-880 [more]
  • Sandstorm Gold (SAND) falls in New York after announcing it will acquire Nomad Royalty (NSR) for $590M and buy nine royalties and one stream from BaseCore Metal for total consideration of $525M [more]


5. EARNINGS/GUIDANCE: 

  • WEC Energy (WEC) provided a "beat and raise" report for Q1 [more]
  • Moody's (MCO) reported downbeat Q1 earnings and lowered its FY22 earnings guidance [more]
  • Global Payments (GPN) reported better-than-expected Q1 results [more]
  • ON Semiconductor (ON) reported upbeat Q1 earnings and revenue [more]
  • Itron (ITRI) reported downbeat Q1 revenue [more]


INDEXES: 

Near midday, the Dow was down 92.53, or 0.28%, to 32,884.68, the Nasdaq was up 34.03, or 0.28%, to 12,368.67, and the S&P 500 was down 9.49, or 0.23%, to 4,122.44.
