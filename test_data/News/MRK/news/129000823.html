Merck Swings to 2Q Profit Ahead of Views, Lowers FY24 Adjusted EPS Outlook

Published: 2024-07-30 07:18:56 ET MRK

By Michael Susin

Merck & Co. on Tuesday reported a better-than-expected swing to profit in the second quarter, and cut its earnings guidance to reflect Eyebiotech and Harpoon Therapeutics acquisitions.

The company reported a swing to net income of $5.46 billion for the second quarter, or $2.14 a share, from a loss of $5.98 billion, or a loss of $2.35 a share, in the comparable quarter a year ago. This compares with market expectations of $2 a share, based on analysts polled by FactSet.

Adjusted earnings were $2.28 a share. According to FactSet, analysts were expecting $2.16 a share.

Sales rose to $16.11 billion from $15.04 billion, beating the $15.87 billion FactSet consensus.

Looking ahead, the company lifted and narrowed its worldwide sales expectations to a range of $63.4 billion to $64.4 billion, compared with previous sales range of $63.1 billion and $64.3 billion.

Adjusted EPS is now expected to be between $7.94 and $8.04 a share, down from the previous target of $8.53 to $8.65 a share, including one-time charges of $0.26 and $0.51 per share for the acquisitions of Harpoon and Eyebiotech, respectively.
