Coca-Cola Europacific manager sells USD645,000 in shares

Published: 2023-12-18 09:36:06 ET KO

Coca-Cola Europacific Partners PLC - Uxbridge, England-based bottling partner for Coca-Cola in 29 countries - Stephen Moorhouse, general manager for Great Britain, sells 10,000 shares at average USD64.47, worth USD644,654, on Friday in New York.

Current stock price: EUR59.10 in London

12-month change: up 16%
