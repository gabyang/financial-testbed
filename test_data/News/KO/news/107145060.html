Correction to Coca-Cola Unit Case Volume Headlines on Feb. 10

Published: 2022-03-14 15:50:02 ET KO

Coca-Cola Co.'s fourth-quarter worldwide unit case volume rose 9%. "*Coca-Cola 4Q Worldwide Unit Case Volume Down 1% >KO" at 7:01 a.m. ET Feb. 10 misstated the change as a 1% decline. Its fourth-quarter Europe, Middle East and Africa unit case volume rose 11%. "*Coca-Cola 4Q EMEA Unit Case Volume Up 4% >KO" misstated the percentage increase. Fourth-quarter unit case volume in North America rose 8%. "*Coca-Cola 4Q North America Unit Case Volume Up 4% >KO" misstated the percentage increase. Fourth-quarter Latin American unit case volume rose 5%. "*Coca-Cola 4Q Latin America Unit Case Volume Down 10% >KO" misstated the change as a 10% drop.

(END) Dow Jones Newswires
